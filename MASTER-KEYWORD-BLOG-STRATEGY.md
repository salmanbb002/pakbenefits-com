# pakbenefits.com — Master Keyword-to-Blog Strategy

**Source of truth for content planning.** Built from the complete `keyword-clusters.csv` (201 keywords, 13 topical clusters, 20 sub-topics) cross-referenced against every live page on the site as of 21 Aug 2026: 8 pillar category pages + 24 published blog articles.

**Core rule:** one blog owns one distinct *sub-topic intent*, not one blog per literal keyword string. The CSV contains many keywords that are the same search intent with a different year, typo, or word order ("8171 check online 2023" / "...2025" / "...2026," "ehsas kafalat" / "ehsaas kafalat"). Publishing a separate page per variant is a doorway-page pattern — it dilutes ranking signal across near-duplicate pages instead of concentrating it on one authoritative page, and Google treats it as a quality problem, not a coverage win. Every cluster below is planned as **one pillar page + a small number of distinct-angle blog posts**, each targeting every keyword variant in its bucket through natural on-page language rather than a 1:1 page-per-keyword mapping.

Site pillar pages (the 9 permanent category hubs each cluster maps to):

| Pillar slug | Name | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `/8171/` | 8171 Eligibility & Status | Official status-check route and next steps |
| `/bisp-registration/` | BISP Registration | Survey and registration-desk preparation |
