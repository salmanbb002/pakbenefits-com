## 7. FAQ draft

1. **How can I check my BISP status by CNIC online?**  
   Open `https://8171.bisp.gov.pk/`, enter the relevant CNIC and the image code, then submit. Save the exact response privately and follow the official next step it provides.

2. **Do I need a BISP username and password for the public CNIC check?**  
   The current public page shows a CNIC field and an image-code field, not a public account login. Do not confuse a separate BISP login service with the public status form.

3. **What does the image code mean on the 8171 portal?**  
   It is a human-verification field used to submit the form. Enter the characters shown in the image; a validation failure is not an eligibility result.

4. **Does checking a CNIC online register a household for BISP?**  
   No. The portal retrieves an available status. A new NSER survey or household update follows a separate process through BISP's registration system.

5. **What does no record mean on the BISP portal?**  
   It means the lookup completed but did not return a record. BISP's guidance directs an adult household member to the nearest Benazir Registration Desk for a household survey.

6. **Does an eligible response mean a payment is ready?**  
   Not necessarily. Follow the exact portal instruction and rely on official 8171 payment communication before traveling to a collection point.

7. **Is the BISP CNIC check the same as NADRA verification?**  
   No. This is a BISP programme-status lookup using a CNIC. NADRA manages identity records and is a separate entity.

8. **What should I do if the public form does not load?**  
   Verify the BISP host and both fields, test another browser or connection, and use the dedicated portal-troubleshooting guide. Do not enter a CNIC on a third-party checker.
