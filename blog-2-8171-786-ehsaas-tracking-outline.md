## 7. FAQ draft

1. **Is 786 an official BISP or Ehsaas number?**  
   BISP identifies 8171 as its official messaging number. If 786 is presented as a BISP status or payment route, do not use it as an alternative without direct confirmation from BISP.

2. **Are 5771 and 7181 BISP check numbers?**  
   They do not match BISP's stated official messaging number. Verify through 8171, the BISP-hosted portal, the BISP helpline, or a tehsil office instead of trying similar number combinations.

3. **What is the current official 8171 website?**  
   The public CNIC status page verified on 14 August 2026 is `https://8171.bisp.gov.pk/`.

4. **Was `8171.pass.gov.pk` an official address?**  
   BISP's 2022 NSER guide documented that address. It is historical context; the current public route verified for this article is hosted at `8171.bisp.gov.pk`.

5. **What was `ehsaastracking.pass.gov.pk`?**  
   It was reported as an Ehsaas-era tracking portal. Readers searching that old phrase should use the current BISP-hosted public portal rather than assume an archived route is still active.

6. **Can I trust a message that contains the number 8171?**  
   Check the actual sender, not only the text inside the message. BISP communication should originate from 8171.

7. **What should I do after sending my CNIC to an unknown number?**  
   Stop engaging, share no OTP or financial credentials, preserve the message privately, and contact BISP through `0800-26477` if the sender claimed to represent the programme.
