## 3. Scope of Services Available at Karachi BISP Centers

Karachi Tehsil Dynamic Registration Centers offer a comprehensive suite of social safety net procedures:

1. **NSER Dynamic Survey Registration**: Families whose circumstances have changed or who have never been surveyed can complete a digital interview covering housing, assets, utility consumption, and employment to calculate their official PMT score.
2. **Benazir Kafaalat Quarterly Cash Disbursals**: Women whose PMT score falls at or below 32 are formally registered for the regular quarterly unconditional cash assistance of Rs. 13,500.
3. **Biometric Troubleshooting & Non-BVS Forms**: Elderly citizens, textile mill laborers, and domestic workers experiencing biometric verification failures (Error 93 / 99) at ATM points can submit official Non-BVS verification applications for alternate cash pickup.
4. **Benazir Nashonuma Desks (Sindh)**: Specialized desks dedicated to pregnant women and mothers with children under 2 years of age, offering nutritional support and specialized cash grants.
5. **Benazir Taleemi Wazaif Education Stipends**: Mothers enrolled in Kafaalat can register their school-going children to receive quarterly educational stipends ranging from Rs. 2,000 to Rs. 4,500.

## 4. Essential Documentation Checklist for Karachi Residents

To ensure smooth processing on your first visit, bring the following original official identification documents:

- **Original Computerized National Identity Card (Smart CNIC)**: The female applicant must present an unexpired NADRA CNIC or Smart Card. Damaged cards or photocopies without the original card will be turned away.
- **Computerized Child Registration Certificates (B-Form / CRC)**: Issued by NADRA for every child residing within the household under the age of 22.
- **Active Mobile Phone & Registered SIM Card**: A mobile SIM registered in the applicant’s own CNIC name. BISP exclusively communicates eligibility decisions and payment updates from `8171`.
- **Household Electricity Bill (K-Electric)**: Latest paid K-Electric bill showing consumer number, residential address, and meter details.
- **Special Category Documents**:
  - *Widows*: Computerized Union Council Death Certificate of the deceased husband recorded at NADRA.
  - *Divorced Women*: Computerized NADRA divorce registration deed.
  - *Special Persons*: Disability certificate and NADRA Special CNIC with the wheelchair emblem.

## 5. Step-by-Step Registration & Resurvey Process at Karachi Desks

The registration workflow across all Karachi BISP centers is fully computerized:

1. **Arrival & Token Generation**: Arrive between 8:00 AM and 10:30 AM. Present your original CNIC at the entry counter to receive a computerized queue token.
2. **Document Pre-Screening**: A desk official inspects your original CNIC, B-Forms, and K-Electric bill to confirm completeness.
3. **NSER Digital Interview**: When your token number is displayed, proceed to the data entry terminal. The operator enters your family composition, income, housing materials, and utility costs into the central NSER system.
4. **Biometric Live Scanning & Photo Capture**: Place your thumbs and fingers on the electronic optical scanner and face the webcam for a live facial snapshot.
5. **Summary Slip Verification**: Review the printed summary sheet to confirm that all recorded details are correct. Sign or thumb-impress the document and retain the stamped customer slip.
6. **System Evaluation & 8171 SMS Notification**: BISP headquarters processes the data against NADRA records and computes the PMT score. An official decision SMS from `8171` is delivered within 4 to 8 weeks.
