# PAVE Electric Bike Scheme 2026: Apply Online at pave.gov.pk & Subsidy Guide

**Written by:** Muhammad Salman | **Published:** October 04, 2026

The **Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification** (**PAVE**) scheme is a federal government initiative that provides an upfront direct **Rs 80,000 Subsidy** on approved electric motorcycles and up to **Rs 400,000 Subsidy** on **Electric Three-Wheelers**. Applicants apply online at **pave.gov.pk** under Phase 2's **First-Come, First-Served Queue Model** with **Zero Percent Markup Financing** options.

---

## What Is the PAVE Electric Bike Scheme in Pakistan?

The **Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification** (**PAVE**) scheme is the federal government’s flagship subsidy program designed to accelerate the adoption of electric two-wheelers and **Electric Three-Wheelers** across Pakistan. Introduced under the **New Energy Vehicle Policy 2025–2030**, which received formal Federal Cabinet approval on August 26, 2025, **PAVE** aims to subsidize 2.2 million electric vehicles over four years. The federal government has committed Rs 100.36 billion in total subsidy funding through 2030 to transition daily commuters, students, and small commercial operators away from expensive petrol-powered transport.

**PAVE** operates through a centralized digital portal at **pave.gov.pk**, establishing a transparent mechanism for applicant verification, quota allocation, and subsidy processing. By directly reducing the retail acquisition cost of electric motorcycles and e-rickshaws, the policy addresses Pakistan's heavy foreign exchange burden caused by imported fossil fuels.

*Section Tier 1/2 Coverage Notes:* Covered Tier-1 entities **Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification**, **New Energy Vehicle Policy 2025–2030**, **pave.gov.pk**; Tier-2 entity **Electric Three-Wheelers**. Unused Tier-3 terms for this section: *Metro E-Vehicles* (parked for competitor-specific section).

### Which Government Agencies Implement PAVE?

The **Engineering Development Board** (**EDB**), operating under the **Ministry of Industries and Production**, serves as the primary executive authority for the **PAVE** scheme. The **Engineering Development Board** evaluates **Original Equipment Manufacturers** (**OEMs**), verifies EV technical standards, pre-qualifies eligible electric bike models, and coordinates with the **State Bank of Pakistan** (**SBP**) to manage direct financial disbursements.

*Section Tier 1/2 Coverage Notes:* Covered Tier-1 entities **Engineering Development Board**, **Ministry of Industries and Production**, **Original Equipment Manufacturer**; Tier-2 entity **State Bank of Pakistan**.

## How Much Subsidy Does PAVE Offer on Electric Bikes & Rickshaws?

Under the federal **PAVE** framework, approved electric motorcycles qualify for an **Upfront Point-of-Sale Deduction** of **Rs 80,000 Subsidy** per unit. **Electric Three-Wheelers**, including passenger e-rickshaws and commercial cargo loaders, receive higher financial support ranging up to **Rs 400,000 Subsidy** depending on vehicle battery capacity and **OEM** specifications. Additionally, qualifying applicants can finance the remaining vehicle balance through partner commercial banks with **Zero Percent Markup Financing** over a 24-month repayment tenure.

To secure the financial subsidy, applicants must choose an **Engineering Development Board**-approved vehicle model from a pre-qualified **Original Equipment Manufacturer**. Purchasing an uncertified electric bike from the open retail market renders the buyer ineligible for government financial assistance.

*Section Tier 1/2 Coverage Notes:* Covered Tier-1 entities **Rs 80,000 Subsidy**, **Original Equipment Manufacturer**, **Engineering Development Board**; Tier-2 entities **Rs 400,000 Subsidy**, **Zero Percent Markup Financing**, **Upfront Point-of-Sale Deduction**.

### How Does the Upfront Point-of-Sale Subsidy Deduction Work?

In Phase 2, the subsidy is applied as an **Upfront Point-of-Sale Deduction**, allowing approved buyers to pay only the net discounted price to the authorized dealer. The **Engineering Development Board** subsequently reimburses the **Rs 80,000 Subsidy** balance directly to the vehicle manufacturer through the **State Bank of Pakistan**. This eliminated the lengthy post-purchase reimbursement waiting period that previously burdened early buyers.

*Section Tier 1/2 Coverage Notes:* Covered Tier-1 entity **Engineering Development Board**; Tier-2 entities **Upfront Point-of-Sale Deduction**, **State Bank of Pakistan**.

## What Changed in PAVE Phase 2 vs. Phase 1?

Phase 2 of the **PAVE** scheme replaced **Computerized E-Balloting** with a strict **First-Come, First-Served Queue Model** following **Economic Coordination Committee** (**ECC**) approval on May 5, 2026. The policy pivot occurred after official **Ministry of Industries and Production** data revealed severe bottlenecks in Phase 1: commercial banks approved only 9 percent of routed applications, delivering just 5,409 vehicles out of a 41,000-unit quota. In contrast, self-finance applicants achieved a 99.6 percent delivery rate, with 1,334 out of 1,339 applicants receiving their electric bikes without delay.

To remedy bank delays, Phase 2 targets 76,000 electric motorcycles and 2,170 three-wheelers under a streamlined application model. Because allocations are now processed chronologically based on submission timestamps, waiting for an arbitrary deadline increases the risk of missing available quotas.

*Section Tier 1/2 Coverage Notes:* Covered Tier-1 entities **Economic Coordination Committee**, **First-Come, First-Served Queue Model**, **Computerized E-Balloting**, **Ministry of Industries and Production**.

| **Total Vehicle Quota** | 41,000 Units (40,000 bikes, 1,000 rickshaws) | 78,170 Units (76,000 bikes, 2,170 loaders) |

## Who Is Eligible for the Federal PAVE E-Bike Scheme?

To qualify for an electric bike subsidy under the federal **PAVE** program, applicants must meet specific civic, age, and documentation standards established by the **Engineering Development Board**:

* **Citizenship:** Must be a Pakistani citizen holding a valid Computerized National Identity Card (**CNIC Verification**). Residents of Azad Jammu & Kashmir (AJK) and Gilgit-Baltistan (GB) are fully eligible.
* **Age Requirement:** Applicants must be between 18 and 65 years old at the time of online registration.
* **SIM Ownership:** The mobile phone number provided during registration must be registered under the applicant’s own CNIC in Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) records.
* **Quota Reserved:** A mandatory **Women Quota** of 25 percent is reserved specifically for female applicants to encourage female mobility in academic and professional sectors.

*Section Tier 1/2 Coverage Notes:* Covered Tier-1 entity **Engineering Development Board**; Tier-2 terms **CNIC Verification**, **Women Quota**.

## How to Apply Online for PAVE Scheme at pave.gov.pk?

Submitting an online application for the **PAVE** electric bike scheme requires completing six structured steps on the official government portal:

1. **Portal Account Registration:** Visit **pave.gov.pk** and click on the "Register" button. Enter your full name, CNIC number, and PTA-registered mobile phone number.
2. **OTP Mobile Verification:** Enter the One-Time Password (OTP) sent to your mobile phone via SMS to complete **CNIC Verification** and activate portal credentials.
3. **Fill Applicant Profile:** Provide personal details, permanent address, occupational status, and monthly household income information.
4. **Vehicle & OEM Selection:** Select your preferred vehicle category (electric motorcycle or loader) and select an **Engineering Development Board**-approved **OEM** manufacturer model.
5. **Upload Verification Documents:** Attach clean scanned copies of your CNIC (front and back), recent passport-size photograph, and proof of income or institutional student ID.
6. **Submit & Receive Tracking ID:** Review all submitted details carefully before final submission. Upon successful entry, the system generates a unique **PAVE** Tracking ID for real-time status monitoring.

*Section Tier 1/2 Coverage Notes:* Covered Tier-1 entities **pave.gov.pk**, **Engineering Development Board**, **Original Equipment Manufacturer**; Tier-2 term **CNIC Verification**.

### Required Documents for PAVE Registration

Applicants should prepare scanned digital files (JPEG or PDF format under 2 MB) of the following mandatory documents before initiating registration:

* Valid CNIC for **CNIC Verification** (front and back sides).
* Passport-size photograph with a light background.
* Proof of income (recent salary slip, bank statement, or business proof).
* Student ID card or active enrollment certificate (if applying under the student quota).
* Driving license or learner's driving permit (required prior to final vehicle handover).

## PAVE Federal Scheme vs. Punjab CM E-Bike Scheme: What Is the Difference?

Many applicants confuse the federal **PAVE** scheme with provincial programs like the **CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme**. While both initiatives promote electric mobility, they operate under distinct governance, funding channels, and portal infrastructures.

| Comparison Feature | Federal PAVE Scheme | CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme |
|---|---|---|
| **Jurisdiction & Reach** | Nationwide (All Provinces, AJK, GB) | Punjab Province Only |
| **Official Application Portal**| **pave.gov.pk** | `bikes.punjab.gov.pk` |
| **Managing Authority** | **Engineering Development Board** | PITB / **Bank of Punjab** |
| **Primary Subsidy Mechanism** | **Upfront Point-of-Sale Deduction** (Rs 80,000) | Bank Subsidized Interest & Down Payment |
| **Target Audience** | General Public, **Women Quota** (25%), Small Business | Enrolled University & College Students |
| **Financing Partner** | Commercial Banks | **Bank of Punjab** |

*Section Tier 1/2 Coverage Notes:* Covered Tier-1 entities **PAVE**, **pave.gov.pk**, **Engineering Development Board**; Tier-2 entities **CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme**, **Bank of Punjab**, **Women Quota**, **Upfront Point-of-Sale Deduction**.

## Why Was My PAVE Application Delayed or Rejected?

Application processing delays or rejections under the **PAVE** scheme typically stem from technical inconsistencies during the automated verification phase. Analyzing historical Phase 1 data highlights three primary failure points:

* **Mobile SIM Ownership Mismatch:** If the mobile number entered on **pave.gov.pk** is registered under a family member's CNIC rather than the applicant's own CNIC, the automated PTA-NADRA **CNIC Verification** check fails instantly.
* **Incomplete Income or Bank Documentation:** Applicants choosing bank-financed installment options who fail to provide verifiable proof of monthly income are rejected during bank credit risk assessments.
* **Selecting Non-Approved Vehicle Models:** Selecting an electric bike model from an unaccredited manufacturer invalidates the subsidy claim. Always confirm your model appears on the **Engineering Development Board** pre-qualified **OEM** vehicle list.

*Section Tier 1/2 Coverage Notes:* Covered Tier-1 entities **pave.gov.pk**, **Engineering Development Board**, **Original Equipment Manufacturer**; Tier-2 term **CNIC Verification**.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the official website for PAVE electric bike registration?
The official website for federal **PAVE** registration is **pave.gov.pk**. Applicants must avoid third-party websites or unofficial registration portals to protect personal CNIC data and prevent scam attempts.

### How much is the federal subsidy on electric bikes under PAVE?
The federal government provides an **Upfront Point-of-Sale Deduction** of **Rs 80,000 Subsidy** on approved electric motorcycles. For **Electric Three-Wheelers**, such as e-rickshaws and cargo loaders, the subsidy reaches up to **Rs 400,000 Subsidy**.

### Is PAVE Phase 2 based on balloting or first-come, first-served?
**PAVE** Phase 2 operates on a **First-Come, First-Served Queue Model** following **Economic Coordination Committee** authorization on May 5, 2026. Submissions are processed strictly in the order of portal timestamp receipts.

### Can I apply for PAVE if I am already enrolled in the Punjab CM E-Bike Scheme?
Yes, citizens may apply for the federal **PAVE** scheme at **pave.gov.pk** even if registered for provincial programs like the **CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme**, provided they have not already received a subsidized vehicle under either scheme.

### How is the Rs 80,000 subsidy disbursed to the buyer in Phase 2?
In Phase 2, the **Rs 80,000 Subsidy** is applied as an **Upfront Point-of-Sale Deduction**. The buyer pays only the net discounted price to the dealer, and the **State Bank of Pakistan** transfers the subsidy to the **Original Equipment Manufacturer**.

### What is the age limit for PAVE electric bike applicants?
Applicants applying for an electric motorcycle or rickshaw under the **PAVE** scheme must be between 18 and 65 years of age on the date of portal registration.

### Is there a reserved quota for female applicants in the PAVE scheme?
Yes, the federal **PAVE** policy reserves a mandatory **Women Quota** of 25 percent specifically for female applicants across all provinces and federal territories.

### What documents are needed to complete the PAVE online application?
Applicants must upload a scanned copy of their valid CNIC for **CNIC Verification**, a passport-size photo, proof of income or salary slip, and a student ID card if applying under educational quotas.

### Why did Phase 1 of PAVE face delays in vehicle deliveries?
Phase 1 faced delays because commercial banks approved only 9 percent of financing applications, delivering 5,409 out of 41,000 target units. Phase 2 resolved this by introducing upfront point-of-sale deductions and first-come processing.

### Which banks are offering 0% markup financing for PAVE e-bikes?
Participating financial institutions include commercial partner banks such as BankIslami and **Bank of Punjab**, which provide 24-month **Zero Percent Markup Financing** installment plans for approved applicants.
