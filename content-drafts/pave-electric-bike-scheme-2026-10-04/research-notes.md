## 2. Head-Entity Research (Authoritative Sources)

### Core Head Entities

1. **Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification (PAVE)**
   * **Canonical Name:** Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification
   * **Type:** Government Program / EV Policy Initiative
   * **Aliases:** PAVE, PAVE Scheme, PM Electric Bike Scheme, Federal EV Subsidy Scheme
   * **`sameAs` / Official URL:** `https://pave.gov.pk/`
   * **Attributes / Relationships:**
     * Formally launched under the **New Energy Vehicle (NEV) Policy 2025–2030** (approved by Federal Cabinet on August 26, 2025).
     * Implemented by the **Engineering Development Board (EDB)** under the **Ministry of Industries and Production**.
     * Subsidises electric two-wheelers (up to **Rs 80,000**) and three-wheelers/rickshaws (up to **Rs 400,000**).
     * Total budget allocation: **Rs 100.36 billion** over 5 years (2025–2030).

2. **Engineering Development Board (EDB)**
   * **Canonical Name:** Engineering Development Board
   * **Type:** Government Organization
   * **Aliases:** EDB, EDB Pakistan
   * **`sameAs` URL:** `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering_Development_Board`
   * **Attributes / Relationships:**
     * Apex government body framing industrial & automotive manufacturing policy in Pakistan.
     * Evaluates and pre-qualifies Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) for PAVE eligibility.
     * Manages direct subsidy disbursement to OEMs via the State Bank of Pakistan (SBP).

3. **New Energy Vehicle Policy 2025–2030 (NEV Policy)**
   * **Canonical Name:** New Energy Vehicle Policy 2025–2030
   * **Type:** National Policy Framework
   * **Aliases:** NEV Policy 2025-30, Federal EV Policy
   * **`sameAs` URL:** `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_vehicle_industry_in_Pakistan`
   * **Attributes / Relationships:**
     * Target: Subsidise 2.2 million electric two- and three-wheelers by 2030.
     * ECC approval of Phase 2 on May 5, 2026 introduced point-of-sale upfront subsidy deductions.

## 7. Information-Gain Analysis & Original Element

### Competitor Blind Spots & Stale Information
* Competitors focus heavily on Phase 1 balloting instructions, failing to clarify why Phase 1 stalled (banks approved only 9% of applications, delivering just 5,409 out of 41,000 vehicles).
* Competitors do not clearly explain how the **Phase 2 upfront point-of-sale deduction** works (buyer pays the net price directly to the OEM, and SBP reimburses the Rs 80,000 to the manufacturer, ending buyer reimbursement delays).
* Most articles confuse federal PAVE (`pave.gov.pk`) with provincial programs (`bikes.punjab.gov.pk`), leading applicants to apply on the wrong portal.
