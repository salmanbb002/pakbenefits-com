# Prime Minister Fuel Relief Scheme: 9771 SMS Registration, Eligibility & Subsidy Rates

The Prime Minister’s Fuel Relief Scheme provides targeted petrol subsidies to low-income vehicle owners in Pakistan. Eligible citizens owning motorcycles, rickshaws, Qingqis, or cars up to 800cc can register by sending a free SMS to **9771**. Qualifying two/three-wheelers receive **Rs 500 weekly**, while eligible 800cc cars receive a **Rs 100 per litre discount** on up to 30 litres monthly.

---

## What is the Prime Minister Fuel Relief Scheme in 2026?

The Prime Minister’s Fuel Relief Scheme is a targeted social assistance initiative administered by the Ministry of Energy (Petroleum Division) to shield vulnerable citizens from fluctuating global oil prices. Unlike broad subsidies that benefit high-income commuters, this targeted mechanism transfers financial relief directly to low-income vehicle owners across all four provinces, Islamabad Capital Territory, Azad Jammu & Kashmir, and Gilgit-Baltistan.

### Key Objectives of the Petrol Subsidy Program

The central objective of the program is reducing daily transport expenditure for commercial commuters and low-wage workers. By integrating real-time identity verification through the National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA), the government ensures that subsidies reach genuine vehicle owners while mitigating ghost claims and retail leakages.

## Who is Eligible for the PM Fuel Relief Scheme?

Eligibility for the PM Petrol Relief Scheme depends on vehicle engine displacement, registration recency, and verified mobile SIM ownership. Applicants must possess a valid 13-digit Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC) matched with a registered cellular connection.

### Vehicle Categories (Motorcycles, Rickshaws & 800cc Cars)

The scheme classifies eligible transport into two distinct tiers:

* **Two/Three-Wheelers:** Motorcycles, auto-rickshaws, and Qingqis used for personal commuting or daily fare transport.
* **Small Cars:** Four-wheeled passenger vehicles with an engine displacement capacity up to **800cc** (such as Suzuki Mehran, Alto 800cc, and Suzuki Bolan). Vehicles exceeding 800cc capacity are strictly excluded.

### Vehicle Age Cutoff & CNIC Ownership Rules

Vehicles registered on or after **January 1, 2006** are eligible for registration under the 20-year vehicle age policy. Additionally, each applicant can link only one vehicle per CNIC. The applicant’s mobile SIM card must be registered under the exact same CNIC to pass automated NADRA database checks.

## How to Register for PM Fuel Relief Scheme via 9771 SMS?

Citizens can complete their registration for free by sending an SMS to the official shortcode **9771**. The system does not charge any service fee or require internet connectivity.

### Step-by-Step 9771 SMS Syntax & Example Format

To apply, compose a new text message on your mobile phone and send it to **9771** using the standard `REG` command:

```text
REG [CNIC Number] [Vehicle Plate Number] [Province Code] [Registration Date]
```

* **CNIC Format:** Enter your 13-digit CNIC without hyphens or spaces (e.g., `6110114620675`).
* **Vehicle Plate Number:** Enter your complete plate number without extra dashes (e.g., `ADV811`).
* **Province Code:** Enter a single capital letter corresponding to your registration authority.
* **Registration Date:** Enter the exact registration date printed on your smart card in `DDMMYYYY` format (e.g., `16052017` for May 16, 2017).

> **Worked Example:**  
> `REG 6110114620675 ADV811 P 16052017`

## How to Claim Your Petrol Subsidy Token (TOK Command)?

Once you receive a confirmation SMS verifying successful registration, you must generate a temporary digital fuel token before purchasing petrol.

### Presenting Fuel Tokens at Registered Petrol Stations

1. Send the word **`TOK`** via text message to **9771**.
2. Receive an automated 6-digit digital token code on your phone.
3. Show the token code to the attendant at any participating petrol station prior to refueling.
4. The attendant verifies the token on their digital terminal, applying the instant discount to your transaction.

Tokens expire after 72 hours if unused, after which a fresh token can be requested by sending `TOK` to 9771 again.

## What are the Monthly Subsidy Rates and Quotas?

The scheme provides structured financial caps based on vehicle class to ensure equitable distribution of welfare funds across Pakistan.

### Worked Example: Weekly vs Monthly Subsidy Breakdown

| Vehicle Category | Subsidy Structure | Token Frequency | Monthly Quota / Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Motorcycles & Rickshaws** | Rs 500 flat discount per token | 1 token per week | 4 tokens / **Rs 2,000 per month** |
| **Qingqis** | Rs 500 flat discount per token | 1 token per week | 4 tokens / **Rs 2,000 per month** |
| **Small Cars (Up to 800cc)** | Rs 100/litre discount on 10L | 1 token every 10 days | 30 litres / **Rs 3,000 per month** |

For instance, a motorcycle owner requesting a weekly token receives Rs 500 off their fuel bill. A Suzuki Mehran owner receives three 10-litre discount tokens per month, saving Rs 1,000 per 10-litre transaction (Rs 3,000 total monthly saving).

## What is the Difference Between PM Fuel Relief (9771) and BISP (8171)?

The PM Fuel Relief Scheme is an independent initiative administered by the Petroleum Division and is **not** part of the Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP 8171).

> [!NOTE] Key Disambiguation
> While BISP (8171) provides direct quarterly cash transfers (Rs 13,500) based on PMT poverty scores, the PM Fuel Relief Scheme (9771) provides fuel-specific point-of-sale discounts to verified vehicle owners. You do not need to be enrolled in BISP to receive the 9771 fuel subsidy.

## How to Fix 9771 Registration Errors and Rejections?

If your registration SMS receives a rejection notice, review the following common resolution steps:

* **SIM Ownership Mismatch:** Ensure the cellular SIM card used to send the SMS is registered under your own CNIC. If using a family member's SIM, transfer the SIM or re-send from your registered line.
* **CNIC Formatting Errors:** Do not include hyphens (`-`) or spaces in your CNIC string.
* **Date Format Mistakes:** Always enter eight digits for the date (`DDMMYYYY`). Writing `16-05-2017` or `2017` will trigger an automated error.
* **Vehicle Age Limit:** Vehicles registered prior to January 1, 2006 do not meet the 20-year eligibility window.

Retailers or petrol station operators encountering system verification failures can contact the official Ministry of Energy Control Room helpline at **9772**.

## Safety Warning: How to Avoid Fuel Subsidy Scams

The Government of Pakistan strictly maintains that registration for the PM Fuel Relief Scheme is **100% free of charge**.

* **No Web Registration Fees:** Official registration occurs exclusively via SMS to **9771**. Do not pay money to third-party agents or unofficial websites.
* **Never Download Unofficial APKs:** Avoid downloading smartphone applications claiming to provide "instant 9771 approvals."
* **Protect Personal Data:** Government representatives will never contact you requesting bank account numbers, OTP codes, or ATM PINs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. What is the official SMS shortcode for the PM Fuel Relief Scheme?
The official SMS shortcode is **9771**. All registration requests and token generations are processed exclusively through this shortcode free of cost.

### 2. How much fuel subsidy do motorcycle owners receive in 2026?
Motorcycle owners receive a subsidy of **Rs 500 per week**, distributed as four tokens per month, providing a total monthly relief of Rs 2,000.

### 3. Can 1000cc or 1300cc car owners apply for the PM Petrol Relief Scheme?
No, the scheme strictly caps car eligibility at an engine capacity of **800cc**. Vehicles exceeding 800cc displacement are ineligible.

### 4. What is the correct SMS syntax to register a vehicle on 9771?
The correct format is `REG [CNIC] [Vehicle Number] [Province Code] [Registration Date]`. For example: `REG 6110114620675 ADV811 P 16052017`.

### 5. Is there any fee for registering via 9771 SMS?
No, sending an SMS to 9771 is completely **free of charge**. The government does not charge any processing fees for registration or token generation.

### 6. How do I request a fuel token code before going to the petrol pump?
After your registration is approved, send the text message **`TOK`** to **9771**. You will receive a digital token code to show at the petrol pump.

### 7. Does the PM Fuel Relief Scheme require enrollment in BISP (8171)?
No, the PM Fuel Relief Scheme operates independently under the Ministry of Energy (Petroleum Division). BISP registration is not required.

### 8. What should I do if my vehicle was registered before January 1, 2006?
Vehicles registered prior to January 1, 2006 fall outside the scheme's 20-year eligibility criteria and cannot be registered under current guidelines.

### 9. What helpline number can petrol station operators call for system technical issues?
Petrol pump operators and retailers can call the dedicated Ministry of Energy control room helpline at **9772** for real-time terminal support.

### 10. Can I register multiple vehicles under a single CNIC?
No, the system enforces a strict limit of **one vehicle per CNIC** to ensure fair distribution of targeted welfare funds.
