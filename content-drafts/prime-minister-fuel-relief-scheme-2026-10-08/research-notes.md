* **Competitor Blindspots & Errors Identified:**
  1. Most competitors fail to list all 7 official single-letter province codes (`P`, `S`, `K`, `B`, `I`, `A`, `G`), leading to user registration failures in AJK and Gilgit-Baltistan.
  2. Competitors confuse 9771 citizen helpline with 9772 retailer helpline.
  3. Competitors do not provide a clear worked syntax example with exact spacing and date formatting (`DDMMYYYY`).
