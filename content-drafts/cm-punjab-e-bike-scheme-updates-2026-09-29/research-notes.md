## Step 7 — Information-Gain Pass (Value Add)

### What Competitors Miss or State Inaccurately
- **Competitor Flaw 1:** Most sites do not explain what happens if an applicant uploaded a learner permit that expires before the balloting date. (Clarified: DLIMS permit must be valid at submission; renewals can be presented during physical verification).
- **Competitor Flaw 2:** Inaccurate financial numbers. Old articles cite a Rs. 20,000 down payment and 10,000/month installment from early draft proposals. Phase 2 strictly features **Zero Down Payment** and **Rs. 3,028/month**.
- **Competitor Flaw 3:** Confusion between petrol and electric variants. Many sites still instruct applicants to choose petrol 125cc/70cc bikes. Phase 2 is **100% electric bikes** to support Punjab's Smog Mitigation Strategy.
- **Original Value Elements Added:**
  1. *Comprehensive Phase 1 vs. Phase 2 Transformation Matrix* highlighting vehicle type, down payment, monthly cost, and district distribution.
  2. *36-Month Bank of Punjab Financial Amortization Breakdown Table* detailing Govt subsidy vs borrower liability.
  3. *Step-by-Step E-Balloting Result Verification & BOP Post-Selection Protocol Checklist*.
  4. *"What's New in the 2026 Expansion" High-Impact Update Box*.
