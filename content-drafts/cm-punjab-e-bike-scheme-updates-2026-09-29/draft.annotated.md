# CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme Updates 2026: Phase 2 Portal, Balloting Results & BOP Installment Plan

Under the latest **CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme** updates, Phase 2 provides **100,000 electric bikes** to college and university students across all 36 Punjab districts. The **Government of the Punjab** provides a **Rs. 90,000 capital subsidy**, waives 100% of the **zero down payment**, and sponsors interest-free financing through **The Bank of Punjab** at **Rs. 3,028 monthly installment** over **36 months tenure**. Online registration remains open at **bikes.punjab.gov.pk** until **October 4, 2026**, followed by computerized **electronic balloting**.

> **Latest Phase 2 Official Notice (September 2026):** Chief Minister **Maryam Nawaz Sharif** has officially abolished all initial **down payments** (previously Rs. 20,000–25,000) for student applicants. Phase 2 has eliminated petrol motorcycle options entirely, dedicating the entire **100,000 electric bikes Phase 2 quota** to battery-powered electric two-wheelers with a dedicated **50% female student allocation**.

## What Are the Latest Updates on the CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme Phase 2?
The **CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme** Phase 2 expands provincial coverage from an initial five-city pilot to all 36 districts of Punjab, allocating **100,000 electric bikes** exclusively to enrolled higher education students. This major policy transition reflects the **Government of the Punjab**'s aggressive **Punjab smog mitigation action plan** and environmental transport targets.

Under the direct supervision of Chief Minister **Maryam Nawaz Sharif**, the provincial cabinet restructured the program's financial mechanics to eliminate student entry barriers. Rather than requiring families to arrange upfront cash deposits during inflationary pressures, the provincial treasury absorbs the complete initial capital outlay, registration levies, number plate charges, and mandatory **first-year comprehensive Takaful** insurance coverage.

### 100,000 Electric Bikes Allocation Across All 36 Punjab Districts
The vehicle volume for Phase 2 stands at **100,000 electric bikes Phase 2 quota** distributed across every tehsil and district of Punjab based on accredited student population ratios. Unlike Phase 1, which restricted access to Lahore, Faisalabad, Rawalpindi, Multan, and Bahawalpur, Phase 2 ensures that degree colleges and universities in rural, southern, and western Punjab receive proportionate vehicle quotas.

District quotas prevent metropolitan centers from consuming the entire provincial vehicle pool. Institutional quotas are subdivided into male and female categories, ensuring equitable regional distribution whether an applicant studies at a major university in Lahore or a postgraduate degree college in Rajanpur, Bhakkar, or Layyah.

### Total Down Payment Waiver & Rs. 90,000 Government Capital Subsidy
The **Government of the Punjab** directly disburses a non-repayable **Rs. 90,000 capital subsidy** toward the ex-factory retail invoice of every electric motorbike issued under the scheme. Furthermore, the provincial government has completely eliminated the student down payment, achieving a genuine **zero down payment waiver** where selected applicants incur zero upfront acquisition cost prior to vehicle delivery.

In standard commercial asset financing, electric two-wheelers require a 20% to 30% advance deposit alongside security margin retention. By absorbing both the **Rs. 90,000 capital subsidy** and the initial equity margin, the Punjab Government reduces the total financed loan principal to a manageable level that low- and middle-income families can easily amortize.

## How Does the Bank of Punjab (BOP) Installment Plan Work?
**The Bank of Punjab** (**BOP**) executes the financing facility as an interest-free, asset-backed soft loan structured over a **36 months tenure** at **0% markup**. The Punjab Government directly compensates **BOP** for all commercial borrowing markups and administrative processing costs, guaranteeing that students repay only the net principal amount.

Installment recovery is managed through digital collection channels, automated direct debit mandates linked to student or parent accounts, and over-the-counter payments at any **The Bank of Punjab** branch across Pakistan. Repayments commence only after the physical handover of the electric motorbike and verification of the official delivery challan.

### Monthly Repayment Calculation: Rs. 3,028 Over 36 Months at 0% Markup
The monthly repayment amount for standard electric motorbikes under Phase 2 is fixed at approximately **Rs. 3,028 monthly installment** over a 36-month schedule. Because the provincial government covers the **Rs. 90,000 capital subsidy**, markups, and documentation fees, the monthly liability remains roughly equivalent to the cost of a few liters of petrol.

### Guarantor, e-CIB & Debt Burden Ratio (DBR) Requirements
To comply with State Bank of Pakistan consumer lending regulations, **The Bank of Punjab** requires each student applicant to designate an eligible **parent / legal guardian guarantor (co-borrower)**, typically a parent, legal guardian, spouse, or employed sibling. The co-borrower must possess a valid Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC) and demonstrate sufficient monthly cash flow to support the micro-installment.

**BOP** conducts an automated electronic Credit Information Bureau (e-CIB) inquiry to ensure the co-borrower is not an active financial defaulter on existing banking facilities. Under SBP guidelines, the co-borrower's combined **Debt Burden Ratio (DBR) 40% cap** must not exceed 40% of their verifiable net household income, ensuring that family debt servicing remains sustainable throughout the 3-year term.

## Who Is Eligible for the Punjab E-Bike Scheme Phase 2?
Eligibility for the **CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme** Phase 2 requires applicants to be regular, enrolled students at an HEC-recognized degree college or university located within the territorial jurisdiction of Punjab. Applicants must hold a verified Punjab domicile certificate or Punjab-addressed CNIC and meet statutory transport licensing requirements.

The scheme excludes private candidates, distance-learning students, and casual diploma enrollees to ensure that publicly subsidized vehicles directly alleviate daily inter-city and intra-city academic transit burdens. Teachers are accommodated through a separate channel at **ptf.punjab.gov.pk teacher quota**.

### Academic Criteria for Regular College and University Students
Applicants must be enrolled in full-time morning or evening degree programs, including intermediate (in select recognized public colleges), undergraduate (BS, BA, BSc), postgraduate (MS, MPhil, MSc), or doctoral programs. Institutional registrars and college principals verify student enrollment electronically through the **Higher Education Department** and **PITB** integration.

Students must provide their active institutional roll number, department designation, student identity card number, and current semester or academic session details during portal submission. Suspended students or individuals with terminated academic standings are automatically flagged and disqualified during data cross-matching.

### Mandatory DLIMS Driving License and Learner Permit Rules
Every applicant must possess a valid **DLIMS motorcycle driving license / learner permit** issued by the Driving License Issuance Management System of the Punjab Police. Applications submitted without a valid DLIMS computerized registration tracking number are rejected at the initial database validation stage.

Students holding a learner's permit can successfully apply and participate in the **automated electronic balloting**. However, selected candidates must maintain their learner permit in valid status and are strongly advised to secure their permanent computerized driving license before final vehicle delivery to avoid insurance endorsement complications.

### Gender Quotas: 50% Allocation for Female Students and Pink Scooty Options
Phase 2 mandates an unprecedented **50% female student allocation & pink scooties** reserved exclusively for female students across all 36 districts of Punjab. Female applicants have the choice between standard commuter electric motorbikes and specially configured step-through electric scooties, designed for comfortable daily riding in modest attire.

This affirmative gender allocation addresses urban mobility hurdles that frequently force young women to discontinue higher education due to prohibitive van fares or overcrowded public transport routes. Female students also receive dedicated priority slots in post-balloting delivery schedules.

## How to Apply Online at bikes.punjab.gov.pk Before the October 4, 2026 Deadline?
Online application submission for Phase 2 is conducted exclusively through the centralized digital **official portal bikes.punjab.gov.pk**, developed and managed by the **Punjab Information Technology Board** (**PITB**). The portal remains active 24 hours a day until the strict application deadline of **October 4, 2026 registration cutoff**.

Manual paper forms, bank counter submissions, and third-party franchise registrations are strictly prohibited. Applicants should avoid unverified third-party websites claiming to offer registration shortcuts, as these platforms are unaccredited and compromise personal identity security.

```
Step 1: Account Creation (CNIC + Active Mobile Number Verification)
   ↓
Step 2: Educational Biodata Entry (University / Degree College Selection)
   ↓
Step 3: Vehicle Preference & Model Selection (Electric Bike / Electric Scooty)
   ↓
Step 4: DLIMS Driving Document Linking (Permit Number / License Scan)
   ↓
Step 5: Co-Borrower / Guarantor Particulars (CNIC, Relationship & Income)
   ↓
Step 6: Document Upload & Final Affidavit Verification
   ↓
Submission Complete: Automated Application Tracking ID Generated
```

### Step-by-Step Student Portal Registration Workflow
1. **Access the Secure Portal:** Open **https://bikes.punjab.gov.pk** on a mobile browser or computer desktop.
2. **Register a User Account:** Click "Register", enter your full legal name matching your CNIC, provide your 13-digit CNIC number without hyphens, input an active mobile number registered under your or your parent's name, and create a strong alphanumeric password.
3. **Verify via OTP:** Input the automated 6-digit one-time password (OTP) received via SMS to activate your dashboard.
4. **Complete Educational Profile:** Select your educational institution type (Public or Private University / Degree College), locate your specific campus from the drop-down directory, and enter your current department and enrollment roll number.
5. **Vehicle Configuration Selection:** Select the electric bike category and specify whether you prefer a standard electric motorbike or an electric scooty.
6. **Enter Driving Permit Credentials:** Input your valid **DLIMS motorcycle driving license / learner permit** number alongside the date of issuance and expiry.
7. **Input Guarantor / Co-Borrower Data:** Fill in your co-borrower’s full name, CNIC, employment classification (salaried, business, or self-employed), monthly approximate household income, and residential address.
8. **Final Review and Confirmation:** Carefully review all entered parameters, check the legal affidavit confirming data authenticity, and click "Submit Application". The portal immediately issues a unique digital Application Tracking Number.

### Mandatory Document Uploads & Common Submission Errors to Avoid
Applicants must upload clear, legible digital scans or high-resolution smartphone photographs in JPG, PNG, or PDF format (maximum file size 2MB each):
- Scanned copy of the applicant’s original CNIC or NADRA Smart Card (front and back).
- Valid student identity card issued by the educational institution or an attested bonafide student certificate signed by the Head of Department.
- Scanned copy of the valid **DLIMS motorcycle driving license / learner permit**.
- Scanned copy of the **parent / legal guardian guarantor (co-borrower)** original CNIC (front and back).

Common errors leading to immediate disqualification include submitting expired learner permits, selecting an unaccredited private academy not listed on the HEC directory, entering mismatched CNIC numbers, or uploading blurry photos where text is illegible to automated optical character recognition (OCR) systems.

## How Will the Electronic Balloting (E-Balloting) and Merit Lists Be Conducted?
The selection of beneficiaries across all 36 Punjab districts is conducted through **automated electronic balloting (e-balloting)** designed, coded, and monitored by the **Punjab Information Technology Board** (**PITB**). The balloting process eliminates human discretion, third-party recommendations, or manual quotas, guaranteeing total transparency.

The e-balloting draw takes place shortly following the closure of the registration window on **October 4, 2026 registration cutoff**. Representatives from the **Transport Department Government of the Punjab**, **Higher Education Department**, civil society observers, and media personnel witness the computerized script execution in Lahore.

### PITB Computerized Draw Mechanism & District-Wise Quota Balancing
The **PITB** balloting algorithm segregates the applicant database into distinct district, gender, and institutional buckets before running randomized selection routines. This ensures that every district's allocated quota is fulfilled independently, preventing students from smaller tehsils from competing directly against candidates from high-density cities like Lahore or Rawalpindi.

Once the primary quota for a specific district is exhausted, the algorithm automatically generates a secondary computerized **Waiting List (Reserve List)**. If an initially selected applicant fails bank credit scrutiny, withdraws voluntarily, or provides unverifiable academic credentials, the system immediately promotes the next student in sequence from the official reserve queue.

### How to Check Selected Applicant Status on the bikes.punjab.gov.pk Dashboard
Applicants can independently verify their selection status within seconds once the official balloting concludes:

1. Visit the **official portal bikes.punjab.gov.pk** homepage at **https://bikes.punjab.gov.pk**.
2. Click on the prominent banner titled **"Phase 2 Balloting Results & Merit List"**.
3. Log into your applicant dashboard by entering your registered 13-digit CNIC number and password.
4. Your personal status banner will clearly indicate one of three official statuses:
   - **Selected (Approved in E-Ballot):** Indicates successful selection in the primary quota and directs you to initiate **The Bank of Punjab** branch verification.
   - **Waiting List (Reserve Status):** Indicates provisional placement on the contingency list with your exact numerical reserve standing.
   - **Not Selected:** Indicates that your application was unsuccessful in the current computerized draw.
5. In addition to online dashboards, the Punjab Government publishes full downloadable PDF merit lists categorized by district and gender, searchable via keyboard shortcut (`Ctrl + F`). Selected candidates also receive an automated official SMS alert from the government gateway.

## Post-Selection Procedure: BOP Branch Verification, Takaful & Delivery Timeline
Being selected in the **automated electronic balloting** marks the completion of the preliminary stage; final vehicle ownership requires successful asset financing approval through **The Bank of Punjab**. Successful candidates must complete document verification at designated **BOP** branches within 10 to 14 business days following the balloting announcement.

Failure to present required documents or bring the designated co-borrower to the bank within the stipulated window results in automatic cancellation of the provisional allocation, with the seat transferred to the next waiting-list candidate.

### In-Person Bank Verification & Co-Borrower Documentation
Selected applicants must visit the assigned **The Bank of Punjab** branch in their home district accompanied by their designated **parent / legal guardian guarantor (co-borrower)**. The applicant and co-borrower must present:
- Original CNIC cards of both the student and co-borrower alongside four attested passport-sized photographs.
- Original bonafide student letter issued by the college or university registrar within the last 30 days.
- Original **DLIMS motorcycle driving license / learner permit**.
- Proof of residence (recent paid utility bill—electricity, gas, or water—for the family home).
- Proof of income for the co-borrower (salary slip, employment letter, bank statement, or formal income affidavit for informal workers).

**BOP** credit officers verify physical signatures, capture biometric verification through NADRA e-Sahulat terminals, and complete the formal interest-free loan agreement.

### Free Helmet, Safety Rod Installation & Mandatory 2-Day Riding Orientation
Under directives issued by Chief Minister **Maryam Nawaz Sharif**, the **Transport Department Government of the Punjab** bundles every electric vehicle with a **complimentary rider safety kit**. This kit includes an internationally certified motorcycle crash helmet and pre-installed heavy-duty steel safety rods (crash leg protectors) designed to minimize lower-limb injury risks.

Furthermore, all selected students must complete a **two-day mandatory traffic safety course** organized by City Traffic Police (CTP) training academies before taking vehicle possession. The curriculum covers defensive driving, emergency braking, **lithium-ion battery pack & charging infrastructure** safety management, wet-weather riding stability, and lane discipline.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the official deadline to apply for CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme Phase 2?
The official deadline for online application submission under Phase 2 is **October 4, 2026**. All eligible college and university students must complete their digital registrations at **bikes.punjab.gov.pk** before midnight on this date.

### How much monthly installment do selected students have to pay?
Selected students pay a fixed monthly installment of approximately **Rs. 3,028** over a **36 months tenure**. Because the **Government of the Punjab** provides a **Rs. 90,000 capital subsidy** and absorbs all interest charges, the loan carries **0% markup**.

### Is there any down payment or advance deposit required?
No down payment is required from students under Phase 2. Chief Minister **Maryam Nawaz Sharif** has completely waived the initial equity deposit, enabling selected students to receive their electric bikes with a complete **zero down payment waiver**.

### Can students holding only a motorcycle learner's permit apply?
Yes, students holding a valid **DLIMS motorcycle driving license / learner permit** issued by DLIMS Punjab are fully eligible to apply and participate in the electronic ballot. However, candidates must keep their permit active and are encouraged to acquire a full computerized license prior to final vehicle delivery.

### How can I check my name in the Punjab E-Bike balloting merit list?
You can verify your balloting result by logging into your personalized applicant dashboard at **bikes.punjab.gov.pk** using your 13-digit CNIC and password. The Punjab Government also publishes official district-wise downloadable PDF merit lists that can be searched using your CNIC number.

### Are petrol motorcycles available in the Phase 2 registration?
No petrol motorcycles are offered in Phase 2. To combat urban smog and promote clean environmental energy under the **Punjab smog mitigation action plan**, the Government of the Punjab has made Phase 2 an exclusively electric vehicle program comprising **100,000 electric bikes**.

### Who can serve as a guarantor (co-borrower) for The Bank of Punjab?
A **parent / legal guardian guarantor (co-borrower)**, spouse, or employed sibling can act as a guarantor or co-borrower for **The Bank of Punjab** financing. The guarantor must possess a valid CNIC, a clean credit history free from active banking defaults, and satisfy the **Debt Burden Ratio (DBR) 40% cap**.

### Are students from private universities and degree colleges eligible?
Yes, regular students enrolled in private universities and private degree colleges recognized by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) and Punjab Higher Education Commission (PHEC) are fully eligible to apply alongside public-sector students.

### What happens if an applicant fails the Bank of Punjab verification?
If a selected candidate fails the **BOP** credit appraisal, provides unverifiable academic documentation, or fails to visit the branch within the designated timeframe, their allocation is cancelled. The vacant seat is then immediately offered to the next candidate on the computerized waiting list.

### Does the Punjab Government cover insurance and vehicle registration costs?
Yes, the **Government of the Punjab** provides **first-year Takaful & token tax exemption**, covering 100% of the vehicle registration fees, computerized number plate charges, token tax, and the complete first-year comprehensive Takaful insurance premium.

### Can female students apply for electric scooties instead of standard motorbikes?
Yes, female applicants can specifically select electric scooties (scooters) with a step-through frame design on the application portal under the **50% female student allocation & pink scooties**. The Punjab Government has reserved this dedicated quota to enhance mobility and female higher-education enrollment across the province.
