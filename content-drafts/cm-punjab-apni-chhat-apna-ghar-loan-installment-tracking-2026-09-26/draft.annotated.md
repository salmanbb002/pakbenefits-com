# Apni Chhat Apna Ghar Loan: Instant Qist Tracking

CM Punjab **Apni Chhat Apna Ghar Scheme** ke teht **Rs 15 Lakh** interest-free loan ki pehli qist (installment) track karne ke liye official **PITB portal** (`acag.punjab.gov.pk`) par apna **CNIC number** dakhil karein. Portal par **application approval status**, verification stage, aur **disbursement date** ki tafseelat instant dekhi ja sakti hain.

---

## CM Punjab Apni Chhat Apna Ghar Loan Installment Tracking Overview

Chief Minister Punjab Maryam Nawaz Sharif ke visionary project **Apni Chhat Apna Ghar (ACAG) Scheme** ke teht Punjab ke zaroorat-mand **shanaakti card holders** ko ghar ki ta'meer ke liye **15 Lakh Rupee tak ka Bila-Sood (Interest-Free) Loan** faraham kiya ja raha hai.

Application submit karne ke baad aksar applicants ko ye masla pesh aata hai ke unki application approve hui hai ya nahi aur **pehli qist (first installment)** kab unke bank account mein credit hogi. Is article mein **PITB portal** ke zariye **loan status check** karne aur **1st installment track** karne ka mukammal aasan tariqa bataya gaya hai.

---

## ACAG Punjab Gov Pk Loan Status Check Online Step-by-Step

Official **Punjab Housing and Town Planning Agency (PHATA)** aur **PITB portal** par apni application ka status check karne ke liye mandarija zail tareeqay par amal karein:

### Step 1: Portal Access & Login

1. Official **ACAG Web Portal** (`https://acag.punjab.gov.pk`) open karein.
2. Form par apna **13-Digit CNIC Number** (bina dashes ke) dakhil karein.
3. Registered Mobile Number par aane wala **OTP Verification Code** enter karke login karein.

### Step 2: Dashboard Application Tracking

* Dashboard open hotay hi aap ki application ka current Status show hoga:
  * **Submitted / Pending Scrutiny:** Aap ki documents verification under-process hai.
  * **Approved / Shortlisted:** Application verify ho chuki hai aur **15 lakh loan approval list** mein shamil hai.
  * **Bank Processing / Sanction Issued:** Microfinance Bank (**Bank of Punjab / BOP**) account verification complete ho chuki hai.
  * **Disbursed:** Pehli Qist (Installment) aap ke account mein transfer kar di gayi hai.

## Apni Chhat Apna Ghar First Installment Date & Disbursement Schedule

Loan approval hone ke baad total **15 Lakh Rupee Loan** 3 se 4 aasan qiston (installments) mein jaari kiya jata hai taake ghar ki ta'meer step-by-step mukammal ho sake:

| Installment Stage | Construction Stage Completed | Release Percentage / Amount | Release Schedule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **First Installment (1st Qist)** | Land Verification & Layout Approval | 20% to 25% (Approx Rs 3 Lakh - 4 Lakh) | Approval ke 7 se 14 Days baad |
| **Second Installment** | DPC / Foundation Construction Complete | 30% (Approx Rs 4.5 Lakh) | Site Inspection ke 5 Days baad |
| **Third Installment** | Roof Slab (Chhat) Completion | 30% (Approx Rs 4.5 Lakh) | Physical Verification Report |
| **Final Installment** | Finishing & Plaster Work | Remaining Balance (Rs 2.5 Lakh - 3 Lakh) | Final Handover Stage |

## Repayment Schedule & Interest-Free Loan Details

* **Total Loan Amount:** Up to Rs 1,500,000 (15 Lakh).
* **Interest / Mark-up Rate:** 0% (Mukammal Bila-Sood).
* **Repayment Duration:** 9 Years (108 Monthly Installments).
* **Monthly Installment Amount:** Max Rs 14,000 per month (pehlay 3 se 6 maah ka grace period shamil hai).

---

## Troubleshooting Common ACAG Portal Tracking Errors

1. **CNIC Not Found / Record Not Matching:** Form par dakhil karte waqt CNIC ke darmayan dashes (-) na daalein.
2. **OTP Code Not Received:** Apni Registered SIM ka network conversion (MNO porting) check karein ya 5 minute wait karke Resend OTP par click karein.
3. **Application Rejected Status:** Rejection reason check karein (jaise land registry error ya income proof missing) aur 15 dino ke andar Appeal Submit karein.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How can I check ACAG loan status online?
Aap official portal `acag.punjab.gov.pk` par apna 13-digit CNIC dakhil karke aur OTP verification complete karke instant online status check kar sakte hain.

### What is the website link for Apni Chhat Apna Ghar loan tracking?
Official tracking portal address `https://acag.punjab.gov.pk` hai jo PITB aur PHATA dwara maintain kiya jata hai.

### When will the first installment date of Apni Chhat Apna Ghar loan be released?
Application approval aur bank document signing ke 7 se 14 Working Days ke andar pehli qist (1st installment) beneficiary ke bank account mein credit kar di jati hai.

### Is the CM Punjab 15 Lakh Loan completely interest-free?
Jihan, CM Punjab Maryam Nawaz Sharif ki is scheme ke teht 15 lakh rupe ka loan bilkul bila-sood (0% interest) hai.

### How much is the monthly repayment installment for 15 lakh loan?
Loan ki wapsi 9 saal (108 maah) ke aasan arsay mein taqreeban Rs 14,000 monthly qist ke zariye hoti hai.

### What land size is eligible for Apni Chhat Apna Ghar loan?
Shehri ilaqon (Urban) mein 5 Marla tak aur dehi ilaqon (Rural) mein 10 Marla tak ki zameen ke malik is loan ke liye eligible hain.

### How to download the 15 lakh loan approval list pdf?
Official portal `acag.punjab.gov.pk` par Login karke 'Successful Applicants List' tab par click karke District-wise PDF download ki ja sakti hai.

### Which bank is partner with Punjab Government for ACAG loan disbursement?
Bank of Punjab (BOP) aur designated Microfinance Institutions Punjab Govt ke sath payment disbursement ke liye partner hain.

### What should I do if my ACAG loan status shows Pending Scrutiny?
Pending Scrutiny ka matlab hai ke aap ki land registry aur NADRA record ki physical aur digital verification jari hai. Kuch din wait karein ya nearest PHATA office contact karein.

### Can I apply for Apni Chhat Apna Ghar loan if I already own a built house?
Nahi, ye loan sirf un afraad ke liye hai jinke paas apni zameen (plot) majood hai lekin pukhta ghar ta'meer karne ke liye wasail nahi hain.
