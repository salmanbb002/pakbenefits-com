## Step 7 — Information-Gain Pass
1. **Omissions in Competitors:** All competitors fail to explain what the exact portal response messages mean in practice ("Jaanch Partaal", "Survey Zaroori Hai", "Aap Ahl Hain").
2. **Myth-Busting:** We clearly debunk the widespread myth that you can "register online" on the 8171 portal. The portal is *only for status verification*; actual registration requires an in-person NSER Dynamic Registry survey at a BISP Tehsil Office.
3. **Diagnostic Decision Matrix:** A real-world table matching every portal response message with its exact root cause, action required, and payment collection route.
