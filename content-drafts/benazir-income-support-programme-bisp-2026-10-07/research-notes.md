## 1. Intent & SERP Analysis

### Dominant & Secondary Search Intent
- **Dominant Intent:** *Know / Informational* — Users seeking comprehensive information on what the Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP) is, its sub-programs (Kafaalat, Taleemi Wazaif, Nashonuma), eligibility rules, PMT score cutoffs, and latest stipend amounts (Rs 13,500 / Rs 14,500).
- **Secondary Intent:** *Do / Procedural* — Users searching for step-by-step instructions on checking their payment status online via the 8171 web portal (`8171.bisp.gov.pk`), sending CNIC via SMS to 8171, registering through the NSER Dynamic Survey at NADRA Tehsil Offices, and resolving biometric verification failures.

## 5. Information-Gain Pass

### What Competitors Miss / Fail to Update
1. **Outdated Payment Figures:** Competitors frequently cite obsolete amounts (Rs 9,000 or Rs 10,500) rather than the updated 2026 quarterly Kafaalat stipend (Rs 13,500 / Rs 14,500).
2. **PMT Score Clarity:** Competitors fail to explain that raw numerical PMT scores are not displayed on the public 8171 portal due to privacy; the portal instead shows status outcomes ("Eligible", "Ineligible", "Survey Required").
3. **Biometric Verification Troubleshooting:** Competitors offer vague advice for biometric failures. This guide provides concrete steps for elderly/hard-labor beneficiaries (hand lotion, NADRA fingerprint update, BISP Tehsil office manual form).
4. **Scam Prevention & 8171 Verification:** Competitors gloss over SMS fraud. This guide explicitly flags fake lottery/cash SMS scams and emphasizes that ONLY messages from `8171` are official.

### Original Elements Added
1. **BISP Sub-Program & Eligibility Matrix (Table):** Comparing Program Name, Target Beneficiaries, PMT Cutoff, Payment Amount, and Required Documents.
2. **Step-by-Step CNIC Verification & Troubleshooting Checklist:** Clear procedural guide covering portal check, SMS check, and biometric failure resolution.
