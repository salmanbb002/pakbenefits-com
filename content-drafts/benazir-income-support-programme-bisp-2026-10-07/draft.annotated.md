# Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP): Complete 8171 Payment, Eligibility, and Registration Guide (Annotated Tier-1/2 Coverage Draft)

> The **Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP)** is Pakistan's premier social safety net, providing financial cash assistance to targeted low-income households across the country. Beneficiaries can check their quarterly payment status, eligibility, and dynamic survey registration online through the official **8171 Web Portal** (`8171.bisp.gov.pk`) or by sending their 13-digit **Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC)** number via SMS to the **8171 short code**.

---

## What is the Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP)?

The **Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP)** is an autonomous federal social protection initiative established by the Government of Pakistan in July 2008. Operating under the administrative oversight of the **Ministry of Poverty Alleviation and Social Safety**, **BISP** serves as the primary cash transfer mechanism designed to buffer vulnerable, socio-economically disadvantaged families against inflation and fiscal shocks. The program operates under the executive leadership of **BISP** Chairperson **Senator Rubina Khalid**, managing an annual federal budget allocation exceeding Rs 700 Billion to support over 9.3 million registered beneficiary households nationwide.

**BISP** prioritizes female heads of eligible households to foster financial inclusion, gender empowerment, and direct family welfare management. Rather than operating as a temporary food charity, **BISP** provides predictable cash transfers paired with conditional human capital development incentives. The foundational backbone of **BISP** is the **National Socio-Economic Registry (NSER)**, a nationwide socio-economic database that calculates a **Proxy Means Test (PMT) Score** poverty score for every surveyed household to determine eligibility objectively across all provinces.

## How to Check BISP 8171 Payment Status Online by CNIC?

To check your **BISP** 8171 payment status online, visit the official **8171 Web Portal** (`8171.bisp.gov.pk`) and enter your 13-digit **CNIC** number without dashes. The system instantly queries the **National Socio-Economic Registry (NSER)** database to display your current **Quarterly Cash Stipend** availability, eligibility status, or **Dynamic Survey** renewal requirement.

### Step-by-Step 8171 Web Portal Verification

1. **Access the Official 8171 Web Portal:** Open your web browser on a smartphone or computer and navigate directly to `https://8171.bisp.gov.pk/`. Ensure you are using the official government website ending in `.gov.pk`.
2. **Input CNIC Number:** Locate the designated form field labeled "Form No. / CNIC Number" and enter your 13-digit identity card number (e.g., `3520212345671`) without hyphenation or spaces.
3. **Complete Security Captcha:** Enter the 4-digit numeric image code displayed in the security captcha box to verify you are a human visitor.
4. **Submit & Review Results:** Click the green "Check Status" button. The portal will display one of three outcomes:
   - **Eligible & Payment Transferred:** Indicates your **Quarterly Cash Stipend** of **Rs 13,500** has been credited to your account and is ready for withdrawal at designated bank campsites or ATMs.
   - **Ineligible (PMT Score High):** Indicates your household **PMT Score** exceeds the program eligibility threshold (score > 32).
   - **Dynamic Survey Required:** Indicates your survey data is outdated or missing, requiring a visit to the nearest **NADRA Tehsil Office** or **BISP Tehsil Center**.

### How to Verify BISP Status via 8171 SMS Service

For citizens without internet access or smartphones, **BISP** provides a direct cellular verification gateway through the official **8171 SMS Service**.

To check your status via mobile phone, open your SMS text messaging application, type your 13-digit **CNIC** number without spaces, and send the text message to **8171** from your registered mobile SIM. Within a few moments, you will receive an automated response from **BISP** detailing your eligibility status, stipend allocation, or **NADRA Tehsil Office** registration instructions. Please note that text messages sent to **8171** may incur a nominal mobile network transaction charge.

## What is the BISP PMT Score and How Does Eligibility Work?

The **Proxy Means Test (PMT) Score** is a calculated numerical index between 0 and 100 that measures a household's poverty level based on income, physical assets, family size, housing structure, and living conditions recorded in the **NSER** database. Lower **PMT Scores** reflect higher financial vulnerability, establishing the numerical criteria used by **BISP** to approve unconditional cash transfers.

### PMT Score Cutoff Thresholds for Kafaalat & Special Categories

- **Standard Household Threshold (PMT ≤ 32):** For general families seeking enrollment in the **Benazir Kafaalat Program**, the standard **PMT Score** eligibility cutoff is fixed at **32 or below**. Households scoring 32.00 or lower automatically qualify for quarterly cash stipends.
- **Persons with Disabilities (PMT ≤ 37):** To provide targeted support for vulnerable groups, the federal government maintains a relaxed **PMT Score** cutoff threshold of **37 or below** for households containing certified **Persons with Disabilities (PWD)**, provided the disability is officially endorsed on their Special **CNIC** issued by NADRA.
- **Regional Special Relief Thresholds:** In specific remote or crisis-affected regions (such as select districts in Balochistan and Gilgit-Baltistan), temporary **PMT Score** threshold expansions (up to 60) are periodically authorized under special federal executive directives.

### How to Update Household Data via NSER Dynamic Survey

If your household financial condition has deteriorated or if your eligibility status displays "Survey Required," you must update your records through the **Dynamic Survey** executed at local **NADRA Tehsil Offices**.

Beneficiaries must visit their local **NADRA Tehsil Office** with their original **CNIC**, electricity/gas utility bills, and children's Computerized Registration Certificates (B-Form). A trained **BISP** data entry operator executes a fresh interview, updating household asset details and recalculating the **PMT Score** within 30 to 60 days. **BISP** rules mandate that all registered families refresh their **NSER** **Dynamic Survey** data every three years to preserve active beneficiary status.

## What Are the Core BISP Sub-Programs and Payment Amounts?

**BISP** executes three primary social assistance schemes designed to address immediate financial poverty, school dropouts, and maternal-child stunting across Pakistan.

### Benazir Kafaalat Program (Unconditional Cash Transfer)

The **Benazir Kafaalat Program** is **BISP**'s core initiative, offering direct unconditional quarterly cash stipends to deserving female family heads. As of the 2025-2026 fiscal budget updates, the **Quarterly Cash Stipend** amount has been enhanced to **Rs 13,500** per eligible household (with targeted increases expanding up to **Rs 14,500** during specific disbursement cycles). Payments are disbursed every three months through designated biometric distribution channels.

### Benazir Taleemi Wazaif (School Stipends for Children)

**Benazir Taleemi Wazaif** is a conditional cash transfer initiative that provides cash bonuses to active **Benazir Kafaalat Program** beneficiary mothers on the condition that their children maintain at least a 70% quarterly school attendance rate. Stipends are tiered by gender and educational level:

- **Primary Level:** Rs 2,500 per quarter for boys; Rs 3,000 per quarter for girls.
- **Secondary / Matric Level:** Rs 3,500 per quarter for boys; Rs 4,000 per quarter for girls.
- **Higher Secondary / Intermediate Level:** Rs 4,500 per quarter for boys; Rs 5,000 per quarter for girls.
- **Graduation Bonus:** A one-time cash bonus of Rs 3,000 is awarded to girls completing primary school education.

### Benazir Nashonuma (Health & Nutrition Program)

**Benazir Nashonuma** is a specialized conditional health and nutrition program targeted at preventing chronic stunting among pregnant women, lactating mothers, and children under two years of age (24 months). Beneficiaries receive specialized nutritious food packages alongside quarterly cash assistance:

- **Pregnant & Lactating Mothers:** Rs 2,500 per quarter upon attending routine health check-ups.
- **Infants (Boys):** Rs 2,500 per quarter upon fulfilling vaccination schedules.
- **Infants (Girls):** Rs 3,000 per quarter, incentivizing healthcare access for female infants.

## How to Receive BISP Cash Payments and Resolve Biometric Errors?

**BISP** beneficiaries collect their quarterly cash assistance through authorized retail agent points, designated campsite distribution centers, and partner bank ATMs established in cooperation with **Habib Bank Limited (HBL)** in Punjab, Sindh, and Balochistan, and **Bank Alfalah** in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Azad Jammu & Kashmir, and Gilgit-Baltistan.

### Collecting Stipends at Partner Banks & Payment Campsites

To collect your cash payment, present your physical original **CNIC** to an authorized **Habib Bank Limited (HBL)** Konnect or **Bank Alfalah** retail agent or visit a designated **BISP** campsite. Place your thumb or index finger on the biometric point-of-sale (POS) scanner to complete live **Biometric Verification**. Once verified, the agent will disburse your exact **Quarterly Cash Stipend** in full and provide a printed transaction receipt. Beneficiaries are strongly advised never to pay any agent commission or service fee; **BISP** stipends must be paid without any deductions.

### How to Fix Biometric Fingerprint Verification Failures

**Biometric Verification** failure (where the POS biometric scanner fails to read worn or damaged fingerprints of manual workers or elderly women) is a common challenge during cash collection. If your thumb impression is repeatedly rejected, follow these corrective steps:

1. **Hydrate & Clean Hands:** Wash your hands thoroughly with soap and apply hand lotion or oil prior to scanning. Excessive dryness or skin calluses frequently prevent optical sensors from capturing ridge patterns.
2. **Try Multiple Finger Impressions:** Agents are authorized to test all eight fingers and both thumbs on the biometric POS reader.
3. **Update Fingerprints at NADRA:** If authentication fails across all fingers, visit the nearest NADRA Registration Center to update your **Biometric Verification** fingerprint data on your **CNIC** record.
4. **Submit BISP Manual Verification Form:** If **Biometric Verification** failure persists after updating NADRA records, visit your local **NADRA Tehsil Office** or **BISP Tehsil Center** and request the **BISP Biometric Exemption Form**. Upon physical verification by the **BISP** Assistant Director and verification of your NADRA records, your payment will be routed through designated bank branch counters via manual **CNIC** verification.

## How to Avoid 8171 SMS Scams and Protect Personal Data?

To protect yourself against fraudulent financial scams, remember that **BISP** communicates with beneficiaries **exclusively through the official 8171 SMS Service**. Any text message or phone call received from standard 11-digit mobile numbers claiming you have won a cash grant, prize, or emergency allotment is a fraudulent attempt to steal your personal data or money.

### Essential Security Guidelines:

- **Never Share Banking OTPs or PINs:** **BISP** officials will never ask for mobile banking passwords, secret PIN codes, or scratch card numbers.
- **Never Pay Upfront Registration Fees:** Registration for **BISP** and **NSER** **Dynamic Survey** at **NADRA Tehsil Offices** is 100% free of charge.
- **Report Fraudulent Scammers:** If you receive a fake payment message from an unofficial number, lodge an immediate complaint with the **BISP Helpline 0800-26477** or report the scammer's phone number to the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) and FIA Cybercrime Wing.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the official BISP 8171 web portal URL?
The official **BISP** web portal URL is `https://8171.bisp.gov.pk/`. Beneficiaries should only enter their 13-digit **CNIC** number on government websites ending in `.gov.pk` to protect personal data from fake fraud portals.

### How can I check my BISP eligibility by CNIC via SMS?
To check eligibility via SMS, type your 13-digit **CNIC** number without dashes or spaces in a text message and send it to the official **8171 SMS Service**. You will receive an automated reply detailing your status, eligibility, or survey instructions.

### What is the current BISP Benazir Kafaalat payment amount in 2026?
The standard **Benazir Kafaalat Program** **Quarterly Cash Stipend** in 2026 is **Rs 13,500** per eligible household, with targeted disbursement rounds expanding up to **Rs 14,500**. Payments are distributed every three months through authorized bank distribution points.

### What PMT score is required to qualify for BISP Kafaalat?
A household generally requires a **Proxy Means Test (PMT) Score** of **32 or below** to qualify for the **Benazir Kafaalat Program** unconditional cash transfer program. For households with certified **Persons with Disabilities (PWD)**, the eligibility threshold is relaxed to **37 or below**.

### Can men apply for the Benazir Income Support Programme?
No, financial stipends under the **Benazir Kafaalat Program** are issued exclusively to the female head of an eligible household (wife or mother). However, male family members benefit indirectly as part of the registered household, and male students can receive **Benazir Taleemi Wazaif** educational grants.

### What should I do if my BISP payment shows a biometric fingerprint error?
If your fingerprint fails **Biometric Verification** POS verification, clean and moisturize your hands and test all ten fingers. If failures persist, update your fingerprints at a NADRA office or visit your local **NADRA Tehsil Office** to apply for a biometric exemption manual payment.

### How often do I need to update my BISP NSER Dynamic Survey?
**BISP** policy mandates that registered families must update their **NSER** **Dynamic Survey** data every **three years** at a **NADRA Tehsil Office** to ensure their **PMT Score** poverty score accurately reflects their current financial status.

### How do I enroll my children in Benazir Taleemi Wazaif?
To enroll children in **Benazir Taleemi Wazaif**, a female beneficiary already receiving **Benazir Kafaalat Program** must visit a **NADRA Tehsil Office** with her **CNIC**, the children's NADRA B-Form, and an official Admission Verification Form stamped by the school principal confirming at least 70% attendance.

### Are there any fees for registering for BISP at the Tehsil Office?
No, registration for the **Benazir Income Support Programme** and the **NSER** **Dynamic Survey** at all official **NADRA Tehsil Offices** is completely free. Beneficiaries should report any agent demanding illegal fees to the **BISP Helpline 0800-26477**.

### How do I report a fake 8171 SMS scam message?
Report fraudulent messages received from standard mobile numbers to the **BISP Helpline 0800-26477** or forward the scammer's number to the PTA complaint portal. Official **BISP** communications are sent strictly from the **8171 SMS Service**.

### What documents are required for new BISP Tehsil office registration?
Applicants must bring the original 13-digit **CNIC** of the female head of household, NADRA B-Forms for all unmarried children, electricity or gas utility bills, and an active mobile SIM card registered in the applicant's own name.
