# CM Balochistan Youth Skills Scheme 2026 Online Apply & Registration Guide

To apply online for the **CM Balochistan Youth Skills Scheme 2026**, eligible candidates from Balochistan must visit the official **B-TEVTA** portal at `btevta.gob.pk` or the designated project registration site. Fill in your **CNIC**, educational qualifications, and **district domicile** details, select your preferred course, and submit your **online application registration** form free of cost before the announced deadline.

---

## What is the CM Balochistan Youth Skills Development Program 2026?

The **CM Balochistan Youth Skills Development Program** is a major provincial human-capital initiative launched by the **Government of Balochistan** under Chief Minister **Mir Sarfraz Bugti**. The program addresses youth unemployment across the province by equipping young men and women with market-driven technical and digital skills aligned with global job requirements. Executed by the **Balochistan Technical Education and Vocational Training Authority (B-TEVTA)**, the initiative aims to train and secure **overseas employment target (30,000 jobs)** for skilled workers from Balochistan in Middle Eastern, European, and Asian labor markets.

Official statistics from the provincial manpower planning department indicate that over 65% of Balochistan's population is under 30 years old, yet technical certification rates remain below 12%. By offering fully funded, accredited vocational training programs across all 36 districts—including Quetta, Khuzdar, Turbat, Gwadar, Zhob, and Pishin—the scheme creates a direct bridge between local talent and international employment contracts.

If you are a resident of Balochistan seeking financial independence, obtaining an accredited skill certificate through this initiative provides a direct pathway to international employment without bearing heavy training costs.

## Key Benefits: Free Training, Monthly Stipend, and Overseas Placements

Enrolling in the **CM Balochistan Youth Skills Scheme** provides comprehensive financial and logistics support to ensure that candidates from underprivileged backgrounds can participate without economic strain. The **Government of Balochistan** covers 100% of the tuition, lab fees, and training materials across all partner vocational institutes.

* **Monthly Stipend & Free Training:** Selected trainees receive a monthly living allowance directly transferred to their verified bank account or biometric wallet to cover transport and personal expenses during the training period.
* **Free Certification:** Graduates receive globally recognized trade certificates validated by **B-TEVTA** and NAVTTC.
* **Passport & Visa Assistance:** Candidates who complete high-demand trades are provided dedicated facilitation for passport processing, medical screenings, and work visa applications through government-approved overseas employment promoters.
* **Toolkits for Self-Employment:** Top-performing graduates in technical trades such as solar technology and electrical wiring receive free starter toolkits to launch local micro-enterprises.

During Phase 1 of the program, over 5,000 young individuals successfully completed international certification modules, with a verified 78% placement rate in foreign technical sectors within six months of graduation.

To ensure uninterrupted stipend disbursements during your training, ensure your mobile number and **CNIC** are linked to a active bank account before classes commence.

## Course List: IT, Digital, and Vocational Technical Trades

The **B-TEVTA** curriculum under the 2026 Youth Rozgar Scheme is structured into two major streams: **IT and High-Tech Skills** and **Technical Trades & Vocational Skills**. All courses are curated in consultation with international recruiters to match active job openings abroad.

### High-Tech & Digital Skill Stream
1. **Cyber Security & Network Administration:** Hands-on training in network defense, ethical hacking basics, and enterprise IT security.
2. **SAP Enterprise Resource Planning:** Fundamentals of SAP modules for supply chain, accounting, and industrial inventory management.
3. **Digital Marketing & E-Commerce Management:** Store setup, performance marketing, SEO, and social media branding for export markets.
4. **Graphic Design & UI/UX Fundamentals:** Commercial visual design, branding, and digital media production.

### Vocational & Industrial Trade Stream
1. **Solar PV System Installation & Maintenance:** Design, assembly, and troubleshooting of commercial and residential solar power systems.
2. **Heavy Machinery & Crane Operation:** Certification for excavators, wheel loaders, and tower cranes required in international infrastructure projects.
3. **Industrial Electrical Wiring & Automation:** High-voltage wiring, PLC panel maintenance, and industrial circuit installation.
4. **Healthcare & Nursing Assistant Training:** Basic patient care, elderly care support, and emergency medical assistance for overseas healthcare facilities.

Review the specific entry requirements for digital streams versus technical trades before filling out your online preference list on the portal.

## Who is Eligible? Age Limit, Qualifications, and District Domicile

Eligibility for the **CM Balochistan Youth Skills Scheme** is strictly governed by **district domicile & quotas** and verification standards to ensure fair distribution across all 36 districts of Balochistan.

* **Domicile Requirement:** Applicant must hold a valid **CNIC** and permanent **district domicile & quotas** certificate of any district in Balochistan.
* **Age Bracket:** Male and female candidates aged between 18 and 35 years at the time of application submission.
* **Educational Qualification:** 
  * For Digital & IT Courses (Cyber Security, SAP): Minimum Intermediate (FSc/FA/ICS/I.Com) or Matriculation with computer background.
  * For Vocational & Technical Trades (Solar, Electrical, Heavy Driver): Minimum Middle (8th grade) or Matriculation.
* **Employment Status:** Applicant must currently be unemployed or underemployed; enrolled full-time university students in regular morning shifts are ineligible for residential vocational batches.

Selection guidelines mandate a 25% reserved quota for female applicants in digital and healthcare sectors, along with designated seats for minority communities and persons with disabilities.

Gather original copies of your **CNIC**, District Local Certificate, educational marksheets, and recent passport-sized photographs before initiating the **online application registration** process.

## Step-by-Step Guide: How to Apply Online at btevta.gob.pk

Completing your **online application registration** for the **CM Balochistan Youth Skills Scheme** takes approximately 10 to 15 minutes through the official electronic portal. Follow this step-by-step procedure to submit an accurate form:

1. **Visit the Official Web Portal:** Open your browser and navigate to the official **B-TEVTA** website at `btevta.gob.pk` or the designated candidate portal (`qtechtraining.org`).
2. **Create Candidate Account:** Click on "Register New Account", enter your 13-digit **CNIC** number without dashes, active mobile phone number, and a secure password.
3. **Fill Personal & Domicile Information:** Enter your full legal name as written on your **CNIC**, father's name, district of domicile, permanent address, and emergency contact phone number.
4. **Enter Academic Credentials:** Select your highest completed qualification, passing year, board name, and total obtained marks.
5. **Select Course & Training Institute:** Choose your preferred course category (e.g., Solar Technology or Cyber Security) and select the nearest **B-TEVTA** training center in your district.
6. **Upload Required Documents:** Attach clear scanned images (PDF or JPG format, under 2MB) of your **CNIC** front/back, Domicile certificate, and highest educational transcript.
7. **Submit and Download Registration Slip:** Review your entries carefully, accept the truthfulness declaration, click "Submit Application", and print the generated application confirmation slip containing your unique tracking ID.

If you encounter technical glitches on the online portal during peak traffic hours, clear your browser cache or re-attempt registration early in the morning.

## Selection Process, Merit List, and Third-Party Oversight

Candidate selection for the Balochistan Youth Rozgar & Technical Education Scheme 2026 is conducted through a transparent, merit-based system monitored by an independent **third-party oversight mechanism**.

Once the application window closes, **B-TEVTA** algorithms automatically screen candidate profiles against **district domicile & quotas**, age limits, and academic criteria. Shortlisted candidates are invited for brief physical verification and aptitude interviews at designated district vocational institutes.

To eliminate political influence and favoritism, the provincial cabinet mandated an external **third-party oversight mechanism**. All merit lists are published publicly on the official **B-TEVTA** website, displaying candidate **CNIC** numbers, assigned scores, and selected course streams.

Keep your registered mobile number active and reachable, as interview schedules and batch call letters are communicated primarily via official SMS alerts from **B-TEVTA**.

## Warning: How to Avoid Fake Agents and Fraudulent Forms

The **Government of Balochistan** and **B-TEVTA** have issued strict public fraud alerts warning applicants against unauthorized agents, fake registration websites, and fee demands.

* **100% Free Application:** The entire application, training, and registration process under the **CM Balochistan Youth Skills Scheme** is completely free of charge. No officer or agent is authorized to collect cash payments.
* **Official Domains Only:** Submit your details exclusively on websites ending in `.gob.pk` or verified partner portals announced on official government social media pages (`@DGPRBalochistan`).
* **Never Share Mobile OTPs:** Beware of fraudulent SMS messages claiming you have been selected and demanding advance security deposits or bank account details.

During the previous enrollment cycle, law enforcement authorities apprehended three fraudulent agents attempting to collect money from candidates under the pretense of guaranteed overseas job visas.

Report any suspicious individual claiming to guarantee admission in exchange for money to the official **B-TEVTA** helpline or local police authorities immediately.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Is the application form for the CM Balochistan Youth Skills Scheme free?
Yes, the application form and complete technical training under the **CM Balochistan Youth Skills Scheme** 2026 are 100% free of cost. **B-TEVTA** does not charge any processing or registration fees.

### 2. What is the official website to apply online for Balochistan Youth Skills Scheme 2026?
The official website for online application is `btevta.gob.pk`. Certain project batches are also registered via authorized portals such as `qtechtraining.org`.

### 3. What is the monthly stipend given to trainees during the program?
Selected trainees receive a **monthly stipend & free training** allowance to cover transport and personal expenses. Stipend amounts are transferred directly to candidate bank accounts or biometric wallets during the course duration.

### 4. What is the age limit for applying to the Balochistan Youth Skills Scheme?
Male and female candidates between 18 and 35 years of age possessing a valid Balochistan **CNIC** and **district domicile & quotas** are eligible to apply.

### 5. Can female candidates apply for technical and digital courses?
Yes, female candidates are highly encouraged to apply. The **Government of Balochistan** has allocated a 25% reserved quota for female applicants in digital skills like E-Commerce, Graphic Design, and Healthcare.

### 6. Do candidates from all districts of Balochistan have reserved seats?
Yes, seats are allocated on a strict **district domicile & quotas** basis across all 36 districts of Balochistan, including Quetta, Turbat, Khuzdar, Gwadar, Zhob, and Chaman.

### 7. What documents are required for online application submission?
Applicants need a scanned copy of their **CNIC**, Balochistan Domicile/Local certificate, highest educational marksheet, and recent passport-sized photograph.

### 8. Will B-TEVTA provide overseas jobs after course completion?
Yes, the program specifically targets **overseas employment target (30,000 jobs)**. High-performing graduates receive placement support, medical test guidance, and visa processing assistance.

### 9. Can I apply if I am currently a full-time university student?
Regular full-time morning university students are ineligible for residential vocational courses, as classes require mandatory full-time physical attendance at **B-TEVTA** centers.

### 10. How will I know if my name is selected in the merit list?
Shortlisted candidates are notified via official SMS on their registered mobile numbers, and final merit lists are published on the official **B-TEVTA** website (`btevta.gob.pk`).
