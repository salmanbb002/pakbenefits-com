# BISP New Direct Bank Transfer System: Shifting from Cash Camps to Commercial Bank Accounts

The BISP direct bank transfer system in 2026 allows eligible Benazir Kafalat beneficiaries to receive their quarterly stipend of Rs 13,500 directly into individual BISP Sahulat bank accounts or interoperable digital wallets across partner commercial banks, permanently eliminating cash campsite queues, agent extortion, and unauthorized fee deductions.

---

## What Is the BISP Direct Bank Transfer System in 2026?

The direct bank transfer system is a government initiative introduced by the Benazir Income Support Programme in partnership with the State Bank of Pakistan to disburse quarterly social assistance directly into personal commercial bank accounts. Under this digital model, cash distribution campsites and temporary point-of-sale agent retailers are replaced by formal banking channels.

Beneficiaries open a zero-balance BISP Sahulat Account at designated partner commercial branches or register an interoperable digital wallet linked to their computerised national identity card. The quarterly Kafalat payment of Rs 13,500 is credited electronically into the recipient's account on the disbursement release date. Beneficiaries receive a real-time confirmation SMS from the official 8171 short code and can withdraw their cash at automated teller machines or branch counters at their own convenience without paying commissions to middlemen.

---

## Which Banks Are Partnered for BISP Direct Account Transfers?

BISP has signed agreements with six commercial and microfinance banking institutions to manage the nationwide disbursement of social welfare funds. This consortium provides physical branch networks, biometric ATM access, and digital wallet infrastructure across all provinces.

The six approved partner financial institutions include:
1. **Habib Bank Limited (HBL):** Facilitates branch banking, biometric ATM withdrawals, and HBL Konnect digital wallets across designated clusters.
2. **Bank Alfalah:** Manages digital disbursement desks, Sahulat account opening, and retail agent networks across Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and Punjab.
3. **Bank of Punjab (BOP):** Handles direct account credits, branch-level biometric counters, and PayPak debit card issuance across Punjab districts.
4. **HBL Microfinance Bank:** Provides targeted micro-banking desks and community-level account management for rural women.
5. **Mobilink Microfinance Bank (JazzCash):** Operates mobile wallet integrations, enabling recipients with registered SIMs to receive and manage funds via their mobile phones.
6. **Telenor Microfinance Bank (Easypaisa):** Delivers digital branchless banking solutions for remote Union Councils.

## Direct Bank Transfer vs Traditional Cash Camps: What Changes?

The shift from manual campsite distribution to commercial bank accounts modernizes the delivery of social safety net funds and secures beneficiary rights.

| Feature | Traditional Cash Campsite System | BISP Direct Commercial Bank Transfer |
|---|---|---|
| **Disbursement Channel** | Temporary school grounds and outdoor POS agents | Formal commercial bank branches, biometric ATMs, and digital wallets |
| **Deductions & Fee Cuts** | Widespread illegal cuts of Rs 500 to Rs 1,500 by rogue agents | Exact full payment of Rs 13,500 credited with 0% fee deduction |
| **Withdrawal Timing** | Restricted to specific camp operation dates | 24/7 access through ATMs and round-the-clock digital wallet transfers |
| **Dignity & Security** | Long queues in harsh weather with crowd control risks | Respectable banking environment with air-conditioned branch seating |
| **Account Ownership** | No formal bank account created | Beneficiary holds a sovereign BISP Sahulat Account in her own name |
| **Alert Verification** | Unverified word of mouth or delayed agent claims | Instant automated SMS notifications sent directly from 8171 |

## How to Open a BISP Sahulat Bank Account (Step-by-Step Guide)

Opening a BISP Sahulat Account is a straightforward, free process carried out at designated commercial bank branches mapped to your local tehsil.

### Step 1: Confirm Eligibility on the 8171 Portal
Before visiting a bank branch, verify that your National Socio-Economic Registry survey status is active and approved. Open the official 8171 web portal ([8171.bisp.gov.pk](https://8171.bisp.gov.pk/)), enter your 13-digit CNIC number and the captcha code, and confirm that your profile shows active Kafalat eligibility.

### Step 2: Ensure Your Mobile SIM Is Registered in Your Name
Commercial banks require a functional mobile number issued against your own CNIC for account creation and SMS transaction OTPs. Visit your telecom franchise with your CNIC to verify SIM ownership if you currently use a SIM registered under a relative's name.

### Step 3: Visit Your Assigned Partner Bank Branch
Take your original valid CNIC and registered mobile phone to the designated commercial bank branch (such as HBL, Bank Alfalah, or Bank of Punjab) operating in your tehsil. Request the dedicated BISP Desk officer for a BISP Sahulat Account opening form.

### Step 4: Complete On-Site Biometric Verification
The bank representative will scan your thumbs and fingers on the NADRA-integrated biometric verification device. The real-time system confirms your identity against national civil records within 30 to 60 seconds.

### Step 5: Receive Account Details and Linkage Confirmation
Once biometric authentication succeeds, the bank officer generates your account number and issues your confirmation slip. You will receive an official SMS from both the bank and the 8171 gateway confirming that your BISP payments are permanently routed to your direct bank account.

## Required Documents for BISP Bank Registration

You must bring standard identification paperwork to avoid delays during bank account processing:
- **Original National Identity Card (CNIC):** Must be computerised, valid, and unexpired.
- **Active Mobile Phone:** Carrying the SIM card registered under your personal CNIC.
- **Children's B-Form (Nadra CRC):** Required if enrolled in the Taleemi Wazaif education stipend program.
- **BISP 8171 SMS Notification or Survey Token:** Helpful for rapid counter verification.
- **Special Attestation (if applicable):** Disability certificate from the medical board for special persons or death certificate of spouse for widow status verification.

## How to Withdraw BISP Tranche Funds from ATMs and Digital Wallets

Beneficiaries have multiple convenient options to withdraw their quarterly Rs 13,500 stipend once the 8171 deposit SMS arrives.

### Option 1: Biometric ATM Cash Withdrawal (Cardless)
1. Visit any 1Link biometric-enabled ATM belonging to your partner bank.
2. Press the screen button labelled "Biometric Transaction" or "BISP / Social Welfare".
3. Select your preferred language (Urdu or English).
4. Enter your 13-digit CNIC number.
5. Place your thumb or index finger firmly on the flashing biometric scanner.
6. Select "Cash Withdrawal", enter the full amount of 13500, and collect your cash along with the receipt.

### Option 2: PayPak ATM Debit Card
If the bank issued you a PayPak debit card, insert the card into any ATM across Pakistan, enter your confidential 4-digit PIN code, choose "Current/Sahulat Account", and withdraw your money without queueing at teller counters.

### Option 3: Digital Mobile Wallet (Raast & Mobile App)
Beneficiaries registered through mobile microfinance wallets can check their balance directly through the mobile application or USSD menu. Funds can be transferred instantly to other bank accounts via the State Bank of Pakistan's Raast instant payment system or withdrawn through verified retail agents.

## How to Resolve Biometric Failures and Account Linkage Issues

Fingerprint mismatches or verification errors at banking terminals are resolved through systematic institutional channels.

If an ATM or bank scanner fails to read your fingerprints due to skin erosion or aging:
1. **Try Multiple Fingers:** Ask the bank officer to test alternate fingers registered in your NADRA record (such as left thumb, index finger, or middle finger).
2. **Visit the Tehsil NADRA Registration Center (NRC):** If your biometrics fail repeatedly across multiple devices, visit the nearest NADRA office for biometric renewal and fingerprint re-capture.
3. **Obtain NADRA Biometric Verification Slip:** If you are over 60 years old or possess completely unreadable skin ridges, NADRA issues an official biometrics failure exemption slip.
4. **Submit Exemption at BISP Tehsil Office:** Present the NADRA slip to your local BISP Assistant Director desk to enable manual identity override and designated teller disbursement.

## How to Report Illegal Deductions and Banking Grievances

The Benazir Income Support Programme enforces a zero-tolerance policy against unauthorized fees, agent deductions, or bank counter mistreatment.

To report violations:
- **Toll-Free BISP Helpline:** Call **0800-26477** (toll-free from any landline or mobile) to register an official complaint against specific branch personnel or field agents.
- **BISP Complaint Portal:** Submit grievances online at [bisp.gov.pk](https://bisp.gov.pk/) with your CNIC number and transaction details.
- **State Bank of Pakistan Banking Mohtasib:** If a partner bank branch refuses account opening or wrongfully locks your Sahulat account, lodge a consumer dispute via the Banking Mohtasib portal ([bankingmohtasib.gov.pk](https://www.bankingmohtasib.gov.pk/)).
- **Tehsil Grievance Desk:** Visit your local BISP Tehsil Office and submit a written grievance form to the monitoring supervisor.

## Frequently Asked Questions About BISP Direct Bank Transfers

### Can I open a BISP Sahulat Account in any commercial bank of my choice?
You can open an account at any of the six official BISP partner banks assigned to your district, including HBL, Bank Alfalah, and Bank of Punjab.

### Is there any fee or minimum initial deposit required to open a BISP Sahulat Account?
Opening a BISP Sahulat Account is completely free of cost and requires zero initial deposit.

### How much money is disbursed in the 2026 BISP Kafalat quarterly tranche?
The standard 2026 quarterly BISP Kafalat payment is Rs 13,500 per eligible household.

### Will I receive Taleemi Wazaif school stipends in the same bank account?
Taleemi Wazaif education stipends for verified school-going children are deposited directly into the same BISP Sahulat bank account alongside the Kafalat tranche.

### Can an agent or bank officer deduct service charges from my payment?
No bank officer, retailer, or campsite agent is legally permitted to deduct any fee from your BISP stipend.

### What should I do if my registered mobile SIM is lost or blocked?
Visit your mobile operator's customer service center with your CNIC to reissue the SIM card, and update your phone number at the BISP Tehsil dynamic survey desk.

### Can my husband or son withdraw money from my BISP bank account?
The BISP Sahulat account is strictly tied to the female beneficiary's own CNIC and biometric verification, preventing third-party unauthorized withdrawals.

### How do I check if my BISP payment has been transferred to my bank account?
You can check your balance through an ATM inquiry, bank mobile app, or by verifying your CNIC status on the 8171 web portal at [8171.bisp.gov.pk](https://8171.bisp.gov.pk/).

### What happens if I forget my ATM PIN code?
Visit your parent bank branch with your original CNIC to generate a new PIN through on-counter biometric authentication.

### How long does it take for a BISP Sahulat Account to activate after biometric verification?
Most BISP Sahulat accounts activate immediately upon successful NADRA biometric validation, with full transaction services enabled within 24 to 48 hours.
