## What is the Apna Khet Apna Rozgar Scheme 2026 in Punjab?

The Apna Khet Apna Rozgar (AKAR) Scheme 2026 is a major provincial agricultural initiative launched by Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif under the Government of Punjab. The program transfers temporary ownership rights of cultivable state land to landless rural citizens to generate self-employment and boost crop yields.

### Main Objectives and Benefits of the AKAR Initiative

The primary objective of the Apna Khet Apna Rozgar Initiative is to reduce rural poverty and eradicate unemployment across agricultural districts in Punjab. By providing landless agricultural laborers and unemployed rural youth with guaranteed farming ground, the initiative transforms uncultivated state land into productive agricultural holdings. Beneficiaries gain direct access to sustainable livelihood opportunities, financial independence, and inclusion in provincial agricultural subsidy networks.

### Key Financial Breakdown: Land Size, Lease Rate & Development Grant

Under the Apna Khet Apna Rozgar framework, selected beneficiaries receive 3 to 5 acres of cultivable state land under a long-term 10-year lease agreement. The Government of Punjab charges a symbolic lease fee of just Rs 100 per acre per year, making land access affordable for low-income households. In addition, the Agriculture Department Punjab disburses a land development grant of Rs 50,000 per acre directly to the allottee to cover soil levelling, tube-well setup, and initial seed purchases.

## Who is Eligible for Land Allotment Under the Scheme?

Eligibility for the Apna Khet Apna Rozgar Scheme is restricted to permanent rural residents of Punjab who do not own agricultural land. The criteria prioritize small-scale agricultural workers, landless families, and unemployed youth to ensure maximum socioeconomic impact.

### Mandatory Applicant Requirements and CNIC Verification

To qualify for the scheme, applicants must possess a valid 13-digit Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC) and a verifiable Punjab Domicile Certificate. Candidates must be between 18 and 50 years of age at the time of application. Furthermore, the National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA) enforces a strict single-applicant limit per household identity, ensuring that only one member of an eligible family receives a land allotment parcel.

### Disqualification Factors and Ineligibility Rules

Applicants are disqualified from the Apna Khet Apna Rozgar program if they currently own agricultural property or have previously received state land allotments. Government employees, bank loan defaulters, and individuals with active criminal records are legally barred from participation. Revenue land record databases automatically cross-check CNIC submissions against provincial land registries, rejecting ineligible entries prior to the balloting stage.

## How to Apply Online for Apna Khet Apna Rozgar Scheme at akar.pulse.gop.pk?

Applying online for the Apna Khet Apna Rozgar Scheme requires completing a digital registration form on the official PULSE portal at `akar.pulse.gop.pk`. Applicants can register using a computer or smartphone by following a three-step digital workflow.

### Step 1: Account Creation and CNIC Registration on PULSE Portal

Open your web browser and navigate to the official portal domain `akar.pulse.gop.pk`. Click on the "Register New Applicant" button and enter your 13-digit CNIC number without hyphens. Provide an active mobile phone number registered under your own CNIC to receive a 6-digit One-Time Password (OTP) for account activation. Set a secure password to complete your user profile.

### Step 2: Filling Household, Domicile, and Revenue Land Record Details

Log into the `akar.pulse.gop.pk` portal using your registered CNIC and password. Complete the application form by entering your full legal name, father’s or husband’s name, permanent residential address, and district domicile. Select your preferred district and tehsil for land allotment from the drop-down menu. Check the mandatory self-declaration box confirming that neither you nor any dependent family member owns cultivable land.

### Step 3: Document Upload and Application Verification Submission

Upload clear scanned copies or photographs of your original CNIC (front and back), Punjab Domicile Certificate, and recent passport-sized photograph in PNG or JPEG format (under 2MB per file). Review all entered details carefully for typographical errors. Click the final "Submit Application" button to generate your unique 10-digit Application Tracking Number and save the downloadable PDF confirmation receipt.

## How Does the Selection Process and Computerized Balloting Work?

Land allotment under the Apna Khet Apna Rozgar Scheme is executed through an automated Computerized Balloting System managed by the Punjab Land Records Authority (PLRA). This digital selection mechanism eliminates manual intervention and guarantees total transparency.

### Balloting Schedule, Merit Lists, and Allotment Letter Issuance

Following the closing date of application intake, submitted entries undergo automated data verification against NADRA and revenue databases. Verified applicants are entered into regional balloting draws organized per district and tehsil. Successful candidates are published on the official merit list at `akar.pulse.gop.pk`. Winners receive an official SMS notification and are summoned to the Deputy Commissioner (DC) Office to sign the formal 10-year lease agreement and receive their physical Allotment Letter.

## How Does AKAR Scheme Integrate with Kissan Card and Agriculture Subsidies?

Selected allottees under the Apna Khet Apna Rozgar Scheme are automatically enrolled in provincial agricultural support programs managed by the Agriculture Department Punjab. Beneficiaries receive priority approval for the [CM Punjab Kissan Card scheme](https://pakbenefits.com/cm-punjab-kisan-card-online-apply-2026/), granting access to interest-free production loans up to Rs 150,000 for purchasing certified seeds, high-grade fertilizers, and approved pesticides.

Furthermore, allottees become eligible to apply for subsidized farm machinery through the [CM Punjab Green Tractor Scheme](https://pakbenefits.com/cm-punjab-green-tractor-scheme-2026-online-apply/) and receive free soil testing services from local Agriculture Extension officers. Applicants can also check existing land ownership data using the [Punjab Land Record Authority (PLRA) portal](https://pakbenefits.com/punjab-land-record-check-guide/) or complete baseline welfare scoring through [PMT score verification](https://pakbenefits.com/bisp-eligibility-criteria-guide/).

## How to Avoid Scams and Verify Official Government Communications?

Applicants must remain vigilant against fraudulent individuals, agents, or unauthorized third-party websites claiming to guarantee land allotment for payment. The Government of Punjab does not charge any application or processing fee for the Apna Khet Apna Rozgar Scheme.

Always ensure you access the official domain `akar.pulse.gop.pk` and avoid clicking unverified links sent via WhatsApp or social media. Official SMS notifications regarding balloting results are issued exclusively through official government shortcodes such as 8070 or 8171. If an individual asks for cash or personal banking PINs, report the fraud immediately to the Agriculture Department Helpline at 0800-17000 or your local AC office.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the official website to apply online for Apna Khet Apna Rozgar scheme?
The official web portal for online registration is `akar.pulse.gop.pk`, maintained by the Punjab Land Records Authority and PULSE project.

### How much land is allotted under the Apna Khet Apna Rozgar scheme?
Eligible landless farmers and unemployed youth receive 3 to 5 acres of cultivable state land depending on district land availability.

### What is the annual lease rate for the allotted land?
The Government of Punjab charges a nominal lease fee of Rs 100 per acre per year for a standard 10-year lease tenure.

### Is a financial grant provided for land development?
Yes, allottees receive a land development grant of Rs 50,000 per acre to assist with levelling, irrigation setup, and initial crop cultivation.

### Who is eligible to apply for the scheme?
Permanent rural residents of Punjab aged 18 to 50 who do not own agricultural land and have a valid CNIC are eligible.

### Can multiple family members from the same household apply?
No, the scheme enforces a strict limit of one application per household, verified automatically through NADRA family database records.

### How are land allotment winners selected?
Selection is conducted through a computerized balloting system to ensure complete transparency and merit-based distribution.

### Where can I get physical help if I cannot apply online?
Applicants can visit the dedicated AKAR Help Desks located at the offices of the Assistant Commissioner (AC) or Deputy Commissioner (DC) in their tehsil.

### Do I need to pay any application fee to submit my form?
No, registration for the Apna Khet Apna Rozgar Scheme is completely free of charge on the official portal.

### Can the allotted state land be sold or transferred to another person?
No, the allotted land is non-transferable and non-sellable; it remains state property leased strictly for agricultural cultivation.

### Can allottees apply for the CM Punjab Kissan Card?
Yes, all selected allottees receive priority registration for the CM Punjab Kissan Card to access interest-free crop loans and subsidized seeds.
