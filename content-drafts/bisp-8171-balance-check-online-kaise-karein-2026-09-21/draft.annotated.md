# BISP 8171 Balance Check Online Kaise Karein: Benazir Kafaalat Paise Check Aur ATM Se Nikalne Ka Mukammal Tarika

**BISP 8171 balance check online kaise karein**: official web portal (**8171.bisp.gov.pk**) par apna **13-digit Computerized National Identity Card** number enter karein ya **8171 SMS service** par SMS bhejein. **Benazir Kafaalat quarterly installment** ki taaza tareen raqam **Rs 10,500** hai, jo aap apne qareebi **HBL ATM biometric cash withdrawal** ke zariye ya **HBL Konnect biometric retailer network** se baghair kisi kataoti ke poori wasool kar sakte hain.

## BISP 8171 Balance Check Online Kaise Karein? (Web Portal Ka Asaan Tarika)
**8171 web portal** ke zariye **Benazir Income Support Programme** ke paise check karne ka tareeqa nihayat aasan, muft aur fori hai. Government of Pakistan ne BISP beneficiaries ki sahulat ke liye central database ko online portal se munsalik kiya hai taake khawateen ko dukanon ke chakkar na lagane paren.

Apne mobile ya computer ke zariye balance maloom karne ke liye mandarja zail tareeqa ikhtiyar karein:

1. Apne mobile ke browser mein official website **8171.bisp.gov.pk** open karein.
2. Pehle box mein apna **13-digit Computerized National Identity Card** number darj karein (bina space ya dash ke).
3. Samne screen par nazar aane wala 4-hinson ka captcha code doosre box mein type karein.
4. Sabz (green) rang ke button "Maloom Karein" par click karein.
5. Agar aap ki qist transfer ho chuki hai, to screen par wazeh likha aayega ke "Aap ke account mein **Rs 10,500** ki raqam muntaqil kar di gayi hai."

## Mobile SMS 8171 Ke Zariye Kafaalat Balance Maloom Karne Ka Tarika
Jin khawateen ya afrad ke paas internet ya smartphone ki sahulat nahi hai, woh saday key-pad walay phone se **8171 SMS service** ke zariye bhi apna balance maloom kar sakte hain. Yeh service pooray Pakistan mein tamam mobile networks (Jazz, Telenor, Zong, Ufone) par kaam karti hai.

SMS ke zariye balance check karne ke liye darj zail steps follow karein:

1. Mobile phone ka Message / SMS inbox open karein.
2. Naya message banayein aur apna **13-digit Computerized National Identity Card** number bina kisi space ya dash ke likhein (maslan: `3520112345671`).
3. Is message ko official number **8171** par send kar dein.
4. Chand lamhon mein BISP ke automated system ki janib se ek tasdeeqi SMS mosool hoga jis mein aap ki qist ki raqam aur wasooli ka status likha hoga.

## HBL ATM Se BISP Payment Nikalne Ka Step-by-Step Tarika (Baghair ATM Card)
BISP beneficiaries ko ATM se paise nikalne ke liye kisi plastic ATM debit card ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Habib Bank Limited ke tamam ATMs par **cardless ATM biometric transaction** ki sahulat mojood hai:

1. Apne shehar ya tehsil ke qareebi **HBL ATM machine** par tashreef le jayen.
2. ATM screen ke neechay keypad par mojood **green button cardless ATM transaction** (Enter / OK button) dabayein.
3. Screen par mukhtalif zabanon ke options aayenge; wahan **Urdu** zaban ka intekhab karein.
4. Screen par samne aane walay options mein se **BISP / Benazir Kafaalat** ke option par click karein.
5. Apna **13-digit Computerized National Identity Card** number screen par type karein aur tasdeeq karein.
6. Machine par lagi hui biometric fingerprint scanner device par apna seedha (right) angootha ya ungli rakhein.
7. Biometric tasdeeq mukammal hote hi screen par aap ka balance (**Rs 10,500**) nazar aayega; wahan **Raqam Nikalwayen (Cash Withdrawal)** select karein.
8. Machine se cash aur **official computerized transaction slip** wasool karein aur foran wahi khare ho kar raqam poori gin lein.

## HBL Konnect Aur Bank Alfalah Biometric Retailer Se Cash Collection
Agar aap ke ilaqay mein ATM machine mojood nahi hai ya ATM par rash zyada hai, to aap BISP ke registered **HBL Konnect biometric retailer network** ya **Bank Alfalah biometric campsite** se bhi apni raqam wasool kar sakti hain. Punjab, Sindh, aur Balochistan mein HBL Konnect jabke KPK aur Gilgit mein Bank Alfalah ke agents payment disburse karte hain:

Agent ke paas jane se pehle apna asal NADRA CNIC card lazmi sath rakhein. Agent ki biometric device par apna CNIC enter karwayen aur angootha scan karein. Payment success hone par agent se apni poori raqam wasool karein. Yaad rakhein ke agent ko paisa dene ki koi fee nahi hoti aur poori installment daina agent ki qanooni zimmedari hai.

## BISP 10,500 Installment Mein Illegal Deduction (Kataoti) Se Kaise Bachein?
BISP ki raqam wasool karte waqt sab se bara masla dukanon aur agents ki **illegal deduction kataoti** hai, jahan agents bholi-bhali khawateen se 500 se 1,000 rupay tak kat letay hain. BISP rules ke mutabiq yeh sangeen jurm hai:

- **Poori Raqam Wasool Karein:** Government of Pakistan ki janib se muqarrar karda qist **Rs 10,500** poori hai. Kisi agent ko 1 rupay bhi kataoti ya service charges ke naam par na dein.
- **Official Computerized Transaction Slip Lazmi Lein:** Har transaction ke baad POS machine ya ATM se print hone wali receipt (slip) zaroor hasil karein. Is slip par saaf likha hota hai ke kitni raqam nikali gayi aur bank charges Rs. 0 hain.
- **Pehle Angootha Na Lagwayen:** Agar koi agent kahe ke "Pehle angootha lagao kal paise milenge", to hargiz angootha na lagayein kyunke angootha lagte hi aap ki raqam agent ke account mein chali jati hai.
- **0800-26477 BISP Official Helpline Par Fori Shikayat:** Agar koi agent kataoti kare ya bad-tameezi kare, to foran BISP ki toll-free helpline **0800-26477** par us dukandar ki location aur CNIC ke sath complaint darj karwayen.

## Biometric Verification Failed (Error 93) Ka Asal Hal Kya Hai?
Bohat si mehnat kash aur buzurg khawateen ko ATM ya shop par angootha lagane par **biometric fingerprint failure error 93** ka masla pesh aata hai. Iska hal darj zail tareeqon se nikala ja sakta hai:

Agar angoothay ke nishan mitao ya khushk hone ki wajah se scan nahi ho rahay, to angoothay ko saaf paani se dho kar achi tarah saaf karein ya halka sa tel/lotion lagayein taake skin ki lakirein wazeh ho sakein. Agar phir bhi masla hal na ho, to BISP ke Tehsil Office mein qayam shuda "Biometric Facilitation Desk" par jayen. Wahan BISP assistant director aap ko NADRA ke zariye verification ka special token issue karta hai ya doosri ungliyon ki tasdeeq se payment release karwayi jati hai.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### BISP 8171 balance check karne ki official website konsi hai?
BISP balance aur ahal hone ka status check karne ki wahid official website 8171.bisp.gov.pk hai. Kisi bhi ghair-sarkari portal par apna CNIC number hargiz enter na karein.

### 8171 par SMS bhej kar balance kaise check karte hain?
Apne mobile phone ke Write SMS mein apna 13-hinson ka CNIC number bina dash ke type karein aur 8171 par send karein. Chand lamhon mein tasdeeqi jawab mil jayega.

### Kya ATM se BISP ke paise nikalne ke liye ATM card hona zaroori hai?
Nahi, ATM card ki koi zaroorat nahi hoti. Aap HBL ke biometric ATM par sabz rang ka button daba kar bina card angoothay ke nishan se paise nikal sakti hain.

### Benazir Kafaalat ki taaza tareen qist ki raqam kitni hai?
Benazir Kafaalat ki standard te-maahi qist Rs. 10,500 hai. Jin khawateen ke bachay Taleemi Wazaif mein registered hain unko school wazaif ki izafi raqam bhi sath milti hai.

### Agar dukan wala 500 ya 1000 rupay kataoti kare to kya karein?
Dukan wale ko koi kataoti na dein aur foran BISP ki toll-free helpline 0800-26477 par call kar ke dukandar ke khilaf complaint darj karwayen.

### Biometric verification failed ya Error 93 ka matlab kya hai?
Iska matlab hai ke machine par aap ke angoothay ke nishan match nahi hue. Haath dho kar doosri ungli scan karein ya BISP Tehsil Office ke verification desk se rabta karein.

### HBL Konnect shop se paise wasool karte waqt receipt lena kyun zaroori hai?
Receipt par nikaali gayi raqam aur zero fees saaf darj hoti hai, jis se retailer aap se ghair-qanooni kataoti nahi kar sakta aur payment ka pakka sabot rehta hai.

### Kya shauhar ya beta aurat ki jagah ja kar BISP ke paise nikal sakta hai?
Nahi, BISP ke qawaneen ke mutabiq registered mustahiq khatoon ka khud physical mojood hona aur biometric angootha lagana lazmi hai.

### Agar 8171 portal par "Record Not Found" aaye to kya karein?
Iska matlab hai ke aap ka NSER survey mojood nahi hai. Apne qareebi BISP Tehsil Office ja kar Dynamic Registry Desk par naya survey karwayen.

### BISP ki official complaint helpline ka number kya hai?
BISP ki official toll-free complaint helpline ka number 0800-26477 hai, jis par subah 9 se raat 11 baje tak muft call kar ke madad li ja sakti hai.
