# BISP Taleemi Wazaif 70% Attendance Rule & Verification Procedure

The **Benazir Taleemi Wazaif 70% attendance rule** requires registered school and college students to maintain at least 70% classroom attendance per quarter to receive cash stipends. If attendance falls below 70%, payments are suspended. Beneficiaries must submit a headmaster-stamped **school verification slip** at their local **BISP Tehsil Office** to resume **wazaif** transfers.

## What Is the BISP Taleemi Wazaif 70 Attendance Rule?

The **Benazir Taleemi Wazaif 70 attendance rule** is a mandatory conditional cash transfer requirement established by the **Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP)** for active **Benazir Kafaalat** beneficiary families. Under this policy, enrolled primary, secondary, and higher secondary students must attend school for a minimum of 70% of total academic days in each quarterly cycle. Official school holidays, gazetted breaks, and verified medical leaves endorsed by the institution head are excluded from missing days calculations.

The policy aims to reduce school dropout rates across Pakistan while ensuring that cash transfers translate into active educational engagement. When a child is enrolled via the official **BISP Wazaif Admission Slip**, the **BISP Compliance Monitoring System** tracks attendance logs uploaded by school headteachers or collected by regional compliance monitors. If a student's verified attendance falls below the 70% threshold during a quarterly evaluation, the system automatically marks the stipend status as paused or stopped for that specific quarter.

## Why Are BISP Wazaif Suspended or Stopped Due to Attendance?

**BISP wazaif** are suspended when the quarterly compliance audit detects attendance below 70% or when school attendance records fail to upload before the system disbursement deadline. The primary causes of stipend stoppage include prolonged unexcused school absences, unverified student transfers between schools, delays by school administration in submitting quarterly attendance registers, or duplicate **NADRA B-Form** entry errors in the database.

When the **BISP Compliance Monitoring System** runs its automated quarterly batch processing, any student lacking a verified 70% attendance record is flagged. The mother's **BISP Kafaalat** core cash payment (Rs 13,500) continues normally, but the child's educational stipend add-on (ranging from Rs 1,500 to Rs 4,500 depending on gender and education level) is put on hold until verified attendance documentation is re-submitted.

## How to Verify and Fix 70% Attendance Rule Stoppage (Step-by-Step)

Resolving a **Taleemi Wazaif** payment stoppage requires obtaining an official **BISP School Verification Slip**, securing the school principal's stamp, and submitting it to the nearest **BISP Tehsil Office**. Beneficiaries can restore blocked stipends and receive retroactive quarterly payments within 14 to 21 working days by following this verified procedure.

### Step 1: Obtain the Official BISP Wazaif Verification Slip
Visit your local **BISP Tehsil Office** or download the official **BISP Taleemi Wazaif Verification Slip** from the **8171 web portal**. Ensure the slip displays your child's full name, **NADRA B-Form** number, school registration number, and current class grade.

### Step 2: Present the Slip to the School Headmaster or Principal
Take the form to your child's government or BISP-registered private school. Request the headmaster or class teacher to fill in the exact attendance percentage for the preceding quarter, record total working days vs attended days, and apply the official school rubber stamp and signature.

### Step 3: Submit Document to BISP Tehsil Office Data Entry Desk
Bring the stamped verification slip along with the mother's original **CNIC** and child's original **NADRA B-Form** to the **BISP Tehsil Office** compliance desk. The data entry operator will scan the document, enter the updated attendance logs into the BISP portal, and issue a digital tracking receipt code.

### Step 4: Verify Status on 8171 Web Portal
After 7 to 10 working days, check the mother's **CNIC** on the **8171 web portal** or send an SMS with the 13-digit **CNIC** to **8171**. Once the status updates from "Pending Compliance" to "Eligible", the accrued stipend will be released in the upcoming payment cycle.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens if my child's attendance is exactly 69%?
If attendance is below 70%, the BISP automated system automatically blocks the quarterly stipend. You must obtain a school headmaster statement showing total working days excluding authorized leaves to reach the 70% threshold before resubmitting the verification slip.

### How can I check BISP Taleemi Wazaif attendance status online?
You can check stipend eligibility by entering the mother's 13-digit **CNIC** on the official **8171 web portal** (8171.bisp.gov.pk) or by sending the **CNIC** number via SMS to **8171**. If attendance is incomplete, the portal will display a compliance pending message.

### Are school summer and winter vacations counted against the 70% rule?
No, official government school vacations, gazetted holidays, and emergency closures are excluded from total academic days. BISP calculates 70% attendance strictly based on active school open days.

### Will I get previous unpaid wazaif after attendance verification?
Yes, once the school verification slip is cleared and approved at the **BISP Tehsil Office**, all retroactive arrears for the paused quarters are disbursed alongside the next quarterly **BISP Kafaalat** payment.

### Can private school students receive BISP Taleemi Wazaif?
Yes, provided the private school is registered with the provincial education department and enrolled in the **BISP Compliance Monitoring System** database. Unregistered private schools cannot issue valid verification slips.

### What should I do if the school headmaster refuses to sign the slip?
If a registered school headmaster refuses to sign the verification slip, contact the BISP Field Compliance Officer at your district **BISP Tehsil Office** or call the BISP Toll-Free Helpline (0800-26477) to request an official school visit.

### How often must school attendance be verified for BISP Wazaif?
Attendance is monitored continuously every quarter. While initial enrollment requires a physical admission slip, subsequent quarterly checks are updated automatically by school compliance monitors or through periodic verification slip updates.

### What if my child transfers to a new school mid-academic year?
In case of a school transfer, you must submit a new BISP School Transfer & Verification Form at the Tehsil Office containing the new school's registration code and headmaster verification to prevent payment interruption.

### Does missing BISP Kafaalat verification affect Taleemi Wazaif?
Yes, Benazir Taleemi Wazaif is an add-on benefit linked to the core **Benazir Kafaalat** program. If the mother's Kafaalat status becomes inactive or unverified, all associated child wazaif payments are automatically frozen.

### What is the quarterly stipend amount for Taleemi Wazaif in 2026?
As of 2026, quarterly stipends are Rs 1,500 for primary boys, Rs 2,000 for primary girls, Rs 2,500 for secondary boys, Rs 3,000 for secondary girls, Rs 3,500 for higher secondary boys, and Rs 4,000 for higher secondary girls, with a bonus Rs 3,000 graduation stipend for girls completing primary education.
