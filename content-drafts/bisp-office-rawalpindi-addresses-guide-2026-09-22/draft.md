## 3. Core Services Provided at Rawalpindi Tehsil Offices

Each BISP Tehsil Dynamic Registration Center in Rawalpindi operates digital counters directly linked with NADRA's citizen identity database:

1. **NSER Dynamic Survey Execution**: Families seeking new registration or undergoing mandatory 3-year resurvey can answer the socio-economic questionnaire to establish or recalculate their Proxy Means Test (PMT) score.
2. **Benazir Kafaalat Quarterly Registration**: Low-income female household heads whose PMT score is 32 or lower receive official enrollment status for the recurring Rs. 13,500 quarterly financial aid.
3. **Biometric Failure Troubleshooting (Non-BVS Forms)**: Beneficiaries who encounter "Biometric Verification Failed" (Error 93 / Error 99) at retail shops or ATMs can undergo official optical fingerprint testing. When prints cannot be captured, staff process Non-BVS verification slips allowing cash collection through designated branch counters.
4. **Benazir Taleemi Wazaif School Stipends**: Eligible mothers can submit verified admission slips for enrolled children to activate quarterly primary (Rs. 2,000 to Rs. 2,500), secondary (Rs. 3,000 to Rs. 3,500), and higher secondary (Rs. 4,000 to Rs. 4,500) educational bonuses.
5. **Beneficiary Data Modification**: Updating address changes, adding newborn infants via new B-Forms, or registering a deceased spouse's death certificate to transition from dependent to primary beneficiary.

## 4. Checklist of Required Documents for Rawalpindi Applicants

To prevent being turned away at the document verification counter, gather these mandatory official records prior to your visit:

- **Original Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC / Smart Card)**: Must be an authentic, unexpired NADRA identity card. Laminated photocopies or police report tokens are strictly rejected.
- **Child Registration Certificates (NADRA B-Form)**: Computerized CRC/B-Forms for all biological and adopted children living within the household under the age of 22.
- **Active Mobile Phone & Registered SIM Card**: A mobile SIM card registered in the applicant's own name and CNIC. BISP strictly delivers official transaction passwords and eligibility confirmations via `8171`.
- **Electricity / Utility Bill**: Most recent household electricity bill (IESCO) reflecting consumer number, connection address, and tariff details.
- **Certificates for Special Circumstances**:
  - *Widow Beneficiaries*: Computerized Death Certificate of the husband issued by the local Union Council and recorded at NADRA.
  - *Special Needs / Disabled Citizens*: NADRA Disability CNIC bearing the wheelchair emblem and disability assessment board certificate.

## 5. Step-by-Step Desk Workflow for Beneficiaries in Rawalpindi

All BISP centers across Rawalpindi follow a streamlined, computerized protocol to ensure transparent queue management:

1. **Step 1 — Security & Initial Screening**: Present your original CNIC at the reception entrance to confirm jurisdiction and receive a computerized token number.
2. **Step 2 — Document Scrutiny**: A BISP desk assistant reviews your original CNIC, B-Forms, and utility bill to confirm completeness.
3. **Step 3 — NSER Digital Interview**: When your token appears on the digital calling board, sit with the data entry officer. Provide honest details regarding household assets, monthly family income, livestock, motor vehicle ownership, and family members.
4. **Step 4 — Biometric Live Capture**: Place your thumbs and fingers on the electronic scanner and face the digital camera for live facial capture.
5. **Step 5 — Receipt Issuance & Verification**: The system prints a confirmation slip containing your unique NSER tracking number. Verify all typed information before signing or thumb-marking the form.
6. **Step 6 — Central Verification**: The information is uploaded securely to the central data warehouse in Islamabad. You will receive an official status SMS from `8171` within 4 to 8 weeks confirming your eligibility status.

The Government of Pakistan strictly prohibits any fees or commissions for BISP administrative services:

- **Absolute Zero Fees**: Registration, dynamic surveys, token issuance, Non-BVS form verification, and Taleemi Wazaif enrollment are 100% free of cost.

If an agent demands a deduction, refuse the payment, demand a full electronic receipt, and immediately report the agent's shop address and terminal ID.
