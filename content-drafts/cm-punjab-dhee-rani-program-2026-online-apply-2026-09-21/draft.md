# CM Punjab Dhee Rani Program 2026 Online Apply: CMP Portal Registration, Eligibility & Salami ATM Card

To apply for the CM Punjab Dhee Rani Program 2026 online, eligible applicants can submit their details through the official government portal (cmp.punjab.gov.pk) or register in person at the local office of the Deputy Director of Social Welfare & Bait-ul-Maal. Under this flagship social protection initiative introduced by Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif, the Punjab government sponsors collective wedding ceremonies for 3,000 deserving daughters annually. Beneficiary brides receive a direct cash gift of Rs. 100,000 (Salami) through a Bank of Punjab ATM card, a bridal household essentials package worth Rs. 200,000, and a fully hosted wedding feast for 20 family guests.

## What Is the Chief Minister Punjab Dhee Rani Program 2026?
The Chief Minister Punjab Dhee Rani Program is a comprehensive social welfare and female empowerment initiative designed to alleviate the crushing financial burdens of wedding expenses for impoverished, orphaned, and vulnerable families in Punjab. Administered by the Punjab Social Welfare & Bait-ul-Maal Department in partnership with the Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB), the program honors newly-wed daughters with dignity rather than treating social assistance as charity.

In Pakistani society, inflation and social pressures often force low-income parents into predatory debt or delay daughters' marriages for years. The Dhee Rani Program resolves this hardship by providing direct financial capital, necessary domestic durable goods, and an honorable, state-facilitated collective marriage ceremony supervised by district administration officials.

## Complete Financial and Material Assistance Package: Salami & Jahez Box
Every couple selected through the verified Dhee Rani selection process receives a comprehensive three-tier support package:

1. **Rs. 100,000 Direct Cash Salami:** Disbursed directly into the bride’s newly issued Bank of Punjab (BOP) account and accessible via an active ATM card. This unencumbered cash allows the young couple to start their marital life with immediate financial security.
2. **Comprehensive Bridal Essentials Box (Jahez Package):** A formal gift package worth approximately Rs. 200,000 comprising 24 essential household items, including a Holy Quran with translation and prayer mat, a formal bridal jewelry set, double bed with mattress, 72-piece dinner set, 14-piece non-stick cookware set, electric stand fan, sewing machine, and heavy-duty electric iron.
3. **Fully Catered Reception for 20 Guests:** The government arranges a dignified wedding reception hall, floral decorations, and meals for 20 invited family members and close relatives per couple, ensuring an honorable celebration without catering debts.

## Eligibility Criteria: Age, Domicile & Vulnerability Thresholds
To ensure transparent selection and prevent commercial exploitation, the Social Welfare Department enforces strict statutory eligibility criteria:

- **Provincial Domicile:** The bride must be a permanent resident of Punjab holding a verified Punjab domicile certificate and a valid Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC).
- **Age Window:** The bride must be aged between **18 and 40 years** at the time of online application submission.
- **Priority Vulnerability Categories:** Preference is legally accorded to destitute young women, orphans who have lost one or both parents, persons with disabilities (holding a NADRA Special Disability CNIC), and daughters of disabled or chronically ill parents.
- **Marital Status:** The bride must be unmarried and must have a verified, confirmed marriage match (intended groom) with formal parental consent or guardian authorization.
- **Applicant Identity:** The application can be submitted either directly by the bride herself, her father, mother, or legal guardian.

## How to Apply Online via the CMP Punjab Portal (cmp.punjab.gov.pk)
The online registration process is simple, secure, and accessible from any smartphone or computer:

1. **Navigate to the Official Portal:** Open **cmp.punjab.gov.pk** or download the official "Maryam Ko Batayn" mobile application.
2. **Create Applicant Account:** Register using the applicant’s 13-digit CNIC, active mobile phone number, and a secure password.
3. **Fill Out Bridal Profile:** Enter the bride's personal biodata, date of birth, educational background, father’s/guardian’s details, and exact residential address.
4. **Enter Groom’s Particulars:** Provide the prospective groom's full name, CNIC number, residential address, and family contact information.
5. **Upload Required Scanned Documents:** Attach clear photographs or scans of the bride’s CNIC, groom’s CNIC, bride’s Punjab domicile, father’s/guardian’s CNIC, and death certificate of parents (if applying under the orphan quota) or disability certificate (if applying under the PWD quota).
6. **Submit Application:** Review all entered information, tick the legal affidavit checkbox confirming the accuracy of the data, and click "Submit Application". The portal will issue an automated Application Tracking ID and send a confirmation SMS.

## In-Person Application Option at District Social Welfare Offices
For applicants living in rural villages without internet connectivity or smart devices, the Punjab government provides an accessible offline registration channel:

- **Visit District Headquarters:** Applicants can visit the office of the **Deputy Director of Social Welfare & Bait-ul-Maal** in their respective district headquarters.
- **Free Physical Form:** Collect the official physical Dhee Rani application form free of charge from the reception facilitation desk.
- **Staff Assistance:** Dedicated social welfare case officers are stationed to assist illiterate applicants in filling out the form, scanning documents, and uploading the data onto the provincial CMP database.
- **Official Receipt:** Ensure you obtain a signed and stamped counterfoil receipt displaying your application registration serial number.

## Verification Process: Special Verification Committees & On-Ground Inspection
To preserve program integrity and prevent fake marriage claims or multiple submissions, a robust dual-stage scrutiny protocol is conducted:

- **Desktop Scrutiny:** PITB and NADRA cross-verify identity records, age eligibility, and marital history to ensure neither party has claimed duplicate benefits or misrepresented their marital status.
- **On-Ground Field Inquiries:** Specially notified District Scrutiny Committees—headed by the Deputy Commissioner and comprising the Deputy Director of Social Welfare, Assistant Commissioner, and local notables—conduct discreet physical field inquiries at the applicant’s neighborhood to verify genuine financial distress.
- **Helpline 1312 Support:** Applicants can call the dedicated toll-free helpline **1312** at any stage to check application scrutiny progress, report delays, or request guidance.

## Collective Wedding Ceremony Organization, Logistics & Guest Arrangements
Once applications are approved, the Social Welfare Department schedules regional collective marriage ceremonies across all administrative divisions:

- **Divisional Event Venues:** Ceremonies are organized at prestigious public convention centers, sports complexes, or marquee halls managed by the government.
- **Formal Nikah Registration:** Registered Nikah Khawans (marriage registrars) are arranged on-site to execute and officially register the Nikah-Nama with the local Union Council without registration fees.
- **Logistical Transport:** The district administration coordinates designated passenger vans or buses to transport couples and their 20 invited guests from tehsil centers to the venue and back.
