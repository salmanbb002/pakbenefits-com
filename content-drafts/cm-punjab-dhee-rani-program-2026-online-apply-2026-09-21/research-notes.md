# Research Notes: CM Punjab Dhee Rani Program 2026 Online Apply

- **Primary Keyword:** cm punjab dhee rani program 2026 online apply
- **Secondary / LSI Keywords:** cmp punjab gov pk dhee rani registration, dhee rani scheme eligibility criteria, dhee rani program helpline 1312, maryam nawaz collective marriage program 2026, dhee rani online form submission, punjab social welfare mass marriage grant
- **Language / Voice:** Dignified, supportive, highly practical and informative English.
- **Search Intent:** Procedural (Do) and Informational (Know). Needy brides, parents, or guardians seeking dignified collective marriage financial support, online registration on cmp.punjab.gov.pk, eligibility rules (age 18-40, orphan/disabled/destitute), Rs. 100,000 Salami ATM card, and ceremony details.
- **Author:** Muhammad Salman (Single author)
- **Target Portal:** https://cmp.punjab.gov.pk/
- **Official Helpline:** 1312 (Dedicated Punjab Social Welfare helpline)
- **Key Official Facts:**
  1. Financial grant: Rs. 100,000 monetary wedding gift (Salami) paid directly to the bride through a dedicated Bank of Punjab (BOP) ATM card.
  2. Bridal package (worth ~Rs. 200,000): Holy Quran with translation, prayer mat, formal bridal clothes, double bed with mattress, 14-piece cooking set, 72-piece dinner set, stand fan, sewing machine, and iron.
  3. Wedding ceremony arrangements: Full catering and meal services hosted by the district administration for up to 20 wedding guests per registered couple.
  4. Eligibility: Punjab resident brides aged between 18 and 40 years who are destitute, orphans, persons with disabilities (PWDs), or daughters of disabled parents.
  5. Multi-channel registration: Online through cmp.punjab.gov.pk, via the "Maryam Ko Batayn" application, or physical submission at the office of the Deputy Director of Social Welfare & Bait-ul-Maal in each district.
