# Apni Zameen Apna Ghar Balloting Result 2026: How to Check CNIC Status & Plot Rules

The **Apni Zameen Apna Ghar Balloting Result 2026** is officially available for verification online via the **Government of Punjab** portal at **azag.punjab.gov.pk**. Applicants can check their **balloting status verification** for the **2000 plot Phase 1 quota** of **3 marla free plots** across **19 designated districts** by entering their valid **13-digit CNIC** without dashes.

---

## How Do You Check the Apni Zameen Apna Ghar Balloting Result Online by CNIC?

To check your **Apni Zameen Apna Ghar Program** balloting result online, visit the **AZAG official web portal** at `azag.punjab.gov.pk/ballot/result`, enter your **CNIC number** without dashes, solve the **Captcha verification** image, and click "Submit". Your status will display instantly on screen.

### What Information Is Required on the Official AZAG Portal?

Checking your status on the official portal requires only your 13-digit **Computerized National Identity Card** (**CNIC number**). You do not need a password or application tracking number. Ensure you enter the CNIC digits continuously (for example, `3520212345671`) and complete the visual **Captcha verification** code correctly. The service is completely free, and no fee is charged for checking your result online.

### What Do the Different Balloting Statuses Mean?

When you submit your CNIC on the portal, system records return one of three official status classifications. The table below outlines what each result indicates and the required action.

| Portal Status Output | Meaning of Result | Immediate Required Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Selected / Successful** | Your CNIC won a **3 marla free plot** in the Phase 1 balloting. | Await official SMS/call; collect your **allotment letter** from **PHATA**. |
| **Not Selected** | Your application was eligible but not drawn in the Phase 1 allotment. | Retain your application records for Phase 2 or sister housing schemes. |
| **Pending / Under Verification** | Documents or residency verification are currently undergoing audit. | Contact the **PITB** helpline at **0800-09100** or visit your local **PHATA** office. |

## What Is the Apni Zameen Apna Ghar (AZAG) Scheme Quota for Phase 1?

The **2000 plot Phase 1 quota** of the **Apni Zameen Apna Ghar Program** comprises **3 marla free plots** allocated across low-income families in Punjab. Initiated by Chief Minister **Maryam Nawaz Sharif** and executed by the **Punjab Housing and Town Planning Agency**, the program targets homeless and landless citizens.

### Which 19 Districts Are Included in the 2,000-Plot Distribution?

The 2,000 free plots in Phase 1 are distributed across **19 designated districts** in Punjab where state land was cleared and developed by **PHATA**. Key participating districts include:

- **Central & North Punjab:** Lahore, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Sargodha, Gujranwala, Sialkot, and Kasur.
- **South Punjab:** Multan, Bahawalpur, Dera Ghazi Khan, Rahim Yar Khan, Muzaffargarh, and Sahiwal.
- **Western & Central Districts:** Jhang, Okara, Sheikhupura, Attock, Chakwal, and Mianwali.

Plot allocations within each district were executed strictly via computerized transparent balloting to ensure equal opportunity for all verified residents of that specific district.

### Who Was Eligible to Participate in the Balloting?

Eligibility for the **AZAG** balloting required applicants to be permanent residents and **CNIC** holders of Punjab with a verified family income below Rs. 60,000 per month. Applicants could not own any residential property anywhere in Pakistan and had to be registered in the **PSER portal**. Only one application per household unit was permitted to maintain social equity.

## What Should You Do If Selected in the AZAG Balloting Result?

If your CNIC is marked as "Selected" in the **Apni Zameen Apna Ghar Balloting Result 2026**, you must follow official post-selection protocols to claim physical possession of your plot. Winners must obtain their **allotment letter** from **PHATA** and comply with strict state construction guidelines.

```
[Selected on Portal] ➔ [Receive Official SMS/Intimation] ➔ [CNIC Verification at PHATA] ➔ [Collect Allotment Letter] ➔ [Start House Construction within 6 Months]
```

### What Are the Possession and Allotment Letter Procedures?

Selected applicants must visit their designated district **Punjab Housing and Town Planning Agency** office or **e-Khidmat Markaz** with their original **CNIC**, original domicile certificate, and two passport-sized photographs. Upon biometric verification, **PHATA** issues the physical **allotment letter** specifying your scheme location, block number, and exact **3 marla free plot** number.

### How Does the 6-Month Construction Rule Apply to Winners?

Under Section 14 of the scheme framework, every successful beneficiary must comply with the **6-month construction rule**, initiating house construction within 6 months of taking physical plot possession. This rule prevents speculative land holding and ensures that allocated plots serve immediate family housing needs. Failure to start construction within the stipulated timeframe without valid justification can lead to cancellation of the allotment.

### Can You Sell or Transfer Your Allotted 3-Marla Plot Before 5 Years?

No, beneficiaries are strictly bound by the **5-year transfer restriction**, prohibiting them from selling, transferring, renting, or mortgaging their allotted plot for a mandatory period of 5 years from the date of allotment. The **Government of Punjab** retains underlying title conditions during this non-transferable window to prevent property dealers and commercial investors from exploiting low-income beneficiaries.

## Can AZAG Plot Winners Apply for the Apni Chhat Apna Ghar (ACAG) Construction Loan?

Yes, winners of free plots under the **Apni Zameen Apna Ghar Program** are fully eligible to apply for the **Apni Chhat Apna Ghar Program** (**ACAG**) interest-free construction loan scheme.

Once you possess your 3-marla plot **allotment letter**, you can apply through `acag.punjab.gov.pk` for an interest-free loan of up to **Rs. 1,500,000 (15 Lac PKR)** to construct your home. Key loan highlights include:

- **Loan Amount:** Up to Rs. 15 Lacs (1.5 Million PKR).
- **Interest Rate:** 0% (100% Interest-Free, subsidized by Punjab Government).
- **Repayment Tenure:** Up to 7 years (84 monthly installments).
- **Monthly Installment:** Approximately Rs. 14,000 per month, starting 3 to 6 months after first installment disbursement.

## What Should You Do If Your Balloting Result Shows Not Selected or Pending?

If your result shows "Not Selected", your application remains registered in the housing database for potential future phases or supplementary allotments. If your status shows "Pending", submit an inquiry to the **Punjab Information Technology Board** (**PITB**) helpline at **0800-09100** or visit the nearest **e-Khidmat Markaz** to clear document discrepancies.

## How Can You Avoid Scams and Verify Official AZAG Communications?

All official communications regarding the **Apni Zameen Apna Ghar Program** are conducted exclusively through official government domains (`.punjab.gov.pk`) and official SMS handles (such as `8171` or `8070`). 

> [!WARNING]
> The **Government of Punjab** never asks for processing fees, cash deposits, or bank transfers over phone calls or WhatsApp to issue allotment letters. Report any suspicious demands to the official **PITB** helpline **0800-09100** immediately.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is the 3-marla plot in the Apni Zameen Apna Ghar scheme completely free?
Yes, the 3-marla residential plot awarded through the **AZAG** balloting is 100% free of land cost for successful eligible applicants. Winners are not required to pay land purchase fees to the government.

### Where can I check the Apni Zameen Apna Ghar balloting result online?
You can check your balloting result online by visiting the **AZAG official web portal** at `azag.punjab.gov.pk/ballot/result` and entering your 13-digit **CNIC number**.

### Can I check the AZAG balloting result by sending an SMS?
Currently, official web verification at `azag.punjab.gov.pk/ballot/result` is the primary real-time method. Official SMS notifications are sent directly by the government to selected winners on their registered mobile numbers.

### What should I do if the result portal displays "Invalid CNIC"?
Double-check that you entered all 13 digits of your **CNIC** correctly without hyphens or spaces. If the message persists, verify whether your initial application was successfully submitted during the open registration window.

### Can a selected applicant sell the plot immediately after winning?
No, successful applicants cannot sell, lease, or transfer their plot for at least 5 years from the date of receiving possession under the **5-year transfer restriction**.

### How much time is given to start constructing a house on the allotted plot?
Beneficiaries are legally required to begin house construction within 6 months of obtaining physical possession of their plot under the **6-month construction rule**.

### Can I get a loan to build a house on my newly allotted 3-marla plot?
Yes, **AZAG** plot winners can apply for the **Apni Chhat Apna Ghar Program** (**ACAG**) interest-free construction loan scheme to receive up to Rs. 15 Lacs for building their house.

### Which department oversees plot allotments under the AZAG scheme?
The **Punjab Housing and Town Planning Agency** (**PHATA**), under the Housing, Urban Development & Public Health Engineering Department (HUD&PHED), manages plot allotments and physical handovers.

### Are there any application or processing fees to collect the Allotment Letter?
No, official allotment processing is transparent and free of illegal surcharge fees. Only standard civic documentation verification is required at **PHATA** offices or **e-Khidmat Markaz**.

### Who can I contact for official help regarding my AZAG balloting status?
You can call the official **Government of Punjab** toll-free helpline operated by **PITB** at **0800-09100** or visit your district **e-Khidmat Markaz** for direct administrative support.
