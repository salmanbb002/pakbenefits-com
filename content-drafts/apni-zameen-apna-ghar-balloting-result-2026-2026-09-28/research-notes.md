## Step 2 — Head-Entity Research (Authoritative Sources)

1. **Apni Zameen Apna Ghar Program (AZAG)**
   - *Canonical Name:* Apni Zameen Apna Ghar Program
   - *Type:* Government Housing Scheme
   - *Official URL:* `https://azag.punjab.gov.pk/`
   - *Managing Authority:* Punjab Housing and Town Planning Agency (PHATA) / HUD&PHED Punjab.
   - *Key Facts:* Initiated under Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif to provide 2,000 free 3-marla plots in Phase 1 across 19 districts for low-income Punjab residents.

2. **Government of Punjab**
   - *Canonical Name:* Government of Punjab
   - *Type:* Provincial Government
   - *Wikipedia/Wikidata:* `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Punjab,_Pakistan`
   - *Head of Government:* Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif.

3. **CNIC (Computerized National Identity Card)**
   - *Canonical Name:* Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC)
   - *Type:* Identifier / Citizen Identification Document
   - *Issuer:* National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA)
   - *Format:* 13 digits without dashes (e.g., 3520212345671).
