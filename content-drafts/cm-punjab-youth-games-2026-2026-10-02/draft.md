# CM Punjab Youth Games 2026: Online Registration, Eligibility, Schedule & Sports List

The CM Punjab Youth Games 2026 is a province-wide athletic initiative organized by the Sports Board Punjab to discover grassroots sporting talent across schools, colleges, universities, and Deeni Madaris. Featuring 19 popular sports disciplines, 5 age brackets, and Rs 500 Million in cash prizes, eligible students register online at youthgames.punjab.gov.pk.

## What Are the CM Punjab Youth Games 2026?

The CM Punjab Youth Games 2026 represent the largest youth sports development movement in the history of Punjab, Pakistan. Launched under the patronage of Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif and executed by the Sports Board Punjab (SBP), the program aims to revitalize competitive sports within educational institutions and build a sustainable talent pipeline for national teams.

The initiative connects more than 62,000 educational institutions and is projected to engage over 5 million male and female students across all 36 districts of Punjab. Backed by a historic cash prize pool of Rs 500 Million, the tournament structures structured competitive pathways spanning institutional selection trials up to high-stakes provincial championship finals.

## Who Is Eligible to Apply for the Punjab Youth Games 2026?

Eligibility for the CM Punjab Youth Games 2026 is extended to enrolled students across public and private educational institutions, religious seminaries, labor schools, and special education centers throughout Punjab. Applicants must meet strict age verification standards aligned with their academic level and validated via national identity documentation.

### Age Brackets and Exact Date of Birth Cutoffs

Candidates are categorized into five distinct competition brackets based on their age calculated as of December 31, 2026. Official registration requires entering a valid NADRA B-Form number for minors or a Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC) number for adult participants.

| Competition Bracket | Eligible Institutions | Age Range | Allowed Date of Birth Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| Middle Schools | Public and private middle schools | 10 to 15 years | Jan 1, 2011 – Dec 31, 2016 |
| Secondary Schools | High schools, labor schools, non-formal centers, madaris | 13 to 17 years | Jan 1, 2009 – Dec 31, 2013 |
| Colleges | Higher secondary schools, intermediate colleges, TEVTA | 13 to 21 years | Jan 1, 2005 – Dec 31, 2013 |
| Universities | Public and private universities, medical/engineering colleges | 17 to 50 years | Jan 1, 1976 – Dec 31, 2009 |
| Special Persons | Hearing, visually, or physically impaired students | 15 to 35 years | Jan 1, 1991 – Dec 31, 2011 |

### Eligible Educational Institutions (Schools, Madaris & Universities)

Participation is institutionally grounded, ensuring that every registered athlete represents a verified academic or specialized entity. In addition to mainstream government and private institutions, Deeni Madaris (religious seminaries) are integrated into the secondary and college brackets, while special education institutions compete in dedicated adaptive categories.

## Which Sports Disciplines Are Featured in the 2026 Youth Games?

The tournament features 19 core sports disciplines tailored to match the physical development stages and institutional facilities of each competition bracket. Disciplines range from traditional athletics and field sports to modern digital e-gaming.

### School and College Sports Disciplines

Middle and secondary school brackets prioritize fundamental physical conditioning and popular team sports, whereas college-level competition expands into tactical martial arts and racket sports.

- Middle Schools: Athletics (sprints, long jump) and tape ball cricket.
- Secondary Schools: Athletics, badminton, hard ball cricket, football, tug of war, and kabaddi (boys only).
- Colleges: Athletics, badminton, basketball, chess, cricket, football, hockey, kabaddi, karate, table tennis, taekwondo, tug of war, volleyball, and wrestling (boys only).

### University, Special Persons and E-Gaming Categories

The university bracket introduces advanced technical disciplines and emerging competitive sports, while the special persons category features customized adaptive events designed for safety and maximum athletic expression.

- Universities: All college sports disciplines plus e-sports (digital gaming), fencing, padel tennis, rifle shooting, rowing, handball, and squash.
- Special Persons: Tape ball cricket, blind cricket, adaptive badminton, 100m sprint, 4x100m relay, tug of war, standing board jump, wheelchair racing, and futsal.

## How to Register Online for CM Punjab Youth Games 2026 at youthgames.punjab.gov.pk

Online registration for the CM Punjab Youth Games 2026 is conducted through the official web portal at youthgames.punjab.gov.pk. The digital application system allows individual students to submit their credentials directly or through designated sports teachers at their respective institutions without paying any registration fee.

### Step-by-Step Registration Guide for Students and Institutions

1. Access the Official Portal: Visit youthgames.punjab.gov.pk on any smartphone or computer and click the "Register Now" button.
2. Select Category & Institution: Choose your competition bracket (e.g., College or University), type at least five characters of your institution's name, and select it from the verified drop-down list. If your institution is unlisted, select the manual entry option.
3. Enter Personal & Contact Details: Input your full name, father's name, B-Form or CNIC number, gender, exact date of birth, division, district, tehsil, union council, and active mobile phone number.
4. Select Sport & Uniform Dimensions: Choose your preferred athletic discipline along with your required sports kit size and athletic footwear size.
5. Upload Photograph & Submit: Attach a clear digital headshot image, complete the security math captcha, check the declaration box, and press submit to receive your confirmation receipt.

### Document Requirements and Photo Upload Guidelines

Applicants must ensure their uploaded photograph is in JPG or PNG format with a file size strictly under 256 KB. Identity details must match official NADRA records exactly, as verification takes place before venue entry during institution-level selection trials.

## What Is the Official Trial Schedule and Competition Roadmap for 2026?

The CM Punjab Youth Games 2026 follow a progressive 7-phase elimination schedule designed to select top provincial athletes through fair local trials. Matches progress sequentially from local school grounds to district stadiums and apex provincial sports complexes.

### Institutional Selection Trials and Union Council Rounds

The initial competitive phase begins directly within registered schools and colleges, allowing internal sports committees to select institutional squads across each sport.

| Tournament Phase | Competition Level | Scheduled Date Range |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 | Online Student & Institutional Registration | September 15 – September 30, 2026 |
| Phase 2 | Institutional Selection Trials | October 7 – October 13, 2026 |
| Phase 3 | Union Council Level Competitions (School Brackets) | October 23 – October 26, 2026 |
| Phase 4 | Tehsil Level Competitions | November 2 – November 5, 2026 |
| Phase 5 | District Level Championships | November 9 – November 12, 2026 |
| Phase 6 | Divisional Level Championships | November 17 – November 20, 2026 |
| Phase 7 | Provincial Grand Finals | December 1 – December 5, 2026 |

### Tehsil, District, Divisional, and Provincial Final Schedules

Victorious teams at the Union Council level move forward to Tehsil and District competitions supervised by District Sports Officers. Divisional champions earn full sponsorship to compete in the Provincial Grand Finals held from December 1 to December 5, 2026, in Lahore.

## What Are the Cash Prizes and Grievance Support Details?

The Government of Punjab has allocated Rs 500 Million in direct cash prizes and sports scholarships for high-performing athletes, winning institution squads, and certified coaches. Prizes are awarded at District, Divisional, and Provincial championship stages to incentivize long-term athletic commitment.

To guarantee transparent evaluation during trials, the Sports Board Punjab under Director General Muhammad Tariq Qureshi operates an online Grievance Redressal Portal on youthgames.punjab.gov.pk. Any student or institution facing unfair trial scoring can submit a formal complaint detailing their registration ID, district, tehsil, and trial panel location for immediate administrative review.

### Where can I register for the CM Punjab Youth Games 2026?

Yes, students enrolled in registered Deeni Madaris are fully eligible to compete under the secondary school and college competition brackets.

### What sports are available for middle school students?
Middle school students aged 10 to 15 years can participate in athletics events (sprints and jumps) and tape ball cricket competitions.

### What is the maximum file size for photo upload during registration?
The digital photograph uploaded to the registration form must be in PNG or JPG format and must not exceed 256 KB in file size.

### What is the total prize money for the CM Punjab Youth Games 2026?
The Government of Punjab has allocated a total cash prize pool of Rs 500 Million for winning athletes, teams, and participating educational institutions.

### How can I file a complaint if I face unfair scoring during trials?
Participants can file a formal complaint using the online Grievance Redressal Portal available on youthgames.punjab.gov.pk by submitting their trial details and registration number.

The Provincial Grand Finals for the CM Punjab Youth Games 2026 are scheduled to take place from December 1 to December 5, 2026.
