The **CM Punjab Electric Bike Scheme** (Phase 2) provides **100,000 electric scooties** to **students** across Punjab at **PKR 199,000** each, with a **Rs 90,000 capital subsidy**, zero down payment, **0% interest**, and approximately **Rs 3,000 monthly installments** over **3 years**. Apply online at **bikes.punjab.gov.pk** before **October 4, 2026**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Deadline alert:** Phase 2 applications close on **October 4, 2026**. Only **bikes.punjab.gov.pk** accepts applications.

---

## What Is the CM Punjab Electric Bike Scheme?

### A Green Youth-Mobility Initiative of the Punjab Government

The **CM Punjab Electric Bike Scheme** is a provincial programme launched by Chief Minister **Maryam Nawaz Sharif** under the CM Youth Initiative and run by the **Transport & Mass Transit Department**. The **Bank of Punjab (BOP)** finances the bike, while the **Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB)** runs the portal and the **computerised e-balloting**.

### What You Actually Receive

A successful applicant receives an electric scooty with a 2026 make, a 72V 30Ah LiFePO4 battery, a 1,000 W BLDC rear-wheel hub motor, and a top speed of 50–55 km/h. GoPb covers **insurance, registration and token tax**, plus a **free helmet and safety rods**.

---

## What Is the Price, Subsidy and Installment Plan in 2026?

### PKR 199,000 Price and Rs 90,000 Capital Subsidy

The e-bike is priced at **PKR 199,000**, and the Government of Punjab contributes a **Rs 90,000 capital subsidy** per unit. Independent guides still repeating the July 2026 **Rs 70,000 subsidy / Rs 14,000 down payment** are outdated.

### Rs 3,000 Monthly Installment Over 3 Years

The balance is repaid in ~**Rs 3,000 monthly installments** over a **3-year** term, with **no down payment**. GoPb absorbs interest, insurance, registration and token tax.

## Who Is Eligible for the CM Punjab Electric Bike Scheme?

### Student Status and Institution Requirements

Applicants must be bonafide students of **public and private educational institutions** registered with a Government of Punjab authority, with proof of enrolment and the **latest paid fee slip**. Students of **federally chartered institutes** are not eligible.

### Age and Driving-License Requirements

The **minimum age is 16 years**, and the applicant must hold a **driving license, learner's permit or juvenile driving permit**. Identification is a valid **CNIC, B-Form or CRC**.

---

## What Are the Guarantor and Income Requirements?

### Who Can Be a Guarantor

A **guarantor** (normally a parent or legal guardian) must have a verifiable income, a valid CNIC and a recent bank statement for BOP verification.

### The PKR 40,000 Minimum Income Rule

The guarantor must earn a minimum of **PKR 40,000 per month**, and the application's mobile number must be registered in the guarantor's own name against their CNIC.

---

## What Documents Do I Need to Apply?

### Student Documents

- Valid **CNIC (or B-Form/CRC)**
- Student card / bonafide certificate
- **Latest paid fee slip**
- **Learner's permit or driving license**
- Passport-size photo

### Guarantor Documents

Guarantor **CNIC**, recent **bank statement**, and proof of regular income.

---

## How to Apply Online at bikes.punjab.gov.pk (Step by Step)

1. Open **bikes.punjab.gov.pk**.
2. Register with **CNIC** + mobile number, verify **OTP**.
3. Fill the application form.
4. Upload documents and select the bike.
5. Submit and save the **Application ID**.
6. **E-balloting** by PITB.
7. **BOP verification** and delivery.

---

## How to Check Application Status and Balloting Results

Track by **Application ID** or **CNIC** on the portal dashboard. **E-balloting** is a random PITB draw; selection is not final until **Bank of Punjab** verification is complete.

---

## CM Punjab vs PM PAVE Scheme: What's the Difference?

The **PAVE** federal scheme (pave.gov.pk) covers Pakistan-wide applicants with Rs 50,000 (leasing) / up to Rs 80,000 (self-finance), distinct from the Punjab scheme's Rs 90,000 subsidy and 100,000 scooties.
