## What Is the CM Punjab Electric Bike Scheme?

### A Green Youth-Mobility Initiative of the Punjab Government

The CM Punjab Electric Bike Scheme is a provincial electric-vehicle programme launched by Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif under the CM Youth Initiative and run by the Transport & Mass Transit Department. Its purpose is to give students affordable, eco-friendly transport by letting them acquire a subsidised electric scooty through interest-free instalments instead of paying the full market price up front. The scheme is a direct response to the cost of daily commuting — buses, rickshaws and petrol — that weighs heaviest on students and young riders.

Implementation is shared across three institutions. The Bank of Punjab (BOP) finances the bike and handles verification and disbursement, while the Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB) runs the online portal and the transparent, computerised e-balloting used to select beneficiaries when applications exceed the available quota. Because the provincial government pays the markup, the applicant never carries an interest burden — the real cost of the bike is split between a state subsidy and the student's own instalments.

### What You Actually Receive

A successful applicant receives an electric scooty — not cash — with a 2026 make, a 72V 30Ah LiFePO4 battery, a 1,000 W BLDC rear-wheel hub motor, and a top speed of 50–55 km/h. The Government of Punjab also covers the vehicle's insurance, registration and token tax, and the delivery package includes a free helmet and safety rods. In practice, this means the student only has to finance the difference between the bike's price and the Rs 90,000 subsidy, spread over three years with no down payment.

## What Is the Price, Subsidy and Installment Plan in 2026?

### PKR 199,000 Price and Rs 90,000 Capital Subsidy

The e-bike is priced at PKR 199,000, and the Government of Punjab (GoPb) contributes a capital subsidy of Rs 90,000 toward every unit. This subsidy is a grant — it does not have to be repaid. The remaining amount is what the applicant finances through the Bank of Punjab, and because GoPb also bears the financing's interest cost, the student repays only the principal, with no markup added.

Several independent guides still repeat the earlier July 2026 announcement of a Rs 70,000 subsidy and a Rs 14,000 down payment. That package was superseded. As of the current Phase 2 window (closing October 4, 2026), the official portal confirms the improved terms below.

### Rs 3,000 Monthly Installment Over 3 Years

The financed balance is repaid in approximately Rs 3,000 monthly installments over a 3-year (36-month) term, and no down payment is required. The Government of Punjab further absorbs the insurance, registration and token-tax costs that would otherwise be added on top of the installment. In short, a selected student pays roughly Rs 3,000 a month and nothing upfront, while the state covers the subsidy, interest and statutory charges.

| Cost item | Who pays |
| :--- | :--- |
| E-bike price (PKR 199,000) | Shared — Rs 90,000 GoPb subsidy + financed balance |
| Interest / markup | Government of Punjab (0% for applicant) |
| Down payment | None required |
| Insurance | Government of Punjab |
| Registration & token tax | Government of Punjab |
| Monthly installment (~Rs 3,000 × 36 months) | Student |

## Who Is Eligible for the CM Punjab Electric Bike Scheme?

### Student Status and Institution Requirements

The applicant must be a bonafide student enrolled in an educational institution duly registered or recognised by the relevant department, board, authority or regulatory body of the Government of Punjab. Crucially, both public-sector and private-sector institutions qualify — the scheme is not limited to government colleges. Students must provide proof of enrolment, such as a valid student card or an official letter or certificate from the institution, together with the latest paid fee slip.

One exclusion that is easy to miss: students enrolled in **federally chartered institutes**, including their campuses, are not eligible under this provincial scheme. If your institute is chartered by the federal government rather than the Government of Punjab, you should look at the federal PAVE programme instead (covered below).

### Age and Driving-License Requirements

The minimum age to apply is 16 years at the time of submission, and the applicant must hold a valid driving license, learner's driving permit, or juvenile driving permit, as applicable. Applicants aged 16 who need a juvenile permit can obtain one by visiting their nearest Sahulat Center with a guardian. For identification, the applicant must hold a valid CNIC, B-Form or Child Registration Certificate (CRC) as applicable to their age.

## What Are the Guarantor and Income Requirements?

### Who Can Be a Guarantor

Because most student applicants are not yet earning, the scheme requires a guarantor — normally a parent or legal guardian — who carries valid proof of income. The guarantor must have a verifiable and regular source of income and a valid CNIC. They must also provide a recent bank statement as evidence of financial capacity, since the Bank of Punjab conducts its own verification before approving financing.

### The PKR 40,000 Minimum Income Rule

The guarantor must have a minimum monthly income of PKR 40,000. This threshold is applied under the bank's regulations and is the figure most applicants overlook when planning their application. In addition, the mobile number entered on the application must be registered in the guarantor's own name against their CNIC — or in the applicant's name if the student has a documented source of income. A SIM registered to a relative or an agent will fail verification, so confirm SIM ownership before submitting.

## What Documents Do I Need to Apply?

### Student Documents

Before opening the portal, gather clear, legible copies of every document. Blurred scans, expired licences or name mismatches are the most common reason for rejection or delay. The student set includes:

- Valid CNIC (or B-Form / CRC where allowed for the age bracket)
- Student card or bonafide/enrolment certificate from the institution
- Latest paid fee slip proving current enrolment
- Motorcycle learner's permit or driving license
- Recent passport-size photograph (clear, plain background)

### Guarantor Documents

The guarantor must supply their valid CNIC, a recent bank statement showing financial capacity, and proof of a regular income (such as a salary slip or equivalent). The mobile number used for OTPs and status updates must be registered in the guarantor's own name. Keep the originals ready, because the Bank of Punjab re-verifies guarantor and income details at the financing stage.

## How to Apply Online at bikes.punjab.gov.pk (Step by Step)

The application is entirely digital — there are no paper forms, office visits or agents. Follow these steps inside the Phase 2 window (deadline October 4, 2026):

1. **Open the official portal** — go to [bikes.punjab.gov.pk](https://bikes.punjab.gov.pk/). This is the only legitimate student application portal.
2. **Create your account** — register with your CNIC number and an active mobile number, then verify the OTP sent by SMS. Use a SIM registered in your own (or your guarantor's) name.
3. **Fill the application form** — enter personal, academic and contact details exactly as they appear on your documents, including your institution and enrolment information.
4. **Upload documents** — attach the scans from the checklist above and select your bike option from the official catalogue.
5. **Submit and save your Application ID** — review everything, submit, and note the Application ID. You will need it to track your status and balloting result.
6. **E-balloting** — after the deadline, PITB runs a computerised draw. Selected applicants are notified by SMS and the result appears on the portal.
7. **BOP verification and delivery** — the Bank of Punjab verifies your documents (and guarantor), and once financing is finalised, the bike is delivered through the assigned dealer. Installments then begin as per your plan.

## How to Check Application Status and Balloting Results

### Track by Application ID or CNIC

After submission, you can monitor progress by logging into the official portal dashboard with your Application ID or CNIC. The portal reports the current stage of your file — submitted, under verification, selected in ballot, approved for financing, or delivered. Because the process is digital, the same dashboard also shows your SMS-notification history, so keep the registered mobile number active throughout the window.

### What Balloting Means for You

When valid applications exceed the 100,000-scooty allocation, selection is made through PITB's computerised e-balloting, a random draw that cannot be influenced by any person, agent or website. Being selected in the ballot is not the final step — you must still clear the Bank of Punjab's verification and financing formalities. A name on a selection list, without completed verification, is not a guaranteed bike.

## CM Punjab vs PM PAVE Scheme: What's the Difference?

The CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme is a provincial programme; the Prime Minister E-Bike Scheme operates under the federal Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification (PAVE) initiative. They use different portals and rules, and being eligible for one does not automatically make you eligible for the other.

| Parameter | CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme | PM PAVE Scheme |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Government level | Provincial (Punjab) | Federal |
| Coverage | Punjab only | Pakistan-wide (incl. AJK & Gilgit-Baltistan) |
| Main beneficiaries | Punjab students (teachers/employees announced) | Eligible applicants across Pakistan |
| Latest allocation | 100,000 electric scooties | 40,000 two-wheelers + 1,000 three-wheelers (first ballot) |
| Financial support | Rs 90,000 subsidy per e-bike | Rs 50,000 (bank leasing) / up to Rs 80,000 (self-finance) |
| Official portal | bikes.punjab.gov.pk | pave.gov.pk |
| Selection | PITB computerised e-balloting + BOP verification | Federal computerised e-balloting + verification |

If you are a Punjab student at a provincially-registered institution, apply through the Punjab portal. If your institute is federally chartered or you live outside Punjab, the PAVE programme at pave.gov.pk is the correct route.

## Official Portals, Helpline and Scam Alerts

### Only Two Portals Are Official

For students, the only official application portal is **bikes.punjab.gov.pk**. The separate teacher track uses **ptf.punjab.gov.pk**. Any other website that asks for your CNIC, OTP, bank details or a "registration fee" is not official — and domains such as `ptfpunjabgov.com` are not government sites despite the name.

### Helpline and Safety Rules

The official helpline is **042-99212260**, with email support at **support@bikes.punjab.gov.pk**. Remember these safety rules:

- Registration is free across all channels — never pay an agent or "middleman" who promises guaranteed selection.
- Never share your CNIC image, OTP, password or bank PIN with anyone.
- Never transfer money through JazzCash, Easypaisa or any wallet to an unknown person for "fast approval".
- Computerised e-balloting cannot be influenced — no private person can guarantee a bike.
- Confirm the full `.gov.pk` domain before entering any information.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the price of the e-bike under the scheme?

The electric scooty is priced at PKR 199,000. The Government of Punjab contributes a Rs 90,000 capital subsidy toward this price, and the remaining balance is financed interest-free.

### How much capital subsidy is provided by the Government of Punjab?

The Government of Punjab provides a capital subsidy of Rs 90,000 per e-bike. This amount is a grant and does not have to be repaid by the student.

### Is a down payment required for the e-bike?

No down payment is required. The financed balance is repaid entirely through approximately Rs 3,000 monthly installments over a 3-year term.

### What is the financing period, and who pays the interest?

The financing period is 3 years, and the Government of Punjab (GoPb) pays the full interest cost. The applicant pays only the principal, at 0% markup.

### Who pays the insurance, registration and token-tax costs?

The Government of Punjab bears the insurance, registration and token-tax costs of the e-bike. These charges are not added to the student's installments.

### How many e-bikes are being provided under the scheme?

Phase 2 provides 100,000 electric scooties to students across Punjab, covering all 36 districts.

### Which students are eligible based on their educational institution?

Students of both public and private educational institutions are eligible, provided the institution is registered or recognised by the relevant Government of Punjab authority. Students of federally chartered institutes are not eligible under this provincial scheme.

### What is the minimum age to apply?

The minimum age is 16 years at the time of application. Applicants must also hold a valid driving license, learner's permit or juvenile driving permit.

### Who can act as a guarantor, and what income is required?

A parent or legal guardian can act as guarantor, provided they have a valid CNIC and a verifiable monthly income of at least PKR 40,000, supported by a recent bank statement.

### What documents do I need to apply?

You need a valid CNIC (or B-Form/CRC), a student card or bonafide certificate, the latest paid fee slip, a learner's permit or driving license, a passport-size photo, and your guarantor's CNIC, income proof and bank statement.

### What is the last date to apply?

The last date to apply for Phase 2 is October 4, 2026. Applications are submitted only through bikes.punjab.gov.pk.

### Are female students treated differently?

Phase 2 introduced a dedicated scooty allocation with a reserved quota for female students at the divisional level to promote women's mobility, while core eligibility (age, licence, enrolment) applies equally to male and female applicants. Confirm the exact female quota on the official portal.

### Is the CM Punjab scheme the same as the PM PAVE scheme?

No. The CM Punjab scheme is provincial and covers Punjab students, while PAVE is a federal programme covering applicants across Pakistan (including AJK and Gilgit-Baltistan) with different subsidy amounts and a different portal (pave.gov.pk).

### Can government teachers and employees apply?

Government school teachers apply through a separate track using their PESS number via ptf.punjab.gov.pk, and a track for serving government employees has been announced. Both tracks have their own eligibility rules and should be confirmed on the relevant official portal.

### Is there any fee to apply?

No. Registration is completely free on the official portal. Never pay an agent, and never share your OTP or bank details with anyone claiming to speed up selection.
