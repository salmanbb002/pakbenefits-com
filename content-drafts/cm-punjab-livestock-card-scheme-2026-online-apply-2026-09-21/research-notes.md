## Information Gain Pass (What Competitors Miss)
1. **The Exact Animal Ratio & Financial Formula:** Many competitors quote vague lump-sum numbers. This guide provides the exact formula: Rs. 27,000 per animal over 4 months for 5 to 20 male calves (minimum 5 calves = Rs. 135,000; maximum 20 calves = Rs. 540,000).
2. **The SPMS-9211 Ear-Tagging Prerequisite:** You cannot just apply online and receive cash; veterinary doctors must physically inspect, vaccinate, and ear-tag the animals into the provincial 9211 system.
3. **Restricted Spend Architecture (POS Only):** The Livestock Card is not an open ATM cash-withdrawal card; it is locked to registered agricultural feed, silage, and mineral mixture dealers via Point of Sale (POS) machines to ensure 100% funds reach animal nutrition.
4. **Loan Repayment Cycle & Credit Discipline:** Complete explanation of the 120-150 day bullet repayment cycle after fattened animals are sold in livestock markets, protecting farmers from compound debt.
