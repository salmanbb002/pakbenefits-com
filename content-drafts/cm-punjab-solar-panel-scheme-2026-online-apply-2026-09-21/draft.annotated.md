## What Is the CM Punjab Roshan Gharana Solar Panel Scheme 2026?
The **Chief Minister Punjab Roshan Gharana Solar Scheme** is a flagship social welfare and energy independence initiative launched by Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif to shield low- and middle-income families from soaring electricity tariffs. Managed jointly by the **Punjab Energy Department** and the **Punjab Information Technology Board**, the program provides comprehensive, field-ready rooftop solar energy systems to hundreds of thousands of domestic consumers across all 36 districts of Punjab.

Unlike traditional commercial solar leasing programs that mandate cumbersome interest charges or recurring service fees, the **Roshan Gharana Program** provides 100% free solar power packages to the poorest **protected domestic consumers** and heavy 90% capital subsidies to middle-tier households. By generating clean solar electricity right at the domestic roof level, the scheme drastically reduces baseline grid demand and permanently lowers monthly utility bills for vulnerable working-class citizens.

## Complete Eligibility Criteria: Who Qualifies for Free vs Subsidized Solar Systems?
Eligibility for the Chief Minister Solar Scheme is governed strictly by verifiable historical electricity billing data rather than arbitrary political discretionary quotas. The **Punjab Energy Department** interfaces directly with regional **electricity distribution companies (LESCO, MEPCO, FESCO, GEPCO, IESCO)** to audit consumer consumption histories across the prior six billing cycles.

### Tier 1: 100% Free Solar Systems for Protected Consumers (Up to 200 Units)
Domestic power consumers classified officially as **protected domestic consumers** who maintain an average monthly consumption below **200 units** qualify for a 100% government-funded solar installation with zero upfront capital cost. Within this tier, households utilizing up to 100 electricity units receive a **550W solar photovoltaic system** complete with an inverter and storage battery, while households utilizing between 101 and 200 units receive a dual-panel **1,100W solar photovoltaic system** complete setup.

### Tier 2: 90% Government Subsidized Financing for 201 to 500 Unit Consumers
Households that consume between 201 and 500 units per month fall under the subsidized co-financing window where the Government of Punjab pays 90% of the total equipment and installation cost. The beneficiary contributes only the remaining 10% equity, with flexible, interest-free installment repayment plans structured over a 5-year tenure through **The Bank of Punjab**.

### DISCO Reference Number & Non-Defaulter Requirements
Applicants must possess an active, non-defaulter domestic electricity meter connection registered under a verifiable **14-digit consumer electricity bill reference number** with a **sanctioned load of up to 2 kilowatts**. Meters with unresolved theft FIRs, outstanding bill arrears, court litigations, or commercial tariff categorizations are automatically disqualified during automated database screening.

## How to Register Online for the Roshan Gharana Solar Scheme via cmsolarscheme.punjab.gov.pk
Registering for the **cm punjab solar panel scheme 2026 online apply** takes less than five minutes through the unified PITB energy portal without requiring any paper application forms. Applicants must follow these verified steps to record their application:

1. Open your web browser on a smartphone or computer and navigate to the official portal: **cmsolarscheme.punjab.gov.pk**.
2. Select your relevant **electricity distribution company (LESCO, MEPCO, FESCO, GEPCO, IESCO)** from the dropdown list.
3. Enter your **14-digit consumer electricity bill reference number** exactly as printed on the upper section of your recent monthly utility bill.
4. Input your 13-digit Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC) number without hyphens or spaces.
5. Provide an active, biometrically verified mobile phone number registered under the applicant's own CNIC to receive one-time SMS verification pins.
6. Verify your declared home address and click "Submit Application" to generate your official computerized tracking ID.

## How to Check Application Status & Eligibility via 8800 SMS Service
For citizens who lack stable internet connectivity or desktop computers, the Punjab government maintains a direct, paperless SMS verification gateway through the official **8800 solar scheme SMS gateway**. This service allows domestic power consumers to verify eligibility or monitor balloting status directly from any standard mobile phone:

1. Open the default SMS or text messaging application on your mobile handset.
2. In the message body, type your **14-digit consumer electricity bill reference number**, add a single space, and then type your 13-digit CNIC number (for example: `12345678901234 3520112345671`).
3. Send this text message to the official government shortcode **8800**.
4. Within a few moments, the PITB central server returns an automated status SMS confirming whether your meter reference qualifies as a **protected domestic consumer**, whether your data has been matched, or whether your balloting result is successful.

## Critical Scam Alert: Protecting Yourself from Fake Solar Registration Fees
The widespread popularity of the Roshan Gharana Solar Scheme has given rise to organized cyber syndicates and fraudulent street agents preying on low-income consumers. Legitimate government protocols mandate complete transparency and zero advance cash handling:

- **Zero Application Fees:** The Punjab Government never charges any application fee, registration tax, or form cost. Any website, social media page, or individual asking for Rs. 1,000 to Rs. 5,000 is an outright scam.
- **No EasyPaisa or JazzCash Transfers:** Government representatives will never ask you to transfer money to personal mobile wallet accounts for "urgent token issuance" or "guaranteed winning in balloting."
- **Official Domains Only:** Ensure all web visits are hosted strictly on official `.punjab.gov.pk` domains. Never enter your CNIC or meter details on unauthorized third-party `.com` or `.xyz` portals.
- **Official Complaints:** If approached by an unauthorized middleman, immediately lodge a formal complaint with the **0800-02345 solar scheme helpline** or contact local law enforcement.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the official website to apply for the CM Punjab Solar Scheme?
The only official portal for the CM Punjab Roshan Gharana Solar Scheme is cmsolarscheme.punjab.gov.pk. Applicants should never enter personal CNIC or electricity bill information on unofficial commercial portals or social media pages.

### How can I check my solar scheme eligibility via SMS?
You can check eligibility by sending your 14-digit electricity bill reference number followed by a space and your 13-digit CNIC number to the official shortcode 8800. The automated central server will reply with your current eligibility tier.

### Is the CM Punjab Solar Panel Scheme completely free?
The scheme is 100% free with zero fees for protected domestic consumers consuming up to 200 electricity units per month. For households consuming between 201 and 500 units, the Punjab government provides a 90% subsidy, requiring the consumer to pay only a 10% share.

### What equipment is included in the free solar panel package?
The free package includes Tier-1 monocrystalline solar panels (550W or 1,100W), a pure sine wave hybrid inverter, a storage battery for evening hours, heavy-duty mounting structures, DC wiring, and free certified installation.

### What is the maximum electricity consumption limit to qualify for a free system?
To qualify for a 100% free system, the household's average electricity consumption must not exceed 200 units per month over the preceding six billing cycles, and the meter must be classified as a protected domestic connection.

### Can tenants apply for the Roshan Gharana Solar Scheme using their rented home meter?
Tenants can apply provided they submit the 14-digit bill reference number of the rented property alongside written owner consent and tenancy documentation during post-balloting technical verification.

### How are winners chosen if applicant volume exceeds available solar kits?
Winners are selected through a transparent computerized e-balloting system designed and operated by the Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB) across all 36 administrative districts.

### What does it mean if the 8800 SMS returns "Record Not Found"?
A "Record Not Found" reply means the entered 14-digit bill reference number was mistyped or the regional DISCO has not yet synchronized recent consumer billing data with the central PITB server.

### What should I do if an agent asks for money to guarantee a solar panel allotment?
You should immediately refuse payment and report the individual to the official Punjab Energy Department helpline at 0800-02345, as government solar kits are distributed strictly through merit without any cash fees.

### What is the official helpline number for the CM Punjab Solar Scheme?
The official toll-free helpline for inquiries, complaints, and status updates regarding the Roshan Gharana Solar Scheme is 0800-02345, operated during business hours by the Punjab Energy Department.
