## Head Entities & Authoritative Sources
1. **Chief Minister Punjab Roshan Gharana Scheme** (`GovernmentService`) — Flagship renewable energy welfare program initiated by Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif to shield vulnerable households from soaring tariff inflation.
2. **Punjab Energy Department** (`GovernmentOrganization`) — The primary administrative body formulating policy, vendor pre-qualification, and quality standards for solar PV inverters and tier-1 photovoltaic panels.
3. **Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB)** (`GovernmentOrganization`) — Technical architect of `cmsolarscheme.punjab.gov.pk`, automated SMS dispatch engine (8800), and the computerized e-balloting system.
4. **Power Distribution Companies (DISCOs)** (`GovernmentOrganization`) — LESCO, GEPCO, FESCO, MEPCO, and IESCO (in Punjab territories) providing real-time API verification of 14-digit consumer reference numbers and historical 6-month unit consumption data.
5. **The Bank of Punjab (BOP)** (`FinancialService`) — Financing partner facilitating interest-free installments and subsidized loans for consumer tiers exceeding 200 units.

## Information Gain Pass (What Competitors Miss)
1. **The Protected Consumer Status Myth:** Most low-quality blogs state that anyone paying an electricity bill qualifies. In reality, NEPRA and Punjab Energy Department rules strictly mandate that applicants must hold "Protected Domestic Consumer" status with consistent monthly consumption below 200 units across the prior 6 consecutive billing cycles.
2. **Phase 1 Balloting Delivery vs Phase 2 Waitlist:** Competitors publish generic claims that "registration is open forever." This guide provides the factual 2026 timeline: Phase 1 balloting winners verification, ongoing door-to-door physical installations by certified EPC contractors, and preparation for Phase 2 registration intake.
3. **The 8800 SMS Response Error Guide:** Detailed breakdown of SMS replies from 8800, explaining what "Record Not Found," "Non-Protected Consumer," and "Balloting List Pending" mean in practice.
4. **Anti-Scam Consumer Warning:** Direct expose of fraudulent WhatsApp messages and social media ads requesting Rs. 2,000 to Rs. 4,500 registration or delivery fees. All official government solar provisions are completely free of advance cash deposits.
