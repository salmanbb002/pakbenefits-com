The **Wazir-e-Azam Apna Ghar Program** — "Ghar Ho Tu Apna" — is Pakistan's federal housing finance scheme. It offers **first-time homeowners** home loans up to **Rs 10 million** at a **5% fixed markup** for the first 10 years, repayable over a **20-year** term, through partner banks. Apply at **apnaghar.gov.pk**.

## What Is the Wazir-e-Azam Apna Ghar Program?

### A Federal Markup-Subsidy Housing Scheme

The **Wazir-e-Azam Apna Ghar Program** is a federal, government-backed housing finance scheme launched by Prime Minister **Shehbaz Sharif** on 30 April 2026 and regulated by the **State Bank of Pakistan**. Its purpose is to let **first-time buyers** purchase or build a home through partner banks at a markup far below the market rate — **5% fixed** for the first decade — instead of paying the 20%-plus rates typical of ordinary home loans. The slogan "Ghar Ho Tu Apna" captures the goal: working and middle-class families should own their home, not rent someone else's.

The mechanism is a **markup subsidy and risk-sharing** arrangement. The government pays the difference between the 5% rate the borrower gets and the bank's commercial rate, and it shares part of the credit risk on the loans. That is what lets commercial banks, Islamic banks, the **House Building Finance Company (HBFC)**, and — since July 2026 — **non-bank financial companies (NBFCs)** offer a 20-year mortgage at 5% in the first place. The scheme succeeds the earlier **Mera Pakistan Mera Ghar (MPMG)** markup subsidy programme, which the new initiative rebranded and expanded.

### What the Loan Actually Covers

The financing can be used for any one of four purposes: buying a constructed house, buying a flat, buying a residential plot together with construction, or building on a plot you already own. Two things are explicitly out of scope — a plot-only purchase with no construction, and pure renovation finance. The property itself must fall within the size limits: a house of up to **10 marla (2,720 square feet)** or a flat of up to **1,500 square feet**, with no cap on the property's price.

## How Much Can I Borrow, and What Is the Markup?

### Loans Up to Rs 10 Million in Four Slabs

The maximum financing is **Rs 10 million**, offered in four standard slabs. The exact installment depends on the slab, the markup, and the tenure; the figures below are the indicative monthly payments announced by the Prime Minister's Office at launch, calculated at the 5% rate over 20 years.

| **Loan slab** | Typical use | Indicative monthly installment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Rs 2,500,000 | Smaller home or 3-marla construction | Rs 16,499 |
| Rs 5,000,000 | 5-marla home or ~1,000 sq ft flat | Rs 32,997 |
| Rs 7,500,000 | 7-marla home or larger flat | Rs 49,497 |
| Rs 10,000,000 | Up to 10-marla home or 1,500 sq ft flat | Rs 65,996 |

These installments hold for the first 10 years. From year 11 onward the rate moves to **1-year KIBOR plus 3%**, so the monthly payment can change with market conditions. The bank must disclose this re-pricing schedule before you sign.

### How the 90:10 Split Works

The bank finances up to **90%** of the property value; you contribute the remaining **10% as equity**. On a Rs 10 million home, that means you bring roughly Rs 1 million and the bank finances the rest. There is **no processing fee and no prepayment penalty**, so you can settle the loan early at no extra cost. This is the single biggest difference from a standard mortgage, where early settlement is usually charged.

## Who Is Eligible for the Apna Ghar Program?

### The Core Requirements

Eligibility is deliberately broader than the provincial housing schemes because this programme targets the working and middle class, not only the poorest households. To qualify you must be:

- A resident Pakistani citizen holding a valid **CNIC** — or a **Non-Resident Pakistani (NRP)** holding a **NICOP or POC**.
- A **first-time homeowner**, meaning no residential property is currently registered in your name anywhere in Pakistan.
- Earning a stable, verifiable income — salary, business income, or remittance.
- Free of loan defaults, with a clean credit history (a clean e-CIB record).

Owning agricultural land or a commercial shop does not disqualify you. The "first-time homeowner" rule applies only to residential property.

### Income, Age and Work History

The **minimum monthly income** and age rules are set by each participating bank, so they vary slightly. As a general guide, most banks require a minimum gross income of around **Rs 25,000 to Rs 50,000** per month, and the applicant must not exceed about 60 years of age (salaried) or 65 years (self-employed) at loan maturity. Work history matters too: salaried applicants typically need six months to two years of continuous employment, while self-employed applicants usually need two to three years in the same business.

## What Documents Do I Need?

### Identity and Income Documents

Gather clear, complete copies before you start — incomplete files are the most common cause of delay. You will need:

- Valid **CNIC** (and your spouse's CNIC if married)
- Recent passport-size photographs
- Proof of income — salary slips or a salary certificate for salaried applicants, or bank statements and tax/NTN records for the self-employed
- Recent bank statements showing your income and your 10% equity amount

### Property and Compliance Documents

For the property itself, prepare the sale agreement or title document, the Fard / registry / allotment letter, an approved building plan for construction, and any NOC from the housing society or development authority. You must also sign an affidavit declaring you own no other residential property, and you may be asked for personal and professional references. The financed property is mortgaged in the bank's name as security, as standard for housing finance.

## How to Apply Online at apnaghar.gov.pk (Step by Step)

The application is digital. Follow these steps:

1. **Open the official portal** — go to [**apnaghar.gov.pk**](https://apnaghar.gov.pk/). This is the only official federal application portal; it is hosted on the `.gov.pk` domain and linked from the **State Bank of Pakistan** website.
2. **Create your account** — register with your 13-digit **CNIC** (no dashes), a mobile number registered in your own name, and an email address. Verify the OTP sent by SMS and the confirmation link sent by email.
3. **Complete the application form** — enter your personal, income, employment and property details, then select your loan slab (Rs 2.5M / 5M / 7.5M / 10M) and preferred bank.
4. **Upload documents** — attach clear scans of the checklist above as PDF or JPG files, each under 2 MB. Blurry scans are rejected at verification.
5. **Choose your bank** — pick from the participating commercial banks, Islamic banks, **HBFC**, or (from July 2026) eligible **non-bank finance and microfinance companies**.
6. **Submit and save your Tracking ID** — review everything, submit, and keep the Tracking ID. The bank contacts you within 3–5 working days for valuation and verification.
7. **Approval and disbursement** — banks are mandated to complete approval within **15 working days** of a complete application. For a purchase, funds go to the seller; for construction, funds are released in tranches as work progresses.

## Which Banks and NBFCs Offer the Apna Ghar Loan?

### The Participating Lenders

The scheme is delivered through a wide network of financial institutions, not a single bank. Confirmed participants include **National Bank of Pakistan (NBP)**, **Bank of Punjab (BOP)**, **Meezan Bank**, **HBL**, **Allied Bank**, **UBL**, **MCB Bank**, **Bank Alfalah**, **Bank AL Habib**, **Faysal Bank**, **Askari Bank**, **Mobilink Bank** and **Standard Chartered**, plus the **House Building Finance Company (HBFC)**. Both conventional and Shariah-compliant financing are available — Islamic banks use the **Diminishing Musharakah** mode, where you and the bank co-own the property and you buy out the bank's share over time.

### The July 2026 NBFC Expansion

A change most older guides miss: from July 2026 the programme is no longer bank-only. **Non-bank housing finance and investment finance companies** can now lend up to **Rs 10 million**, and **microfinance companies** up to **Rs 5 million**. This matters for applicants who are closer to a microfinance institution than to a commercial bank, and it widens the reach of the scheme into smaller cities and towns.

## How to Check Application Status and File a Complaint

### Tracking Your Application

After submission you receive a Tracking ID that you use to follow the file through the selected bank — submitted, under verification, approved, or disbursed. Because approval is mandated within **15 working days**, a stalled application is a sign to follow up with the bank directly rather than wait silently.

### The SBP Complaint Route

If a bank delays, overcharges, or refuses a legitimate application without reason, the **State Bank of Pakistan** operates a dedicated complaint mechanism for this scheme. Applicants can register a grievance through the **SBP complaint portal at complaint.sbp.org.pk**, supported by SBP and bank staff. This is the official escalation path — not an agent, and not a "facilitation" website.

## Apna Ghar vs Apni Chhat Apna Ghar: What's the Difference?

The names are confusingly similar, but these are different schemes run by different governments. The **Wazir-e-Azam Apna Ghar Program** is federal; **Apni Chhat Apna Ghar (ACAG)** is a Punjab-only scheme launched by Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif.

| Parameter | Wazir-e-Azam Apna Ghar (Federal) | Apni Chhat Apna Ghar (Punjab) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Government level | Federal | Punjab |
| Coverage | All provinces + Islamabad + GB + AJK | Punjab only |
| Maximum loan | **Rs 10 million** | Rs 1.5 million |
| Markup | **5% fixed** for 10 years, then KIBOR + 3% | 0% (interest-free) |
| Tenure | Up to **20 years** | 7–9 years |
| Income rule | Verifiable stable income | Household income below ~Rs 60,000/month |
| Property limit | Up to **10 marla** / 1,500 sq ft flat | Up to 5 marla urban / 10 marla rural |
| PMT score | Not required | Must be 60 or below |
| Portal | **apnaghar.gov.pk** | acag.punjab.gov.pk |

The rule of thumb: if you are a salaried or self-employed buyer anywhere in Pakistan looking for a 5–10 marla home, the federal programme is your route. If you are a low-income Punjab household that already owns a small plot, the interest-free ACAG scheme may suit you better. You can apply to both if you qualify, but you can only take one final loan disbursement.

## What Changed in 2026: Timeline and Expansion

The programme has moved fast in 2026, and early coverage is already stale. Here is the dated sequence.

| Date | What happened |
| :--- | :--- |
| Sept 2025 | SBP revised the **MPMG** scheme, raising the loan cap to **Rs 10 million** and fixing the subsidised rate at **5%** for the first 10 years. |
| 30 April 2026 | PM **Shehbaz Sharif** formally relaunched it as the **Wazir-e-Azam Apna Ghar Program**, announcing a **Rs 3.2 trillion** commitment and a **500,000-home** target (50,000 in year one). |
| May–June 2026 | Partner banks onboarded; applications opened nationwide through **apnaghar.gov.pk**. |
| July 2026 | **NBFCs** and microfinance companies were allowed to lend under the scheme. |

By mid-2026 banks had received over **100,000 applications**, with the large majority coming after the September 2025 revision. The takeaway for applicants: figures quoted in pre-launch articles about "only four banks" or older loan limits are outdated — verify the current lender list on apnaghar.gov.pk.

## Official Portal, Scam Alerts and Safety

### Only One Portal Is Official

The sole official application portal is **apnaghar.gov.pk**. Since the April 2026 launch, lookalike websites and Facebook pages have appeared offering paid "registration". The real portal has three tell-tale features: it ends in `.gov.pk`, it is linked from the **State Bank of Pakistan**'s website, and it never charges a fee.

### Safety Rules

- Registration and application are free — never pay an agent, a "middleman", or a website that promises guaranteed approval.
- Never share your **CNIC** image, OTP, password or bank PIN with anyone.
- Never send money through JazzCash, Easypaisa or any wallet to an unknown person for "fast processing".
- Escalate genuine bank problems through the official **SBP complaint portal (complaint.sbp.org.pk)**, not through private fixers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Wazir-e-Azam Apna Ghar Program?

It is Pakistan's federal housing finance scheme, a markup subsidy and risk-sharing programme launched by Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on 30 April 2026 and regulated by the State Bank of Pakistan. It gives first-time buyers subsidised home loans through partner banks.

### What is the maximum loan amount?

The maximum loan is Rs 10 million, offered in slabs of Rs 2.5 million, Rs 5 million, Rs 7.5 million and Rs 10 million. The slab you qualify for depends on your income, equity and the property value.

### What is the markup rate?

The markup is 5% fixed for the first 10 years, subsidised by the government. After year 10 it moves to 1-year KIBOR plus 3%, a market-linked rate, so your installment may change.

### Is the Apna Ghar Program interest-free?

No. It carries a fixed 5% markup for the first decade, not zero interest. The interest-free scheme is the separate Punjab-only Apni Chhat Apna Ghar programme.

### How much down payment do I need?

You contribute 10% of the property value as equity, while the bank finances up to 90%. There is no processing fee and no prepayment penalty.

### Who is eligible?

Any resident Pakistani holding a valid CNIC — or an NRP with a NICOP or POC — who is a first-time homeowner with a stable, verifiable income and a clean credit history. Both salaried and self-employed applicants can apply.

### What kind of property is covered?

A house of up to 10 marla (2,720 sq ft) or a flat of up to 1,500 sq ft, with no cap on price. You can buy a house or flat, buy a plot with construction, or build on a plot you already own.

### Which banks offer the loan?

Participating lenders include NBP, Bank of Punjab, Meezan Bank, HBL, Allied Bank, UBL, MCB, Bank Alfalah, Bank AL Habib, Faysal Bank, Askari Bank, Mobilink Bank, Standard Chartered and HBFC. From July 2026, eligible NBFCs and microfinance companies can also lend.

### Can overseas Pakistanis apply?

Yes. Non-Resident Pakistanis holding a NICOP can apply through the Roshan Apna Ghar track, which requires a Roshan Digital Account, with tenure extendable up to 25 years.

### How do I apply online?

Visit apnaghar.gov.pk, register with your CNIC and mobile number, complete the application form, upload your documents, choose a participating bank, and submit. You will receive a Tracking ID to monitor progress.

### How long does approval take?

Banks are mandated to complete approval within 15 working days of receiving a complete application. Delays can be escalated through the SBP complaint portal.

### Is there any application fee?

No. Application is free, with no processing fee and no prepayment penalty. Anyone charging a registration fee is running a fraud.
