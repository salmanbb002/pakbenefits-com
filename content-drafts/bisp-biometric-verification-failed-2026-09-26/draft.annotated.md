# BISP Biometric Failed? Guaranteed Rs 13,500 Fix

Agar POS machine ya ATM par BISP biometric verification fail ho jaye, to Ghareeb Dost / **BISP Tehsil Office** se **Form B (Biometric Verification / Failure Form)** hasil karke fill karein. **NADRA office** se **fingerprint update** ya **BISP Assistant Director** se **manual payment approval** ki Request Form Submit karein taake **Rs 13,500** ki qist bina rukawat hasil ho sake.

---

## BISP Biometric Verification Failed Fingerprint Solution Overview

**Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP)** ki **Rs 13,500** ki **kafaalat qist** hasil karte waqt sab se bari rukaawat **POS machine** ya bank **ATM** par **biometrics fail** hona hai. Ye masla aksar mazoori, bari umar ke zaiyf afraad, ya mehnaykash khawateen/hazraat ke **fingerprints** ghis jane ki wajah se pesh aata hai.

**Biometric verification failure** ka matlab ye hargiz nahi ke aap ki payment cancel ho gayi hai. BISP aur **NADRA** ne aisi tamam khawateen ke liye ek Ba-Zabta (official) **manual payment procedure** aur **Form B complaint mechanism** wazeh kiya hai. Is tariqe se aap apni **biometric nishan-dahi** ke baghair bhi apni raqam received kar sakte hain.

---

## BISP Fingerprint Matching Problem Key Causes

**POS agents** aur **HBL Connect** ya **Bank Alfalah** ke ATMs par **fingerprint match** na hone ki mandarija zail wujoohati hain:

* **Elderly & Worn Fingerprints:** Umar raseeda afraad aur hard manual labor karne wale afraad ke **fingerprint lines** ghis jati hain.
* **Dry or Dirty Skin:** **Biometric scanner** par khushki ya dhool ki wajah se sensor lines read nahi kar pata.
* **Outdated NADRA Data:** **NADRA record** mein majood fingerprints buhat purane hote hain jo maujuda fingerprints se match nahi karte.
* **POS Machine / Scanner Defect:** Agent ki machine ka **fingerprint sensor** kharab ya ganda hona.

---

## BISP Biometrics Fail Hone Par Kya Karein: Step-by-Step Guide

Agar aap ki **biometric verification** baar baar fail ho rahi hai to mandarija zail mu'tamad (proven) tariqa-e-kar par amal karein:

### Step 1: Preliminary Remedies at POS Agent

1. **Finger Cleaning & Moisturization:** Apne hathon ko achi tarah sabun se dho kar saaf karein aur halka sa moisturizer, glycerine, ya pashm oil lagayein taake skin lines wazeh ho sakein.
2. **Try Different Fingers:** POS scanner par sirf angutha (thumb) lagane ke bajaye baqi tamami ungliyan (index, middle, ring finger) bari bari try karein.
3. **Change POS Machine / Agent:** Agar ek retailer ki machine issue kar rahi ho, to qareebi dusre **BISP agent** ya bank branch (**HBL Connect** / **Bank Alfalah**) par try karein.

### Step 2: NADRA Biometric Re-Verification & Update

Agar POS machine par bilkul verification na ho, to immediate Step **NADRA center** jana hai:

* Apne original **CNIC** ke sath qareeb-tareen **NADRA Tehsil Center** / E-Sahulat center tashreef le jayen.
* **Biometric Verification Update** counter par request karein aur apne tamami fingerprints ko biometric system mein dobara update karwayen.
* Update ke 24 se 48 ghante baad BISP payment point par ja kar dobara biometric try karein.

## BISP Manual Payment Form B Process & Requirements

Agar NADRA update ke bawajood fingerprints Verification fail hoti rahe, to BISP ka official **Form B (Manual Payment Application)** fill karna hoga.

| Step | Action Required | Responsible Office | Document Needed |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | BISP Tehsil Office Visit | Local BISP Center | Original CNIC + Mobile No |
| **2** | Form B Collection & Verification | BISP Registration Desk | Token & Verification |
| **3** | NADRA Non-Matching Certificate | NADRA Tehsil Office | NADRA Verification Slip |
| **4** | Assistant Director Approval | BISP AD Office | Approved Form B |
| **5** | Manual Cash Disbursement | Designated Bank Branch / Tehsil Office | Verification Slip + CNIC |

### Documents Required for Form B Submission:

1. Original **CNIC** (National Identity Card) ki 2 copy-an.
2. NADRA ki taraf se issued **Biometric Failure Certificate** / Slip.
3. Registered **Mobile SIM Number** jo beneficiary ke apne CNIC par ho.
4. **BISP Registration Token** / Household survey slip.

## Important Precautions & Scam Safety Warnings

* **Never Pay Any Fee:** BISP **Form B** aur biometric complaint registration bilkul FREE hoti hai. Agent ya kisi shaksh ko koi riswat na dein.
* **Official 8171 SMS Only:** Verification ya payment status ke liye sirf **8171 SMS service** par hi aitbaar karein.
* **Biometric Exemption Fraud:** Fingerprint bypass karwane ke naam par kisi ghair-muqaddas agent ko apna CNIC aur OTP share na karein.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I do if BISP biometric verification fails at POS machine?
Sab se pehle hathon ko saaf aur moisturize karke baqi ungliyan try karein. Agar phir bhi verification na ho to NADRA center se biometrics update karwayen ya BISP Tehsil Office se Form B fill karein.

### What is BISP Manual Payment Form B?
Form B ek zaroori Sarkari Application Form hai jo un BISP beneficiaries ke liye hota hai jinke fingerprints biometric machines par match nahi hote. Is form ke zariye manual payment ki manzoori milti hai.

### How much payment is disbursed in the BISP Kafaalat scheme?
BISP Kafaalat program ke teht 2026 mein har eligible khatoon ko Rs 13,500 ki quarterly installment ada ki ja rahi hai.

### Can elderly citizens get BISP payment without fingerprint matching?
Haan, 60 saal se zaid umar ke zaiyf beneficiaries ke liye BISP Tehsil Office se Special Manual Payment Exemption Form B ke zariye 13,500 Rs hasil karne ka aasan tariqa mojud hai.

### How long does it take for Form B approval?
Form B submit hone aur BISP Assistant Director ki manzoori ke baad 3 se 7 Working Days mein manual payment issue kar di jati hai.

### Does NADRA charge a fee for biometric update?
Normal BISP biometric re-verification aur updating ke liye NADRA standard processing fee chara sakti hai. Hamesha official NADRA counter par hi slip hasil karein.

### Where can I lodge a complaint against POS agents asking for fee?
Aap BISP helpline 0800-26471 par call karke ya apne zila ke BISP Regional Office mein Agent ki shakayat darj karwa sakte hain.

### Can I get BISP payment from Bank ATM if POS fingerprint fails?
Agar aap ka bank account HBL ya Bank Alfalah biometric system se linked hai, to aap ATM machine par touch screen aur biometric scanner par try kar sakte hain. Agar wahan bhi fail ho to Form B hi wahid hal hai.

### What documents are required for BISP Form B?
Original CNIC, CNIC copies, NADRA non-matching biometric slip, registered SIM number, aur household survey token required hote hain.

### Is BISP 8171 biometric verification available on weekends?
BISP Tehsil Offices aur NADRA Tehsil Centers Monday se Friday subah 8:00 AM se 4:00 PM tak khule hote hain. Certain designated centers Saturday ko bhi open rehte hain.
