## Step 2 — Head-Entity Research
1. **CM Punjab Himmat Card Programme:**
   - Type: Social Protection Initiative / Targeted Cash Transfer
   - Authority: Social Welfare and Bait-ul-Maal Department, Government of Punjab.
   - Lead Official: Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif.
   - Quarterly Stipend: Rs. 10,500 disbursed via Bank of Punjab (BOP) ATM cards.
2. **Disabled Persons Management Information System (DPMIS):**
   - Official Portal: `https://dpmis.punjab.gov.pk/`
   - Function: Online registration, biometric profiling, and application tracking for PWDs.
3. **Bank of Punjab (BOP):**
   - Role: Partner commercial bank responsible for issuing co-branded Himmat ATM cards and biometric cash disbursement.
4. **National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA):**
   - Role: Issuance of Special CNIC with wheelchair symbol / disability indicator.
5. **District Social Welfare Office & Medical Board:**
   - Function: Clinical assessment of disability and issuance of official Disability Certificates.
