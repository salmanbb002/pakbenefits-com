# Benazir Mazdoor Card Registration Online 2026: Sindh SESSI Apply & Benefits

The **Benazir Mazdoor Card registration online 2026** is a Sindh Labour Department initiative managed by the **Sindh Employees' Social Security Institution (SESSI)**. Eligible industrial and commercial workers register through the **SESSI Mazdoor portal** or employer submission, followed by **NADRA biometric verification** at local **SESSI field offices** to access free healthcare, education grants, and death relief.

## What Is the Benazir Mazdoor Card in Sindh?

The **Benazir Mazdoor Card** is a smart social security card issued by the **Government of Sindh** in technical partnership with **NADRA** and the **Sindh Employees' Social Security Institution (SESSI)**. Designed for registered industrial, commercial, and informal workers across Sindh, the digital card consolidates worker identity, family medical coverage, and cash welfare grants under a unified biometric database.

Unlike general poverty alleviation initiatives like **BISP Kafaalat** or provincial agricultural cards like the Benazir Hari Card, the **Benazir Mazdoor Card** specifically caters to formal and semi-formal labor personnel. By replacing paper-based social security cards with chip-enabled biometric cards, **Sindh SESSI** ensures that workers and their verified dependents can claim cashless medical treatments at designated **SESSI hospitals** and dispensaries without administrative delays.

## Benefits of the Benazir Mazdoor Card in 2026

The **Benazir Mazdoor Card** grants comprehensive social security coverage to registered workers and their immediate family members. Key benefits managed by **SESSI** and the **Sindh Workers Welfare Board** include full medical care, educational scholarships for children, cash allowances, and lump-sum death and marriage grants.

*   **Free Medical Treatment:** Full cashless inpatient and outpatient medical care across all **SESSI dispensaries**, specialty hospitals, and empanelled private health centers for the worker, spouse, and unmarried children.
*   **Worker Education Grants:** Full tuition fee coverage, free textbooks, and monthly stipends for workers' children enrolled in primary schools, technical institutes, and universities under the **Sindh Workers Welfare Board**.
*   **Marriage Grant (Jahez/Wedlock Grant):** A financial grant of Rs 400,000 paid to registered workers upon the marriage of each daughter.
*   **Death Grant (Financial Compensation):** Immediate financial aid of Rs 700,000 provided to legal heirs in the event of an active worker's natural or workplace death, along with survivor pension stipends.
*   **Maternity and Sickness Benefits:** Cash compensation for female workers during maternity leave and cash sickness allowances for workers temporarily incapacitated by workplace injuries or illness.

## How to Apply for Benazir Mazdoor Card Online in 2026 (Step-by-Step)

Applying for the **Benazir Mazdoor Card** in 2026 involves a two-stage process: employer registration on the official **SESSI portal** followed by worker **NADRA biometric verification** at a **SESSI field office** counter. Follow this step-by-step guide to complete your registration.

### Step 1: Employer Registration on SESSI Web Portal
The industrial or commercial employer visits the official **SESSI portal** (sessi.gov.pk) or uses the **SESSI Mazdoor portal**. The employer logs into their institutional account, uploads worker details (**CNIC**, monthly wage structure, job designation), and submits the quarterly SESSI contribution report.

### Step 2: Download & Submit the Worker Registration Form (Form R-2)
Workers who are not yet registered digitally must fill Form R-2 (Worker Registration Form). Provide exact details matching the **NADRA CNIC**, including full name, father's/husband's name, residential address, and family dependent list.

### Step 3: Biometric Verification at SESSI Field Office Counter
Once the employer uploads worker records, the worker receives an SMS notification. Visit the nearest **SESSI field office** or dedicated NADRA Biometric Counter carrying the original **CNIC**, two passport-sized photographs, and employer endorsement letter. The operator will capture thumbprints and record family biometric data.

### Step 4: Card Issuance and Activation
Upon successful **NADRA biometric verification** cross-matching, the smart **Benazir Mazdoor Card** is printed and delivered to the employer or collected directly from the **SESSI office**. The card is activated immediately for medical services at **SESSI hospitals**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can an individual worker apply for the Benazir Mazdoor Card independently?
No, registration requires verification through an employer registered with **SESSI**. However, self-employed or informal workers can apply through recognized labor unions or informal sector registration drives launched by the **Sindh Labour Department**.

### Is the Benazir Mazdoor Card valid outside Sindh?
The card is primarily valid across all **SESSI hospitals** and social security offices within Sindh province. Reciprocal medical arrangements exist with EOBI and Punjab PESSI hospitals for emergency treatments.

### How can I check my Benazir Mazdoor Card online status?
You can verify your registration status by visiting the official **SESSI portal** (sessi.gov.pk) and entering your 13-digit **CNIC** number in the Mazdoor Status Verification tool or through your employer's SESSI dashboard.

### What is the fee for obtaining the Benazir Mazdoor Card?
The **Benazir Mazdoor Card** is issued completely free of charge to eligible workers. All card printing and **NADRA biometric verification** costs are borne by **SESSI** and the **Sindh Labour Department**.

### What documents are required for SESSI biometric verification?
Workers must present their original **NADRA CNIC**, family members' CNICs or B-Forms, two passport photos, employment offer letter or employer verification certificate, and Form R-2.

### What happens to the card if I change my employer?
If you change employers within Sindh, your new employer must submit a transfer request on the **SESSI portal** using your existing SESSI registration number to maintain uninterrupted benefits.

### Is there a minimum salary limit for Benazir Mazdoor Card eligibility?
Yes, eligibility applies to workers earning up to the maximum wages limit prescribed under the Sindh Employees' Social Security Act (currently updated annually by the **Sindh Labour Department**).

### What is the difference between Benazir Mazdoor Card and Benazir Hari Card?
The **Benazir Mazdoor Card** covers industrial, factory, and commercial labor under **SESSI**, while the Benazir Hari Card is designed specifically for agricultural farmers and landless tenants in Sindh.

### How long does it take to receive the card after biometric verification?
After completing **NADRA biometric verification** at the **SESSI field office**, the physical smart card is typically issued within 15 to 30 working days.

### What healthcare facilities are covered under SESSI?
Cardholders can access treatment at **SESSI Hospital** Valika Karachi, SESSI Hospital Landhi, SESSI Hospital Kotri, SESSI Hospital Hyderabad, and over 40 district dispensaries across Sindh.
