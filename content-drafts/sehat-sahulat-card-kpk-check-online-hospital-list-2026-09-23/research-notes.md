## 2. Head-Entity Research (Authoritative Sources)

1. **Sehat Card Plus Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (Sehat Sahulat Program KP)**
   - **Canonical Name**: Sehat Card Plus Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
   - **Entity Type**: Government Health Insurance Program / Social Protection Scheme
   - **Governing Body**: Health Department, Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
   - **Implementing Partner**: State Life Insurance Corporation of Pakistan (SLIC)
   - **`sameAs`**: `https://sehatcardplus.gov.pk/`, `https://pmhealthprogram.gov.pk/`
   - **Core Attributes**: Rs 1,000,000 (10 Lakh PKR) annual inpatient coverage per family; universal coverage for 100% of permanent KP CNIC holders; 1,000+ empaneled hospitals across Pakistan.

2. **State Life Insurance Corporation of Pakistan**
   - **Entity Type**: State-Owned Life & Health Insurance Underwriter
   - **Role**: Underwrites and manages claims, hospital empanelment, biometric patient desk authorization, and cashless hospital billing.
   - **`sameAs`**: `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Life_Insurance_Corporation_of_Pakistan`

3. **National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA)**
   - **Entity Type**: National Identification & Civil Registry Authority
   - **Role**: Validates family tree linkages (Family Registration Certificate / B-Form) and authenticates CNIC permanent provincial residence.
   - **`sameAs`**: `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Database_and_Registration_Authority`

## 4. Competitor Analysis (Top 4 Distinct Domains)

1. **Competitor 1: Official Portal (`sehatcardplus.gov.pk`)**
   - Headings: Eligibility Check, Empaneled Hospitals, Treatment Packages, Contact Us.
   - Strengths: Authentic hospital list links and direct CNIC query engine.
   - Weaknesses: Lacks detailed step-by-step troubleshooting, admission desk guidance, and provincial family tree boundary explanations.
2. **Competitor 2: National Health Portal (`pmhealthprogram.gov.pk`)**
   - Headings: About Program, How to Use Card, Hospital Search, Citizen Rights.
   - Strengths: Clear national framework context.
   - Weaknesses: Mixes Federal Sehat Sahulat Program with KPK Sehat Card Plus rules; confusing for KP-specific citizens.
3. **Competitor 3: Pakistani Tech & Benefit Blogs (`propakistani.pk` / `urdupoint.com`)**
   - Headings: Sehat Card KPK eligibility, 8500 SMS method, covered surgeries, hospital names.
   - Strengths: Easy conversational language.
   - Weaknesses: Stale numbers, fails to clarify OPD exclusion vs IPD coverage, and lacks hospital desk document checklist.
4. **Competitor 4: Regional Public Welfare Guides (`bolnews.com`)**
   - Headings: How to check Sehat Card by CNIC, panel hospital list Peshawar/Mardan.
   - Strengths: City-wise mentions.
   - Weaknesses: No schema markup, no comprehensive district-wise breakdown or emergency admission flowchart.
