# Research Notes: BISP and Ehsaas Difference

## 1. Intent & SERP Landscape
- **Primary Search Query**: `bisp and ehsaas difference`
- **Dominant Search Intent**: Informational / Comparison. Users are searching to understand whether BISP and Ehsaas are two competing welfare bodies, whether both still exist, which organization disburses the Rs. 10,500/13,500 stipends, and whether separate registrations or cards are required.
- **Top Competitor Gaps**:
  - Most competitor articles offer superficial 300-word summaries that fail to explain the legal standing (BISP Act 2010 vs. Ehsaas executive policy framework).
  - Many outdated sites still list obsolete 2020 SMS portals (`pass.gov.pk` or `8171.pass.gov.pk`), confusing beneficiaries who get error screens.
  - Zero competitors provide a 1:1 scheme transition table illustrating how every sub-program (Kafaalat, Taleemi Wazaif, Nashonuma, Undergraduate Scholarships) evolved from BISP to Ehsaas and back to BISP.

## 2. Core Historical & Institutional Facts
- **BISP Origin (2008)**: Enacted by Parliament via the BISP Act 2010 as an autonomous statutory body. Remains the legal custodian of the National Socio-Economic Registry (NSER).
- **Ehsaas Framework (2019-2022)**: Launched as an umbrella social protection strategy overseen by Poverty Alleviation and Social Safety Division (PASSD). It housed BISP as its primary delivery arm while launching sub-initiatives (Ehsaas Emergency Cash, Ehsaas Rashan, Ehsaas Langar).
- **Current Legal Status (2022-2026)**: The federal government reorganized the administrative framework, restoring the standalone operational prominence of BISP. Today, all regular social cash transfers (Kafaalat, Taleemi Wazaif, Nashonuma) operate directly under BISP, utilizing the single, verified `8171.bisp.gov.pk` web portal and `8171` SMS code.
- **Unified Registry**: There was never a separate "Ehsaas database" vs "BISP database"; both utilized NADRA's NSER household survey and Poverty Means Test (PMT) score.
