## Step 4 — Competitor Analysis & Gaps

### What Competitors Got Wrong or Omitted
1. **Outdated Amounts:** Competitors quote Rs 9,000, Rs 10,500, or Rs 12,000. In 2026, the official quarterly stipend is **Rs 13,500**.
2. **Missing the 3-Year Dynamic Registry Expiry Rule:** Most blogs do not explain that BISP policy automatically pauses accounts if the household has not undergone an NSER dynamic resurvey within 36 months.
3. **No Distinction Between "Blocked/Paused" vs "Disqualified":** A paused account still holds the beneficiary's registration and can be resolved at a Tehsil office; outright disqualification (due to PMT > 32 or vehicle ownership) requires an appeal or 2-year cooling period.
4. **Failure to Address the New Digital SIM/Wallet Requirement:** In modern disbursement pilots, SIMs not registered in the beneficiary's own name block digital wallet activation.
5. **Vague Solutions:** Competitors simply say "visit your nearest office" without listing the exact documents needed (original CNIC, children B-forms, utility bills, token procedure).
