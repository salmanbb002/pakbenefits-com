## 2. Head-Entity Research (Canonical Non-Competitor Sources)

Canonical facts established from official Ministry of Finance sources and the notified rules before reading competitors.

### Core Entity 1: Federal Government Defined Contribution (FGDC) Pension Fund Scheme
- **Canonical Name:** Federal Government Defined Contribution (FGDC) Pension Fund Scheme Rules, 2024
- **Entity Type:** GovernmentProgram / Legislation
- **Authority Source:** Ministry of Finance (Finance Division), Government of Pakistan
- **SameAs / Reference URL:** `https://www.finance.gov.pk/` (no Wikipedia/Wikidata page — record as unlinked entity)
- **Attributes & Relationships:**
  - Notified via SRO 1728(I)/2025 dated 27 August 2025; gazetted 8 September 2025.
  - Statutory basis: Public Finance Management Act 2019.
  - Regulated under: Voluntary Pension System Rules 2005 and Non-Banking Finance Companies and Notified Entities Regulations 2008.
  - Replaces the August 2024 directive (which had set the government share at 20%).
  - Applies to civil servants appointed on/after 1 July 2024; armed forces on/after 1 July 2025.
  - Employee contributes 10% + government 12% of pensionable pay = 22% total (final notified rate).

### Core Entity 2: Ministry of Finance (Finance Division), Government of Pakistan
- **Canonical Name:** Ministry of Finance (Finance Division), Government of Pakistan
- **Entity Type:** GovernmentOrganization
- **SameAs / Reference URL:** `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Finance_(Pakistan)`, `https://www.finance.gov.pk/`
- **Attributes & Relationships:**
  - Issuer of the FGDC Rules and implementation Office Memoranda (2025–2026).
  - Runs the pensioners' facilitation portal (finance.gov.pk/pensioners.html).
  - Published "List of Eligible Pension Fund Managers" (2 Aug 2026) and implementation guidelines (11 Aug 2026).

### Core Entity 3: Accountant General Pakistan Revenues (AGPR)
- **Canonical Name:** Accountant General Pakistan Revenues (AGPR)
- **Entity Type:** GovernmentOrganization
- **SameAs / Reference URL:** `https://www.agpr.gov.pk/`
- **Attributes & Relationships:**
  - Handles deduction and remittance of monthly contributions.
  - Verifies/certifies employee data under the scheme; consolidates employee lists by ministry/division/department/BPS.
  - Works with the Controller General of Accounts (CGA) on consolidated employee data.

## 7. Information-Gain Pass

### What Competitors Omit or Get Wrong
1. **Stale government contribution rate:** SubKuch Web (and several Oct-2024 posts) still publish the provisional "government 20%" figure. The final notified rate is **12%** (employee 10% + government 12% = 22%). This is the single most damaging inaccuracy for readers.
2. **The withdrawal mechanics:** Almost no competitor explains the retirement benefit clearly — that you may withdraw **up to 25%** of the accumulated balance at retirement, and the **remainder stays invested for 20 years or until age 80** (whichever first). This is the part every new employee actually wants to know.
3. **No side-by-side old-vs-new comparison:** Competitors cover either the new contributory scheme OR the old-scheme reforms, rarely both in one place. The 24-month-average and one-pension rules are buried in Urdu text (FutureRozgar) or absent entirely.
4. **No implementation timeline:** The Aug 2026 operational phase (focal persons, 15-day data verification, pension fund manager list) is only in PkRevenue's separate news item — not integrated into any guide.

### Unique Original Elements Committed
1. **Old vs New pension side-by-side comparison table** (contribution, calculation base, portability, withdrawal, risk) — a single reference table no competitor offers.
2. **Dated "Implementation Timeline" box** — SRO (Aug 2025) → Oct 2025 notification → Aug 2026 operational phase → PFM list, so readers see the scheme is live.
3. **Retirement withdrawal walkthrough** — the 25% / 20-year / age-80 mechanics explained step-by-step with a worked illustration of how the balance compounds.
