## Taleemi Wazaif Ke Liye Ahal Bacchay Kaun Hain? (Eligibility Conditions)

Benazir Taleemi Wazaif mein har bacha shamil nahi ho sakta. Is ke liye darj zail 3 bunyadi sharait lazmi hain:

1. **Maa Ka BISP Kafaalat Beneficiary Hona:** Bacchay ki walida ka Benazir Kafaalat programme mein active aur ahal hona lazmi hai.
2. **B-Form Tasdeeq:** Bacchay ka NADRA se jari shuda Child Registration Certificate (B-Form) mojood ho aur maa ke shanakhti card ke sath link ho.
3. **Umar Aur Class Ki Hadh:** Bacchay ki umar Primary (4 ta 12 saal), Middle (8 ta 15 saal), Secondary/Matric (13 ta 18 saal), aur Higher Secondary (15 ta 20 saal) ke darmiyan ho.

## School Dakhila Tasdeeq Slip Kaise Bharein? (Step-by-Step)

Jab aap BISP Tehsil Desk se bacchay ki printed enrollment slip hasil karein, toh is mein darj zail khaney school intizamia se pur karwayen:

1. **School Ka Naam Aur EMIS Code:** Sarkari school ka EMIS code ya registered private school ka registration number likhein.
2. **Bacchay Ka Dakhila Number (Admission/Reg No):** School register mein bacchay ka jo dakhila number hai woh darj karein.
3. **Mojooda Class Aur Section:** Bacha jis class mein parh raha hai (maslan Class 4-A).
4. **Headmaster / Principal Ka Daskhat Aur Stamp:** School ke sarbarah ka daskhat aur school ki gol mohr (official round seal) lagwana laazmi hai. Baghair mohr ke form reject ho jata hai.

## Form Jama Karwane Ke Baad Wazeefa Kab Shuru Hota Hai?

School slip BISP Tehsil Office mein jama karne ke baad bacchay ka status 'Enrolled' ho jata hai. Is ke baad BISP ka Compliance Monitor har maah school se bacchay ki haziri ka record hasil karta hai.

Agar bacchay ki attendance kam az kam 70 feesad ho, toh agle sah-mahi cycle mein maa ki Benazir Kafaalat qist (Rs. 13,500) ke sath bacchay ke Taleemi Wazaif ke paise khud-ba-khud account mein jama ho kar ATM par show ho jate hain.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Benazir Taleemi Wazaif ka form kahan se milta hai?

Taleemi Wazaif ka enrollment form (school slip) kisi dukan ya website se nahi milta, yeh sirf BISP Tehsil Office ke registration desk se muft milta hai.

### Kya private school ke bacchon ko Taleemi Wazaif milte hain?

Haan, agar private school government ke District Education Department se baqaida registered ho aur bacchay ki 70% haziri tasdeeq kare.

### Kya ek maa ke sab bacchon ke wazaif lag sakte hain?

Haan, ahal khatoon ke tamam parhne wale bacchon (primary ta higher secondary) ke alag alag forms jama karwa kar sab ke wazaif lagwaye ja sakte hain.

### School slip par kis ke sign aur stamp zaroori hain?

School ke headmaster ya principal ke daskhat aur school ki official gol mohr (round stamp) hona laazmi hai.

### Bacchay ka wazeefa aana kyun band ho jata hai?

Agar bacchay ki school mein teen maah ki majmooi haziri 70% se kam ho jaye ya bacha school chor de, toh system khud-ba-khud wazeefa rok deta hai.

### Kya form jama karwane ke koi charges hain?

Nahi, slip lene se lekar jama karwane tak poora process 100% muft hai. Kisi ko koi fees na dein.

### Agar bacha doosre school mein dakhila le le toh kya karein?

Aap ko BISP Tehsil Office ja kar bacchay ka naya school update karwana hoga aur naye school se fresh slip jama karwani hogi.

### B-Form mein walida ka naam galat ho toh kya form jama hoga?

Nahi, B-Form aur walida ke CNIC ka data match hona zaroori hai. Agar naam mein farq ho toh pehle NADRA se B-Form durust karwayen.
