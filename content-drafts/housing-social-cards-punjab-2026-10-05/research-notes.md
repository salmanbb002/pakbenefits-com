## Step 7 — Information-Gain Pass

### Identified Competitor Gaps & Blind Spots
1. **Federal vs. Provincial Confusion:** Most competitor sites mix up Punjab provincial cards (Himmat, ACAG, Kisan) with Federal BISP 8171, confusing readers on where to apply.
2. **Outdated Financial Figures:** Sites list outdated 2024 loan limits or old stipend amounts. 2026 actual limits (PKR 1.5 Million for ACAG, PKR 10,500 for Himmat Card) must be explicitly date-stamped.
3. **Missing Eligibility Thresholds:** Competitors rarely detail the exact PMT score cutoffs required by PSER for each scheme.
4. **Lack of Comparison Table:** None of the top 4 competitors offer a single consolidated matrix comparing loan terms, monthly stipends, targeted beneficiary groups, and official portals side-by-side.

### Original Information-Gain Commitments
1. **Punjab Housing & Social Cards 2026 Master Comparison Matrix:** A complete tabular breakdown detailing Card Name, Targeted Beneficiary, Benefit Amount / Loan Limit, PMT Score Cutoff, Official Portal, and SMS / Helpline Code.
2. **Step-by-Step PSER Registration & Troubleshooting Guide:** Explaining how to update household data on `pser.punjab.gov.pk` and resolve CNIC verification failures.
3. **Clarification Box:** Explicitly delineating Federal BISP (8171) vs. Punjab Provincial Cards (8070 / PSER / 1221).
