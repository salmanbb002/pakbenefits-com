## 2. Head-Entity Research (Authoritative Sources)

1. **Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP)**
   * **Canonical Name:** Benazir Income Support Programme
   * **Aliases:** BISP, BISP 8171, Ehsaas Programme (historical link)
   * **Type:** Government Organization / Unconditional Cash Transfer Program
   * **sameAs URL:** `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benazir_Income_Support_Programme` / `https://bisp.gov.pk`
   * **Core Attributes:** Established 2008; disburses quarterly cash stipends (Rs 13,500 as of 2026) to eligible women; evaluated via National Socio-Economic Registry (NSER) Dynamic Survey; requires PMT score ≤ 32.

2. **Punjab Socio-Economic Registry (PSER)**
   * **Canonical Name:** Punjab Socio-Economic Registry
   * **Aliases:** PSER, Punjab Social Economic Registry, PSER Punjab Portal
   * **Type:** Government Database / Provincial Registry Scheme
   * **sameAs URL:** `https://pser.punjab.gov.pk`
   * **Core Attributes:** Launched by Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif under Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB); centralized digital registry targeting socio-economic profiling across Punjab; mandatory prerequisite for provincial welfare initiatives (Solar Panel Scheme, Kisan Card, Himmat Card, Honahar Scholarship, Apni Chhat Apna Ghar).

3. **National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA)**
   * **Canonical Name:** National Database and Registration Authority
   * **Aliases:** NADRA
   * **Type:** Government Organization
   * **sameAs URL:** `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Database_and_Registration_Authority`
   * **Core Attributes:** Verifies CNIC validity, family tree (Form-B), marital status, biometric identification, and calculates baseline household PMT scores.

4. **Proxy Means Test (PMT) Score**
   * **Canonical Name:** Proxy Means Test Score
   * **Aliases:** PMT Score, NSER PMT Cutoff
   * **Type:** Metric / Socio-economic Index
   * **Core Attributes:** Numerical score between 0 and 100 derived from household assets, vehicle ownership, utility consumption, land holding, and family size. BISP Kafaalat requires PMT ≤ 32.

## 6. Information-Gain Pass

1. **What Competitors Omit or Get Wrong:**
   * Most competitor blogs confuse **BISP NSER Dynamic Survey** with **Punjab PSER Registration**, falsely claiming they are the exact same database.
   * Competitor articles contain outdated payment figures (stating Rs 10,500 instead of the updated 2026 Rs 13,500 stipend).
   * Lack of step-by-step guidance on how PSER score impacts specific Punjab welfare schemes vs BISP federal cash assistance.

2. **Original Information Gain Element Committed:**
   * A side-by-side comparative matrix: **BISP NSER Dynamic Survey vs. Punjab PSER Registration** detailing scope, administering authority, eligible schemes, mandatory documentation, and PMT score impact.
   * A step-by-step troubleshooting workflow for fixing "NSER Survey Not Found" and "Biometric Verification Failed" errors at payment centers.
