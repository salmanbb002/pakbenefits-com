This payment increase addresses rising living expenses for vulnerable families across all four provinces, Azad Jammu & Kashmir, and Gilgit-Baltistan. 

Simultaneously, the Government of Punjab, under the direction of Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif, expanded the Punjab Socio-Economic Registry (PSER). Unlike one-off registration forms, PSER data now serves as the mandatory screening criterion for all major Punjab provincial welfare programs, ranging from agricultural input subsidies to clean energy initiatives.

To ensure uninterrupted financial aid, households must maintain active status in both registries. While BISP relies on the National Socio-Economic Registry (NSER) Dynamic Survey managed at local Tehsil registration offices, PSER allows self-service online registration via `pser.punjab.gov.pk`.

## What Is the Punjab Socio-Economic Registry (PSER) and How Does It Connect to BISP?

The Punjab Socio-Economic Registry (PSER) is a provincial digital repository established to record the income, assets, housing conditions, and family composition of households across Punjab. Operated by PITB in coordination with district administrative authorities, PSER eliminates duplicate welfare applicants and ensures targeted subsidy distribution.

Although PSER functions as a Punjab-specific database and BISP operates as a federal cash transfer program, the two systems cross-validate citizen records using the National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA) database. Both registries rely on 13-digit Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC) numbers and Form-B child registration records to determine household vulnerability.

| System Feature | Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP) | Punjab Socio-Economic Registry (PSER) |
|---|---|---|
| **Jurisdiction & Scope** | Federal (All Provinces & Territories) | Provincial (Punjab Residents Only) |
| **Primary Portal** | `8171.bisp.gov.pk` / SMS 8171 | `pser.punjab.gov.pk` |
| **Managing Authority** | BISP Board & Federal Ministry of Poverty Alleviation | Government of Punjab & PITB |
| **Primary Stipend / Benefit** | Rs 13,500 Unconditional Quarterly Cash Grant | Targeted Provincial Subsidies & Loans |
| **Survey Mechanism** | In-Person NSER Dynamic Survey at Tehsil Offices | Online Self-Registration & e-Khidmat Centers |
| **Key Qualifying Indicator** | PMT Score Cutoff ≤ 32 | Verified Household Socio-Economic Profile |

While BISP provides direct cash transfers to female family heads, PSER enrollment determines qualification for provincial physical assets and targeted credits. Registering on PSER does not automatically enroll a citizen in BISP; however, updated PSER profiles assist provincial authorities in identifying households that require an NSER Dynamic Survey re-assessment.

## How to Complete PSER Online Registration on pser.punjab.gov.pk?

Citizens residing in Punjab can complete their PSER socio-economic profiling online without visiting government offices. The self-registration portal is accessible through desktop computers and mobile browsers.

### Step 1: Account Creation & Mobile OTP Verification

Visit the official portal at `pser.punjab.gov.pk` and select **Register New Account**. Enter the full legal name of the household head, valid 13-digit CNIC number, active mobile phone number, and district of residence. The system generates a one-time password (OTP) via SMS to verify ownership of the mobile number. Upon successful verification, log in using your CNIC and secure password.

### Step 2: Entering Household, Monthly Income, and Utility Details

Once logged in, fill out the comprehensive socio-economic survey form divided into four sections:
1. **Family Profile:** Enter the CNIC numbers of all adult family members and NADRA Form-B numbers for minor children.
2. **Housing & Facilities:** Record housing ownership (owned, rented, or shared), structural type (pucca or kutcha), number of rooms, and water supply sources.
3. **Utility Consumption:** Input your electricity reference number (consumer ID) and monthly gas bill account details. High electricity consumption (exceeding 300 units monthly) impacts eligibility ratings.
4. **Income & Agricultural Assets:** Declare total household monthly income, employment status, land holding acreage, and registered vehicles (motorcycles, tractors, or cars).

### Step 3: Document Upload and Final Submission

Upload clear scanned copies or photographs of essential documents, including the applicant's CNIC (front and back), recent electricity or gas bills, and agricultural land title documents (if applicable). Review all entered details for accuracy, accept the legal declaration confirming data truthfulness, and submit the application. The system provides a unique **PSER Registration Tracking ID** via SMS for future query tracking.

## How to Check BISP 8171 Payment Status Online by CNIC?

Beneficiaries expecting the quarterly BISP Kafaalat payment can check their payment status online using the official 8171 web portal. This eliminates the need to stand in long queues at bank distribution sites or retail partner counters.

### Checking BISP Kafaalat Rs 13,500 Disbursement Online

To check your account balance and payment release status:
1. Open your browser and navigate to the official portal `8171.bisp.gov.pk`.
2. Enter your 13-digit CNIC number in the designated field without hyphens or spaces.
3. Type the 4-digit security captcha code displayed on the screen.
4. Click **Submit / Check Status** to view your records.

The screen displays your current qualification status, payment release notification for the Rs 13,500 quarterly instalment, and the designated payment venue (such as HBL E-Connect retail agents or Bank Alfalah POS centers). ### SMS 8171 Verification vs. Online Web Portal Inspection

In addition to the web portal, BISP operates an official 8171 SMS service. Citizens can send their 13-digit CNIC number via SMS to `8171` from their registered mobile SIM. The automated gateway replies with eligibility details and payment status within minutes. 

Note that sending an SMS incurs standard cellular carrier charges (approximately Rs 2+tax), whereas checking via `8171.bisp.gov.pk` is completely free. Beware of fraudulent SMS messages originating from private 11-digit mobile numbers; BISP sends official updates exclusively through the shortcode **8171**.

## Who Is Eligible for BISP and PSER Welfare Schemes in 2026?

Eligibility across both federal cash programs and provincial welfare schemes is strictly determined by objective socio-economic thresholds rather than manual discretionary approvals.

### PMT Score Thresholds and Income Cutoffs

The National Socio-Economic Registry (NSER) calculates a **Proxy Means Test (PMT) score** ranging from 0 to 100 for every surveyed household. 

* **BISP Kafaalat Cash Stipends:** Requires a verified household PMT score of **32 or lower**.
* **BISP Taleemi Wazaif (School Stipends):** Available to children of active Kafaalat beneficiaries who maintain at least 70% school attendance.
* **Special Assistance & Disabled Persons:** Special relaxations apply for households with registered disabled members, allowing PMT score cutoffs up to 37.

Households earning a cumulative monthly income exceeding Rs 45,000, government employees, tax filers, or individuals possessing foreign travel histories are automatically excluded from BISP eligibility. For households facing exclusion due to outdated records, consult our guide on [PMT score re-survey guide](/pmt-score-above-32-bisp-re-survey-guide).

### Key Punjab Government Schemes Linked Directly to PSER

Registration in the Punjab Socio-Economic Registry is a mandatory prerequisite for accessing Punjab state initiatives:

1. **CM Punjab Solar Panel Scheme:** Distributes complete off-grid solar systems to low-consumption electricity consumers (under 100-200 units monthly) verified through PSER utility record checks. Learn more in our [CM Punjab Solar Panel Scheme guide](/cm-punjab-solar-panel-scheme-2026-online-apply).
2. **Kisan Card Program:** Provides interest-free agricultural production loans up to Rs 150,000 per crop season for farmers holding up to 12.5 acres of land listed in PSER.
3. **Himmat Card Scheme:** Grants quarterly stipends of Rs 10,500 to certified Persons with Disabilities (PWDs) who are ineligible for regular employment. Check eligibility via our [Himmat Card online registration guide](/cm-punjab-himmat-card-online-apply).
4. **Honhaar Scholarship Program:** Covers full tuition fees for high-achieving undergraduate students from low-income families verified via PSER income brackets.
5. **Apni Chhat Apna Ghar Housing Loan:** Provides interest-free housing construction loans up to Rs 1.5 million with easy repayment periods over 7 years.

## BISP NSER Dynamic Survey vs. Punjab PSER Registration: Key Differences

To help citizens navigate federal and provincial registration processes, the table below highlights the operational differences between the BISP NSER Dynamic Survey and the Punjab PSER Registration system.

| Operational Feature | BISP NSER Dynamic Survey | Punjab PSER Registration |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary Objective** | Identify low-income families for federal cash stipends (Rs 13,500) | Profile Punjab citizens for multi-sector provincial welfare programs |
| **Registration Channel** | Physical visit to BISP Tehsil Offices | Online portal (`pser.punjab.gov.pk`) or e-Khidmat Markaz |
| **Target Beneficiary** | Female head of the household | Head of household (Male or Female resident of Punjab) |
| **Required Verification** | Live biometric capture & NADRA family tree audit | CNIC, Form-B, and uploaded utility bill verification |
| **Validity Period** | Valid for 3 years (mandatory dynamic re-survey required after 36 months) | Continuous profile update as household financial conditions change |
| **Disbursal Method** | Biometric bank retail agents (HBL E-Connect / Bank Alfalah) | Direct bank transfer, specialized portal cards, or physical assets |

## How to Update BISP Dynamic Survey at Local Tehsil Offices?

If your BISP account is marked as invalid, expired, or if your PMT score requires re-assessment, you must complete an in-person **NSER Dynamic Survey**. BISP operates dedicated registration centers across every Tehsil in Pakistan.

To complete the dynamic survey:
1. **Gather Documentation:** Bring your original valid CNIC, original NADRA Child Registration Certificates (Form-B) for all minor children, and recent household electricity or gas utility bills.
2. **Visit BISP Tehsil Center:** Arrive at your nearest Tehsil office during working hours (Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM). You can locate local office addresses in our [BISP Tehsil office locations directory](/bisp-tehsil-office-lahore-addresses-guide).
3. **Token Issuance:** Obtain a survey entry token at the reception desk.
4. **Data Entry & Survey Interview:** Present your documents to the data entry operator. The staff inputs your family structure, housing assets, employment, and utility consumption into the NSER database.
5. **Biometric & SMS Confirmation:** Provide live fingerprint scans and confirm your registered mobile number. A confirmation SMS is sent upon successfully saving your survey record.

After completing the survey, NADRA updates your household PMT score within 30 to 90 days. You can subsequently check your updated eligibility status on `8171.bisp.gov.pk`.

### Resolving "NSER / PSER Record Not Found" Status

When entering your CNIC on the 8171 web portal or PSER portal, receiving an "Information Not Found" or "Survey Record Not Found" notice indicates that your household has never undergone an NSER/PSER survey or that your previous record has expired after 3 years. 

To fix this:
* For BISP: Visit the nearest BISP Tehsil Office with your original CNIC and family Form-B to complete a fresh Dynamic Survey.
* For PSER: Create a new account at `pser.punjab.gov.pk` and complete online registration.
* If portal outages block access, refer to our troubleshooting guide for [8171 portal issues](/8171-portal-not-working).

### Fixing Biometric Fingerprint Verification Failures at Cash Points

Biometric mismatch is a frequent issue faced by elderly beneficiaries and manual laborers at HBL E-Connect or Bank Alfalah retail agents. When the biometric device fails to authenticate thumb impressions after multiple attempts, payments cannot be disbursed.

**Step-by-Step Fix:**
1. Clean your fingers thoroughly with a wet wipe or alcohol swab before placing them on the optical scanner.
2. Request the retail agent to restart or recalibrate the POS biometric scanner.
3. If biometric verification fails repeatedly across multiple agents, visit the nearest NADRA Registration Center (NRC) to update your biometric fingerprint profile in the national database.
4. In persistent cases involving senior citizens with worn fingerprints, submit a formal **Biometric Exemption Application** at the BISP Tehsil Office to receive manual payment authorization.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the updated BISP Kafaalat payment amount for 2026?
The updated BISP Kafaalat payment amount for 2026 is **Rs 13,500** per quarter. This updated stipend is disbursed to eligible female beneficiaries who meet the PMT score criteria of 32 or lower through designated partner bank retail counters.

### How can I check my PSER online registration status by CNIC?
You can check your PSER online registration status by logging into the official Punjab Socio-Economic Registry portal at `pser.punjab.gov.pk` using your registered CNIC and password. The dashboard displays your application submission status, verification stage, and eligibility for Punjab welfare schemes.

### Is PSER registration mandatory for Punjab residents?
Yes, PSER registration is mandatory for Punjab residents who wish to apply for provincial government subsidy programs and welfare initiatives. Initiatives such as the CM Solar Panel Scheme, Kisan Card, Himmat Card, and Honhaar Scholarship select beneficiaries strictly from verified PSER database records.

### What is the official website for checking BISP eligibility?
The official website for checking BISP eligibility and payment status is `8171.bisp.gov.pk`. Users can enter their 13-digit CNIC number and captcha code to view real-time payment release records and eligibility status without any fees.

### How much does it cost to register for PSER or BISP Dynamic Survey?
Registration for both PSER and BISP Dynamic Survey is completely free of charge. Neither the federal government nor the Punjab provincial government charges any fees for account creation, dynamic survey interviews, or payment verification. Report any agent demanding money to the BISP helpline at `0800-26477`.

### What documents are required for PSER online registration?
The documents required for PSER online registration include the original CNIC of the household head, NADRA Form-B numbers for minor children, recent electricity and gas utility bills, and proof of agricultural land holding (if applicable). Clear digital copies or photos of these documents must be uploaded during registration.

### Can a household receive both BISP and Punjab PSER benefits?
Yes, a household can receive both BISP cash stipends and Punjab PSER benefits provided they meet the eligibility criteria for each program. BISP provides federal cash assistance based on NSER PMT scores, while PSER enables access to Punjab provincial asset subsidies and specialized credit cards.

### What should I do if my BISP payment is blocked or on hold?
If your BISP payment is blocked or put on hold, visit your local BISP Tehsil Office to update your household Dynamic Survey. Payments are typically paused when household records exceed 3 years without re-verification, or when NADRA updates reveal changes in family marital or financial status.

### How long does it take to process PSER registration applications?
PSER registration applications are typically processed within 15 to 30 working days following online submission. The Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB) cross-verifies submitted utility and asset data against NADRA and utility company databases before approving eligibility.

### What is the PMT score limit for BISP Kafaalat eligibility in 2026?
The PMT score limit for BISP Kafaalat eligibility in 2026 is **32**. Households with a Proxy Means Test score equal to or less than 32 qualify for the quarterly cash stipend of Rs 13,500, while special quotas for disabled citizens permit scores up to 37.
