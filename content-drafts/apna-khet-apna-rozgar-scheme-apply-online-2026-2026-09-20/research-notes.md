1. **Central Entity 1: Apna Khet Apna Rozgar Scheme (AKAR)**
   - *Type*: GovernmentScheme / PublicBenefitProgram
   - *Aliases*: AKAR Scheme, Apna Khet Apna Rozgar Program, Punjab Agricultural Land Lease Scheme 2026
   - *Canonical Portal*: `https://akar.pulse.gop.pk/`
   - *Governing Body*: Government of Punjab (supervised by Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif)
   - *Implementing Agency*: Punjab Land Records Authority (PLRA) in collaboration with the World Bank-assisted PULSE Project and Punjab Agriculture Department.
   - *Core Objective*: Providing cultivable state-owned land on long-term lease to landless farmers, rural laborers, and unemployed youth to eliminate rural poverty and maximize provincial crop yields.
   - *Key Provisions*: 2 to 5 acres (standard 4-acre plots) leased for 10 to 20 years at a nominal fee of Rs. 100 per year, bundled with Rs. 200,000 initial cultivation grant and Punjab Kisan Card eligibility.

## Step 7 — Information-Gain Pass

- **What Competitors Omit or Get Wrong**:
  1. *Phishing & Fake Portals*: Competitors fail to highlight that unauthorized third-party websites attempt to charge application fees, whereas the official Punjab government application via `akar.pulse.gop.pk` is completely free of charge.
  2. *Exact Land Tenure Terms*: Many sites vaguely state "permanent ownership," which is factually incorrect. The scheme grants a **10-year lease** (extendable up to 20 years) with strict prohibitions against selling, gifting, or subletting the land.
  3. *Financial Aid Breakdown*: Competitors fail to explain how the **Rs. 200,000 grant** connects with the **Punjab Kisan Card** (which provides up to Rs. 150,000–300,000 in interest-free agricultural input credit).
  4. *Grievance Channels*: None of the competitor articles explain what applicants should do if their CNIC is flagged as possessing land due to outdated manual patwari records.

  2. **Step-by-Step Online Portal Walkthrough (`akar.pulse.gop.pk`)**: Explicit technical guide from CNIC input and OTP verification to plot preference and provisional slip generation.
  3. **Mandatory Compliance & Anti-Subletting Warning Box**: Detailed legal parameters explaining why subletting causes instant revocation of lease rights.
  4. **Patwari / Revenue Record Dispute Resolution Guide**: Explicit instructions on how to clear false landholder flags at Arazi Record Centers (PLRA).
