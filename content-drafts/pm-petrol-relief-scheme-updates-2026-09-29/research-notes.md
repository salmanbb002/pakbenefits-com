## Step 7 — Information-Gain Pass

### What Competitors Missed or Stated Confusingly:
1. **The SIM Mismatch Trap:** Competitors fail to explain that even if vehicle documents are in your name, sending the SMS from a SIM registered to a spouse, parent, or employer results in an immediate silent rejection or "Record Mismatch" error.
2. **Old Registration Cutoff (Pre-2006):** Competitors overlook the strict January 1, 2006 vehicle age cutoff, leading owners of 1990s motorbikes or classic cars to waste time troubleshooting.
3. **Token Expiry & Redemption Rules:** Competitors do not clearly specify how long the `TOK` numeric code remains active or whether partial quota redemptions are allowed.

### Original Elements Added:
1. **Financial Savings Matrix Table:** Side-by-side breakdown showing monthly fuel costs, subsidised litre quotas, monthly cash savings, and net fuel expense for bikes, rickshaws, and 800cc cars.
2. **Comprehensive 9771 Syntax & Province Code Reference:** Clear tabular breakdown preventing format typos.
3. **5-Point Troubleshooting Checklist:** Direct fixes for "Record Not Found", SIM mismatch, and dealer POS offline situations.
