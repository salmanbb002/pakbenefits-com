# PM Petrol Relief Scheme Updates: Registration, Quotas, and 9771 SMS Guide

Pakistan’s Prime Minister’s Fuel Relief Scheme provides an ongoing petrol subsidy of Rs 100 per litre for eligible motorcycles, rickshaws, and cars up to 800cc. Eligible citizens register by sending an SMS with their CNIC, vehicle plate, province code, and registration date to 9771, receiving redeemable weekly fuel discount tokens for participating filling stations.

---

## What is the PM Petrol Relief Scheme and How Much is the Subsidy?

The Prime Minister’s Fuel Relief Scheme provides a direct consumer price reduction of Rs 100 per litre on motor gasoline for vulnerable vehicle owners across Pakistan. The federal government enacted this targeted relief framework to protect lower-income commuters from regional oil price volatility without reinstating universal unfunded fuel subsidies.

### Official Subsidy Rate and Approved Vehicle Categories

The official subsidy provides a flat discount of Rs 100 per litre deducted immediately at the fuel dispenser. This relief applies exclusively to three private transport categories: two-wheel motorbikes, three-wheel auto-rickshaws (including Qingqi rickshaws), and four-wheel passenger cars with engine displacement up to 800cc. Commercial fleets, heavy transport vehicles, and luxury passenger vehicles are strictly excluded from the package.

The program runs nationwide across Islamabad Capital Territory, Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan, Azad Jammu and Kashmir, and Gilgit-Baltistan. The Ministry of Energy (Petroleum Division) allocated an initial Rs 75 billion for the first operating quarter, with provisions to maintain price relief for up to 10 months based on international crude benchmarks.

## Who is Eligible for the PM Petrol Relief Scheme?

Eligibility for the petrol subsidy requires owning an approved vehicle category, a valid computerized national identity card, and an active cellular connection registered in your own name. The system verifies individual applicants automatically against federal identity records and provincial motor vehicle registries.

### Engine Capacity Limits and the 2006 Vehicle Cutoff Rule

Vehicles must meet strict engine displacement and registration date thresholds to pass automated verification. Motorcycles and three-wheelers qualify regardless of engine size, while passenger cars cannot exceed an engine capacity of 800cc, encompassing popular models such as the Suzuki Mehran, Daihatsu Cuore, and 660cc Japanese kei cars.

Under updated Ministry guidelines, all qualifying vehicles must carry an official registration date on or after January 1, 2006. Vehicles registered prior to 2006 fail the automated verification sweep due to incomplete computerized archives in legacy provincial transport databases. Only one vehicle may receive subsidised petrol per CNIC.

### SIM Card Ownership and CNIC Match Requirements

Your mobile telephone connection must be biometric-verified under your own CNIC to complete registration. When an applicant submits an SMS, the telecom gateway verifies the sender’s mobile subscriber identity against the 13-digit national identity number contained in the message payload.

If you send the registration text from a SIM registered to an employer, spouse, or relative, the central verification server rejects the application with an ownership mismatch error. Applicants holding company-issued or family-owned numbers must port their primary number to their personal CNIC at a telecom customer center before applying.

## How Do You Register for the PM Petrol Relief Scheme via 9771 SMS?

Citizens register for the fuel subsidy by sending an SMS to the official shortcode 9771 following a strict text format. Cellular network operators have eliminated all messaging fees for shortcode 9771 under federal direction, making the registration process completely free.

### Exact 9771 SMS Syntax and Province Code Breakdown

To register, open your messaging application and send a single text message to 9771 using the following exact spacing:

`REG [13-Digit CNIC] [Vehicle Plate Number] [Province Code] [Registration Date DDMMYYYY]`

Do not include hyphens, slashes, or special characters in the CNIC or the registration date. The single-letter province identifier must reflect where the vehicle is officially registered with Excise authorities:

| Region / Province | Code | Syntax Example |
|---|---|---|
| Islamabad Capital Territory | **I** | `REG 6110112345671 ICT1234 I 15062018` |
| Punjab | **P** | `REG 3520112345671 LEB5678 P 22032015` |
| Sindh | **S** | `REG 4210112345671 KHI9012 S 10112019` |
| Khyber Pakhtunkhwa | **K** | `REG 1730112345671 PEW3456 K 05012021` |
| Balochistan | **B** | `REG 5440112345671 QTA7890 B 14082017` |
| Azad Jammu & Kashmir | **A** | `REG 8220112345671 MZD2345 A 30092020` |
| Gilgit-Baltistan | **G** | `REG 7150112345671 GLT6789 G 12042022` |

Enter the registration date exactly as printed on your vehicle smart card or official registration book in day-month-year numerical format.

### Automated NADRA and Provincial Excise Verification Flow

Submitting your SMS initiates an automated, multi-tiered digital check between federal and provincial databases. The 9771 routing engine queries the National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA) to confirm your identity, citizen status, and active SIM registration.

Simultaneously, the gateway queries the relevant Provincial Excise & Taxation Department database to verify that the vehicle plate matches the provided registration date, chassis record, and engine displacement limit. Once both regulatory bodies confirm the data, the system transmits an approval SMS containing your registration confirmation within 24 to 48 hours.

## How Do You Generate and Redeem Fuel Tokens at Petrol Pumps?

Beneficiaries redeem the Rs 100 per litre discount by requesting a digital fuel token before refuelling at any participating petrol station. Attendants validate the numeric token through the government-connected Fuel Pass terminal at the point of sale.

### Sending the TOK Command to 9771

To generate a fuel discount token, send an SMS containing only the letters `TOK` to shortcode 9771 from your registered mobile number. Within seconds, the automated system returns a 6-digit numeric token accompanied by your remaining subsidised fuel quota and the token expiry timestamp.

Fuel tokens remain active for 24 hours from the moment of issuance. If your token expires before you reach a fuel station, you may generate a fresh token by sending `TOK` to 9771 without forfeiting your monthly fuel entitlement.

### Presenting the Digital Token at Authorized Fuel Stations

At the petrol pump, inform the attendant that you are availing the PM Fuel Relief discount before the fuel nozzle is inserted. Present the SMS containing your active numeric token.

The fuel station attendant enters your vehicle registration number and the 6-digit token code into the Fuel Pass POS device. The terminal confirms the valid subsidy allocation and adjusts the payable amount downwards by Rs 100 for each litre dispensed. You pay the subsidised net balance using cash or debit card, while the State Bank of Pakistan reimburses the pump dealer for the deducted subsidy balance within 24 to 48 hours.

## What Are the Monthly Petrol Quotas and Financial Savings?

Monthly subsidised fuel volumes are capped according to vehicle class to maintain fiscal discipline while covering essential commuting distances. Two-wheelers and three-wheelers receive a monthly ceiling of 20 litres, whereas 800cc passenger vehicles receive an allowance of 30 litres per month.

### Monthly Fuel Subsidy and Savings Breakdown Table

The following breakdown illustrates the monthly quota, weekly token distributions, cash savings, and net fuel expenditures under prevailing retail petrol rates of Rs 275 per litre:

| Vehicle Class | Engine Displacement | Monthly Subsidised Volume | Token Structure | Full Monthly Price (Without Subsidy) | Total Monthly Subsidy Savings | Net Monthly Fuel Cost (Subsidised) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Motorcycles & Motorbikes** | Up to 150cc | 20 Litres | 4 weekly tokens (5L / Rs 500 each) | Rs 5,500 | **Rs 2,000** | **Rs 3,500** |
| **Auto-Rickshaws & Qingqis** | 150cc – 200cc | 20 Litres | 4 weekly tokens (5L / Rs 500 each) | Rs 5,500 | **Rs 2,000** | **Rs 3,500** |

Beneficiaries who consume fuel beyond their monthly quota purchase additional fuel at standard unregulated market prices. Unused quota does not carry over to the subsequent calendar month.

## Why Does 9771 Registration Fail and How Do You Fix It?

Registration requests fail when submitted data deviates from computerized records or when network transmission errors disrupt verification. Identifying the specific error response allows applicants to correct their records rapidly.

### Common 9771 Error Messages and Solutions

Review the following five diagnostic steps to resolve common SMS rejection messages:

1. **"Record Not Found in Excise Database":** Verify that your vehicle plate number and date of registration match your smart registration card letter-for-letter. If you purchased a vehicle via an open transfer letter, your CNIC is not yet recorded in the provincial system. You must complete biometric transfer at the local Excise office.
2. **"Applicant CNIC Does Not Match SIM Ownership":** You sent the SMS from a cellular SIM registered to another individual. Dial `*#66#` (or your operator’s CNIC verification code) to inspect ownership. Only re-send the registration text once the SIM has been biometrically transferred to your own CNIC.
3. **"Vehicle Registered Prior to Permitted Cutoff":** The vehicle was registered before January 1, 2006. These older models are ineligible for digital validation under current statutory program rules.
4. **"Engine Capacity Exceeds Eligible Limit":** The vehicle registration reflects an engine displacement above 800cc. Vehicles exceeding this threshold (e.g., 1000cc hatchbacks) cannot be registered.
5. **"System Busy / Gateway Timeout":** Server traffic spikes can cause intermittent verification delays. Allow three to four hours before resubmitting the registration string.

### How to Contact the 9772 Complaint Control Room

If your vehicle documentation and SIM ownership are fully compliant but your registration remains stuck, escalate the matter to the dedicated Ministry of Energy control room by dialling shortcode 9772. The central call center operates six days a week to resolve technical discrepancies between NADRA and provincial excise authorities.

Motorists can also report petrol stations that refuse to accept valid fuel tokens or charge illegal processing surcharges. The control room logs pump coordinates and dispatches district monitoring teams to enforce dealer compliance.

## How Can Citizens Protect Themselves from Petrol Subsidy Scams?

Citizens must conduct all registration and token redemptions exclusively through verified government channels to avoid financial fraud. Fraudulent operators frequently impersonate welfare authorities to steal identity credentials and mobile banking passwords.

### Verifying Official Shortcodes and Free SMS Protections

The federal government conducts the Fuel Relief Scheme strictly through shortcode 9771 for registration and tokens, and shortcode 9772 for public assistance. The government never issues subsidy confirmations from standard 11-digit mobile phone numbers or third-party WhatsApp accounts.

Never click on external web links offering instant fuel relief vouchers, and never share OTP codes or mobile wallet PINs with individuals claiming to represent petroleum companies. All official verification SMS transmissions to 9771 remain 100% free of cellular tariffs.

## Frequently Asked Questions About the PM Petrol Relief Scheme

### How much discount is provided under the PM Petrol Relief Scheme?
The scheme provides a flat discount of Rs 100 per litre on petrol at authorized filling stations. Beneficiaries receive this reduction at the pump upon presenting an active numeric fuel token generated via shortcode 9771.

### Which vehicles are eligible for the PM fuel subsidy?
Motorcycles, auto-rickshaws, Qingqis, and small cars with engine displacement up to 800cc qualify for the subsidy. All qualifying vehicles must have been registered on or after January 1, 2006, under the applicant's own CNIC.

### What is the exact SMS format to register for the scheme?
To register, send an SMS to 9771 formatted as `REG [CNIC] [Vehicle Plate Number] [Province Code] [Registration Date DDMMYYYY]`. Ensure spaces separate each component and do not use hyphens or slashes.

### How many litres of subsidised petrol can I purchase each month?
Motorcycle and rickshaw owners can purchase up to 20 litres per month, while 800cc passenger car owners receive up to 30 litres per month. The quota is distributed through periodic tokens across the calendar month.

### How do I generate a fuel discount token when visiting a pump?
Send an SMS containing the text `TOK` to shortcode 9771 from your registered mobile number. The automated system will respond with a 6-digit numeric token valid for 24 hours.

### Can I register if the vehicle is in my father's or brother's name?
No, the vehicle must be legally registered in your own name with the Provincial Excise Department. The automated verification system cross-references your CNIC with excise registration records and rejects non-matching entries.

### Does sending an SMS to 9771 cost any mobile balance?
Sending registration and token requests to shortcode 9771 is completely free of charge. Cellular network operators have eliminated messaging tariffs for this service under federal directives.

### What should I do if a petrol pump attendant refuses to accept my token?
Report the refusal immediately by dialling the official complaint control room at 9772. State the pump's name, oil marketing company brand, geographic location, and your active token number for investigation.

### Can 1000cc cars like Suzuki Cultus or Wagon R qualify for this subsidy?
No, passenger cars with engine displacement above 800cc are excluded from the scheme. Only cars with engine displacement up to 800cc, such as the Suzuki Mehran or 660cc kei cars, qualify.

### What is the deadline to register for the PM Petrol Relief Scheme?
Registration remains continuously open through shortcode 9771 as the program operates nationwide. Federal authorities have financed the scheme for an initial quarter with provisions to extend relief for up to 10 months.

### Does unused subsidised petrol volume roll over to the next month?
Unused monthly fuel allowances do not roll over to subsequent months. Any portion of your 20-litre or 30-litre quota that is not redeemed by the final day of the calendar month expires automatically.
