## BISP Registration Center KPK Services Provided

KPK ke BISP Registration Centers par aane wale shehriyon ko mandarija zail official khidmat (services) muft faraham ki jati hain:

### 1. NSER Dynamic Registration & Survey
Naye khandan jo BISP Kafaalat program mein shamil hona chahte hain, unka NSER Dynamic Registry counter par computerized survey kiya jata hai taake unka PMT Score calculate kiya ja sake.

### 2. Biometric Verification & Complaint Settlement
Agent deductions, biometric failure error, ya CNIC block hone ki surat mein BISP Tehsil Officers Form B issue karte hain aur complaint darj karte hain.

### 3. Taleemi Wazaif & Nashonuma Enrollment
Bacho ke B-Form aur school attendance certificate ke zariye Benazir Taleemi Wazaif aur pregnant khawateen ke liye Nashonuma program ki registration ki jati hai.

## Documents Required Before Visiting BISP KPK Offices

Apne qareebi BISP Tehsil Office janey se pehle mandarija zail original asnad (documents) zaroor sath le jayen:

1. **Original CNIC (National ID Card):** Beneficiary ya Khatoon ka computerized shanaakti card.
2. **Children B-Form:** Tamam bacho ke NADRA se verified B-Form ki copies.
3. **Electricity / Gas Bill:** Ghar ka electricity ya gas utility bill (PMT score evaluation ke liye).
4. **Active Mobile Number:** Shanaakti card par registered active mobile SIM.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the BISP office Peshawar contact number?
Peshawar BISP Regional Office ka official contact number 091-9216298 aur 091-9216299 hai. General inquiries ke liye toll-free helpline 0800-26471 par call karein.

### Where is the BISP registration center located in Peshawar?
Main BISP Registration Center Peshawar University Town (Chinar Road, Khyber Park) aur Hashtnagri GT Road ke paas waqih hai.

### What are the BISP office timings in KPK districts?
KPK ke tamam BISP Tehsil Offices Monday se Friday subah 8:00 AM se shaam 4:00 PM tak open hote hain.

### How can I register for BISP in Mardan, Swat, or Abbottabad?
Aap apne District ke BISP Tehsil Office mein original CNIC aur Bachon ke B-Form ke sath tashreef le ja kar NSER Dynamic Survey Desk par FREE registration karwa sakte hain.

### Is NSER Dynamic Registration free at BISP KPK offices?
Jihan, BISP Tehsil Office ya Registration Center par survey aur registration bilkul 100% FREE hoti hai.

### How can I check my BISP PMT score at KPK offices?
BISP Registration Desk par apna original CNIC pesh karke aap apna updated PMT (Proxy Means Test) score check karwa sakte hain.

### Can disabled persons visit BISP offices without standing in line?
Jihan, BISP KPK Tehsil Offices par elderly (60+ years) aur special / disabled persons ke liye dedicated special counter aur fast-track facility mojud hai.

### What is the address of BISP Tehsil Office Swat?
BISP Swat Tehsil Office Saidu Sharif Road, Opposite Grassy Ground, Mingora Swat mein waqih hai.

### How do I report a BISP POS agent taking fee in KPK?
Aap BISP Regional Office Peshawar ya BISP Helpline 0800-26471 par call karke agent ka location aur POS ID bata kar complaint darj karwa sakte hain.

### Do I need an appointment before visiting BISP KPK Tehsil Office?
Appointment ki zaroorat nahi hai; aap direct office timing mein ja kar token hasil karke apna survey karwa sakte hain.
