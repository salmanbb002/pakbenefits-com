## Step 7 — Information-Gain Strategy

1. **Spelling & Portal Clarity**: Address the spelling variations (`Rehmat Card` vs `Rahmat Card`) and explicitly direct users to `rahmatcard.punjab.gov.pk`.
2. **PSER Integration Step**: Competitor articles skip explaining why users get stuck at the PSER stage. Our article explicitly details how PSER registration at `pser.punjab.gov.pk` links to Rehmat Card verification.
3. **Specific Document Requirements Matrix**: Clear breakdown of required documents for widows vs parentless orphans.
4. **Original Element**: A structured **Eligibility & Document Matrix Table**, a **5-Step Procedural Flowchart**, and a **Troubleshooting Checklist for Verification Failures**.
