# CM Punjab Rehmat Card 2026: Online Registration, Eligibility & Status Check

The **CM Punjab Rehmat Card** 2026 is a social welfare program launched by Chief Minister **Maryam Nawaz Sharif** through the **Zakat & Ushr Department**. It provides a **PKR 100,000 Financial Grant** to eligible **Widows (Bewa)** and **Parentless Orphans (Yateem)** across **Government of Punjab**. Applicants can register online at **rahmatcard.punjab.gov.pk** or via the official **CM Punjab Rahmat Card Mobile App**.

---

## What Is the CM Punjab Rehmat Card 2026?

The **CM Punjab Rehmat Card** (officially registered as the **CM Punjab Rahmat Card**) is a targeted social protection initiative created by the **Government of Punjab** under the leadership of Chief Minister **Maryam Nawaz Sharif**. Administered directly through the **Zakat & Ushr Department** in partnership with the **Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB)**, the program is designed to deliver immediate economic relief to vulnerable citizens who lack a primary financial provider.

Unlike recurring cash stipend schemes that offer small monthly payments, the **CM Punjab Rehmat Card** program focuses on empowering marginalized families through substantial financial support. By establishing a digital verification framework, the provincial government ensures that funds reach deserving beneficiaries directly without political interference or intermediary delays.

### Core Objectives of the Rahmat Card Welfare Program

The primary objective of the **CM Punjab Rehmat Card** scheme is to restore financial dignity and stability to low-income households headed by **Widows (Bewa)** or caring for **Parentless Orphans (Yateem)**. Inflation and rising cost-of-living challenges across Pakistan have severely affected vulnerable populations who fall outside standard employment networks.

Through this scheme, the **Government of Punjab** addresses acute poverty by linking social protection directly with verified demographic records. The initiative also aims to streamline Zakat distribution, ensuring full compliance with Islamic Shariah guidelines while leveraging modern digital technology for transparent administration.

*Section Unused Terms*: None.

### Key Financial Aid Amounts and Payment Distribution Methods

Approved beneficiaries under the **CM Punjab Rehmat Card** program receive a **PKR 100,000 Financial Grant**. This amount is distributed as a dedicated financial assistance allocation meant to cover emergency living expenses, shelter, healthcare, and educational needs for dependent children.

To guarantee zero leakage and eliminate fraudulent collection claims, payment distribution is executed through electronic channels. Beneficiaries receive their funds directly via verified mobile digital wallets such as **JazzCash / Digital Wallet Disbursement** or through biometric withdrawal points at designated partner banks across all 36 districts of Punjab.

## Who Is Eligible for the CM Punjab Rehmat Card?

Eligibility for the **CM Punjab Rehmat Card** is strictly governed by transparent socio-economic criteria established by the **Zakat & Ushr Department** and cross-checked against official state registries.

| Applicant Category | Key Eligibility Requirement | Primary Verification Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Widows (Bewa)** | Verified widowhood status on CNIC; Zakat-eligible household | **NADRA CNIC & Verification** & **Punjab Socio-Economic Registry (PSER)** |
| **Parentless Orphans (Yateem)** | Children who have lost both parents; under 18 years of age | **Official Death Certificate** & **NADRA B-Form** |
| **Residency** | Permanent residency in Punjab province | Original **NADRA CNIC & Verification** / Domicile |
| **Financial Threshold** | Must meet Zakat eligibility criteria; non-Sahib-e-Nisab | **Punjab Socio-Economic Registry (PSER)** Poverty Score & Local Survey |

### Eligibility Requirements for Widows (Bewa)

To qualify as an eligible **Widows (Bewa)** under the **CM Punjab Rehmat Card** scheme, the applicant must be a citizen of Pakistan with permanent residency in Punjab. The applicant must be officially registered as a widow in the **NADRA CNIC & Verification** records, meaning her marital status on her Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC) must reflect her widowed status.

Furthermore, the applicant's household must be classified as needy and eligible for Zakat support under Islamic principles. The household income and assets are evaluated using data from the **Punjab Socio-Economic Registry (PSER)** to confirm that the family lives below the state poverty baseline.

*Section Unused Terms*: None.

### Eligibility Requirements for Parentless Orphans (Yateem)

The program extends dedicated coverage to **Parentless Orphans (Yateem)**—children who have tragically lost both their mother and father. To apply on behalf of an orphan child, the legal guardian must present valid proof of parentage and an **Official Death Certificate** for both deceased parents issued by Union Councils or NADRA.

The child must be registered on an official **NADRA B-Form**. The guardian must also demonstrate that the orphan family lacks sustainable financial means and requires government welfare to ensure basic upkeep and schooling.

*Section Unused Terms*: None.

### Who Is Ineligible? (Exclusion Criteria & Sahib-e-Nisab Rules)

The **Government of Punjab** enforces strict exclusion rules to prevent misuse of Zakat welfare resources. The following categories are explicitly disqualified from receiving the **CM Punjab Rehmat Card** grant:

1. **Government Employees & Pensioners**: Serving or retired employees of federal, provincial, or semi-government organizations, as well as their direct dependents, cannot apply.
2. **Sahib-e-Nisab Exclusion Rule**: Anyone possessing gold, silver, commercial property, or savings exceeding the prescribed Islamic Nisab threshold is strictly ineligible.
3. **Habitual Beggars & Unverified Applicants**: Individuals whose identity details cannot be verified through **NADRA CNIC & Verification** or **Punjab Socio-Economic Registry (PSER)** are excluded.
4. **Beneficiaries of Duplicate Regular Grants**: Households receiving full parallel provincial Zakat grants that exceed allowable welfare thresholds.

## How to Apply Online for the CM Punjab Rehmat Card 2026?

Applying for the **CM Punjab Rehmat Card** is completely free of cost. The **Government of Punjab** has established multiple digital and physical registration channels to accommodate urban and rural applicants alike.

```
[Visit rahmatcard.punjab.gov.pk or Open App]
                │
                ▼
[Enter NADRA CNIC & Registered Mobile Number]
                │
                ▼
[Complete / Sync PSER Household Verification]
                │
                ▼
[Upload Death Certificates & B-Form Documents]
                │
                ▼
[Submit Application & Receive Reference ID]
```

### Method 1: Online Registration via Official Web Portal

The official web portal **rahmatcard.punjab.gov.pk** serves as the primary online platform for registration. Applicants can complete their application by following these steps:

1. Open your internet browser and navigate to the official portal at **rahmatcard.punjab.gov.pk**.
2. Click on the **New Registration / Apply Online** button on the homepage.
3. Input your 13-digit **NADRA CNIC & Verification** number without hyphens, along with an active mobile phone number registered in your own name.
4. Enter the One-Time Password (OTP) sent to your mobile phone to verify your account session.
5. Fill in the required socio-economic details, including family member counts, household address, and **Punjab Socio-Economic Registry (PSER)** survey reference number.
6. Upload scanned copies of required documents (CNIC, death certificates, B-Form).
7. Review your information carefully and click **Submit Application**. Save the displayed tracking reference number for future checks.

*Section Unused Terms*: None.

### Method 2: Registration via the CM Punjab Rahmat Card App

For applicants using smartphones, the **Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB)** developed the dedicated **CM Punjab Rahmat Card Mobile App**, available on the Google Play Store.

After installing the app, users create a profile using their **NADRA CNIC & Verification** and mobile number. The app features a simplified user interface available in Urdu and English. Applicants can capture document photos directly using their smartphone camera and upload them to the central verification server instantly.

*Section Unused Terms*: None.

### Method 3: Registration through PSER & Local Zakat Offices

Applicants who face technical barriers or lack internet access can complete their registration through physical support channels across Punjab:

- **PSER Registration Counters**: Visit the nearest Assistant Commissioner (AC) office or dedicated **Punjab Socio-Economic Registry (PSER)** center in your tehsil to complete your household socio-economic survey.
- **District Zakat Committees**: Visit your local District **Zakat & Ushr Department** office, where designated welfare officers assist widows and guardians in submitting online applications directly into the system.

*Section Unused Terms*: None.

## Required Documents for CM Punjab Rehmat Card Application

Preparing the correct documentation before starting the application process prevents delays and administrative rejections. Ensure all documents are clear, legible, and updated at NADRA.

- **Original NADRA CNIC**: Valid Computerized National Identity Card (**NADRA CNIC & Verification**) of the widow or guardian, displaying updated marital/legal status.
- **Official Death Certificate**: Computerized **Official Death Certificate** of the deceased husband (for widow applications) or death certificates of both parents (for parentless orphan applications) issued by NADRA or the local Union Council.
- **NADRA B-Form**: Official **NADRA B-Form** (Child Registration Certificate) listing all dependent orphan children under 18 years of age.
- **Registered Mobile Number**: SIM card registered legally under the applicant’s own CNIC to receive official SMS notifications and wallet verification codes.
- **Proof of Residency**: Domicile certificate of Punjab or utility bill establishing permanent residence within the province.

## How to Check CM Punjab Rehmat Card Status Online?

Once an application is submitted, candidates can monitor their verification progress online using official tracking portals.

### Tracking Status by CNIC on rahmatcard.punjab.gov.pk

Tracking application status online requires only a valid **NADRA CNIC & Verification** number:

1. Visit the portal at **rahmatcard.punjab.gov.pk**.
2. Click on the **Track Status / Check Application Status** tab.
3. Enter the 13-digit CNIC number of the applicant in the designated field.
4. Click **Check Status**.
5. The system will display your current status: **Under Review**, **Verified/Approved**, **Action Required**, or **Rejected**.

*Section Unused Terms*: None.

### Checking Application Status via Helpline 1077

Applicants who cannot check their status online can dial the official **Helpline 1077**.

When calling **Helpline 1077**, keep your CNIC number and application tracking ID ready. Support representatives verify caller credentials and provide real-time updates regarding application approval, **Punjab Socio-Economic Registry (PSER)** audit status, or payment release schedules.

*Section Unused Terms*: None.

## Troubleshooting Common Rehmat Card Application Issues

During the verification phase, applicants may encounter technical or administrative errors. Understanding how to resolve these issues ensures timely grant approval.

### Biometric Verification Failures & NADRA Record Mismatches

If the portal displays a "NADRA Mismatch" error, it typically means the applicant's marital status is not updated in the central registry. **Widows (Bewa)** must visit the nearest NADRA Registration Center (NRC) with their original marital contract and husband's death certificate to update their CNIC status to widowed before re-applying.

If biometric verification fails during **JazzCash / Digital Wallet Disbursement** payout at retail centers, beneficiaries can request re-verification using **NADRA CNIC & Verification** e-Sahulat counters or visit designated bank branches for direct account verification.

*Section Unused Terms*: None.

### Pending PSER Survey Status & Wallet Transfer Delays

Applications flagged with "PSER Verification Pending" indicate that the household's poverty survey record is either missing or incomplete in the provincial database. Applicants must visit **pser.punjab.gov.pk** or visit their local tehsil PSER counter to complete the household survey.

If your status shows "Approved" but payment is delayed, verify that your mobile wallet account (such as **JazzCash / Digital Wallet Disbursement**) is active, fully upgraded to Level-1 biometric status, and registered under the exact CNIC used during application.

*Section Unused Terms*: None.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the CM Punjab Rehmat Card 2026?
The **CM Punjab Rehmat Card** 2026 is a specialized social welfare program launched by Chief Minister **Maryam Nawaz Sharif** through the **Zakat & Ushr Department**. It provides a **PKR 100,000 Financial Grant** to deserving **Widows (Bewa)** and **Parentless Orphans (Yateem)** across Punjab.

### How much financial assistance is provided under the Rehmat Card?
Eligible beneficiaries receive a **PKR 100,000 Financial Grant**. Funds are transferred directly to verified digital wallets (**JazzCash / Digital Wallet Disbursement**) or biometric bank accounts to ensure transparent delivery.

### Who is eligible to apply for the CM Punjab Rahmat Card?
Eligible applicants must be permanent residents of Punjab who are deserving **Widows (Bewa)** with CNIC status updated at NADRA or **Parentless Orphans (Yateem)** with valid **NADRA B-Form** documents. Applicants must also meet Zakat eligibility criteria and cannot be government employees or pensioners.

### What is the official website for Rehmat Card online registration?
The official website for online application and status tracking is **rahmatcard.punjab.gov.pk**. Applicants can also use the official **CM Punjab Rahmat Card Mobile App** developed by **Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB)**.

### Are government employees or pensioners eligible for the Rehmat Card?
No, government employees, government pensioners, and individuals categorized under the **Sahib-e-Nisab Exclusion Rule** are strictly excluded from receiving Rehmat Card assistance.

### What is the official helpline number for Rehmat Card queries?
The official government helpline number for the CM Punjab Rehmat Card is **Helpline 1077**. Applicants can call **Helpline 1077** for assistance regarding application status, portal issues, and eligibility criteria.

### Is PSER registration required before applying for the Rehmat Card?
Yes, household verification via the **Punjab Socio-Economic Registry (PSER)** database is mandatory. Applicants should ensure their household profile is complete on **pser.punjab.gov.pk**.

### What documents are required for orphan children to apply?
Orphan applicants require a valid **NADRA B-Form**, an **Official Death Certificate** for both mother and father, and the guardian's verified CNIC and active mobile number.

### How are Rehmat Card payments disbursed to approved applicants?
Approved grant payments of PKR 100,000 are disbursed digitally through registered mobile wallets (**JazzCash / Digital Wallet Disbursement**) or biometric verification centers across Punjab.

### When does Phase 2 registration open for the Rehmat Card in 2026?
**Phase 2 Registration 2026** and portal reopening for the **CM Punjab Rehmat Card** are scheduled for late 2026 following the completion of initial Phase 1 disbursements.
