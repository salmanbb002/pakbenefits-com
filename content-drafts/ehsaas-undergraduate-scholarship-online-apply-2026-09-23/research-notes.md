## 2. Head-Entity Research (Authoritative Sources)

1. **HEC Ehsaas Undergraduate Scholarship Project (HEC-EUSP / Benazir Undergraduate Scholarship)**
   - **Canonical Name**: Higher Education Commission Ehsaas Undergraduate Scholarship
   - **Entity Type**: Federal Need-Based Higher Education Grant Program
   - **Governing Body**: Higher Education Commission of Pakistan (HEC) & Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP)
   - **`sameAs`**: `https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/scholarships/Ehsaas/`, `https://ehsaas.hec.gov.pk/`
   - **Core Attributes**: 50,000 annual undergraduate scholarships; 100% full tuition fee coverage + Rs. 4,000 monthly living stipend; 50% female quota; 2% disability quota; 135+ HEC-recognized public sector universities.

2. **Higher Education Commission of Pakistan (HEC)**
   - **Entity Type**: Statutory Higher Education Regulatory Authority of Pakistan
   - **Role**: Administers the online application system, sets academic eligibility guidelines, coordinates with universities, and disburses grants directly to universities and student accounts.
   - **`sameAs`**: `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_Education_Commission_(Pakistan)`

3. **Institutional Scholarship Award Committee (ISAC)**
   - **Entity Type**: University-Level Selection Committee
   - **Role**: Conducts physical document scrutiny, background verification, means-testing interviews, and final shortlisting of eligible candidates at each university.

## 4. Competitor Analysis (Top 4 Distinct Domains)

1. **Competitor 1: HEC Official Portal (`hec.gov.pk`)**
   - Headings: About Scholarship, Eligibility Criteria, How to Apply, Participating Universities.
   - Strengths: 100% authentic policy numbers and quota rules.
   - Weaknesses: Very dense institutional language, lacks step-by-step form walkthrough, and does not provide troubleshooting for income certificate or portal login errors.
2. **Competitor 2: Pakistani Education Portals (`ilmkidunya.com` / `eduvision.edu.pk`)**
   - Headings: HEC Ehsaas Scholarship apply online, last date, stipend, documents.
   - Strengths: Good student-friendly tone and deadline highlights.
   - Weaknesses: Confuses the Undergraduate 4-year degree scheme with school-level Taleemi Wazaif; outdated stipend rates (Rs 2,000 vs current Rs 4,000/mo).
3. **Competitor 3: Benefit Informational Blogs (`propakistani.pk` / `urdupoint.com`)**
   - Headings: How to apply for Ehsaas scholarship, 50,000 scholarships, HEC login.
   - Strengths: Clear overview of the 50% female quota.
   - Weaknesses: Thin section on the Institutional Scholarship Award Committee (ISAC) interview questions and financial aid office submission checklist.
4. **Competitor 4: National Daily News Guides (`tribune.com.pk` / `dawn.com`)**
   - Headings: HEC invites applications for Ehsaas undergraduate scholarship, budget allocation.
   - Strengths: Good policy context.
   - Weaknesses: Pure news reports without actionable application steps or troubleshooting.
