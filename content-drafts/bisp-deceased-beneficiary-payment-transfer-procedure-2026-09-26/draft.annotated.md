# BISP Deceased Beneficiary Payment Transfer & NADRA Cancellation Guide

The **BISP deceased beneficiary payment transfer procedure** requires registering the deceased female beneficiary's death with **NADRA**, obtaining an official **NADRA Death Certificate** and **CNIC Cancellation Certificate**, and reporting the case to the local **BISP Tehsil Office**. BISP payments are not automatically transferred to heirs; instead, a new **BISP Dynamic Survey** re-evaluates the household to appoint an eligible surviving female head.

## What Happens to BISP Payments Upon a Beneficiary's Death?

When a registered **Benazir Kafaalat** female beneficiary passes away, her BISP payments do not automatically transfer to family members or legal heirs. Because BISP operates as a conditional cash transfer program based on individual female biometric identity and household poverty score (**PMT score**), payments to the deceased's account must be formally closed to prevent fraud or unauthorized biometric deductions.

The **National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA)** automatically updates its central database when a death certificate is registered. BISP cross-checks its active beneficiary rolls against NADRA cancellation records during quarterly payment processing. If a beneficiary is marked deceased in NADRA records without a formal **BISP Tehsil Office** household update, the BISP account is frozen, and any accumulated quarterly stipend (Rs 13,500) is held in escrow until legal heir clearance or household re-survey.

## Documents Required for BISP Deceased Beneficiary Reporting

Reporting a beneficiary's death and requesting household payment re-assignment requires presenting official government cancellation records at the **BISP Tehsil Office**. Gathering these mandatory documents prevents procedural delays.

*   **NADRA Death Certificate:** Official death certificate issued by **NADRA** or the local Union Council displaying the exact date of death and registration number.
*   **CNIC Cancellation Certificate:** Formal cancellation slip issued by **NADRA** confirming that the deceased beneficiary's 13-digit **CNIC** has been deactivated in the national registry.
*   **Original CNIC of the Deceased:** Physical original **CNIC** of the deceased beneficiary (which will be surrendered or stamped as cancelled by NADRA).
*   **Original CNIC of the Applicant/Waris:** Valid **CNIC** of the surviving family applicant (daughter, husband, mother, or guardian).
*   **Family Registration Certificate (FRC):** **NADRA**-issued FRC proving the exact family lineage and legal relationships between the deceased and surviving members.

## Step-by-Step BISP Deceased Beneficiary Transfer Procedure (2026)

Transferring household eligibility and clearing pending BISP funds requires following a strict 4-step administrative workflow between **NADRA** and the **BISP Tehsil Office**.

### Step 1: Register Death with NADRA & Obtain Cancellation Certificate
Visit your local **NADRA** Registration Center (NRC) with the hospital death report or Union Council death record. Submit the deceased beneficiary's original **CNIC**, pay the processing fee, and obtain the official **NADRA Death Certificate** along with the **CNIC Cancellation Certificate**.

### Step 2: Report Death at the Nearest BISP Tehsil Office
Bring the **NADRA Death Certificate**, **CNIC Cancellation Certificate**, **NADRA** FRC, and applicant **CNIC** to the Helpdesk at your local **BISP Tehsil Office**. Inform the officer that the registered beneficiary has passed away and request an official Case Update Form.

### Step 3: Conduct BISP Dynamic Survey (NSER Re-Evaluation)
Because **Benazir Kafaalat** stipends are granted to the female head of an eligible household, the BISP Data Entry Operator will initiate a new **BISP Dynamic Survey** for the family. The operator will record the surviving eldest daughter or eligible female relative as the proposed new household head and re-calculate the household **PMT score**.

### Step 4: Claim Pending Funds & Biometric Account Activation
If unpaid quarterly stipends accrued before the beneficiary's death, the family applicant must present a legal heir affidavit or **Succession Certificate** to claim the arrears. Once approved, the new female head will receive an **8171 web portal** SMS to activate her biometric account at partner bank digital counters.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a husband receive his deceased wife's BISP Kafaalat payments?
A husband cannot directly receive **Benazir Kafaalat** cash payments unless he is the sole legal guardian of minor orphan children. BISP policy prioritizes transferring household headship to an adult daughter or eligible female family member.

### What happens to unpaid BISP stipends left in the account before death?
Unpaid stipends that were disbursed prior to the beneficiary's death can be claimed by the legal heirs by submitting a **Succession Certificate** and death verification report at the **BISP Tehsil Office**.

### How long does the BISP deceased transfer procedure take?
The entire process—from **NADRA** death registration to completing the **BISP Dynamic Survey** and receiving approval—typically takes between 30 and 45 working days.

### Can minor children continue receiving Benazir Taleemi Wazaif after their mother dies?
Yes, minor children can continue receiving **Benazir Taleemi Wazaif** stipends once the household completes a fresh **BISP Dynamic Survey** and designates a new adult female guardian on the BISP portal.

### Is a NADRA Succession Certificate mandatory for BISP transfers?
A **Succession Certificate** is not required for routine household head re-assignment during the Dynamic Survey. However, it is required if family members claim substantial accrued funds remaining in the deceased's bank wallet.

### What should I do if someone illegally withdraws BISP money using the deceased's SIM card?
Illegal withdrawal of funds belonging to a deceased person is a punishable offense. Immediately report the incident to the **BISP Tehsil Office** complaint desk and file a ticket on the BISP Toll-Free Helpline (0800-26477).

### Will the household lose BISP eligibility if the PMT score changes during re-survey?
If the new **BISP Dynamic Survey** reveals that the household's **PMT score** has risen above the eligibility threshold (PMT > 32), the household will be marked ineligible regardless of the previous beneficiary's status.

### Where can I get the NADRA Family Registration Certificate (FRC)?
You can apply for an FRC at any **NADRA** Registration Center or online via the Pak-ID mobile app by providing the CNIC numbers of all family members.

### Does BISP charge any fee for updating a deceased beneficiary record?
No, all **BISP Tehsil Office** reporting, Dynamic Survey enrollment, and record updates are completely free of cost. Never pay money to unauthorized agents claiming to expedite transfers.

### What is the official helpline number for BISP deceased case inquiries?
You can call the official BISP Toll-Free Helpline at 0800-26477 or visit your district **BISP Tehsil Office** for verified guidance regarding deceased beneficiary cases.
