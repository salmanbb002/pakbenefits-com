## 3. Core Procedures & Field Rules

- **Official SMS Shortcode**: `8171`.
- **Standard Working Hours**: Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM (Public token issuance closes daily at 2:00 PM to process registered queues). Closed on Saturday, Sunday, and gazetted national holidays.
- **Mandatory Documents**:
  1. Original valid Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC / Smart Card) issued by NADRA.
  2. Computerized Child Registration Certificates (B-Form / CRC) for all children aged 4 to 22 years for Benazir Taleemi Wazaif enrollment.
  3. Active mobile phone SIM registered in the female applicant's own CNIC.
  4. Most recent household electricity or Sui gas bill (to record exact consumer number and locality code in the NSER database).
  5. Special proof documents where applicable: Death certificate of husband (for widows applying for single-breadwinner Kafaalat), NADRA Disability Certificate with Special CNIC logo (Wheelchair icon) for persons with disabilities (PWDs).
