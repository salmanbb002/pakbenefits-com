## 3. Core Services Available at Lahore BISP Tehsil Centers

When visiting an official BISP Tehsil Dynamic Registration Center in Lahore, beneficiaries can access a full spectrum of social welfare services:

1. **NSER Dynamic Registry Survey**: New households, newly married women, and families seeking an updated poverty scorecard can complete a computerized questionnaire covering income, assets, housing conditions, and family composition to determine their **Proxy Means Test (PMT)** score.
2. **Benazir Kafaalat Enrollment**: Women whose household PMT score meets or falls below the official eligibility threshold (currently **PMT 32**) are enrolled for the quarterly unconditional cash transfer of **Rs. 13,500**.
3. **Biometric Verification & Non-BVS Forms**: Elderly beneficiaries, agricultural workers, and women whose fingerprints have faded due to age or domestic labor can complete specialized biometric troubleshooting or submit an official **Non-BVS verification form** to receive manual payment approval.
4. **Benazir Taleemi Wazaif Registration**: Mothers registered in Kafaalat can enroll their children attending primary, secondary, and higher secondary schools to receive quarterly educational stipends ranging from Rs. 2,000 to Rs. 4,500.
5. **Household Profile Updates**: Reporting the death of a family member, updating marital status from single to married, or revising an active mobile phone number linked to the **8171** notification service.

## 4. Mandatory Documents Required for Registration at Lahore DRCs

To ensure seamless registration without multiple trips, applicants must bring original, valid identification documents:

- **Original Valid NADRA CNIC**: The female applicant must possess an unexpired **Computerized National Identity Card** (Smart Card or standard CNIC). Laminated photocopies or token slips without original cards are not accepted.
- **Computerized Child Registration Certificates (B-Form / CRC)**: Issued by **NADRA** for every child living in the household. This is essential for calculating family dependency ratios and enrolling students in Taleemi Wazaif.
- **Active Registered Mobile SIM Card**: The mobile SIM card must be officially registered under the female applicant's own CNIC. All official eligibility confirmations and payment disbursement notices are delivered exclusively via SMS from **8171**.
- **Recent Household Utility Bill**: A copy of the latest paid electricity bill (LESCO) or Sui gas bill (SNGPL) to verify residential address, meter reference number, and household energy consumption.
- **Special Status Documentation**:
  - *Widows*: Computerized Death Certificate of the deceased spouse issued by the local Union Council and updated on NADRA records.
  - *Divorced Women*: Official divorce registration certificate from NADRA.
  - *Special Persons / Disabilities*: Disability certificate issued by the Social Welfare Department and NADRA Special CNIC with the wheelchair logo.

## 5. Step-by-Step Procedure at the Lahore BISP Registration Desk

The registration process at all Lahore Tehsil Dynamic Registration Centers follows a standardized, computerized workflow:

1. **Arrival & Token Issuance**: Arrive during morning hours (between 8:00 AM and 11:00 AM). Staff at the entry counter will verify your original CNIC and issue a computerized queue token.
2. **Document Screening**: A verification assistant inspects your CNIC, B-Forms, and utility bill to confirm that all required papers are complete.
3. **NSER Computerized Interview**: When your token number is called, proceed to the data entry counter. The BISP data entry operator will record your household demographic details, housing type, employment, and livestock ownership into the central NSER system.
4. **Live Biometric & Photograph Capture**: The applicant places her thumb and fingers on the optical biometric scanner and poses for a digital webcam photograph.
5. **Undertaking & Receipt Printout**: The operator prints an NSER survey confirmation slip displaying your tracking number and entered household details. Review the information, verify accuracy, and retain the stamped receipt for future tracking.
6. **System Processing & 8171 SMS Notification**: BISP’s central server in Islamabad cross-checks the data with NADRA and calculates the PMT score. Within 4 to 8 weeks, an official eligibility status message is delivered to your registered mobile number from **8171**.

## 7. Public Protection: Zero-Fee Policy & Reporting Fraud Near Lahore Centers

The Benazir Income Support Programme enforces a strict, legally binding **Zero-Fee Policy** for all civic services:

- **Never Pay Roadside Agents**: No BISP official, NADRA assistant, or private individual is authorized to charge any fee for NSER tokens, registration forms, survey interviews, or payment verification. All services are 100% free of charge.
- **Beware of Photocopy Shop Scams**: Fraudulent agents operating near government centers in Township, Harbanspura, and Gujjarpura often falsely promise "guaranteed BISP approval" or "online form submission" in exchange for Rs. 500 to Rs. 2,000. These claims are entirely fictitious. Registration can only be performed inside the official BISP office on government biometric terminals.
- **How to Report Illegal Activity**:
  - Immediately inform the Center In-Charge / Assistant Director present at the Tehsil office.
  - Call the national toll-free helpline at **0800-26477**.
  - File an online complaint through the BISP Official Portal at `bisp.gov.pk` or the Pakistan Citizen's Portal mobile app under "Poverty Alleviation".
