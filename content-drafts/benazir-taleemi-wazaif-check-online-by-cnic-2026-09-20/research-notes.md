## Step 2 — Head-Entity Research
1. **Benazir Taleemi Wazaif Programme:**
   - Type: Conditional Cash Transfer (CCT) / Social Welfare
   - Authority: Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP), Government of Pakistan.
   - Core Condition: Mandatory 70% quarterly school/college attendance.
2. **Benazir Kafaalat Programme:**
   - Role: Prerequisite program. Only active female Kafaalat beneficiaries are eligible to enroll their children in Taleemi Wazaif.
3. **National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA):**
   - Role: Verification of Child Registration Certificate (B-Form) and Family Tree linkage to the mother's CNIC.
4. **8171 Web Portal & SMS Service:**
   - Public status verification gateway: `https://8171.bisp.gov.pk/` / SMS `8171`.
