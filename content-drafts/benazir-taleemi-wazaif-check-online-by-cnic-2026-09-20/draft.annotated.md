## How to Check Benazir Taleemi Wazaif Status Online by Mother's CNIC

The **Benazir Taleemi Wazaif Programme** (formerly known as Waseela-e-Taleem) is a conditional cash transfer initiative designed to boost school enrollment, reduce dropout rates, and encourage higher education among children from underprivileged households. Beneficiary families can verify their children’s educational stipend status through two official government routes.

### Step-by-Step 8171 Web Portal Verification Guide
The official **BISP** **8171 Web Portal** at `https://8171.bisp.gov.pk/` allows registered mothers to inspect both their quarterly Kafaalat grant and their children's education stipends simultaneously. To perform an online check:
1. Open `https://8171.bisp.gov.pk/` in your mobile or desktop browser.
2. Enter the mother’s 13-digit **Computerized National Identity Card** (**CNIC**) number without hyphens or spaces.
3. Type the 4-digit security captcha code displayed in the adjacent image.
4. Click the green "Maloom Karein" (Check Status) button.

The portal returns a comprehensive status dashboard. Under the primary Kafaalat installment banner, you will find the "Taleemi Wazaif" section displaying the names of all verified children, their enrolled grade levels, and whether their **Quarterly Educational Stipends** have been credited or held for attendance verification.

### Checking Children's Stipend Status via 8171 SMS Service
If you do not have internet access, you can verify your status via SMS. Open your mobile phone’s messaging app, type the mother's 13-digit **CNIC** number, and send it to **8171**. 

Within minutes, the official 8171 gateway returns an SMS outlining the household's overall payment release, including the aggregate stipend amount disbursed for all enrolled school children. Ensure the SMS is dispatched from a SIM registered in the mother's name to prevent system delivery delays.

## Benazir Taleemi Wazaif Stipend Amounts 2026 (Quarterly Rates Table)

The federal government provides differentiated stipend rates based on education tier and student gender. Girls receive a higher stipend across all educational levels to bridge the gender gap in literacy across Pakistan:

| Education Level | Grade / Class | Boys (Per Quarter) | Girls (Per Quarter) | Primary Graduation Bonus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary Level** | Nursery to Class 5 | Rs. 2,500 | Rs. 3,000 | Rs. 3,000 (Girls only) |
| **Secondary / Matric** | Class 6 to Class 10 | Rs. 3,500 | Rs. 4,000 | N/A |
| **Higher Secondary / Inter** | Class 11 to Class 12 | Rs. 4,500 | Rs. 5,000 | N/A |

### Primary School Level (Class 1 to 5) Stipends
Children enrolled in primary school (Nursery through Grade 5) receive quarterly stipends to support basic schooling expenses, stationery, and uniforms. Male students receive Rs. 2,500 every three months, while female students receive Rs. 3,000 per quarter.

### Secondary & Matric Level (Class 6 to 10) Stipends
As children transition into middle and high school, financial pressures often force families into child labor. To combat this, **BISP** provides Rs. 3,500 per quarter for boys and Rs. 4,000 per quarter for girls enrolled in Classes 6 through 10.

### Higher Secondary & Intermediate Level (Class 11 to 12) Stipends
At the college and higher secondary level (FA, FSc, ICS, ICom), quarterly assistance increases to Rs. 4,500 for male students and Rs. 5,000 for female students, ensuring students can complete their intermediate certification.

### Special Rs 3,000 Graduation Bonus for Girls
In addition to regular quarterly stipends, **BISP** awards a one-time cash incentive of **Rs. 3,000** to every female student who successfully completes primary school (Grade 5) and enrolls in secondary school (Grade 6). This graduation bonus is credited directly to the mother’s account alongside her regular quarterly disbursement.

## Mandatory Eligibility Criteria: Who Qualifies for Benazir Taleemi Wazaif?

Unlike general scholarships, **Benazir Taleemi Wazaif Programme** is strictly targeted at households already verified as underprivileged under the National Socio-Economic Registry (NSER).

### Mother's Active Benazir Kafaalat Enrollment Requirement
The foundational prerequisite for Taleemi Wazaif enrollment is that the child’s mother must be an active, verified beneficiary of the **Benazir Kafaalat Programme** unconditional cash transfer scheme (household PMT score ≤ 32). If the mother is disqualified, deceased without an updated guardianship record, or not enrolled in Kafaalat, the children cannot be admitted to the Taleemi Wazaif stipend program.

### NADRA B-Form and Age Criteria Breakdown (4 to 22 Years)
Every child must be officially registered in the **NADRA** database with an authentic **NADRA B-Form** linked to the mother’s **CNIC**. **BISP** enforces strict age limits for initial enrollment:
- **Primary Education:** Child must be between **4 and 12 years** of age at the time of admission.
- **Secondary Education:** Student must be between **8 and 18 years** of age.
- **Higher Secondary Education:** Student must be between **13 and 22 years** of age.

## How to Register Children for Benazir Taleemi Wazaif (Step-by-Step)

Registration for **Benazir Taleemi Wazaif Programme** cannot be completed entirely online. Because school attendance and enrollment must be certified by educational institutions, applicants must complete a physical registration workflow:

### Step 1: Obtaining the School Enrollment Slip from BISP Tehsil Office
The mother must visit her nearest **BISP Tehsil Office** accompanied by her original Smart **CNIC** and the original **NADRA B-Forms** of her school-going children. The **BISP** dynamic registration desk verifies the mother’s Kafaalat status and issues an official printed **School Enrollment Slip** (Dakhla Form) for each eligible child.

### Step 2: Head Teacher Verification, School Stamp & Admission Entry
Take the **School Enrollment Slip** to the government or recognized private school where the child is currently studying. The school head teacher or principal must fill in:
- The school's official Education Management Information System (EMIS) or registration code.
- The child’s admission number, class section, and date of admission.
- The head teacher’s official signature, contact number, and school rubber stamp.

### Step 3: Document Submission and Biometric Slip Issuance
Return the stamped and signed **School Enrollment Slip** to the **BISP Tehsil Office**. The data entry operator scans the document into the central **BISP** database, pairs the school record with the child’s **NADRA B-Form**, and issues a final computerized submission receipt. The child’s stipend begins disbursing in the following quarterly payment cycle.

## The 70% School Attendance Rule: Why Stipends Stop and How to Reactivate Them

**Benazir Taleemi Wazaif Programme** is a **conditional** cash transfer program. Financial assistance is strictly contingent on regular school attendance.

### How School Attendance Is Tracked and Synced with BISP Servers
Under program rules, every enrolled child must maintain a minimum of **70% School Attendance Rule** compliance during every academic quarter. **BISP** compliance monitors visit public schools quarterly to collect physical attendance registers, while an increasing number of districts now sync attendance digitally via provincial education portals.

### Resolving Attendance Mismatches and Restoring Paused Payments
If a child’s attendance falls below 70% in any given quarter due to unexcused absences, the **BISP** banking system automatically pauses that child's stipend for that cycle. To restore paused payments:
1. Ensure the child resumes regular school attendance so their quarterly average exceeds 70%.
2. If the absence was due to verified illness, obtain a medical certificate and an attendance correction letter from the school headmaster.
3. Submit the certified attendance letter to the **BISP Tehsil Office** compliance officer to unfreeze subsequent **Quarterly Educational Stipends**.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. How can I check Benazir Taleemi Wazaif online by CNIC?
Visit `8171.bisp.gov.pk`, type the mother's 13-digit **CNIC** number without spaces or dashes, enter the security captcha code, and click submit. The portal displays both your Kafaalat status and the Taleemi Wazaif status for each enrolled child.

### 2. What is the stipend amount for primary school children in 2026?
In 2026, boys in primary school (Nursery to Class 5) receive Rs. 2,500 per quarter, while girls receive Rs. 3,000 per quarter. Girls also receive a one-time Rs. 3,000 bonus upon graduating primary school.

### 3. Can a father apply for Benazir Taleemi Wazaif using his CNIC?
No. Under **BISP** statutory rules, all child education stipends are issued through the mother’s account. The mother must be an active **Benazir Kafaalat Programme** recipient with a valid **CNIC**.

### 4. What is the minimum school attendance required for Taleemi Wazaif?
Children must maintain at least **70% School Attendance Rule** compliance in school each quarter. If attendance drops below 70%, the quarterly stipend is temporarily withheld until attendance improves.

### 5. What documents are required to register a child for Taleemi Wazaif?
You need the mother’s original **CNIC**, the child’s original **NADRA B-Form**, and a **BISP** **School Enrollment Slip** signed and stamped by the school head teacher.

### 6. Can children studying in private schools receive Taleemi Wazaif?
Yes, provided the private school is registered with the district education department and the head teacher certifies the child's admission and 70% attendance on the official **BISP** **School Enrollment Slip**.

### 7. What is the age limit for Benazir Taleemi Wazaif enrollment?
The age limits at admission are: 4 to 12 years for Primary (Grades 1–5), 8 to 18 years for Secondary (Grades 6–10), and 13 to 22 years for Higher Secondary (Grades 11–12).

### 8. Why did my child's Taleemi Wazaif payment not arrive this quarter?
Common reasons include attendance falling below 70%, an expired **NADRA B-Form** or **CNIC**, school data synchronization delays, or the child exceeding the maximum age limit for their grade.

### 9. How is the Taleemi Wazaif money collected?
The stipend is disbursed alongside the mother’s **Benazir Kafaalat Programme** payment and can be collected via biometric ATMs or partner bank payment campsites (Bank Alfalah and HBL Konnect).

### 10. What is the BISP official helpline for Taleemi Wazaif complaints?
You can contact the official **BISP** toll-free helpline at **0800-26477** for inquiries regarding delayed stipends, attendance disputes, or agent deductions.
