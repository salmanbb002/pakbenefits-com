# PAVE Electric Vehicle Scheme 2026: Complete Guide to EV Subsidies, Eligibility & Online Application

The **PAVE Electric Vehicle Scheme** (Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification) is a federal government initiative operating under the New Energy Vehicles Policy 2025–2030. It provides direct financial subsidies of **Rs 80,000** for electric motorcycles and up to **Rs 400,000** for electric rickshaws and loaders. Applicants apply online via the official portal at **pave.gov.pk**.

---

## What Is the PAVE Electric Vehicle Scheme in Pakistan?

The Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification (PAVE) scheme is the federal government’s flagship program designed to accelerate the national transition toward clean, affordable, and sustainable transportation. Approved by the Federal Cabinet under the New Energy Vehicles Policy (NEVP) 2025–2030, PAVE addresses the high upfront purchase price of electric two-wheelers and three-wheelers by offering direct capital subsidies and low-interest bank financing. The government has allocated a total subsidy envelope of Rs 100.36 billion through 2030 to electrify Pakistan's transport sector, reduce urban smog, and slash the country's multi-billion-dollar oil import bill.

### Who Approves and Manages the PAVE Program?
The Ministry of Industries and Production (MoIP) exercises political and administrative oversight over the PAVE scheme, while technical execution is led by the Engineering Development Board (EDB). The EDB pre-qualifies Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs), certifies electric vehicle models, and ensures local manufacturing standards. Financial transactions and direct subsidy transfers are coordinated through the State Bank of Pakistan (SBP), commercial banking partners, and digital verification systems managed by NADRA and the Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB).

### How Does PAVE Support the New Energy Vehicles Policy (NEVP 2025–2030)?
PAVE serves as the primary consumer-facing mechanism of the NEVP 2025–2030, which targets converting 30% of all new vehicle sales in Pakistan to electric by 2030. By subsidizing high-volume vehicle segments—specifically two-wheelers and three-wheelers, which account for over 75% of active road traffic in Pakistan—PAVE creates rapid scale for domestic EV manufacturing and battery assembly plants.

## How Much Subsidy Does the PAVE Scheme Offer on EVs?

The PAVE scheme provides a tiered subsidy framework based on vehicle category and operational intent. As of the 2026 program cycle, financial subsidies cover up to 60% of the cost differential between petrol and electric vehicles.

```
+------------------------------------+-----------------------+-------------------------------+
| Vehicle Category                   | Maximum Subsidy (PKR) | Primary Target Audience       |
+------------------------------------+-----------------------+-------------------------------+
| Electric Motorcycles & Scooters    | Rs 80,000             | Students, Commuters, Workers  |

| Electric Loaders (3-Wheelers)      | Rs 400,000            | Small Business & Logistics    |

```

### Electric Bike & Motorcycle Subsidy Breakdown (Rs 80,000)
Approved electric two-wheelers receive a flat government subsidy of Rs 80,000 per unit. This reduction brings the net price of quality lithium-ion electric bikes close to standard 70cc petrol motorcycles, making zero-emission daily commuting economically viable for middle-income households.

### Electric Rickshaw & Loader Subsidy Breakdown (Up to Rs 400,000)
Electric three-wheelers receive higher subsidies reaching up to Rs 400,000 per vehicle due to their larger battery packs and commercial utility. Subsidizing rickshaws and cargo loaders targets urban fleet operators, lowering daily operating expenses by over 60% compared to traditional auto-rickshaws.

## What Is the Difference Between Self-Finance and Bank Lease Schemes?

The PAVE program operates through two distinct payment mechanisms to accommodate both cash buyers and installment applicants.

### How the Self-Finance Reimbursement Works via State Bank of Pakistan
Under the Self-Finance Scheme, successful applicants purchase their selected electric bike or rickshaw upfront from an authorized OEM showroom. Once the vehicle is registered with provincial motor licensing authorities, the State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) directly reimburses the Rs 80,000 subsidy into the applicant's verified IBAN bank account within 14 to 21 business days.

### How the Bank Lease Installment Plan Works via Commercial Banks
Under the Bank Lease Scheme, participating commercial banks (such as BankIslami and partner financial institutions) offer subsidized financing with minimal down payments and interest-free or subsidized monthly installment structures spread across 12 to 36 months. The government subsidy is applied directly to lower the financing principal, reducing monthly payments for the applicant.

## What Changed in PAVE Phase 2 for 2026 Applicants?

Phase 2 of the PAVE scheme introduced structural administrative changes following directives from the Economic Coordination Committee (ECC).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **2026 Policy Update:** PAVE Phase 2 has officially eliminated the randomized computerized balloting system used in Phase 1. All allocations under Phase 2 are processed on a strict **first-come, first-served** basis according to the application submission timestamp on pave.gov.pk.

### Shift from Balloting to First-Come, First-Served Model
In Phase 1, applicant selection relied on computerized draw lotteries, which created uncertainty for early applicants. In Phase 2, speed and complete documentation dictate success. Applications are processed chronologically; once the allocated vehicle quota for a batch is fulfilled, the registration portal temporarily pauses until the next fiscal release.

### Phase 1 vs Phase 2 Quota & Budget Allocation Comparison

| Total Subsidy Allocation | ~Rs 4.1 Billion | Rs 8.95 Billion |
| Applicant Selection Method | Computerized Balloting | First-Come, First-Served Queue |

## Who Is Eligible to Apply for the PAVE Scheme?

Eligibility criteria ensure that federal EV subsidies reach genuine Pakistani residents and priority demographic groups.

### Standard Applicant Requirements (CNIC, Age, Licensing)
To qualify for the PAVE scheme, an applicant must satisfy the following core criteria:
1. **Citizenship:** Must be a Pakistani citizen possessing a valid CNIC (including residents of AJK and Gilgit-Baltistan).
2. **Age Limit:** Minimum age of 18 years at the time of application submission.
3. **Driving Authorization:** A valid motorcycle or 3-wheeler driving license (or official learner permit where permitted) is mandatory.
4. **Clean Record:** Applicants must not have previously drawn dual subsidies under duplicate federal or provincial vehicle schemes.

### Special Quotas for Female Applicants, Students, and Commercial Drivers
PAVE reserves dedicated quota allocations to encourage inclusive mobility:
- **Female Quota:** Reserved quota allocations exist for female students, working women, and healthcare staff.
- **Student Quota:** Enrolled university and college students qualify with valid institutional ID verification.
- **Commercial Driver Quota:** Licensed rickshaw drivers and small delivery operators receive priority access for 3-wheeler loader allocations.

## How to Apply Online for the PAVE Scheme at pave.gov.pk (Step-by-Step)

Applying for the PAVE scheme is conducted through an online process on the central federal portal.

### Step 1: Account Registration & Profile Setup on pave.gov.pk
Visit the official web portal at **[pave.gov.pk](https://pave.gov.pk)**. Click on "Register New Account" and enter your 13-digit CNIC number, active mobile phone number (registered under your own CNIC), and email address. Verify your account using the One-Time Password (OTP) sent via SMS.

### Step 2: Form Completion & Vehicle Model Selection
Log into your applicant dashboard and complete the personal information fields. Select your preferred scheme type (**Self-Finance** or **Bank Lease**) and choose an approved electric vehicle model from the pre-qualified OEM list (such as Yadea, Metro EV, Vlektra, or ELFA Electric).

### Step 3: Document Upload & Verification Submission
Upload clear digital copies of the required documents:
- Front and back scan of valid CNIC.
- Passport-size photograph with blue background.
- Valid driving license or learner permit copy.
- Academic ID card (for student quota) or commercial license (for rickshaw quota).

Submit the application. Note down your unique **PAVE Application Tracking Number**.

### Step 4: Digital Verification & Pay Order Formalities
Once your application timestamp is verified against NADRA and SBP records, an approval notification is issued via SMS and portal status update. For Self-Finance approvals, applicants submit the remaining net payment (vehicle price minus Rs 80,000 subsidy) via Pay Order to the authorized OEM dealer to receive delivery.

## What Are the Mandatory Vehicle Registration & Safety Rules?

Federal policy dictates strict registration enforcement before subsidy payments are disbursed.

### Why Federal & Provincial Registration Is Required for Subsidy Disbursement
Under PAVE guidelines, subsidies will **not** be released for unregistered vehicles. Every subsidized electric motorcycle and rickshaw must be formally registered with the Excise, Taxation & Anti-Narcotics Department of the respective province or federal territory. Registration pins the chassis number and motor serial number to the applicant’s CNIC in the central database, preventing unauthorized resale or commercial flipping within the mandatory 12-month lock-in period.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the PAVE Electric Vehicle Scheme in Pakistan?
The PAVE scheme (Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification) is a federal government initiative under the NEVP 2025–2030 policy offering financial subsidies of Rs 80,000 for electric bikes and up to Rs 400,000 for electric rickshaws and loaders.

### How much subsidy is provided for electric bikes under PAVE?
Approved electric two-wheelers receive a direct federal government subsidy of Rs 80,000 per unit, reducing the net purchase cost for buyers.

### What is the official website to apply online for the PAVE scheme?
Applications and tracking are handled exclusively through the official federal portal at **pave.gov.pk**.

### Is balloting required for PAVE Phase 2 in 2026?
No, PAVE Phase 2 has eliminated computerized balloting and processes all applications on a strict first-come, first-served queue based on submission timestamp.

### How does the Self-Finance Scheme subsidy transfer work?
Under Self-Finance, the applicant pays the net vehicle price upfront, and the State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) directly transfers the Rs 80,000 subsidy into the applicant's verified bank account after motor registration.

### What is the subsidy amount for electric rickshaws and loaders?
Electric three-wheelers (rickshaws and cargo loaders) receive a government subsidy of up to Rs 400,000 per vehicle depending on battery specification and category.

### Can students apply for the PAVE electric bike scheme?
Yes, enrolled students aged 18 and above with a valid CNIC and educational institution ID can apply under dedicated student quota allocations.

### How does PAVE differ from the CM Punjab Electric Bike Scheme?
PAVE is a nationwide federal scheme managed by the EDB covering 2-wheelers and 3-wheelers via pave.gov.pk, whereas the CM Punjab scheme is restricted to Punjab students and operated via bikes.punjab.gov.pk and Bank of Punjab.

### Is a driving license mandatory to apply for PAVE?
Yes, applicants must possess a valid motorcycle/three-wheeler driving license or official learner permit issued by police licensing authorities.

### Why is vehicle registration required before receiving the PAVE subsidy?
Vehicle registration with excise authorities is mandatory to verify chassis and motor serial numbers against the applicant's CNIC, preventing fraud and unauthorized resale.
