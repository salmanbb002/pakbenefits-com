## Step 2 — Head-Entity Research (Canonical Facts & `sameAs`)

1. **Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification (PAVE)**
   - **Canonical Name:** Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification Scheme
   - **Type:** Government Policy Program / Subsidy Initiative
   - **sameAs:** `https://pave.gov.pk`
   - **Core Attributes:** Approved by Federal Cabinet under NEVP 2025–2030; total budget allocation of Rs 100.36 billion through 2030; Phase-I covers 41,000 EVs (40,000 e-bikes, 1,000 e-rickshaws/loaders); Phase-II allocation Rs 8.95 billion for 78,170 EVs in FY 2025–26; offers up to Rs 80,000 subsidy on electric motorcycles and up to Rs 400,000 on three-wheelers.
2. **Engineering Development Board (EDB)**
   - **Canonical Name:** Engineering Development Board
   - **Type:** Government Agency
   - **sameAs:** `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering_Development_Board`
   - **Core Attributes:** Technical arm under Ministry of Industries and Production (MoIP); pre-qualifies Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) and certifies eligible EV models.
3. **Ministry of Industries and Production (MoIP)**
   - **Canonical Name:** Ministry of Industries and Production
   - **Type:** Government Agency / Cabinet Ministry
   - **sameAs:** `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Industries_and_Production_(Pakistan)`
   - **Core Attributes:** Oversees national EV transition policies and supervises PAVE program implementation across federal and provincial jurisdictions.
4. **New Energy Vehicles Policy 2025–2030 (NEVP)**
   - **Canonical Name:** New Energy Vehicles Policy 2025–2030
   - **Type:** Policy Framework / Legislation
   - **sameAs:** N/A (Unlinked Entity)
   - **Core Attributes:** National policy setting a target for 30% of all new vehicle sales in Pakistan to be electric by 2030.
5. **State Bank of Pakistan (SBP)**
   - **Canonical Name:** State Bank of Pakistan
   - **Type:** Central Bank
   - **sameAs:** `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Bank_of_Pakistan`
   - **Core Attributes:** Manages direct electronic fund transfer (EFT) of verified Rs 80,000 subsidies into bank accounts under the Self-Finance Scheme.

## Step 7 — Information-Gain Pass

- **Competitor Gaps Identified:**
  1. Most competitor articles fail to distinguish between **Federal PAVE** (national policy managed by EDB across all provinces) and **CM Punjab Electric Bike Scheme** (provincial student scheme run via BOP).
  2. Outdated procedural information: Competitor pages still emphasize computerized balloting, missing the **May 2026 ECC rule change** shifting Phase 2 to a strict **first-come, first-served** application timestamp queue.
  3. Superficial coverage of the reimbursement mechanics under **Self-Finance** (upfront purchase followed by SBP bank transfer) vs **Bank Lease** (subsidized monthly installments).
