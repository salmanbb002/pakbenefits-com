# Apni Chhat Apna Ghar Scheme Online Apply 2026: ACAG Portal Registration, Eligibility & Rs 15 Lakh Loan Guide

To apply online for the CM Punjab Apni Chhat Apna Ghar Scheme in 2026, register on the official government portal at **acag.punjab.gov.pk** using your 13-digit CNIC and mobile number, upload your land ownership documents (Fard/Registry for up to 5 Marla urban or 10 Marla rural plots), and complete your household socio-economic profile. Eligible low-income citizens receive an interest-free construction loan of up to **Rs. 15 Lakh** (1.5 Million PKR) with an affordable monthly installment of approximately **Rs. 14,000** over a 7-year repayment period and 0% markup.

## What Is the CM Punjab Apni Chhat Apna Ghar (ACAG) Scheme 2026?

The Chief Minister Punjab Apni Chhat Apna Ghar (ACAG) Scheme is a transformative public housing initiative launched by Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif to address severe housing deficits and provide shelter security to underprivileged families across Punjab. Administered by the Punjab Housing and Town Planning Agency (PHATA) under the Housing, Urban Development and Public Health Engineering Department (HUD&PHED), the program aims to construct over 100,000 sustainable homes.

Unlike commercial banking mortgages that burden low-income households with exorbitant interest rates, the ACAG scheme operates on an Islamic, non-interest financial model where the Government of Punjab pays the entire markup subsidy. Borrowers only repay the exact principal borrowed, making monthly payments manageable for families earning modest wages.

### Three Distinct Project Implementation Models

The ACAG program is structured across three execution pathways to accommodate diverse socio-economic backgrounds:

1. **Model 1 (Private Land Construction):** Targeted at citizens who already own a small residential plot (up to 5 Marla in urban areas or up to 10 Marla in rural areas) but lack the financial capital to build a structure. Beneficiaries receive up to Rs. 15 Lakh in interest-free loans disbursed in tranches.
2. **Model 2 (Government Housing Schemes):** Developed on state-owned land developed by PHATA, providing turnkey houses with subsidized infrastructure to selected low-income applicants via digital balloting.
3. **Model 3 (Urban Multi-Storey Apartments):** Subsidized multi-family apartment complexes built in major metropolitan centers like Lahore, Faisalabad, Rawalpindi, and Multan.

## Eligibility Criteria for the 15 Lakh Interest-Free Loan

To qualify for the Rs. 15 Lakh interest-free housing loan under Model 1, applicants must satisfy the following verified standards:

- **Provincial Domicile & CNIC:** The applicant must be a permanent resident of Punjab holding a valid Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC/NICOP).
- **Plot Ownership:** The applicant or a member of the immediate family must hold clear, undisputed title to a residential plot measuring up to 5 Marlas in urban areas or up to 10 Marlas in rural areas.
- **Poverty Profile (PSER/PMT Score):** The applicant must be registered in the Punjab Socio-Economic Registry (PSER) with a Proxy Means Test (PMT) score of 60 or below.
- **Sole Ownership Rule:** The applicant must not own any other constructed residential property or home within Pakistan.
- **Credit History:** Must have a clean credit record with no outstanding defaults with commercial banks, microfinance institutions, or the State Bank of Pakistan's electronic Credit Information Bureau (e-CIB).

## Step-by-Step Online Registration Guide on acag.punjab.gov.pk

The application process is fully digitized through the dedicated provincial web portal. Follow these systematic instructions to submit your file:

### Step 1: Account Creation and Portal Signup
Visit the official web portal at **https://acag.punjab.gov.pk/**. Click on the "Register / Sign Up" button. Enter your full name as printed on your CNIC, your 13-digit CNIC number, district of residence, and an active mobile number registered in your name. Verify your identity using the One-Time Password (OTP) sent via SMS.

### Step 2: Personal Profile & Socio-Economic Assessment
Log into your dashboard and complete your personal bio-data. Enter your household size, current monthly income, and employment details. If you have already registered with the Punjab Socio-Economic Registry (PSER), link your PSER survey reference number to expedite verification.

### Step 3: Land Details and Documentation Upload
Navigate to the "Property Details" section. Select whether your plot is situated in an urban or rural locality, specify the exact plot size (e.g., 3 Marla, 5 Marla), and enter the Khasra/Khewat number. Upload clear, scanned PDF or JPG copies of your property documents:
- Computerized Fard Malkiat from the Arazi Record Center (PLRA) or registered Sale Deed (Registry).
- Site plan (Aks Shajra) or possession certificate from the local housing authority.
- Clear photographs of the vacant plot showing physical boundaries.

### Step 4: Submission and Tracking ID Generation
Review all entered details, accept the legal declaration confirming that you do not own another house, and click "Submit Application". The portal generates a unique Application Tracking ID (e.g., `ACAG-2026-XXXXX`). Keep this tracking code safe to monitor your scrutiny stages.

## Loan Terms, Disbursement Tranches & Repayment Schedule

The financial framework has been engineered to ensure transparent execution and prevent fund misallocation:

| Milestone / Tranche | Disbursed Amount | Required Construction Milestone |
|---|---|---|
| **Tranche 1 (Foundation)** | Rs. 500,000 (33%) | Mobilization, excavation, plinth beam, and foundation completion |
| **Tranche 2 (Structure & Roof)** | Rs. 600,000 (40%) | Brick masonry walls, door frames, and roof slab casting |
| **Tranche 3 (Finishing)** | Rs. 400,000 (27%) | Plaster, flooring, electrical wiring, plumbing, and fixtures |

### Monthly Installment Structure

- **Total Loan Principal:** Rs. 1,500,000 (15 Lakh PKR)
- **Interest / Markup:** 0% (Completely interest-free)
- **Repayment Period:** 7 Years (84 equal monthly installments)
- **Approximate Monthly Installment:** Rs. 14,000 per month
- **Grace Period:** Beneficiaries enjoy a 3-month grace period during the active construction phase before the first installment falls due.

## Required Documents Checklist for Online Application

Ensure the following authentic documents are scanned and prepared prior to initiating your application on `acag.punjab.gov.pk`:

- Original valid CNIC of the primary applicant and spouse.
- Computerized Land Title: Fard Malkiat issued by the Punjab Land Records Authority within the last 6 months, or an attested registered deed.
- Plot Possession Letter or No-Objection Certificate (NOC) from the concerned local government, union council, or development authority.
- Proof of Household Income: Salary slip, income certificate, or business undertaking.
- Digital Photographs: Passport-size picture of the applicant and high-resolution photos of the construction plot.
- Affidavit of Non-Homeownership: Formal declaration affirming that the applicant does not possess another home.

## Verification, Field Inspection & Balloting Process

Submitting your online application triggers a transparent, multi-tiered scrutiny pipeline:

1. **Automated Digital Pre-Scrutiny:** System algorithms cross-check the CNIC against NADRA databases, verify land ownership against the PLRA cadastre, and confirm PMT score eligibility via PSER.
2. **Physical Site Inspection:** Field inspection officers from the Punjab Housing and Town Planning Agency (PHATA) or local Deputy Commissioner office conduct an on-site physical visit to inspect the plot, confirm boundaries, and ensure structural feasibility.
3. **Computerized Balloting:** In tehsils where eligible applications exceed the quarterly funding allocation, a randomized, computerized public balloting is conducted by the Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB).
4. **Loan Agreement Signing:** Approved candidates sign a standardized interest-free loan agreement and receive their first installment directly into their verified bank account.

For citizens encountering technical difficulties with document uploads or internet access:

- **Official ACAG Toll-Free Helpline:** Call **0800-09100** (Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM) for general inquiries and technical troubleshooting.
- **District Facilitation Centers:** Assistance desks have been established in all 36 District Deputy Commissioner (DC) offices and regional PHATA offices where staff assist citizens with free scanning and online registration.
- **Scam Warning:** The entire ACAG registration process is free of charge. Do not pay any agent, broker, or private entity claiming to guarantee loan approvals.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the official website to apply for Apni Chhat Apna Ghar scheme?
The only official portal for online registration is **https://acag.punjab.gov.pk/**.

### How much loan can I get under the Apni Chhat Apna Ghar scheme?
Under Model 1, eligible plot owners can receive an interest-free construction loan of up to Rs. 15 Lakh (1.5 Million PKR).

### What is the maximum plot size eligible for the scheme?
Applicants can own up to 5 Marlas of land in designated urban areas or up to 10 Marlas in rural areas.

### What is the monthly installment amount and repayment duration?
The loan is repaid over 7 years (84 months) with an estimated monthly installment of approximately Rs. 14,000 with zero interest.

### What is the maximum PMT score required to qualify?
Applicants must be enrolled in the Punjab Socio-Economic Registry (PSER) with a Proxy Means Test (PMT) score of 60 or less.

### Do I have to pay any interest or markup on the 15 lakh loan?
No. The loan is 100% interest-free (0% markup). The Punjab Government absorbs all financing costs.

### Can I apply if I already own a built house?
No. The scheme is strictly reserved for homeless or non-homeowning families who own a vacant plot or unconstructed land.

### How is the loan money disbursed to the applicant?
The loan is disbursed in three phased tranches (33% for foundation, 40% for walls and roof, and 27% for finishing) based on physical inspection milestones.

### What documents are required for proof of land ownership?
A computerized Fard Malkiat from the Arazi Record Center (PLRA) or a registered Sale Deed (Registry) accompanied by a plot possession letter.

### What is the official helpline number for the ACAG scheme?
You can contact the official helpline at **0800-09100** for support and application status inquiries.
