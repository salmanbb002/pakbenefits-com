## Step 7 — Information-Gain Pass

### Blind Spots & Competitor Errors Fixed
1. **Outdated/Confusing BISP Linking:** Competitors frequently confuse the PM Fuel Relief Scheme with BISP 8171. This piece clearly establishes that 9771 is a standalone Petroleum Division project and does not require BISP enrollment.
2. **Missing Province Code Breakdown:** Competitors mention "province letter" vaguely. This guide explicitly lists all 7 province identifiers (`P`, `S`, `K`, `B`, `I`, `A`, `G`).
3. **Exact SMS Syntax & Date Formatting:** Competitors omit the required `DDMMYYYY` date structure or fail to emphasize removing hyphens from CNICs.
4. **Token Claim Lifecycle:** Competitors omit the essential `TOK` command required right before visiting the petrol station.
