## Step 2 — Head-Entity Research

1. **Chief Minister Punjab Electric Bike Scheme**
   - **Canonical Name:** Chief Minister Punjab Electric Bike Scheme
   - **Aliases:** CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme, Punjab Pink Scooty Scheme, Punjab Student EV Initiative
   - **Type:** Government Scheme / Student Subsidy
   - **Operating Authority:** Government of Punjab / Department of Higher Education
   - **Financial Partner:** The Bank of Punjab (BOP)
   - **Official Portal:** `bikes.punjab.gov.pk`
   - **Attributes:** 0% interest markup, zero down payment (Phase 2), Rs 90,000 capital subsidy, ~Rs 3,000/month installment over 36 months, mandatory learner permit/license, free helmet and safety kit.

2. **Sindh Mass Transit Authority (SMTA) Pink EV Scooty Scheme**
   - **Canonical Name:** Sindh Pink EV Scooty Scheme
   - **Aliases:** SMTA Pink Scooty Scheme, Sindh Female EV Scooter Program
   - **Type:** Government Transportation Scheme
   - **Operating Authority:** Sindh Mass Transit Authority (SMTA) / Transport Department Government of Sindh
   - **Official Portal:** `smta.gos.pk`
   - **Attributes:** Subsidized EV scooties for female students, working women, single mothers, and widows; target cities include Karachi, Hyderabad, Sukkur, Larkana, Shaheed Benazirabad, Mirpurkhas.

3. **The Bank of Punjab (BOP)**
   - **Canonical Name:** The Bank of Punjab
   - **Type:** Commercial Bank / Financial Partner
   - **`sameAs`:** `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_of_Punjab`
   - **Role:** Financer, credit & guarantor verifier, installment processing bank.
