# Pink Scooty Scheme 2026 – Registration, Eligibility, Documents & Balloting Guide

> The **Pink Scooty Scheme 2026** provides subsidized electric scooters to female students and working women in Pakistan to improve independent mobility. Managed under provincial initiatives by the **Government of Punjab** (`bikes.punjab.gov.pk`) and the **Sindh Mass Transit Authority** (`smta.gos.pk`), eligible applicants can register online for interest-free financing, zero down payment options, and computerized balloting.

---

## What is the Pink Scooty Scheme 2026 in Pakistan?

The **Pink Scooty Scheme 2026** is a provincial government social mobility project aimed at empowering women by offering subsidized, eco-friendly electric motorbikes and scooters. The initiative tackles daily transportation barriers faced by female university students, working professionals, single mothers, and widows across Punjab and Sindh.

By substituting expensive commercial transport with subsidized electric two-wheelers, the program reduces monthly commute expenses while fostering financial independence. Both provincial programs partner with public financial institutions and transport authorities to ensure structured distribution through transparent digital systems.

### CM Punjab Female Student EV Bike Initiative (`bikes.punjab.gov.pk`)

In Punjab, Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif launched the **CM Punjab Electric Bike Scheme** to distribute 20,000 motorbikes, reserving a dedicated quota of pink electric scooties exclusively for female students. Partnered with **The Bank of Punjab (BOP)**, the government covers the 0% interest markup, registration fees, token tax, and first-year insurance, alongside a capital subsidy of **Rs 90,000** per vehicle.

Female students enrolled in HEC-recognized public or private universities and graduate colleges across Punjab can submit online applications. Each recipient receives a safety package including a full-face helmet, protective guards, and access to mandatory riding training workshops.

### Sindh Mass Transit Authority (SMTA) Pink EV Scooter Program (`smta.gos.pk`)

The **Sindh Mass Transit Authority (SMTA)** operates the Sindh Pink EV Scooty Scheme under the Transport & Mass Transit Department Government of Sindh. Unlike student-only initiatives, the Sindh program caters to both female students and working women across major urban centers including Karachi, Hyderabad, Sukkur, Larkana, Shaheed Benazirabad, and Mirpurkhas.

The Sindh initiative focuses on heavily subsidized electric scooties to assist daily commuters facing rising fuel costs. Applicants apply directly through the official SMTA web portal, undergoing background verification prior to provincial computerized draw allocations.

## Who is Eligible for the Pink Scooty Scheme 2026?

Eligibility criteria ensure that government subsidies reach genuine female applicants who possess valid documentation and meet age and residency requirements.

### Eligibility Criteria for Punjab Female Students

To qualify under the Punjab E-Bike initiative, female applicants must satisfy the following parameters:

* **Educational Status:** Must be a regular, full-time student enrolled in an HEC-recognized public or private university, degree college, or graduate institution in Punjab.
* **Age Requirement:** Applicant must be at least 18 years of age at the time of online application submission.
* **Driving Permit:** Must hold a valid motorcycle driving license or an official motorcycle learner permit issued by Punjab Traffic Police.
* **Residency:** Must possess a valid Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC) and Punjab domicile certificate.
* **Financial Guarantor:** Requires a parent, spouse, or family co-borrower with verifiable monthly income to satisfy BOP loan security conditions.

### Eligibility Criteria for Sindh Working Women & Students

The Sindh Mass Transit Authority applies broad eligibility categories to support women across professional and educational sectors:

* **Target Demographic:** Female working professionals (government or private sector), active university students, registered entrepreneurs, single mothers, and widows.
* **Residency:** Must hold a valid CNIC and Sindh provincial domicile (or Permanent Residence Certificate - PRC Form D).
* **Age Limit:** Minimum 18 years of age; working professionals must provide employment proof or employer verification.
* **Commute Verification:** Preference is given to applicants residing in urban districts with active public transit expansions (Karachi, Hyderabad, Sukkur, Larkana).

## Required Documents for Pink Scooty Scheme Online Registration

Before initiating an online application on `bikes.punjab.gov.pk` or `smta.gos.pk`, prepare clear, scanned digital copies (PDF or JPG format under 2MB) of the following mandatory documents:

1. **Applicant CNIC / B-Form:** Front and back scanned copy of the applicant's valid CNIC.
2. **Provincial Domicile Certificate:** Valid domicile matching the respective province (Punjab or Sindh).
3. **Driving License or Learner Permit:** Official traffic police learner slip or permanent motorcycle license card.
4. **Educational Proof (Students):** Current semester fee slip, university student ID card, or bonafide certificate signed by the head of department.
5. **Employment Proof (Working Women - Sindh):** Official employment ID card, salary slip, or employer reference letter.
6. **Guarantor / Co-Borrower CNIC:** Scanned CNIC of the parent, guardian, or spouse acting as financial guarantor for BOP bank processing.
7. **Guarantor Income Verification:** Recent salary slip, bank statement, or utility bill showing active residential address.
8. **Passport-Sized Photograph:** Recent photograph with a plain blue or white background.

## How to Apply Online for Pink Scooty Scheme 2026 Step-by-Step

Follow these official step-by-step procedures to register your application successfully without risking data rejection.

### Punjab Portal Registration Walkthrough (`bikes.punjab.gov.pk`)

1. **Access the Official Portal:** Open your web browser and navigate directly to [bikes.punjab.gov.pk](https://bikes.punjab.gov.pk/). Avoid third-party links or unofficial forms.
2. **Account Registration:** Click on **Register** and enter your full name, CNIC number, active mobile number (registered under your own CNIC), and create a secure password.
3. **Select Vehicle Type:** Log in to your candidate dashboard and select **Electric Bike (Pink Scooty)** under the female applicant category.
4. **Fill Educational & Personal Details:** Input your university name, enrollment number, current academic year, permanent home address, and driving learner permit number.
5. **Upload Required Documents:** Attach clear scans of your CNIC, Punjab domicile, student ID, learner permit, and guarantor details.
6. **Submit for BOP Credit Verification:** Review all entries carefully before clicking **Submit Application**. Your application will be forwarded electronically to **The Bank of Punjab** for automated verification.

### Sindh SMTA Portal Registration Guide (`smta.gos.pk`)

1. **Visit the SMTA Portal:** Navigate to the official website at [smta.gos.pk](https://smta.gos.pk/).
2. **Navigate to Projects:** Locate the **Pink EV Scooty Registration 2026** banner on the homepage.
3. **Complete Application Form:** Enter your CNIC, select your applicant category (Student or Working Woman), and specify your district (e.g., Karachi Central, Sukkur, Hyderabad).
4. **Attach Documentation:** Upload your CNIC, Sindh domicile, PRC, and employment/student verification documents.
5. **Riding Training Preference:** Indicate whether you already hold a learner permit or require enrollment in the free government motorcycle training program.
6. **Final Submission:** Confirm your contact details and submit the application form. Save your generated registration tracking ID for future reference.

## Pink Scooty Balloting 2026: How Selection & Result Check Work

Demand for government-subsidized electric scooties frequently exceeds available vehicle quotas. To guarantee equity, both provincial governments utilize computerized e-balloting systems.

### Computerized E-Balloting Mechanism & Transparency

Once the online registration phase closes, submitted applications undergo dual-stage verification:
* **Stage 1 (Institutional & Document Check):** Academic institutions and provincial transport departments verify student status and domicile validity.
* **Stage 2 (Bank Credit Inspection):** The Bank of Punjab audits guarantor creditworthiness to ensure smooth installment recovery.

All verified applicants enter an automated, computerized e-balloting draw monitored by provincial oversight committees, audit firms, and media representatives to eliminate manual intervention.

### How to Check Pink Scooty Balloting Result by CNIC

Selected candidates and waiting-list applicants can verify their balloting status through official channels:

1. **Online Portal Lookup:** Visit `bikes.punjab.gov.pk` or `smta.gos.pk` and click on **Balloting Results 2026**.
2. **Enter CNIC:** Input your 13-digit CNIC number without dashes and click **Search Status**.
3. **Automated SMS Notification:** Successful applicants receive official SMS alerts from the government portal (e.g., official 8070 or 8171 government handles).
4. **Bank Contact:** Successful candidates in Punjab will be contacted by their designated BOP branch to sign vehicle financing agreements and collect delivery schedules.

## Common Application Mistakes & Online Scam Alerts

To protect your personal information and financial safety, keep these guidelines in mind:

* **Official Domain Safeguard:** Apply **only** on official `.gov.pk` or `.gos.pk` websites (`bikes.punjab.gov.pk` and `smta.gos.pk`). Never submit personal details on `.blogspot.com`, `.xyz`, or unverified commercial portals.
* **No Fee Requirement:** The government online registration process is completely free of charge. Never pay money to agents, private WhatsApp groups, or individuals claiming to guarantee balloting success.
* **Expired Driving Permits:** Ensure your motorcycle learner's permit is valid at the time of submission. Submitting an expired slip leads to immediate application rejection during bank audits.
* **Incorrect Guarantor Details:** Provide accurate income information for your co-borrower. Mismatched income details cause credit check delays at The Bank of Punjab.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Is a driving license mandatory to apply for the Pink Scooty Scheme 2026?
Yes. Applicants must possess at least a valid motorcycle learner's permit issued by the traffic police. Full driving licenses are also accepted, but a learner slip satisfies registration requirements.

### Can male students apply for the Pink Scooty Scheme?
Male students can apply for black electric or petrol motorbikes under the general CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme portal at `bikes.punjab.gov.pk`. However, the pink scooty allocation is reserved strictly for female applicants.

### What is the last date to register for the Pink Scooty Scheme Phase 2?
The registration deadline for the current active phase of the Punjab E-Bike initiative is October 4, 2026. Applicants should complete portal submission prior to midnight on the closing date.

### What is the monthly installment amount for the Punjab Pink Scooty?
The estimated monthly installment is approximately Rs 3,000 per month for a 3-year (36-month) financing plan, as the government subsidizes Rs 90,000 of the total cost and absorbs all interest markup.

### How can I check my Pink Scooty balloting result online?
Visit the official application portal (`bikes.punjab.gov.pk` for Punjab or `smta.gos.pk` for Sindh), select the Balloting Result section, and enter your 13-digit CNIC number without hyphens.

### Are students from private universities eligible for the Punjab Pink Scooty?
Yes. Regular, full-time female students enrolled in HEC-recognized private universities and degree colleges in Punjab are eligible alongside public university students.

### Is a down payment required for the 2026 Pink Scooty Phase 2?
No. Under the updated Phase 2 terms, the Government of Punjab eliminated the initial down payment requirement for selected students.

### Who is eligible for the Pink Scooty Scheme in Sindh?
Female students, working women, single mothers, and widows residing in Sindh with a valid Sindh domicile or PRC can apply through the Sindh Mass Transit Authority (`smta.gos.pk`).

### What happens if my guarantor fails the Bank of Punjab credit audit?
If your guarantor or co-borrower fails the credit check due to bank default or insufficient proof of income, BOP may request an alternative guarantor or reject the financing application.

### Are the pink scooties electric vehicles (EV) or petrol engine models?
The vehicles provided under the 2026 Pink Scooty initiatives in both Punjab and Sindh are 100% battery-powered Electric Vehicles (EVs), eliminating daily petrol costs for commuters.
