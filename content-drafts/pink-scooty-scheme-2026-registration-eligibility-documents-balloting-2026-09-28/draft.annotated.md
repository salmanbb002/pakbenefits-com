# Pink Scooty Scheme 2026 – Registration, Eligibility, Documents & Balloting Guide

> The **Pink Scooty Scheme 2026** provides subsidized electric scooters to **female students** and **working women** in Pakistan to improve independent mobility. Managed under provincial initiatives by the **Government of Punjab** (`bikes.punjab.gov.pk`) and the **Sindh Mass Transit Authority** (`smta.gos.pk`), eligible applicants can register online for **0% markup interest-free loans**, **zero down payment** options, and **computerized e-balloting**.

---

## What is the Pink Scooty Scheme 2026 in Pakistan?

The **Pink Scooty Scheme 2026** is a provincial government social mobility project aimed at empowering women by offering subsidized, eco-friendly electric motorbikes and scooters. The initiative tackles daily transportation barriers faced by **female students**, **working women**, single mothers, and widows across Punjab and Sindh.

By substituting expensive commercial transport with subsidized electric two-wheelers, the program reduces monthly commute expenses while fostering financial independence. Both provincial programs partner with public financial institutions and transport authorities to ensure structured distribution through transparent digital systems.

### CM Punjab Female Student EV Bike Initiative (`bikes.punjab.gov.pk`)

In Punjab, Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif launched the **Chief Minister Punjab Electric Bike Scheme** to distribute 20,000 motorbikes, reserving a dedicated quota of pink electric scooties exclusively for **female students**. Partnered with **The Bank of Punjab (BOP)**, the government covers the **0% markup interest-free loan**, registration fees, token tax, and first-year insurance, alongside a **Rs 90,000 capital subsidy** per vehicle.

**Female students** enrolled in **HEC recognized institutions** across Punjab can submit online applications. Each recipient receives a safety package including a full-face helmet, protective guards, and access to mandatory riding training workshops.

### Sindh Mass Transit Authority (SMTA) Pink EV Scooter Program (`smta.gos.pk`)

The **Sindh Mass Transit Authority** operates the Sindh Pink EV Scooty Scheme under the Transport & Mass Transit Department Government of Sindh. Unlike student-only initiatives, the Sindh program caters to both **female students** and **working women** across major urban centers including Karachi, **target districts** like Hyderabad, Sukkur, Larkana, Shaheed Benazirabad, and Mirpurkhas.

The Sindh initiative focuses on heavily subsidized electric scooties to assist daily commuters facing rising fuel costs. Applicants apply directly through the official **Sindh Mass Transit Authority** web portal, undergoing background verification prior to provincial **computerized e-balloting** allocations.

## Who is Eligible for the Pink Scooty Scheme 2026?

Eligibility criteria ensure that government subsidies reach genuine applicants who possess valid documentation and meet age and residency requirements.

### Eligibility Criteria for Punjab Female Students

To qualify under the Punjab E-Bike initiative, **female students** must satisfy the following parameters:

* **Educational Status:** Must be a regular, full-time student enrolled in an **HEC recognized institution** in Punjab.
* **Age Requirement:** Applicant must be at least 18 years of age at the time of online application submission.
* **Driving Permit:** Must hold a valid motorcycle driving license or an official **learner driving permit** issued by Punjab Traffic Police.
* **Residency:** Must possess a valid Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC) and **provincial domicile** certificate.
* **Financial Guarantor:** Requires a parent, spouse, or family co-borrower providing **guarantor income proof** to satisfy **The Bank of Punjab (BOP)** security conditions.

### Eligibility Criteria for Sindh Working Women & Students

The **Sindh Mass Transit Authority** applies broad eligibility categories to support women across professional and educational sectors:

* **Target Demographic:** **Working women** (government or private sector), **female students**, registered entrepreneurs, single mothers, and widows.
* **Residency:** Must hold a valid CNIC and **provincial domicile** (or Permanent Residence Certificate - PRC Form D).
* **Age Limit:** Minimum 18 years of age; **working women** must provide employment proof or employer verification.
* **Commute Verification:** Preference is given to applicants residing in **target districts** with active public transit expansions (Karachi, Hyderabad, Sukkur, Larkana).

## Required Documents for Pink Scooty Scheme Online Registration

Before initiating an online application on `bikes.punjab.gov.pk` or `smta.gos.pk`, prepare clear, scanned digital copies (PDF or JPG format under 2MB) of the following **required documents**:

1. **Applicant CNIC / B-Form:** Front and back scanned copy of the applicant's valid CNIC.
2. **Provincial Domicile Certificate:** Valid **provincial domicile** matching the respective province (Punjab or Sindh).
3. **Driving License or Learner Permit:** Official traffic police **learner driving permit** slip or permanent motorcycle license card.
4. **Educational Proof (Students):** Current semester fee slip or student ID from an **HEC recognized institution**.
5. **Employment Proof (Working Women - Sindh):** Official employment ID card or salary slip for **working women**.
6. **Guarantor / Co-Borrower CNIC:** Scanned CNIC of the parent, guardian, or spouse acting as financial guarantor for **The Bank of Punjab (BOP)** processing.
7. **Guarantor Income Proof:** Recent salary slip, bank statement, or utility bill providing **guarantor income proof**.
8. **Passport-Sized Photograph:** Recent photograph with a plain blue or white background.

## How to Apply Online for Pink Scooty Scheme 2026 Step-by-Step

Follow these official step-by-step procedures to register your application successfully without risking data rejection.

### Punjab Portal Registration Walkthrough (`bikes.punjab.gov.pk`)

1. **Access the Official Portal:** Open your web browser and navigate directly to [bikes.punjab.gov.pk](https://bikes.punjab.gov.pk/).
2. **Account Registration:** Click on **Register** and enter your full name, CNIC number, active mobile number, and create a password.
3. **Select Vehicle Type:** Log in to your candidate dashboard and select **Electric Bike (Pink Scooty)** under the **female students** category.
4. **Fill Educational & Personal Details:** Input your **HEC recognized institution** details, home address, and **learner driving permit** number.
5. **Upload Required Documents:** Attach clear scans of your CNIC, **provincial domicile**, student ID, **learner driving permit**, and **guarantor income proof**.
6. **Submit for BOP Credit Verification:** Click **Submit Application**. Your file will be processed electronically by **The Bank of Punjab (BOP)**.

### Sindh SMTA Portal Registration Guide (`smta.gos.pk`)

1. **Visit the SMTA Portal:** Navigate to the official website at [smta.gos.pk](https://smta.gos.pk/).
2. **Navigate to Projects:** Locate the **Pink Scooty Scheme 2026** banner on the homepage.
3. **Complete Application Form:** Enter your CNIC, select your applicant category (**Female Students** or **Working Women**), and specify your **target districts**.
4. **Attach Required Documents:** Upload your CNIC, **provincial domicile**, and employment/student verification documents.
5. **Riding Training Preference:** Indicate whether you already hold a **learner driving permit** or require enrollment in government motorcycle training.
6. **Final Submission:** Confirm contact details and submit the application form.

## Pink Scooty Balloting 2026: How Selection & Result Check Work

Demand for government-subsidized electric scooties frequently exceeds available vehicle quotas. To guarantee equity, both provincial governments utilize **computerized e-balloting** systems.

### Computerized E-Balloting Mechanism & Transparency

Once the online registration phase closes, submitted applications undergo dual-stage verification:
* **Stage 1 (Institutional & Document Check):** Academic institutions and provincial transport departments verify student status and **provincial domicile** validity.
* **Stage 2 (Bank Credit Inspection):** **The Bank of Punjab (BOP)** audits **guarantor income proof** to ensure smooth installment recovery.

All verified applicants enter an automated **computerized e-balloting** draw monitored by provincial oversight committees.

### How to Check Pink Scooty Balloting Result by CNIC

Selected candidates and waiting-list applicants can verify their balloting status through official channels:

1. **Online Portal Lookup:** Visit `bikes.punjab.gov.pk` or `smta.gos.pk` and click on **Balloting Results 2026**.
2. **Enter CNIC:** Input your 13-digit CNIC number without dashes and click **Search Status**.
3. **Automated SMS Notification:** Successful applicants receive official SMS alerts from government portals.
4. **Bank Contact:** Successful candidates in Punjab will be contacted by **The Bank of Punjab (BOP)** to finalize financing agreements.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Is a driving license mandatory to apply for the Pink Scooty Scheme 2026?
Yes. Applicants must possess at least a valid **learner driving permit** issued by the traffic police.

### Can male students apply for the Pink Scooty Scheme?
Male students can apply for black electric or petrol motorbikes under the general **Chief Minister Punjab Electric Bike Scheme** portal at `bikes.punjab.gov.pk`.

### What is the application deadline for the Pink Scooty Scheme Phase 2?
The **application deadline** for the active phase of the Punjab E-Bike initiative is **October 4, 2026**.

### What is the monthly installment amount for the Punjab Pink Scooty?
The **monthly installment** is approximately Rs 3,000 per month under a 36-month repayment plan, supported by a **Rs 90,000 capital subsidy**.

### How can I check my Pink Scooty balloting result online?
Visit `bikes.punjab.gov.pk` or `smta.gos.pk`, navigate to the **computerized e-balloting** results section, and enter your 13-digit CNIC number.

### Are students from private universities eligible for the Punjab Pink Scooty?
Yes. **Female students** enrolled in **HEC recognized institutions** in Punjab are eligible.

### Is a down payment required for the 2026 Pink Scooty Phase 2?
No. Under updated Phase 2 terms, the government offers **zero down payment** options.

### Who is eligible for the Pink Scooty Scheme in Sindh?
**Female students**, **working women**, single mothers, and widows with a valid **provincial domicile** in Sindh can apply through the **Sindh Mass Transit Authority** (`smta.gos.pk`).

### What happens if my guarantor fails The Bank of Punjab credit audit?
If **guarantor income proof** fails verification, **The Bank of Punjab (BOP)** will ask for an alternative guarantor.

### Are the pink scooties electric vehicles (EV) or petrol engine models?
Vehicles provided under the 2026 initiatives in Punjab and Sindh are 100% battery-powered Electric Vehicles (EVs).
