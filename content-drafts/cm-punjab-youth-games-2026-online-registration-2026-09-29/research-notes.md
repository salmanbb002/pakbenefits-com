## 7. Information-Gain Pass

### What Competitors Miss or Get Wrong
1. **Outdated Deadline**: Competitors like pkschemes.pk still display the old September 25 cutoff in several sections, misleading applicants. The portal officially runs through **September 30, 2026**.
2. **Conflicting Prize Figures**: Early news articles quoted Rs. 150 Million. The official government portal and recent sports ministry updates have locked the total cash pool at **Rs. 500 Million**.
3. **Form Upload Blockers**: Competitors do not warn students that the portal strictly crashes/rejects profile photos larger than **256 KB** (JPG/PNG).
4. **Missing Redressal Protocol**: None of the guides explain how an athlete who is unfairly graded during institutional trials can file a formal complaint using the portal's integrated **Grievance Redressal Mechanism**.
5. **Detailed Sport-by-Bracket Matrix**: Most competitor posts list sports haphazardly without showing exactly which sports are available to middle schools vs colleges vs universities vs differently-abled athletes.
