# Ehsaas Kafalat Survey Status: Invalid CNIC & Marriage NSER Update Guide

To resolve **"Invalid CNIC"** or **"Record Not Found"** errors on the **8171 portal** after marriage, beneficiaries must update their marital status and **Family Registration Certificate** at **NADRA** first. Next, visit the nearest **BISP Tehsil Office** to complete a **Dynamic NSER survey update** with your updated **CNIC** and children's **B-Forms**.

## Why Does the 8171 Portal Show "Invalid CNIC" or "Record Not Found"?

The **8171 portal** displays **"Invalid CNIC"** or **"Record Not Found in NSER"** when an applicant's CNIC is not correctly linked to an active **National Socio-Economic Registry (NSER)** survey record. This issue frequently occurs after marriage when a woman changes her marital status, address, or husband's family tree details at **NADRA**, but fails to execute a secondary NSER data update at the **BISP Tehsil Office**.

Because the **BISP** automated database checks against **NADRA** records, any discrepancy between an un-updated CNIC and previous survey data causes the system to flag the entry. Additional causes include expired **CNIC** cards, typos during SMS checking, or families who have never participated in a formal NSER door-to-door or **dynamic survey**.

## How to Update BISP NSER Record After Marriage (Step 1: NADRA Update)

Updating your **BISP eligibility** after marriage is a two-stage process that strictly requires updating your official identity records at **NADRA** before approaching **BISP**.

### 1. Obtain Marriage Registration Certificate (Nikah Nama)
Ensure you have an official computerized **Nikah Nama** issued by the Union Council or local municipal authority.

### 2. Visit NADRA Registration Center (NRC)
The female beneficiary and her husband must visit the nearest **NADRA office** together. Request an update to your marital status from "Single" to "Married".

### 3. Modify CNIC & Family Registration Certificate (FRC)
Apply for a new **CNIC** reflecting your husband's name and updated permanent address. Request a fresh **Family Registration Certificate (FRC)** to establish the new household entity in NADRA central database.

## How to Complete the BISP Dynamic Survey at Tehsil Office (Step 2: NSER Update)

Once your updated **CNIC** is issued by **NADRA**, you must complete the **NSER Dynamic Survey** at your district **BISP Tehsil Office**. Online registration for NSER updates is not available; physical presence at the registration desk is mandatory.

### Step 1: Visit the BISP Tehsil Dynamic Survey Desk
Go to the **BISP Tehsil Office** serving your district. Present your original updated **CNIC** and children's **NADRA B-Forms** at the entry token counter.

### Step 2: Answer NSER Socio-Economic Assessment
A data entry operator conducts an interactive interview to evaluate your household **PMT (Proxy Means Test)** poverty score. Questions cover household assets, housing type, utility bills, livestock, and income sources.

### Step 3: Biometric Thumb Verification & Receipt Generation
Verify all entered data on the operator's computer screen and provide biometric thumb prints. Obtain a printed **NSER Dynamic Survey** confirmation slip containing a tracking QR code and survey reference number.

## What Documents Are Required for Marriage NSER Record Updating?

Bringing complete, verified documentation prevents application rejection at the **BISP Tehsil Office**.

| Document Name | Issuing Authority | Purpose in BISP NSER Survey |
|---|---|---|
| **Original Updated CNIC** | **NADRA** | Verifies updated marital status and head of household identity |
| **Husband's Original CNIC** | **NADRA** | Links spouse identity and family socio-economic status |
| **Children's B-Forms (CRC)** | **NADRA** | Enrolls children for BISP Taleemi Wazaif stipends |
| **Recent Electricity / Gas Bill** | Utility Provider | Confirms physical residential address and household consumption |

## How to Fix "Invalid CNIC" Error on 8171 Portal (Troubleshooting Checklist)

If you encounter the **"Invalid CNIC"** or **"Record Not Found"** error while checking eligibility via **8171 SMS** or web portal, follow this systematic troubleshooting checklist.

1. **Verify CNIC Number Formatting:** Ensure you enter the 13-digit CNIC number without hyphens or spaces when sending an SMS to **8171** or querying **8171.bisp.gov.pk**.
2. **Check CNIC Expiration Date:** Renew expired CNIC cards at **NADRA** immediately; the **8171 system** automatically rejects invalid or expired identification cards.
3. **Confirm Dynamic Survey Completion:** If you have never completed a **Dynamic Survey** at a **BISP Tehsil Office**, your record will remain unlisted regardless of poverty status.
4. **Allow 30 to 45 Days System Sync:** After completing the **NSER Dynamic Survey**, allow up to 45 days for **NADRA** and **BISP** central servers to process and update your PMT eligibility score.

## What Happens to BISP Kafaalat Eligibility After Marital Status Update?

Updating your marital status triggers an automated re-evaluation of your household **PMT poverty score**. If your new family unit's PMT score falls below the eligibility threshold (32.0 or lower as of 2026), you will qualify for the quarterly **Rs 13,500 BISP Kafaalat** cash transfer and associated **BISP Taleemi Wazaif** benefits for your school-going children.

If your household PMT score is calculated above the threshold, the portal will indicate non-eligibility. In cases of sudden hardship, widowhood, or disability, beneficiaries can request a survey review after two years.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### BISP 8171 invalid cnic error solution kya hai?
Invalid CNIC error fix karne ke liye pehle **NADRA** se apna CNIC update ya renew karwayein, phir **BISP Tehsil Office** ja kar **NSER Dynamic Survey** complete karwayein.

### Shaadi ke baad BISP record update kaise karwayein?
Shaadi ke baad pehle **NADRA office** ja kar Nikah Nama ke sath CNIC par marital status change karwayein, phir NSER desk par naya **Dynamic Survey** karwayein.

### BISP record not found in NSER ka kya matlab hai?
Iska matlab hai ke aap ka CNIC BISP ke **NSER survey database** mein majood nahi hai. Aap ko **BISP Tehsil Office** ja kar apna survey karwana hoga.

### Can I update my NSER survey online at home?
No, there is no official online portal for NSER survey registration. Beneficiaries must physically visit a **BISP Tehsil Office** for biometric verification.

### Is there any fee for updating NSER record at BISP office?
No, the **BISP Dynamic Survey** and NSER record updates are 100% free of charge. Never pay money to agents or touts claiming to approve your status.

### How long does it take for 8171 status to update after NSER survey?
It typically takes 30 to 45 working days for newly surveyed records to sync in the BISP central database and reflect updated status on the **8171 web portal**.

### What should widows do to update their BISP status?
Widows should submit their husband's **NADRA Death Certificate** at NADRA to update their status, then visit the **BISP Tehsil Office** for priority survey re-evaluation.

### Why is an active mobile SIM registered in the beneficiary's name required?
BISP sends official payment notifications and verification OTP codes strictly via **8171** to the SIM registered under the female applicant's own CNIC.

### Does changing residential address require an NSER update?
Yes, moving to a new district or province requires updating your residence details during a **Dynamic Survey** so payment campsite locations are assigned correctly.

### What is the PMT score cutoff for BISP Kafaalat in 2026?
As of 2026, the standard eligibility threshold for BISP Kafaalat is a PMT (Proxy Means Test) score of 32.0 or below.
