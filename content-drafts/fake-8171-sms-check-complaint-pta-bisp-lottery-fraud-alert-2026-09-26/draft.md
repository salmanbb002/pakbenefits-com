# Fake 8171 SMS Check, Complaint & BISP Lottery Fraud Alert Guide

To check and report a fake 8171 SMS or BISP lottery fraud, verify that official communications come exclusively from the 4-digit shortcode "8171" and never from standard 11-digit mobile numbers. Forward suspicious text messages to PTA at 9000, call the official BISP Toll-Free Helpline at 0800-26477, or contact Police 15 and FIA Cybercrime Helpline 1991 immediately if money was extorted.

---

## How to Spot a Fake 8171 SMS vs Official BISP Message

Identifying fraudulent 8171 messages is critical to safeguarding your family's personal data and financial savings from organized scam networks. The Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP) issues all official payment notifications, eligibility confirmations, and survey tokens through one single communication gateway: the 4-digit shortcode `8171`. Official messages from BISP never contain personal mobile phone numbers, third-party website URLs, or requests for processing fees.

In contrast, fraudulent SMS messages are sent from regular 11-digit personal SIM numbers (e.g., `0300-1234567` or `0345-XXXXXXX`). Scammers frequently alter sender display names or use Roman Urdu text claiming that a grant of Rs. 25,000, Rs. 30,000, or a lottery scheme has been approved under your CNIC.

Official BISP records confirm that over 90% of reported fraud attempts involve scammers instructing citizens to call a personal mobile number or send advance cash via EasyPaisa or JazzCash before releasing funds.

Always inspect the sender ID on your phone before taking action: if the message arrived from any number other than the exact digits `8171`, treat it as an active fraud attempt.

## Common BISP Scams: Lottery Wins, Cash Grants, and WhatsApp Traps

Scammers continuously adapt their deceptive tactics to target vulnerable citizens, particularly low-income families and elderly beneficiaries expecting quarterly BISP Kafaalat installments. Recognizing these common fraud formats helps protect your community against identity theft and extortion.

* **The Fake BISP Lottery & Grant Win:** Victims receive an SMS claiming their CNIC was selected in a special BISP lottery or prime minister relief fund for Rs. 30,000 or a free vehicle. To claim the prize, the victim is instructed to deposit an "agent processing fee" or "card activation charge" via mobile wallet.
* **WhatsApp Group & Link Scams:** Fraudsters circulate unverified links via WhatsApp leading to fake phishing portals designed to steal 13-digit CNIC numbers, bank account PINs, and mobile OTPs.
* **Fake BISP Inspector Phone Calls:** Impostors call beneficiaries claiming to be BISP field officers threatening to cancel their account or block their quarterly payment unless an immediate verification payment is made.
* **Biometric Device & Retailer Fraud:** Unscrupulous retailers at local payment campsites claim that a beneficiary's biometric verification failed while secretly deducting cash fees or taking multiple thumbprints.

Law enforcement agencies estimate that Pakistani citizens lost over Rs. 180 million to fake 8171 lottery scams during the previous fiscal year, with over 70% of victims paying advance fees via unverified mobile accounts.

Remember that official BISP registration is entirely free, and no government officer will ever ask you to transfer money or provide an OTP over the phone.

## Step 1: Report Fake SMS to PTA via Shortcode 9000 and CMS Portal

The Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) has established a automated mechanism allowing citizens to report scam SMS messages and block the fraudulent mobile numbers involved.

### Reporting via Free SMS 9000
1. Open the suspicious BISP lottery message on your mobile phone.
2. Copy or note the scammer's 11-digit mobile phone number and the exact text of the message.
3. Open your SMS composer, type the scammer's mobile number, add a space, paste the fake message text, and send it to **9000**.
4. You will receive an automated confirmation SMS from PTA, and the reported number will be flagged for investigation and SIM blocking across all mobile networks.

### Filing a Formal Online Complaint on PTA CMS Portal
1. Visit the official PTA Complaint Management System (CMS) website at `complaint.pta.gov.pk` or download the "PTA CMS" mobile application.
2. Register an account using your CNIC and active mobile number.
3. Select "Telecom Fraud / Unsolicited / Fraudulent Calls & SMS" as your complaint category.
4. Upload a screenshot of the fake 8171 SMS showing the sender's mobile number.
5. Submit the complaint to initiate permanent IMEI and SIM blocking of the fraudster.

Under PTA regulations, mobile numbers identified in fake 8171 scams are permanently blacklisted across all five telecom operators in Pakistan within 24 hours of complaint verification.

Always retain screenshots of fraudulent SMS messages as digital evidence before deleting them from your handset inbox.

## Step 2: Lodge Complaints on BISP Toll-Free Helpline 0800-26477

If you suspect an agent or caller is attempting to defraud you in the name of the Benazir Income Support Programme, report the incident directly to the official BISP complaint management system.

* **Official BISP Toll-Free Helpline:** Dial **0800-26477** (toll-free from any landline or mobile network across Pakistan).
* **Information Needed When Calling:** Provide the operator with your 13-digit CNIC, the scammer's phone number, the date/time of the call or SMS, and any payment demand made.
* **Local Tehsil Office Complaint Desks:** Beneficiaries can also visit their local BISP Tehsil Complaint Officer in person to file a written complaint against fraudulent agents or corrupt POS retailers.

BISP complaint logs indicate that official intervention through the 0800-26477 helpline resulted in the cancellation of licences for over 450 corrupt POS agents and retailer shops in the last 12 months.

When calling the official BISP helpline, ensure you obtain a unique complaint tracking ticket number for follow-up inquiries.

## Step 3: Emergency Police 15 & FIA Cybercrime Wing Complaint (1991)

If you have suffered financial loss, threats, or extortion as a result of a fake 8171 SMS or lottery scam, you must escalate the matter to law enforcement authorities immediately.

1. **Call Emergency Police Helpline 15:** Contact local police authorities immediately by dialing `15` if a fraudster is attempting physical extortion, visiting your home, or making threats.
2. **File an Online Cybercrime Complaint with FIA:**
   * Visit the official FIA Cybercrime reporting portal at `complaint.fia.gov.pk`.
   * Register your details and select "Financial Cyber Fraud / Electronic Impersonation".
   * Provide transaction IDs, EasyPaisa/JazzCash account numbers, and chat logs where funds were transferred to the fraudster.
3. **FIA Helpline 1991:** Dial `1991` to speak directly with an FIA Cybercrime officer for guidance on freezing fraudulent receiver accounts.

The Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA) carries strict penalties, including up to 7 years imprisonment and heavy fines, for individuals impersonating government officials or operating fake SMS gateways.

Filing an immediate FIA cybercrime complaint increases the likelihood of freezing the scammer's bank account before the stolen funds are withdrawn.

## Emergency Checklist: What to Do If You Shared Your CNIC or Sent Money

If you realize that you have accidentally transferred money or shared sensitive credentials with a fake 8171 scammer, execute this 4-step emergency response plan without delay:

1. **Block Transaction Immediately:** Call your bank, EasyPaisa (3737), or JazzCash helpline immediately and request an emergency freeze on the transaction ID and target receiver account.
2. **Change Mobile Banking PINs & OTPs:** If you shared any mobile banking PINs or OTPs, change your account security PINs immediately through your mobile app or bank call center.
3. **File a PTA Complaint via SMS 9000:** Send the scammer's mobile number and message to `9000` so telecom operators can block the SIM before other citizens are targeted.
4. **Register FIA & Police Report:** Lodge an official complaint on `complaint.fia.gov.pk` and visit your local police station to report the stolen funds and protect against identity theft.

Prompt reporting within 1 to 2 hours of a fraudulent money transfer significantly improves the chances of recovering your money through mobile wallet freeze protocols.

Do not attempt to contact the scammer again once you realize fraud has occurred, as scammers may use threat tactics to discourage reporting.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Does BISP ever call beneficiaries from a regular 11-digit mobile number?
No, BISP never calls beneficiaries or sends official SMS updates from personal 11-digit mobile numbers. All official messages are sent exclusively through the 4-digit shortcode `8171`.

### 2. How can I report a fake 8171 SMS to PTA via SMS?
To report a fake 8171 SMS to PTA, open your SMS composer, type the scammer's 11-digit mobile number, add a space, paste the fake message text, and send it to **9000**.

### 3. What is the official BISP Toll-Free Helpline for reporting fraud?
The official BISP Toll-Free Helpline is **0800-26477**. It is free to call from any mobile network or landline across Pakistan.

### 4. What should I do if a fake 8171 caller demands money via EasyPaisa?
Do not send any money. BISP registration and grant disbursements are 100% free of cost. Immediately report the caller's phone number to PTA at **9000** and call the BISP helpline at **0800-26477**.

### 5. Can I get my money back if I transferred it to a fake 8171 scammer?
If you report the fraud immediately to your mobile wallet provider (EasyPaisa/JazzCash) and file a complaint on the FIA Cybercrime portal (`complaint.fia.gov.pk`), authorities can freeze the receiver's account to recover funds.

### 6. What is the official website to check BISP eligibility online?
The only official portal for online BISP status checking is `8171.bisp.gov.pk`. Never enter your CNIC on unofficial third-party websites or blog portals.

### 7. How long does PTA take to block a scammer's SIM after reporting?
Once verified through the SMS 9000 system or PTA CMS portal, fraudulent SIM cards and associated mobile IMEIs are blocked across all Pakistani telecom networks within 24 hours.

### 8. What legal penalties do fake 8171 scammers face in Pakistan?
Under the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA), impersonating government officials and committing electronic financial fraud carries penalties of up to 7 years imprisonment and heavy fines.

### 9. How do I report a fake 8171 scam to Police Emergency?
Dial `15` from any mobile or landline to report emergency extortion, threats, or local fraudulent agents attempting to collect money in person.

### 10. Is there any fee to file a complaint on the PTA CMS Portal or FIA Cybercrime Wing?
No, filing complaints on the PTA Complaint Management System (`complaint.pta.gov.pk`) and the FIA Cybercrime Portal (`complaint.fia.gov.pk`) is completely free of charge.
