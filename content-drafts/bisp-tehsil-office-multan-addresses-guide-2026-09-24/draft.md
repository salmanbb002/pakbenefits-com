## Services Offered at Multan Dynamic Registration Centers

Each tehsil facility in Multan provides complete social safety net services at zero cost:
1. **NSER Dynamic Socioeconomic Survey:** In-person survey interviews to evaluate household Poverty Means Test scores.
2. **Benazir Taleemi Wazaif Registration:** Verification of school admission stamps and B-Forms to unlock quarterly education stipends for children.
3. **Benazir Nashonuma Nutrition Desks:** Specialized health checkups, specialized food supplements, and cash incentives for pregnant and lactating mothers.
4. **Biometric Identity Refresh:** Updating eroded thumbprints on NADRA-integrated scanners to resolve payment withdrawal errors.
5. **Beneficiary Account Correction:** Rectifying misspelled names, invalid CNIC linkage, and outdated mobile SIM records.
6. **Widow and Deceased Household Claims:** Transferring active Kafalat accounts to legitimate adult female heirs following a beneficiary's death.

## Step-by-Step Guide to Registering for BISP Dynamic Survey in Multan

Completing your registration at a Multan tehsil center follows a structured five-step workflow:

### Step 1: Collect Entry Token at the Main Gate
Present your original CNIC to the gate receptionist, who confirms your tehsil jurisdiction and issues a printed token number.

### Step 2: Seating in the Facilitation Hall
Take a seat in the shaded waiting area. Staff provide drinking water and priority seating for pregnant women and elderly applicants.

### Step 3: Household Roster Interview
When your token number appears on the display, proceed to the data entry booth. Provide verified details regarding family members, marital status, utility bills, livestock, and residence type.

### Step 4: Live Biometric Scanning
Scan your thumb and fingers on the NADRA biometric device to attach your digital signature to the household record.

### Step 5: Retain Survey Confirmation Token
The operator hands you a stamped dynamic survey confirmation slip. You can track your approval status via SMS from 8171 or through the online portal.

## Mandatory Documents Checklist for Multan Beneficiaries

Bring all original documents during your visit to prevent application rejection:
- **Original Computerised CNIC:** Issued by NADRA, valid and unexpired.
- **NADRA Child Registration Certificate (B-Form):** Required for all children up to 18 years old.
- **School Admission Slip:** Signed and stamped by the school principal (for Taleemi Wazaif applicants).
- **Mobile Phone with Personal SIM:** Registered against the female applicant's own CNIC.
- **Household Electricity Bill:** Most recent paid monthly consumer bill.
- **Disability Board Certificate:** If applying under the special persons social assistance quota.
- **NADRA Death Certificate of Spouse:** For widow status registration and inheritance transfers.

## How to Resolve Payment Blockages and Biometric Errors in Multan

If your payment has stopped or partner bank ATMs fail to read your fingerprints:
- **Submit a Biometric Exception Request:** Visit the Assistant Director's desk at your tehsil office to apply for a manual bank payment authorization slip.
- **Lodge Complaints via BISP Helpline:** Dial **0800-26477** (toll-free) to report unauthorized agent deductions or rude branch staff.
- **Escalate to Regional Headquarters:** For unresolved institutional disputes, submit a written appeal to the BISP Regional Office on Multan Public School Road, Shalimar Colony.
- **Verify Online Balance:** Check your active eligibility status on the official 8171 portal at [8171.bisp.gov.pk](https://8171.bisp.gov.pk/).

### How do I reach the Multan City BISP office using public transport?

Yes, all BISP survey, verification, and registration services are 100% free with no official or unofficial fee.

Offices in Shujabad and Jalalpur Pirwala operate Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Present your original CNIC, your child's NADRA B-Form, and a stamped school admission certificate at the Taleemi Wazaif desk.

Visit the BISP Divisional Headquarters at House No. 203, Multan Public School Road, New Shalimar Colony, Multan.

Visit your tehsil office to obtain a biometric exemption certificate or get your fingerprints updated at the tehsil NADRA counter.

### What time does token distribution begin at Multan BISP centers?
Token distribution begins at 7:45 AM at the main entrance gate on a first-come, first-served basis.

### Can an agent charge money for filling out the BISP survey form in Multan?
No, BISP data entry is performed exclusively by authorized government operators inside the office for free.

### How do I check if my quarterly Rs 13,500 payment has been released in Multan?
Send your 13-digit CNIC number to 8171 via SMS or check your status on the web portal at 8171.bisp.gov.pk.
