- **Canonical Name:** Benazir Income Support Programme
- **Aliases:** BISP, Benazir Kafalat, 8171 Scheme, BISP Ehsaas
- **Type:** GovernmentOrganization / Social Welfare Programme
- **sameAs URLs:**

  - `https://bisp.gov.pk`
- **Core Attributes & Relationships:**
  - Established: July 2008 by Government of Pakistan.
  - Ministry: Ministry of Poverty Alleviation and Social Safety (PASS).
  - Primary Mechanism: Unconditional Cash Transfer (UCT) through Benazir Kafalat.
  - Current Stipend: Rs. 13,500 to Rs. 14,500 per quarter (2026 cycle).
  - Disbursement Partners: Habib Bank Limited (HBL Konnect in Punjab, Sindh, Balochistan), Bank Alfalah (KPK, GB, AJK), biometric ATMs, and dedicated BISP campsites.
  - Verification Gateway: National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA).

### Core Entity 2: National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA)
- **Canonical Name:** National Database and Registration Authority
- **Aliases:** NADRA
- **Type:** GovernmentOrganization
- **sameAs URLs:**
  - `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Database_and_Registration_Authority`
  - `https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q6972076`
  - `https://www.nadra.gov.pk`
- **Core Attributes & Relationships:**
  - Role: Autonomous statutory authority maintaining the national civil registry and biometric database.
  - Verification Services: Citizen biometric identity matching (AFIS), Computerized National Identity Cards (CNIC), Smart National Identity Cards (SNIC).
  - Key Systems: Biometric Verification System (BVS), Live Facial Recognition System, National Socio-Economic Registry (NSER) data processing.

### Core Entity 3: Biometric Verification System (BVS)
- **Canonical Name:** Biometric Verification System
- **Aliases:** BVS, Fingerprint scanner, Thumbprint verification, Biometric POS
- **Type:** TechnicalProcess / ComputingPlatform
- **sameAs URLs:**
  - `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biometrics`
  - `https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q18789`
- **Core Attributes & Relationships:**
  - Function: Real-time optical/capacitive fingerprint scanning matched against NADRA templates.
  - Failure Triggers: Low ridge quality (<60%), epidermal wear (manual laborers/elderly), dermatological dryness, hardware optical distortion, latency timeouts.
  - Alternative Path: Live Facial Recognition System (introduced 2025/2026) and Non-BVS manual disbursement approval.
