## Step 2 — Head-entity research (non-competitor sources)

### Entity 1 — Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification (PAVE) Programme
- **Canonical name:** Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification Programme
- **Aliases / Acronyms:** PAVE Scheme, PAVE EV Scheme, PAVE Electric Bike Programme, پیو الیکٹرک بائیک اسکیم
- **Type:** Federal Government Electric Vehicle Subsidy and Concessional Financing Initiative
- **sameAs URLs:** https://pave.gov.pk/ ; https://www.edb.gov.pk/
- **Attributes / Core relationships:**
  - Executive Authority: Ministry of Industries and Production (MoIP), Government of Pakistan.
  - Implementing Agency: Engineering Development Board (EDB).
  - Primary Objective: Accelerate adoption of 2-wheeler and 3-wheeler Electric Vehicles (EVs) across Pakistan, reduce national fossil fuel import bills, curb urban smog/emissions, and lower daily commuting expenses for citizens.
  - Subsidy Envelope: Flat federal subsidy of up to **Rs. 80,000** per certified electric motorcycle/scooter; up to **Rs. 400,000** for electric rickshaws / 3-wheeler loaders.
  - Financing Structure: Concessional or 0% mark-up installment plans over 12 to 24 (or up to 36) months in partnership with authorized financial institutions and certified EV manufacturers.
  - Selection Mechanism: Phase 1 utilized computerized balloting; Phase 2 (2026) operates on a transparent **first-come, first-served** digital timestamp queue via `pave.gov.pk`.

### Entity 2 — Engineering Development Board (EDB)
- **Canonical name:** Engineering Development Board
- **Aliases:** EDB, ای ڈی بی
- **Type:** Government Regulatory & Technical Body
- **sameAs URLs:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering_Development_Board ; https://www.edb.gov.pk/
- **Attributes / Core relationships:**
  - Operates as the apex technical and industrial development agency under the Ministry of Industries and Production.
  - Responsible for formulating EV standards, evaluating vehicle roadworthiness, certifying local assembly lines, and publishing the official roster of EDB-approved EV manufacturers and models eligible for the PAVE subsidy.

### Entity 3 — Ministry of Industries and Production (MoIP)
- **Canonical name:** Ministry of Industries and Production
- **Aliases:** MoIP, وزارت صنعت و پیداوار
- **Type:** Federal Government Ministry
- **sameAs URLs:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Industries_and_Production_(Pakistan) ; https://www.moip.gov.pk/
- **Attributes / Core relationships:**
  - Formulates the National Electric Vehicle Policy (NEVP).
  - Secures federal budgetary allocations for EV consumer subsidies and industrial localization incentives.

### Supporting Entities:
- **National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA):** Validates CNICs, biometric profile matching, and domicile eligibility via API integration on `pave.gov.pk`. sameAs: https://www.nadra.gov.pk/
- **Approved EV Manufacturers & Brands:** Yadea Pakistan, Crown Micro / Crown Group, Vlektra Electric Motorcycles, Jolta Electric, Metro E-Vehicles, MS Jaguar, Road Prince Electric, Ecodost.
- **Participating Financial Institutions:** Commercial and Islamic partner banks (e.g., Bank of Punjab, Meezan Bank, National Bank of Pakistan) administering installment plans and direct subsidy deduction.

## Step 7 — Information-gain pass

### Competitor Shortcomings Identified:
1. **Conflation with CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme:** Many blogs confuse the Federal PAVE scheme (`pave.gov.pk`) with Maryam Nawaz's Punjab E-Bike Scheme (`bikes.punjab.gov.pk`). They quote Punjab student rules for federal applicants, creating widespread confusion.
2. **Outdated Balloting Claims:** Most competitors still claim that selection is done solely via randomized lottery balloting. They fail to report that Phase 2 (2026) operates on a transparent **first-come, first-served** digital timestamp queue.
3. **Vague Financial Savings:** Competitors make generic claims like "save a lot on petrol" without providing an exact kilowatt-hour (kWh) electricity unit cost calculation versus current petrol prices.
4. **Missing Battery Technology Clarity:** Competitors fail to explain the difference between Graphene lead-acid batteries (cheaper upfront, shorter lifespan) and Lithium-ion/LFP batteries (longer life, higher range) among approved models.

### Three Original Information-Gain Elements Committed:
1. **Definitive Comparison Matrix:** *Federal PAVE Scheme (`pave.gov.pk`) vs CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme (`bikes.punjab.gov.pk`)* (Eligibility, coverage, subsidy amount, target audience, and application portal).
2. **Monthly Economic ROI & Fuel Savings Matrix:** Exact mathematical breakdown comparing a 70cc petrol motorcycle (consuming 35–40 litres/month at ~Rs. 270/L = Rs. 9,500–11,000+) against a 2.5 kWh electric bike (consuming ~30–45 units of electricity at ~Rs. 65/unit = Rs. 1,950–2,925), yielding net savings of over Rs. 8,000 to Rs. 14,000+ every month.
3. **Step-by-Step Portal Navigation & Troubleshooting Blueprint:** Clear visual ASCII workflow and explicit solutions for common submission roadblocks (e.g., OTP delivery failures, learner permit verification, image compression under 500KB).
