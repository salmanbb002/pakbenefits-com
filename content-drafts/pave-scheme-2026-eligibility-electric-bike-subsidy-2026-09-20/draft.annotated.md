The **Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification (PAVE) Scheme 2026** provides a federal government subsidy of up to **Rs. 80,000** for **electric motorcycles** and up to **Rs. 400,000** for **electric rickshaws**. ## What Is the PAVE Scheme 2026 in Pakistan?

The **Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification (PAVE) Scheme** is a federal green mobility initiative designed to transition the country's two-wheeler and three-wheeler transport fleet to electric power. Formulated under the **Ministry of Industries and Production (MoIP)** and executed through the **Engineering Development Board (EDB)**, the program provides direct price subsidies and **interest-free installment options** to make electric motorbikes accessible to everyday commuters, students, and gig economy workers.

By substituting expensive imported petrol with locally generated electricity, the scheme addresses Pakistan's urban air quality crisis while reducing household transportation expenses by up to 80%. Similar to national welfare structures like the [BISP eligibility criteria guide](/bisp-eligibility-criteria-guide/) and federal energy relief programs such as the [fuel relief scheme guide](/fuel-scheme-rs-100-per-litre-petrol-relief-registration-guide-2026-09-19/), PAVE operates through strict biometric and identity checks to ensure equitable public resource distribution. To view how this fits into the wider social support landscape, explore our directory of [other government support schemes in Pakistan](/other-schemes/).

### The National EV Policy (NEVP) & Role of the Engineering Development Board (EDB)

The **PAVE program** operates as the operational consumer arm of the revised **National Electric Vehicle Policy (NEVP 2025–2030)**. While earlier policy drafts prioritized four-wheeler passenger vehicle tax exemptions, the 2026 framework concentrates public funds where 82% of national road traffic operates: motorcycles and auto-rickshaws.

The **Engineering Development Board (EDB)** serves as the apex regulatory and technical authority for the program. The EDB inspects manufacturing plants, certifies vehicle roadworthiness, validates battery standards, and compiles the official roster of **approved EV manufacturers**. Only electric two-wheelers assembled by EDB-certified manufacturers who meet local content requirements qualify for the federal **electric bike subsidy (Rs 80,000)** envelope.

### Key 2026 Shift: Transition from Balloting to First-Come, First-Served Selection

The most significant structural update in the 2026 rollout is the complete discontinuation of computerized lottery balloting. In previous pilot phases, applicants waited months for randomized draws that left thousands of eligible commuters empty-handed.

Phase 2 of the PAVE scheme establishes a transparent, timestamped **first-come, first-served selection** queue managed directly through **`pave.gov.pk`**. Applications that pass automated **NADRA** identity validation and document review are approved strictly in the order they are submitted until the budgetary quota is exhausted. This shift rewards prompt, accurate submissions and eliminates administrative discretion in vehicle allocation.

## What Is the Electric Bike Subsidy Amount and Financial Relief Under PAVE?

The federal government disburses this subsidy directly to authorized manufacturers and partner dealerships, deducting the full amount upfront from the consumer's invoice price.

Rather than receiving cash or a delayed bank rebate, the buyer pays only the net discounted price at the showroom or finances the balance through subsidized **bank installment plans**. The subsidy covers between 25% and 35% of the total ex-factory cost of standard commuter electric bikes, dramatically lowering the financial barrier to clean mobility.

For standard two-wheelers, the **Rs. 80,000 subsidy** applies to all certified models equivalent to 70cc, 100cc, and 125cc petrol motorcycles. When an applicant selects an approved electric bike priced at Rs. 240,000, the invoice sent to the financing bank or payment portal is automatically adjusted to Rs. 160,000.

This discount structure protects consumers from dealership markups and artificial price inflation. The **EDB** sets ex-factory price ceilings for participating manufacturers, preventing assemblers from increasing retail rates prior to applying the government incentive.

### Concessional & 0% Markup Installment Plans via Partner Banks

Applicants who do not wish to purchase their electric vehicle through a single upfront cash payment can opt for **installment financing & 0% markup plans**. Participating commercial and Islamic banks—including the Bank of Punjab (BOP), Meezan Bank, and National Bank of Pakistan (NBP)—offer structured repayment tenors ranging from 12 to 36 months.

Under dedicated youth and student financing agreements, the federal government absorbs the benchmark KIBOR markup, allowing borrowers to repay the principal in equal monthly installments with **zero percent interest**. For general public applicants, partner institutions provide fixed, single-digit concessionary rates significantly below commercial market borrowing costs.

## Federal PAVE Scheme vs CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme: What Are the Key Differences?

The federal **PAVE Scheme** and the **CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme** are two distinct government transport initiatives with different jurisdictions, target demographics, and portal systems. While PAVE is a nationwide program open to all Pakistani citizens, the CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme is a provincial program restricted exclusively to students enrolled in Punjab.

Conflating these two schemes leads to common application errors, such as Punjab students applying on the federal portal expecting provincial terms, or non-Punjab residents attempting to register on the provincial portal. For those interested in broader provincial enterprise funding, review the [CM Punjab Rozgar Scheme](/cm-punjab-rozgar-scheme/). The table below outlines the core operational differences:

| Comparison Feature | Federal PAVE Scheme 2026 | CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| **Governing Authority** | **Ministry of Industries & Production (MoIP) / EDB** | Government of the Punjab (Transport & Higher Education Depts) |
| **Geographic Scope** | **Nationwide (Punjab, Sindh, KPK, Balochistan, AJK, GB, ICT)** | Punjab Province Only |
| **Target Beneficiaries** | **All Citizens (Students, Salaried, Gig Riders, General Public)** | Regular Students of Colleges & Universities Only |
| **Eligible Age Window** | **18 to 65 Years** | 18 Years and above (with active student enrollment) |
| **Vehicle Types Included** | **Electric Bikes, Electric Rickshaws, Cargo Loaders** | Electric Bikes & Petrol Motorbikes (Split Quotas) |
| **Subsidy Mechanism** | **Flat upfront subsidy (Rs. 80,000 for bikes; Rs. 400,000 for rickshaws)** | Down payment subsidy + Government-absorbed interest & insurance |
| **Selection Method** | **First-Come, First-Served Digital Timestamp Queue** | Computerized Balloting across Punjab Districts |
| **Official Application Portal** | **`pave.gov.pk`** | **`bikes.punjab.gov.pk`** |

## Who Is Eligible for the PAVE Electric Bike Scheme 2026?

**Eligibility criteria** for the **PAVE Electric Bike Scheme** requires Pakistani citizenship, a verifiable identity record with **NADRA**, an **age between 18 and 65 years**, and a **valid driving license or learner permit**. The scheme is open to all occupational backgrounds, including private sector employees, self-employed individuals, students, and housewives.

Applicants must possess an active mobile SIM card registered in their own name and **CNIC** to receive one-time authentication passwords (**OTPs**). The application portal verifies every CNIC against national registries in real time to enforce the statutory rule of one subsidized vehicle per citizen.

*Unused Tier 3 terms in this section: None.*

### Core Eligibility Criteria: Age, CNIC, and Driving License Rules

To qualify for an electric motorcycle under PAVE, an individual must fulfill four mandatory statutory conditions:

1. **Pakistani Citizenship:** The applicant must hold a valid 13-digit Computerized National Identity Card (**CNIC**) or Smart National Identity Card (**SNIC**).
2. **Age Threshold:** The applicant must be at least **18 years of age** and not older than **65 years** on the date of submission.
3. **Driving Credentials:** The applicant must possess either a permanent motorcycle **driving license** issued by the Traffic Police or a valid computerized **Learner Driving Permit**.
4. **Clean Subsidy Record:** The applicant must not have previously received a vehicle subsidy under any other federal or provincial transport scheme.

While PAVE is accessible to the general public, the program assigns priority processing queues to three high-impact demographic groups:

- **University & College Students:** Young adults attending accredited institutions who require affordable daily commuting options. Students can upload their institutional enrollment card to enter the expedited student processing window.
- **Gig Economy Delivery Riders:** Food delivery personnel, courier riders, and ride-hailing operators (e.g., Bykea, Foodpanda, Yango) who log high daily mileage. For these riders, switching to an electric bike directly increases take-home earnings by eliminating daily petrol expenses.
- **Low-to-Middle Income Commuters:** Salaried private-sector employees and small shopkeepers spending more than 20% of their monthly income on fuel and motorcycle maintenance.

### Disqualification Criteria: Who Cannot Apply?

Federal program regulations disqualify applicants under the following circumstances:

- **Expired CNIC:** Applications submitted with an expired identity card are rejected automatically by the **NADRA** API validation check.
- **Prior Vehicle Subsidy Beneficiaries:** Anyone who has already acquired a subsidized motorcycle through the **CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme** or prior government transport initiatives is ineligible.
- **Institutional Credit Defaulters:** Applicants seeking bank installment plans who have active defaults or write-offs listed in the State Bank of Pakistan's **Electronic Credit Information Bureau (e-CIB)** cannot be approved for financing.
- **Multiple Submissions Under One CNIC:** Submitting duplicate applications across different vehicle categories results in immediate cancellation of all entries.

### Battery Technology Breakdown: Lithium-ion (LFP) vs Graphene Lead-Acid

Choosing the right electric bike requires understanding the differences between the two **battery technologies** approved under the PAVE program:

1. **Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4 / LFP) Batteries:**
   - **Lifespan:** 1,500 to 2,000 charge cycles (equivalent to 5 to 7 years of daily commuting).
   - **Weight:** Lightweight and compact, delivering superior acceleration and hill-climbing torque.
   - **Charging Time:** Fast charging capable, reaching 80% capacity within 2.5 to 3.5 hours.
   - **Cost Profile:** Higher initial purchase price, but significantly lower long-term replacement costs.

2. **Graphene Lead-Acid Batteries:**
   - **Lifespan:** 600 to 800 charge cycles (approximately 2 to 3 years of regular use).
   - **Weight:** Heavier than lithium packs, requiring robust chassis reinforcement.
   - **Charging Time:** Standard home charging requiring 6 to 8 hours for a full charge from a standard **220V AC wall socket**.
   - **Cost Profile:** Highly economical upfront price, making them the preferred choice for budget-restricted buyers under the **Rs. 80,000 subsidy**.

## Common Application Mistakes and How to Fix Them

Thousands of PAVE applications encounter processing delays or rejections due to preventable administrative mistakes during the online registration process. Understanding these common pitfalls ensures your application moves smoothly through automated verification.

*Unused Tier 3 terms in this section: None.*

### OTP Not Received on Mobile Number

The most frequent issue reported by applicants is failing to receive the 6-digit **SMS OTP** during initial account creation. This problem occurs when the mobile number provided is ported from its original network (e.g., converted from Jazz to Zong) or is registered under a family member's CNIC rather than the applicant's own name.

**How to Fix It:** Ensure the SIM card is registered strictly under your own 13-digit **CNIC**. If you use a converted network number, send an unblocking SMS or temporarily use an unported mobile number registered to your identity. If the OTP does not arrive within 120 seconds, click the "Resend OTP via Call / WhatsApp" option on the portal.

*Unused Tier 3 terms in this section: None.*

### Driving License & Learner Permit Rejection

Applications are frequently placed on hold because the uploaded driving credential is blur, expired, or issued for a car only (LTV without motorcycle endorsement).

**How to Fix It:** Verify that your **driving license** explicitly includes the "Motorcycle" category. If you are using a **Learner Permit**, ensure that the permit has not lapsed beyond its standard 6-month validity window. Renew expired learner permits via the Punjab DLIMS, Sindh Driving License portal, or your local Traffic Police licensing branch before uploading the document.

*Unused Tier 3 terms in this section: None.*

### Document File Size Exceeded (500KB Limit)

The **`pave.gov.pk`** portal strictly enforces a maximum upload file size of 500KB per image (JPEG, PNG, or PDF). Attempting to upload high-resolution smartphone photos (often 3MB to 8MB) causes the submission form to freeze or return a generic upload error.

**How to Fix It:** Use a free online image compression tool or take a screenshot of the photo on your phone to reduce its file size below 400KB before uploading. Ensure all text on your CNIC and license remains sharp, legible, and uncropped.

*Unused Tier 3 terms in this section: None.*

## Official Warning: How to Avoid Fake PAVE Subsidy Scams

Thousands of fraudulent social media accounts, fake SMS notifications, and deceptive WhatsApp groups target citizens eager to receive a subsidized electric bike. Guarding your personal identity and finances requires adherence to strict official protocols.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Essential Anti-Scam Rules for All Applicants:**
> - **No Advance Fee Demands:** The Government of Pakistan, the **Ministry of Industries & Production (MoIP)**, and the **Engineering Development Board (EDB)** **never** demand registration fees, advance processing charges, or courier delivery fees through personal JazzCash, EasyPaisa, UPaisa, or private individual bank accounts.
> - **Exclusively Official Portal:** The only authentic digital registration portal is **`https://pave.gov.pk`**. Never input your **CNIC**, mobile phone number, or bank details into third-party portals ending in `.com`, `.org`, `.xyz`, or unverified blog domains.
> - **Zero Middlemen or Quota Agents:** No individual, dealer agent, or social media administrator possesses authorized quota to guarantee vehicle approval. Selection in Phase 2 is governed strictly by the transparent, timestamped digital queue on the official portal.
> - **Report Fraudulent Activity:** If anyone contacts you claiming to be a PAVE representative requesting money or private passwords, report the mobile number and evidence immediately to the **FIA National Cyber Crime Wing (NCCW)** via helpline **1991** or at `complaint.fia.gov.pk`.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does PAVE stand for in Pakistan?
PAVE stands for **Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification**. It is a federal government program administered by the **Ministry of Industries and Production (MoIP)** and the **Engineering Development Board (EDB)** to promote electric two-wheelers and three-wheelers through direct subsidies.

### How much subsidy does the government provide on electric bikes under PAVE 2026?
The federal government provides a flat subsidy of up to **Rs. 80,000** for certified electric motorcycles and up to **Rs. 400,000** for electric rickshaws. This financial relief is deducted directly from the ex-factory retail price at the time of purchase.

### What is the official website to apply for the PAVE scheme?
The only official portal for the program is **`pave.gov.pk`**. Applicants should avoid unofficial third-party websites, social media links, or WhatsApp groups claiming to process registrations or collect fees.

### Can students apply for the federal PAVE scheme?
Yes, students aged 18 and older are fully eligible to apply for the federal PAVE scheme on `pave.gov.pk`. In addition, students benefit from priority application review by uploading their university or college enrollment card.

### Is a driving license mandatory to apply for an electric bike under PAVE?
Yes, applicants must possess a valid motorcycle **driving license** or an active computerized **Learner Driving Permit**. Applications submitted without valid driving credentials cannot proceed past the verification stage.

### Can citizens living outside Punjab apply for the PAVE scheme?
Yes, the PAVE scheme is a nationwide federal initiative open to residents of all provinces and territories, including Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan, Islamabad Capital Territory, Azad Jammu & Kashmir, and Gilgit-Baltistan.

### How are applicants selected in Phase 2 of the PAVE scheme?
Phase 2 operates on a **first-come, first-served** digital timestamp queue, replacing the randomized lottery balloting used in earlier phases. Applicants whose documents are validated by **NADRA** are approved in the chronological order of their submission.

### Can one person apply for multiple electric bikes under one CNIC?
No, the scheme strictly limits each citizen to one subsidized vehicle per CNIC. The portal's **NADRA** integration blocks duplicate submissions across all vehicle categories.

### Do applicants receive the Rs. 80,000 subsidy as cash in their bank accounts?
No, the subsidy is not disbursed as cash to individuals. Instead, the government pays the Rs. 80,000 directly to the certified manufacturer or dealership, lowering the invoice price the buyer pays or finances.

### Are interest-free installment plans available for PAVE electric bikes?
Yes, partner commercial and Islamic banks offer **0% markup installment plans** over 12 to 36 months for eligible applicants, with the government covering the borrowing markup under youth and green financing allocations.

### What is the average riding range and battery lifespan of PAVE electric bikes?
Approved electric bikes deliver between 70 and 100+ kilometres per charge depending on battery size. Graphene lead-acid batteries last 2 to 3 years, while advanced **Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP)** batteries provide 5 to 7 years of reliable daily service.

### Can government employees apply for the PAVE electric bike subsidy?
Yes, regular government and semi-government employees are permitted to apply for the PAVE scheme, provided they meet the age and driving license criteria. However, priority processing is emphasized for private-sector commuters, students, and gig delivery workers.
