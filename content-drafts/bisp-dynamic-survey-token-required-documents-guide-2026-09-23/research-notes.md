## Step 2 — Head-Entity Research
1. **National Socio-Economic Registry (NSER)**
   - Type: Government Database / Household Registry
   - Organization: Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP)
   - Role: Central socio-economic census database calculating PMT (Proxy Means Test) scores for social protection eligibility.
2. **BISP Dynamic Registration Center / Tehsil Desk**
   - Type: Government Office / Registration Facility
   - Role: Permanent desks established at over 600+ Tehsil BISP offices nationwide for dynamic real-time household data entry.
3. **Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC)**
   - Type: Identity Document / NADRA Product
   - SameAs: `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNIC_(Pakistan)`
   - Role: Mandatory baseline identity document for female applicant and spouse.
4. **NADRA Child Registration Certificate (CRC / B-Form)**
   - Type: Identity Document
   - Role: Mandatory document proving family composition and linking children under 18 for Taleemi Wazaif and household size calculation.
