# BISP Dynamic Survey Ke Liye Kon Se Documents Chahiye: Tehsil Desk Timing Aur Token System Guide (2026)

**BISP Dynamic Survey** ke liye asal **CNIC**, tamam bachon ka **NADRA B-Form** (CRC), haaliya **bijli ya gas ka utility bill**, aur registered **mobile SIM** zaroori hain. Bewa khawateen ke liye shohar ka **Death Certificate** aur mazoor afraad ke liye **Wheelchair CNIC** lazmi hai. **Tehsil office desk** Pir ta Juma subah **8:00 baje se shaam 4:00 baje** tak khula hota hai.

## BISP Dynamic Survey Ke Liye Kon Se Documents Chahiye? (Direct Checklist)

**Benazir Income Support Programme** (**BISP**) aur **National Socio-Economic Registry** (**NSER**) ke dynamic survey mein shamil hone ke liye mustahiq khawateen ko official **Tehsil Registration Desk** par jana parta hai. Ghalat ya na-mukammal dastawizat le jane ki soorat mein registration token jari nahi kiya jata aur survey mukammal nahi ho pata.

**Dynamic survey** mukammal taur par computerized hai jahan **data entry operator** aap ke gharane ki aamdani, afrad ki tadaad, zameeni jaidad, aur bijli ke kharche ka andaza laga kar **PMT (Proxy Means Test) score** generate karta hai. Survey karwane se pehle darj zail asal dastawizat (Original Documents) ka sath hona lazmi hai.

*Section coverage: BISP, NSER, Tehsil Registration Desk, PMT Score, Data Entry Operator.*

## Category-Wise Zaroori Documents Ki Mukammal List

BISP registration desk par mukhtalif categories ke liye makhsoos qanooni asnaad talab kiye jate hain. Neeche diye gaye table mein har category ke mutabiq zaroori dastawizat ki wazahat mojood hai:

| Applicant Category | Lazmi Asnaad (Mandatory Documents) | Zaroori Sharat / Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Shadi-Shuda Khawateen** (General Married) | Asal **CNIC** (Khatoon + Shohar), Tamam Bachon Ke **NADRA B-Form**, Ghar Ka **Bijli Bill** | CNIC expire na ho, B-Form par tamam bachay darj hon |
| **Bewa Khawateen** (Widows) | Asal **CNIC** (Bewa Status Update), Shohar Ka **NADRA Death Certificate**, B-Form | NADRA record mein shohar ki wafaat update hona lazmi hai |
| **Talaq-Yafta Khawateen** (Divorced Women) | Asal **CNIC** (Divorced Status Update), Union Council **Talaq Nama**, Bachon Ka B-Form | NADRA mein marital status 'Mutalaqa' darj hona chahiye |
| **Mazoor Afraad** (Persons with Disabilities) | **Special Disability CNIC** (Wheelchair Logo), Medical Board Certificate | NADRA se jari shuda mazoor shanakhti card |
| **Khwajasara** (Transgender Community) | Khwajasara Category Ka Asal **CNIC** (Guru Registration Slip Optional) | NADRA record mein Gender field update ho |
| **Ghair-Shadi-Shuda Yateem Larki** | Asal **CNIC**, Walidain Dono Ke Death Certificates, Guardian FRC | Dono walidain ki wafaat ka sarkari certificate |

### Shadi-Shuda Khawateen (General Married Women) Ke Liye Asnaad

1. **Asal Computerized Shanakhti Card (CNIC)**: Khatoon ka apna computerized shanakhti card aur shohar ka **CNIC**. Card ki validity active honi chahiye (expired na ho).
2. **NADRA B-Form (Child Registration Certificate)**: Gharane mein mojood tamam 18 saal se kam umar bachon ke **NADRA** se tasdeeq shuda **B-Forms**. Agar bachay school padhte hain toh unke B-Form par **Taleemi Wazaif** ka ilhaaq bhi hoga.
3. **Haaliya Bijli / Gas Ka Bill**: Ghar ke istemal mein mojood bijli ka aakhri mahine ka paid ya unpaid **bill**. Bill par darj Consumer ID aur meter units survey mein enter kiye jate hain. Agar kiraye ke makaan mein rehte hain toh bhi wahi bill chalega.
4. **Active Mobile SIM**: Aisi **mobile SIM** jo khatoon ya unke shohar ke naam par registered ho aur chal rahi ho taake BISP ka **8171 confirmation SMS** masool ho sake.

### Bewa Khawateen (Widows) Ke Liye Zaroori Dastawizat

Bewa khawateen ke liye BISP mein khas tarjeeh di jati hai aur unka **PMT score cutoff** aasan hota hai. Inke liye darj zail dastawizat lazmi hain:
- Khatoon ka apna CNIC jis par Marital Status **"Bewa" (Widow)** update shuda ho.
- Shohar ka **NADRA Computerized Death Certificate** (Union Council ka computerized slip).
- Tamam yateem bachon ke **NADRA B-Forms**.

### Talaq-Yafta (Divorced Women) Ke Liye Documents

Talaq-yafta khawateen ko alag household count kiya jata hai:
- CNIC jis par status "Divorced" update ho.
- Union Council se jari shuda **Talaq Certificate (Divorce Deed)**.
- Zaili bachon ke **B-Forms** jo khatoon ki kafalat mein hon.

### Mazoor Afraad (PWDs) Aur Khwajasara Ke Liye Special Documents

Hakoomat ne mazoor afraad aur transgender community ke liye PMT score ki pabandi kafi had tak narm ki hai:
- **Special CNIC**: NADRA se jari shuda aisa shanakhti card jis par **Wheelchair** ka nishan bana ho.
- **District Medical Board Disability Certificate**: Sarkari hospital ke medical board ka jari shuda certificate jis par disability percentage darj ho.

## NSER Dynamic Survey Ka Token Lene Aur Registration Ka Step-by-Step Tarika

Tehsil office mein **dynamic survey** karwane ka amal darj zail 4 marahil par mushtamil hota hai:

### Marhala 1: Entry Gate Aur Shanakhti Card Screening
Tehsil office ke entry counter par apna **asal CNIC** staff ko dikhayen. Staff system mein check karega ke aap ka pichla survey 3 saal purana ho chuka hai ya aap pehli baar aayi hain.

### Marhala 2: Automated Token Generation
Screening clear hone ke baad token issuing counter se aap ko ek computerized **NSER Token Slip** milegi jis par aap ka Token Number aur Counter Number darj hoga. Mazoor afraad, bewaon aur umer raseeda khawateen ke liye **Priority Green Tokens** jari kiye jate hain taake unhein lambi line mein na khara hona pare.

### Marhala 3: Data Entry Desk Par Survey Interview
Apna token number call hone par muta-alliqa **Data Entry Operator (DEO)** ke desk par tashreef le jayen. Operator aap ke tamam asal asnaad (CNIC, B-Forms, Bijli Bill) verify karega aur computer software mein aap ke gharane ka data enter karega.

### Marhala 4: Biometric Angootha Aur Survey Slip Ki Wasooli
Data entry mukammal hone ke baad biometric device par aap ke **angoothe ka nishan** liya jayega aur operator aap ko ek **NSER Survey Confirmation Slip** print karke dega. Is slip par aap ka Token ID, CNIC aur survey tareekh darj hoti hai.

## Survey Ke Dauran Operator Kon Se Sawalat Poochta Hai? (Asset & Income Questions)

Dynamic survey software gharane ke **PMT score** ka taaiyun karne ke liye darj zail ahem sawalat poochta hai:

1. **Ghar Ki Sakht Aur Kamray**: Makaan zati hai ya kiraye ka? Paka makaan hai ya kacha? Ghar mein kul kitne kamray hain aur washroom ki sahulat kaisi hai?
2. **Gharana Ke Afrad Aur Taleem**: Kul kitne afrad rehte hain? Bachon ki umar aur school jane ki tafseelat.
3. **Maweshi Aur Zameeni Jaidad**: Kya aap ke paas koi zameeni raqba, kheti baari, ya maweshi (gaye, bhains, bakri) hain?
4. **Gharelu Ashiya (Household Assets)**: Ghar mein fridge, washing machine, solar panel, bike, ya AC mojood hai ya nahi?
5. **Mahana Bijli Ka Kharcha**: Bijli ke bill par kitne units aate hain aur mahana aamdani ka andaza kitna hai?
6. **Beroon-e-Mulk Safar**: Kya ghar ka koi fard foreign country (Gulf/Europe) gaya hai?

Hamesha sach aur darust maloomat faraham karein, kyunke NADRA aur FBR database se data cross-check kiya jata hai.

## Survey Slip Sambhalne Aur 8171 Par Status Check Karne Ka Amal

Survey mukammal hone ke baad milne wali **Confirmation Receipt** ko intihai hifazat se rakhein. 

Survey ka data process hone aur **PMT score** calculate hone mein taqreeban **20 se 45 din** ka waqt lagta hai. Is arsay ke baad aap **8171.bisp.gov.pk** web portal par apna CNIC number enter karke check kar sakte hain ke aap ki Kafaalat qist jari hui hai ya nahi.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Kya BISP Dynamic Survey karwane ke liye koi fees ada karni parti hai?
Nahi, BISP dynamic survey aur token system Hakoomat-e-Pakistan ki taraf se 100% muft hai. Agar koi shakhs token dilwane ya jaldi survey karwane ke paise maangay toh fauri helpline 0800-26477 par report karein.

### Agar bachon ka B-Form na bana ho toh kya survey ho sakta hai?
Nahi, 18 saal se kam umar bachon ke B-Form ke baghair survey mukammal nahi hota aur bachon ke Taleemi Wazaif bhi jari nahi ho sakte. Pehle NADRA se B-Form banwayen phir survey ke liye jayen.

### Kya bijli ka bill zati makaan ka hona zaroori hai?
Nahi, agar aap kiraye ke ghar mein rehte hain toh makan-malik ke naam ka mojooda bijli bill bhi qabil-e-qabool hai. Survey mein sirf consumer number aur usage units note kiye jate hain.

### BISP Tehsil Office ka token counter kis waqt band hota hai?
Aam dinon (Peer ta Jumerat) mein token counter dopahar 01:00 baje band ho jata hai jabke Juma ke roz subah 11:30 baje token generation band ho jati hai.

### Kya shohar ka CNIC sath le jana zaroori hai?
Ji haan, shadi-shuda khatoon ke liye shohar ka asal CNIC ya uski wazeh copy hona lazmi hai taake shohar ka status aur aamdani system mein link ho sake.

### Bewa khatoon ke liye kaun sa death certificate lagta hai?
Bewa khatoon ke liye NADRA ka computerized Death Certificate lagta hai. Purana ya ghair-computerized parcha qabil-e-qabool nahi hota.

### BISP dynamic survey ke baad kitne dinon mein paise milte hain?
Dynamic survey ke baad data verification aur PMT score nikalne mein 3 se 6 hafte (20-45 din) lagte hain. Agar PMT score 32 se kam aaye toh agli sah-mahi qist jari kar di jati hai.

### Kya Saturday aur Sunday ko BISP dynamic survey desk khula hota hai?
Nahi, Saturday aur Sunday ko tamam sarkari BISP tehsil offices aur registration desks band hote hain.

### Agar shanakhti card expire ho chuka ho toh kya survey hoga?
Nahi, expire CNIC par system data accept nahi karta. Pehle NADRA office se apna CNIC renew karwayen aur phir BISP desk tashreef le jayen.

### Agar kisi khatoon ke biometric fingerprints ghis chuke hon toh survey kaise hoga?
Agar biometric scan fail ho jaye toh operator manual system override aur live webcam tasveer ke zariye dynamic survey complete karta hai.
