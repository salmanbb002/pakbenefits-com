## Step 7 — Information-Gain Pass

### Identified Competitor Flaws & Gaps
1. **The Study Location Misconception:** Many applicants assume the scholarship funds overseas education in Scotland. Competitors only mention "in Pakistan" in passing without explicitly correcting this widespread myth in a dedicated section.
2. **Hostel vs. Day Scholar Benefit Clarification:** Competitors do not clearly distinguish what day scholars receive (tuition only) versus hostel boarders (tuition + hostel charges + 2x annual travel stipend).
3. **Sub-discipline Breakdown:** Competitors list broad subject titles ("STEM", "Health Sciences") without specifying accepted sub-disciplines like Biotechnology, Public Health, Environmental Engineering, or Data Science.
4. **Document Preparation Guide:** Competitors list document names without explaining how to handle edge cases (e.g. self-employed parent income proof, guardian death certificate, or pending semester admission letters).
