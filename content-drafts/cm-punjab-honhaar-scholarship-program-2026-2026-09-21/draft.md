# CM Punjab Honhaar Merit Scholarship Program 2026: Online Apply, Eligibility & 100% Tuition Fee Guide

To apply online for the CM Punjab Honhaar Merit Scholarship Program in 2026, register on the official portal at **honhaarscholarship.punjabhec.gov.pk** using your CNIC/B-Form and academic registration details. Administered by the Punjab Higher Education Commission (PHEC), the initiative provides **100% full tuition fee coverage** for 30,000 meritorious undergraduate students enrolled in 68 priority disciplines across 50 public sector universities, medical colleges (MBBS/BDS), and selected private institutions, targeting families with a monthly income under **Rs. 300,000**.

---

## What Is the CM Punjab Honhaar Merit Scholarship Program 2026?

The Chief Minister Punjab Honhaar Merit Scholarship Program is a historic higher education funding initiative introduced by Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif to eliminate financial barriers for high-achieving undergraduate students. With an allocated budget of Rs. 130 Billion over an eight-year cycle, the program awards 30,000 comprehensive scholarships annually to students admitted to accredited universities and professional degree colleges across Punjab.

Unlike conventional partial fee waivers or loan schemes, the Honhaar Scholarship provides an unconditional 100% tuition grant. Approved funds are disbursed directly by the Punjab Higher Education Commission (PHEC) to the recipient's university treasury, ensuring that students face zero out-of-pocket tuition costs throughout their standard four-year (BS) or five-year (MBBS/Pharm-D) degree tenures.

---

## Financial Coverage and Scholarship Benefits

The Honhaar Scholarship is designed as an end-to-end academic grant covering the total instructional expense of undergraduate education:

- **100% Tuition Fee Waiver:** Full coverage of standard semester or annual academic tuition fees billed by the university.
- **Direct Institutional Settlement:** PHEC transfers funds directly to the participating higher education institution; students are never required to pay tuition upfront and seek reimbursement.
- **Multi-Year Degree Security:** Once selected, the scholarship remains active for the full prescribed duration of the degree (4 years for BS programs and 5 years for medical/dental degrees), subject to standard academic renewal criteria.
- **Quota Distribution:** Scholarships are distributed equitably based on academic discipline, district population weighting, and gender representation.

## Eligibility Criteria for the Honhaar Scholarship 2026

To qualify for the CM Punjab Honhaar Merit Scholarship, applicants must fulfill strict territorial, demographic, and educational standards:

- **Punjab Domicile:** The applicant must be a citizen of Pakistan possessing a valid domicile from any district within the province of Punjab.
- **Age Limit:** Must not exceed 22 years of age on the final application closing date.
- **Household Income Limit:** Total monthly family income from all sources must not exceed **Rs. 300,000** (supported by verified salary slips, pension records, or an attested income affidavit).
- **Academic Enrollment:** Must be freshly enrolled or currently studying in an approved undergraduate BS, MBBS, or BDS program at a recognized institution.
- **Minimum Academic Cutoff:**
  - Public Sector Universities: Minimum 75% marks in Intermediate (FSc/FA/ICS/I.Com) or equivalent.
  - Public Medical Colleges: Merit admission in MBBS or BDS through UHS.
  - Top-Tier Private Universities: Minimum 80% marks in Intermediate.
- **Non-Duplication Clause:** The applicant must not be in receipt of any other major financial scholarship (such as HEC Need-Based or PEEF) exceeding standard pocket allowances.

## Step-by-Step Online Application on honhaarscholarship.punjabhec.gov.pk

Follow these exact steps to complete your registration on the provincial portal:

### Step 1: Portal Account Registration
Visit the official application portal at **https://honhaarscholarship.punjabhec.gov.pk/**. Click on "Apply Online". Enter your CNIC or B-Form number, email address, and active mobile number. Create a secure password and verify your email through the automated link.

### Step 2: Academic Profile & Matric/Inter Marks Entry
Enter your secondary (Matriculation) and higher secondary (Intermediate / FSc / ICS / A-Levels) examination marks, roll numbers, passing years, and education boards. The portal cross-verifies board data through automated BISE API integrations.

### Step 3: University Enrollment Verification
Select your enrolled university from the drop-down menu, choose your current campus, specify your degree discipline (e.g., BS Computer Science), and input your official University Student Registration / Roll Number.

### Step 4: Income Declaration & Document Upload
Provide the employment details and monthly income of your parents/guardian. Upload clear PDF/JPG scans of the required documents:
- Applicant's CNIC / B-Form and Father's/Guardian's CNIC.
- Punjab Domicile Certificate.
- Intermediate (FSc/ICS) Marks Sheet / Result Card.
- University Admission Fee Challan or Student ID Card.
- Income Certificate / Salary Slip / Attested Income Affidavit (stamp paper).

### Step 5: Submission & Verification by Institutional Focal Person
Carefully review all entries and submit your application. The system sends your file to your university's designated Honhaar Scholarship Focal Person for physical scrutiny and enrollment confirmation.

## Merit List Preparation, Scrutiny & Award Confirmation

The selection protocol ensures transparent meritocracy without discretionary quotas:

1. **Institutional Verification:** University focal persons cross-check each applicant's admission status, academic scores, and attendance standing.
2. **Central Merit Compilation:** The Punjab Higher Education Commission (PHEC) compiles a unified provincial merit ranking calculated from Intermediate marks and university entrance metrics.
3. **Provisional Merit List Release:** Provisional merit lists are published on `honhaarscholarship.punjabhec.gov.pk` allowing a 5-day window for applicants to file grievance appeals.
4. **Final Award Letters:** Successful candidates receive formal electronic Award Confirmation Letters, after which PHEC releases tuition payments directly to the respective university accounts.

## Renewal Guidelines: Maintaining Academic Standing

To retain the Honhaar Scholarship in subsequent semesters, recipients must maintain designated performance standards:

- **Minimum GPA Benchmark:** BS students must maintain a minimum semester CGPA of 3.00 out of 4.00 (or at least 70% in annual examination systems).
- **Classroom Attendance:** A mandatory minimum attendance threshold of 75% in all registered courses is strictly enforced.
- **Disciplinary Record:** The scholar must remain free of university disciplinary violations, examination malpractice, or academic probation.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the official website for CM Punjab Honhaar Scholarship?
The official portal for registration and merit tracking is **https://honhaarscholarship.punjabhec.gov.pk/**.

### Does the Honhaar Scholarship cover hostel and mess expenses?
No. The scholarship strictly covers 100% of the approved university tuition fees. Living, hostel, and mess expenses are borne by the student.

### What is the family income limit to be eligible for Honhaar Scholarship?
The total monthly income of the applicant's family must not exceed Rs. 300,000 per month.

### Can students studying in private universities apply?
Yes, students enrolled in select high-ranking private institutions (such as LUMS, FAST, GIKI, and NUST) in approved computer science and engineering disciplines are eligible.

### What is the minimum Intermediate percentage required?
Applicants from public sector universities must have at least 75% marks in Intermediate, while private university applicants require at least 80% marks.

### How many scholarships are awarded each year?
A total of 30,000 fully funded scholarships are awarded each academic year across Punjab.

### Are medical students (MBBS and BDS) eligible for the scheme?
Yes. Students admitted on merit in all public medical and dental colleges across Punjab are fully eligible.

### How is the scholarship money disbursed?
PHEC transfers the complete tuition amount directly to the student's university bank account every semester.

### What happens if my CGPA drops below 3.00 in university?
If a student's semester CGPA drops below 3.00, the scholarship may be suspended until academic performance is restored according to PHEC policy.

### What is the age limit for applying?
The applicant must not be more than 22 years of age on the official application deadline date.
