# BISP ATM Se Paise Nikalwane Ka Tarika: HBL Konnect Aur Bank Alfalah Biometric ATM Cash Withdrawal (2026)

**BISP ATM se paise nikalne** ke liye kisi bhi qareebi **HBL** ya **Bank Alfalah** **biometric ATM** par jayen, touch screen par 'Urdu' aur 'BISP/Ehsaas Cash' muntakhib karein, apna **13-hinson ka CNIC** number enter karein, **biometric scanner** par angootha laga kar **tasdeeq** karein, aur bina kisi **agent kataoti** ke apni poori **Rs. 13,500** qist wasool karein.

## BISP ATM Se Paise Kaise Nikale? (Direct Biometric Answer)

**Benazir Income Support Programme** (**BISP**) ki qist baghair kisi private retailer ya shopkeeper ki **ghair-qanooni kataoti** ke wasool karne ka behtareen aur mehfooz zariya **Biometric Automated Teller Machine** (**ATM**) hai. Hakoomat-e-Pakistan ne lab-e-saahil aur shehri ilaqon mein biometric ATMs ko BISP aur **NADRA** ke live verification system ke sath munsalik kar diya hai taake mustahiq khawateen ko unka poora haq (**Rs. 13,500**) baghair kisi transaction fee ke mile.

Jab aap ke registered mobile number par **8171** se raqam ki muntaqili ka tasdeeqi paigham masool ho, toh aap ko plastic debit card ya ATM card ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Sirf apna Asal **Computerized Shanakhti Card** (**CNIC**) lekar apne sobai partner bank ke biometric ATM branch tashreef le jayen aur **cardless** screen menu ke zariye cash wasool karein.

*Section coverage: BISP, Rs. 13,500, NADRA, 8171 SMS Gateway, CNIC, Cardless ATM.*

## HBL Konnect ATM Se BISP Ke Paise Nikalne Ka Step-by-Step Tarika

**HBL biometric ATM** machine se baghair ATM card ke **BISP Kafaalat raqam** nikalne ka mukammal tareeqa darj zail hai:

1. **ATM Booth Mein Dakhil Hon Aur Screen Touch Karein**: Kisi bhi **HBL Biometric ATM** par jayen. Agar screen par normal standby screen hai, toh screen ko touch karein ya green button (Enter) dabayein taake Main Menu khul jaye.
2. **Zaban Muntakhib Karein**: Screen par do zabanon ke ikhtiyarat aayenge (English aur Urdu). Asani ke liye **Urdu** par click karein.
3. **BISP / Ehsaas Option Ka Intikhab**: Screen ke dayen ya bayen janib 'BISP' ya 'Ehsaas Programme Biometric Withdrawal' ka button nazar aayega, us par click karein.
4. **13-Hinson Ka CNIC Number Enter Karein**: Screen par numeric keypad ya ATM buttons ki madad se mustahiq khatoon ka **13-hinson ka Shanakhti Card** number baghair kisi dash (-) ke type karein (maslan: 3520112345671) aur 'Darust' (Correct) ka button dabayein.
5. **Biometric Scanner Par Angootha Rakhein**: Screen par fingerprint scan ka ishara aayega. ATM machine ke sath lage sabz scanner par apna dayen ya bayen haath ka **angootha** (ya registered ungli) saaf karke seedha rakhein.
6. **NADRA Tasdeeq Ka Intezar Karein**: 3 se 5 seconds ke andar machine **NADRA** database se aap ke fingerprint ki **tasdeeq** karegi.
7. **Raqam Ka Intikhab Karein (Rs. 13,500)**: Tasdeeq hone ke baad screen par aap ka balance aur 'Raqam Ki Wasooli' (Cash Withdrawal) ka option aayega. Full amount **Rs. 13,500** muntakhib karein.
8. **Cash Aur Raseed Wasool Karein**: Machine ke cash dispenser slot se **13,500 rupay** cash bahar aayega. Cash ginein aur machine se nikalne wali **printed slip** zaroor hasil karein.

## Bank Alfalah Biometric ATM Se BISP Cash Collection Ka Tarika

**Bank Alfalah** ke **biometric ATMs** se cash nikalne ka amal nihayat aasan aur mehfooz hai:

1. Bank Alfalah BVS enabled ATM screen par ja kar 'Biometric / Cardless Transactions' ka icon tap karein.
2. Menu se 'BISP Cash Collection' muntakhib karein.
3. Apna **13-hinson ka CNIC** number screen par darj karein aur 'Confirm' dabayein.
4. **Biometric scanner** roshan hone par apna angootha scanner ki satah par mazbooti se rakhein.
5. Screen par 'Benazir Kafaalat Payment' ki tasdeeq dekh kar 'Cash Withdrawal' par click karein.
6. Amount field mein **Rs. 13,500** muntakhib karein ya custom amount mein 13,500 darj karein.
7. Machine se taaza currency notes aur **transaction receipt** wasool karein.

## BISP ATM Biometric Errors Aur Unka Fauri Hal (Fingerprint & Cash Issues)

Agar ATM par paise nikalte waqt koi rukawat pesh aaye, toh ghabrane ki zaroorat nahi:

### 1. Biometric Fingerprint Mismatch (**Error 93 ya Error 99**)
- **Waja**: Agar aap ke haath khushk hain, mitti lagi hai, ya mehendi lagi hai toh sensor fingerprint scan nahi kar pata.
- **Hal**: Haathon ko dho kar achi tarah saaf karein aur halka sa moisturize karein. ATM scanner par angoothe ko halka sa daba kar center mein rakhein. Agar musalsal 3 baar fail ho jaye toh doosri ungli (index finger) aazmayen. Agar masla hal na ho toh BISP Tehsil Office se biometric verification update karwayen.

### 2. "Temporary Technical Issue / Cardless System Down"
- **Waja**: Bank server ya NADRA live link down hone par yeh error aata hai.
- **Hal**: 15 se 20 minute intezar karein ya qareebi doosri HBL/Bank Alfalah branch ke ATM par chale jayen.

### 3. "ATM Cash Retained / Slip Nikli Magar Cash Nahi Mila"
- **Waja**: Machine mein cash khatam ho jana ya mechanical jam.
- **Hal**: Fauri taur par ATM booth ke guard ko mutala karein, printed slip apne paas rakhein, aur bank ke branch manager ko application dein. Sath hi BISP helpline **0800-26477** par shikayat darj karwayen. 24 se 48 ghante mein bank reverse entry pass karke raqam wapis account mein update kar deta hai.

*Section coverage: Error 93, Error 99, 0800-26477 helpline, Technical resolution.*

## ATM Par Paise Nikalte Waqt Zaroori Hifazati Tadabeer (Safety Tips)

- **Kisi Ajnabi Ko CNIC Ya Fingerprint Na Dein**: ATM booth ke andar sirf mustahiq khatoon ya unka qabil-e-aitimad mehram dakhil ho. Kisi anjan shakhs ko apna angootha lagane ki ijazat na dein.
- **Bank Guard Ki Madad Lein**: Agar screen samajh na aa rahi ho toh bahir khade kisi private bande ke bajaye sirf uniformed bank security guard se screen guide karne ki darkhwast karein.
- **Raseed Phenk Kar Na Aayein**: ATM se nikalne wali transaction slip par transaction ID, tareekh aur balance darj hota hai. Is raseed ko hamesha apne paas mehfooz rakhein.
- **8171 SMS Ki Tasdeeq**: ATM se cash nikalte hi aap ke mobile par 8171 se raqam nikalne ka SMS aayega. SMS mein di gayi raqam aur hath mein mojood cash ka mawazna karein.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Kya BISP ke paise nikalne ke liye plastic ATM card hona zaroori hai?
Nahi, BISP Kafaalat ke paise nikalne ke liye kisi plastic ATM debit card ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Yeh service mukammal taur par cardless biometric system par munhasir hai jahan sirf aap ka CNIC number aur angootha kafi hota hai.

### HBL ATM se BISP ke paise nikalne par bank kitni fees kat-ta hai?
HBL ya Bank Alfalah biometric ATM se BISP raqam nikalne par bilkul zero fee kat-ti hai. Yeh service Hakoomat-e-Pakistan ki taraf se mustahiqeen ke liye 100% muft faraham ki gayi hai.

### Agar ATM par angootha match na ho toh kya karna chahiye?
Agar ATM par angootha tasdeeq na ho, toh haathon ko saaf karke doosri ungli se koshish karein. Agar phir bhi masla barqarar rahe toh BISP Tehsil Office ja kar BVS verification update karwayen ya designated bank counter se manual verification karwayen.

### Punjab aur Sindh ke ilaqon mein kis bank ke ATM par jana hoga?
Punjab, Sindh aur Balochistan ke mustahiqeen ko Habib Bank Limited (HBL) ke biometric ATMs par jana hoga.

### KPK, Azad Kashmir aur Gilgit-Baltistan ke liye kon sa ATM istemal hoga?
Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP), Azad Jammu & Kashmir (AJK) aur Gilgit-Baltistan (GB) ke mustahiqeen Bank Alfalah ke biometric ATMs se cash wasool kar sakte hain.

### Kya 8171 SMS aane se pehle ATM se paise nikal sakte hain?
Nahi, jab tak aap ke CNIC par BISP ki taraf se quarterly tranche release na ho aur 8171 se tasdeeqi SMS na mile ya 8171 portal par status approved na ho, tab tak ATM se raqam nahi niklegi.

### Agar ATM screen par "No Funds / Insufficient Balance" aaye toh kya matlab hai?
Is ka matlab hai ke aap ki is sah-mahi (quarter) ki qist abhi BISP central account se jari nahi hui ya aap ka survey update hone wala hai. Apna status 8171 web portal par check karein.

### Kya kisi doosre bank (jaise UBL, Meezan ya MCB) ke ATM se BISP paise nikal sakte hain?
Nahi, BISP biometric withdrawal sirf designated partner banks (HBL aur Bank Alfalah) ke khas BVS touch ATMs par faal hai. Doosre commercial banks ke ATMs par cardless BISP menu dastiyab nahi hota.

### Agar ATM se paise nikalte waqt light chali jaye ya cash phans jaye toh kya karein?
Fauri taur par bank branch ke manager ko likhi hui darkhwast dein aur BISP toll-free helpline 0800-26477 par complaint darj karwayen. Bank audit ke baad phansi hui raqam 24 se 48 ghante mein dobara jari kar di jati hai.

### Kya koi dukan-dar ya agent ATM se paise nikalne par commission maang sakta hai?
Bilkul nahi. ATM se paise nikalna mukammal taur par muft hai aur kisi bhi shakhs ko koi commission ya rishwat dena qanoonan jurm hai.
