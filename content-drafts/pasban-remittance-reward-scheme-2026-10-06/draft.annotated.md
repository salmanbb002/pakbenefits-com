## What is the Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme (PRRS)?

The **Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme** is a nationwide financial initiative created to reward overseas Pakistanis and their families who send and receive **home remittance** through **formal banking channels**. Launched officially on October 1, 2026, the scheme provides market-based cash incentives directly to remittance beneficiaries across Pakistan.

By channeling foreign inflows into regulated **personal bank accounts** and **mobile wallets**, the program strengthens national foreign exchange reserves while giving ordinary families a transparent, free opportunity to win life-changing cash rewards through a **quarterly lucky draw**.

*Section entity notes: Covered Tier 1/2 terms (PRRS, Home Remittance, formal banking channels, personal bank account, mobile wallet, quarterly lucky draw).*

### Who manages and funds the Pasban Remittance Scheme?

The **Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme** is designed, managed, and funded entirely by the **Pakistan Banks Association** (**PBA**), representing Pakistan's commercial banking sector. The **State Bank of Pakistan** (**SBP**) acts as the official patron and regulatory guide for the initiative.

Crucially, the entire **Rs 16 billion** annual **prize pool** is absorbed by participating banks, imposing zero burden on the national exchequer or taxpayer funds. Technical execution, **digital draw tokens** allocation, and automated draw management are operated by **1LINK (Pvt) Limited** (**1LINK**).

*Section entity notes: Covered Tier 1/2 terms (PRRS, PBA, SBP, Rs 16 Billion, digital draw tokens, 1LINK).*

## Who is Eligible for the Pasban Remittance Scheme?

To qualify for the **Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme** **quarterly lucky draw**, a beneficiary must receive incoming foreign **home remittance** credited directly into an eligible **personal bank account** or registered **mobile wallet** in Pakistan. 

The system automatically scans transactions settled through participating commercial banks, microfinance banks, and branchless banking platforms.

*Section entity notes: Covered Tier 1/2 terms (PRRS, quarterly lucky draw, home remittance, personal bank account, mobile wallet).*

### Mandatory qualifying rules ($100 per month rule)

Eligibility requires strict compliance with the **USD 100 monthly minimum** threshold during the three-month qualifying quarter:

- **Minimum Remittance Amount:** The beneficiary must receive at least **USD 100** (or its equivalent in foreign currencies such as SAR, AED, GBP, EUR, or CAD) in **each separate month** of the 3-month qualifying quarter.
- **Consistency Requirement:** Receiving USD 300 in a single month does not qualify a beneficiary if the remaining two months of the quarter have zero remittance inflows.
- **Account Type:** Only **personal bank accounts** (single ownership) and **joint accounts** qualify for token generation.

*Section entity notes: Covered Tier 1/3 terms (USD 100 Monthly Minimum, personal bank accounts, joint accounts [Tier 3]).*

### Excluded accounts and transaction types

To maintain equity and focus on individual personal inflows, the **State Bank of Pakistan** and **PBA** explicitly enforce **excluded transactions**:

- **Roshan Digital Accounts (RDA):** Inflows into **RDA** accounts are governed by separate investment frameworks and cannot participate in **PRRS**.
- **Cash Over-the-Counter (COC):** Remittances collected in physical **cash-over-the-counter** without crediting a bank account or wallet are excluded.
- **Corporate & Business Inflows:** Commercial accounts, company accounts, and trade-related transfers are ineligible.
- **Institutional Staff:** Employees, officers, and directors of commercial banks, **PBA**, **1LINK**, and the **SBP** are barred from winning.

*Section entity notes: Covered Tier 1/2 terms (SBP, PBA, excluded transactions, RDA, PRRS, cash-over-the-counter, 1LINK).*

## How Does Automatic Entry and Token Generation Work?

Participation in the **Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme** is 100% **automatic entry** and completely free of charge. Beneficiaries do not need to register on any website, complete paper application forms, or pay entry fees.

When an eligible **home remittance** transaction of **USD 100** or more clears into a beneficiary account, the participating bank passes the anonymized transaction record to **1LINK**. The **1LINK** central engine automatically generates encrypted **digital draw tokens** linked to the recipient's Account/IBAN number.

## How Does Pasban Remittance Scheme Differ from Sohni Dharti?

The **Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme** replaces the previous point-accumulation model of the **Sohni Dharti Remittance Programme** with direct, large-scale cash prize draws.

While **Sohni Dharti** required users to download a mobile app, register their CNIC, and manually redeem loyalty points for government services, **Pasban** operates with zero app requirements, zero point tracking, and direct bank cash transfers up to **Rs 100 million** per winner. Unredeemed **Sohni Dharti** loyalty points do not transfer to **Pasban**, as the two schemes run under separate policy structures.

## How to Verify Winning Tickets and Avoid Remittance Scams

Because the **Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme** offers large cash prizes, beneficiaries must remain vigilant with **scam protection & official contact** guidelines to avoid fraudulent phone calls, fake SMS messages, and phishing links.

**1LINK** and participating banks enforce strict security and verification rules:

- **Zero Processing Fees:** Neither **1LINK**, **SBP**, **PBA**, nor your bank will ever ask you to deposit money, pay tax up front, or buy mobile credit to claim a prize.
- **Official Channel Verification:** Winners are notified directly by their respective bank branch or official customer care center.
- **Web Verification:** Beneficiaries can independently verify draw results on the official **1LINK** portal at `1link.net.pk/pasbanrewards` or call the 1LINK Helpline at **021-111-111-722**.
- **Reporting Fraud:** If someone contacts you claiming you won a **Pasban** prize and demands confidential banking details, OTPs, or money transfers, report the caller immediately to your bank helpline.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### How do I register for the Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme?
You do not need to register. **Automatic entry** is entirely free for anyone who receives qualifying **home remittance** through participating banks and **mobile wallets** in Pakistan.

### What is the minimum remittance amount required to enter the lucky draw?
You must receive a **USD 100 monthly minimum** (or equivalent foreign currency) in each of the three months within a quarterly qualifying period.

### When is the first lucky draw of the Pasban Remittance Scheme?
The first **quarterly lucky draw** will take place on **January 15, 2027**, covering remittances received between October 1, 2026, and December 31, 2026.

### How many total winners will be selected in each quarterly draw?
Each **quarterly lucky draw** selects 2,521 winners across four prize categories, distributing **Rs 4 billion** in total quarterly rewards.

### Are Roshan Digital Account (RDA) remittances eligible for Pasban rewards?
No. Inflows into **Roshan Digital Accounts** (**RDA**) are governed by separate investment incentives and are **excluded transactions**.

### Are cash-over-the-counter (COC) remittances included in PRRS?
No. **Cash-over-the-counter** transactions without a bank account or digital wallet transfer are ineligible for **digital draw tokens** generation.

### Is there any registration fee or tax to claim prize money?
There is no registration fee or claim fee whatsoever. Prize money is subject to prevailing tax laws of Pakistan, which are handled through official banking settlement without requiring upfront payments from winners.

### What happened to points accumulated in the Sohni Dharti app?
The **Sohni Dharti Remittance Programme** is a separate legacy initiative. Accumulated loyalty points do not carry over to the **Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme**.

### How will I know if I win a prize in the Pasban draw?
Winners will be contacted directly by their participating bank and can check official winning lists published on the **1LINK** portal (`1link.net.pk/pasbanrewards`).

### Can I choose to opt out of the Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme?
Yes. If a beneficiary does not wish to participate in the draw, they can exercise their **opt-out rights** [Tier 3] by requesting their bank to remove them from automated token generation lists.
