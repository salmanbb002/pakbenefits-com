## Step 7 — Information-Gain Pass

### Identified Competitor Gaps
1. **Confusion between total vs monthly thresholds:** Competitor summaries fail to emphasize that receiving $300 in one month does NOT qualify; the rule requires receiving at least USD 100 in **each separate month** of the 3-month qualifying quarter.
2. **Lack of structured draw timeline table:** No competitor provides a complete 4-quarter timeline mapping qualifying dates to lucky draw dates for 2026-2027.
3. **Clear Scam Warning Protocol:** Weak instructions on how winners are contacted and how to verify legitimate prizes without falling for fake phone calls.

### Committed Original Elements
- **Element 1:** Full 2026–2027 Quarterly Qualifying & Lucky Draw Schedule Table.
