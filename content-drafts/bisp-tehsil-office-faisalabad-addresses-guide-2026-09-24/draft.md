## Services Available at BISP Faisalabad Dynamic Registration Centers

Faisalabad tehsil offices provide a full suite of social welfare and digital registry services free of charge:
1. **NSER Dynamic Survey Registration:** Complete socioeconomic survey profiling for new households and renewal for families whose last survey is older than three years.
2. **Benazir Taleemi Wazaif Enrollment:** Verification of school-going children's admission slips and NADRA B-Forms for quarterly educational grants.
3. **Benazir Nashonuma Health Desks:** Specialized nutrition registration and health check-up linkages for pregnant women, lactating mothers, and infants up to 24 months.
4. **Biometric Thumb Impression Updates:** Refreshing worn-out fingerprint scans for beneficiaries experiencing ATM or bank teller authentication failures.
5. **Deceased Beneficiary Account Transfers:** Facilitating lawful transfer of Kafalat entitlements to eligible adult daughters or next of kin following the death of a registered female head.
6. **Mobile Number & Address Modification:** Updating lost SIM numbers and relocated residential addresses in the central database.

## Step-by-Step Procedure for Completing Dynamic Survey at Faisalabad Centers

Registering or updating your household data at any Faisalabad BISP office involves five sequential steps:

### Step 1: Token Collection and Document Check
Enter the office gate and present your original CNIC to the reception officer. The staff verifies basic documentation and issues a numbered dynamic registry token.

### Step 2: Waiting in the Beneficiary Seating Area
Proceed to the covered waiting hall and observe the digital display or listen for token number announcements.

### Step 3: Household Data Entry Counter
When your token number is called, sit with the data entry operator. Answer questions regarding household size, family members, housing structure, utility bills, livestock, and agricultural land ownership.

### Step 4: NADRA Biometric Fingerprint Scanning
Place your thumbs and index fingers on the biometric scanner connected directly to the NADRA database to authenticate your identity.

### Step 5: Receipt Collection and 8171 Verification
Collect the printed confirmation receipt bearing your survey tracking token. Within 4 to 8 weeks, you will receive an official eligibility status SMS from the 8171 gateway.

## Mandatory Documents Required Before Visiting BISP Faisalabad Desks

To ensure your application is processed in a single visit, bring all required original records:
- **Original CNIC:** Valid, computerised CNIC issued by NADRA (laminated photocopies alone are rejected).
- **NADRA Child Registration Certificate (B-Form):** Original B-Form listing all dependent children under 18 years of age.
- **Children's School Admission Slip:** Signed and stamped by the headmaster of the enrolled government or registered private school for Taleemi Wazaif.
- **Active Mobile Phone:** The SIM must be registered under the applicant's own CNIC.
- **Electricity & Gas Utility Bills:** Recent paid bill for the household address.
- **Disability / Medical Certificate:** Issued by the District Disability Assessment Board (for special persons quota).
- **Death Certificate of Husband:** Computerised NADRA death certificate (for widow registration).

## How to Resolve Biometric and Payment Complaints at Faisalabad Offices

If your quarterly payment is delayed or your biometric authentication fails at commercial bank branches:
- **Visit the Grievance Counter:** Approach the dedicated Complaint & Redressal desk inside your local Tehsil office with your CNIC.
- **Biometric Failure Override:** If fingerprints repeatedly fail at partner ATMs, obtain a Biometric Verification Exception slip from the office Assistant Director.
- **Lodge Helpline Inquiries:** Call the official nationwide toll-free helpline at **0800-26477** to register branch-specific complaints.
- **Online Verification:** Cross-check your current balance and payment tranche status on the 8171 web portal at [8171.bisp.gov.pk](https://8171.bisp.gov.pk/).

## Frequently Asked Questions About BISP Offices in Faisalabad

### Where is the main BISP office located in Faisalabad City?
The Faisalabad City Tehsil Office is located at Al Fateh Sports Complex, Chak No. 224/RB, People's Colony side, Faisalabad.

### Is there any fee for registration or dynamic survey at Faisalabad BISP offices?
All registration, dynamic survey, and complaint resolution services at BISP offices are 100% free of charge.

### What are the daily operating hours of BISP offices in Jaranwala and Samundri?
Offices in Jaranwala and Samundri operate Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

### Can I visit any BISP office in Faisalabad if my CNIC belongs to another tehsil?
You should visit the specific BISP office corresponding to your permanent or present address listed on your NADRA CNIC.

### What should I do if the BISP office runs out of tokens for the day?
Arrive early the following morning before 8:30 AM to receive a fresh priority queue token from the entrance counter.

### How do I enroll my children in Taleemi Wazaif at the Faisalabad center?
Bring your child's NADRA B-Form and a school admission verification slip stamped by the school headmaster to the Taleemi Wazaif desk.

### Where is the BISP Divisional Headquarters for Faisalabad located?
The Divisional Office is situated in Farid Town, Street No. 2, near GTS Chowk, Faisalabad.

### Can an elderly or disabled woman receive assistance without standing in long queues?
Yes, all Faisalabad BISP dynamic centers provide priority queue seating and expedited token processing for elderly and differently-abled women.

### How do I report an agent demanding money outside the Faisalabad BISP gate?
Report the agent immediately to the BISP Tehsil Assistant Director or call the official toll-free anti-corruption helpline at 0800-26477.

### How long does it take for payment to start after dynamic survey completion in Faisalabad?
Socioeconomic PMT scores are calculated within 4 to 8 weeks, after which eligible families receive a notification SMS from 8171.
