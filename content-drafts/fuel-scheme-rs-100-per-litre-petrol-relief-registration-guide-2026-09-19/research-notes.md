## Step 7 — Information-Gain Pass

### What Competitors Omit or Get Wrong
1. **Outdated Vehicle Registration Cutoff:** Many initial guides published on Sep 15 claimed only vehicles registered on or after *January 1, 2011* qualify. On September 18, 2026, the federal government officially relaxed the cutoff to **January 1, 2006**, allowing vehicles up to 20 years old to benefit. Our article explicitly highlights this update.
2. **Shift in Token Mechanics:** Early guides claimed users must buy a minimum of 5 litres per visit. This rule was rescinded. For two/three-wheelers, the system now issues a fixed **Rs. 500 discount token once per week** (totaling 4 tokens / 20 litres / Rs. 2,000 monthly discount), eliminating the rigid 5-litre restriction.
3. **Offline SMS Pump Redemption:** Almost all competitor guides fail to explain what happens when a petrol pump is in a cellular/internet blackspot. We document the official offline SMS verification protocol.
4. **Differentiation from Cash Welfare (BISP / Nigehban / Ramzan):** Unlike BISP Kafaalat or Punjab's Nigehban Card, this scheme is **universal across eligible vehicle owners** with **no PMT score or poverty registry requirement**.
