# Fuel Scheme Rs.100 Per Litre Petrol Relief and Registration Guide (Annotated Coverage Edition)

> **Pakistan's Fuel Scheme** provides an immediate subsidy of **Rs. 100 per litre** on **petrol** for **motorcycles**, **three-wheelers**, and passenger **cars up to 800cc**. Eligible citizens register by sending their **CNIC** and vehicle details via **SMS to 9771**, receiving digital **tokens** to claim discounts directly at participating fuel stations.

## What Is the Fuel Scheme Rs. 100 Per Litre Petrol Relief?

The **Fuel Scheme Rs. 100 Per Litre Petrol Relief** is a targeted federal energy subsidy providing direct price reductions at retail petrol pumps across Pakistan for small-vehicle owners. Administered directly by the **Petroleum Division** of the Ministry of Energy, the initiative offsets escalating global petroleum import costs for commuters, motorcycle delivery couriers, rickshaw operators, and families operating entry-level motor vehicles. Unlike traditional untargeted fuel subsidies that exhaust national treasury reserves on luxury automobiles and commercial fleets, this program uses digital identification to deliver relief exclusively at the point of sale.

The initiative operates without paper coupons, physical ration cards, or bureaucratic center visits. Beneficiaries interact directly with an automated telecommunications gateway to verify their vehicle credentials, receive encrypted transaction **tokens**, and redeem the **Rs. 100 per litre** discount on spot purchases. The system ensures that retail fuel stations sell subsidized petrol at the regulated consumer pump price minus exactly **Rs. 100 per litre**, with the federal government settling the difference directly into the dealer’s designated commercial bank account.

### Program Governance, Approvals, and Funding Mechanism

The program is funded through a dedicated Technical Supplementary Grant of **Rs. 76.73 billion** sanctioned by the **Economic Coordination Committee (ECC)** of the Cabinet under **Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif**. From this federal allocation, exactly **Rs. 75 billion** is ring-fenced exclusively for retail fuel price subsidy absorption, while **Rs. 1.73 billion** funds the telecommunications bandwidth, software architecture, and real-time database synchronization required to maintain national operational stability.

Executive governance is maintained jointly by the **Petroleum Division**, the **Ministry of Information Technology and Telecommunication (MoITT)**, and the **Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA)**. By anchoring the fiscal disbursement in a pre-approved **ECC** supplementary grant, the federal government prevents budgetary delays, ensuring oil marketing companies (OMCs) and independent petroleum dealers maintain uninterrupted fuel inventory without liquidity crunches.

### The Technology Behind the Scheme: MoITT’s Fuel Pass System

The digital architecture powering the scheme is the **Fuel Pass System**, an automated transaction and verification engine custom-engineered by the **Ministry of Information Technology and Telecommunication (MoITT)**. The system integrates national databases from the National Database and Registration Authority (**NADRA**), provincial excise and taxation vehicle registries, and cellular network operator databases in real time.

When a citizen sends an SMS to the designated **9771** shortcode, the **Fuel Pass System** queries the applicant's Computerized National Identity Card (**CNIC**) against provincial motor vehicle records to verify vehicle ownership, engine capacity, and registration dates. Once verified, the platform coordinates with cellular towers to transmit cryptographic alphanumeric fuel **tokens** directly back to the citizen's mobile handset via SMS, enabling instantaneous point-of-sale verification at retail petrol pumps nationwide.

## How Does the Fuel Relief Scheme Compare to BISP and Other Welfare Programs?

The **Fuel Relief Scheme** differs fundamentally from cash welfare programs like the Benazir Income Support Programme (**BISP**) because it is an asset-linked subsidy rather than a poverty-score-tested welfare transfer. While **BISP Kafaalat**, the Punjab **Nigehban Card**, and the **Ramzan Relief Package** require applicants to undergo a National Socio-Economic Registry (**NSER**) or Punjab Socio-Economic Registry (**PSER**) survey to achieve a Poverty Means Test (**PMT**) score below designated cutoff thresholds, the **Fuel Relief Scheme** evaluates vehicle ownership alone.

A middle-income salaried employee, school teacher, or small shopkeeper whose household income exceeds **BISP** eligibility limits qualifies fully for the fuel subsidy provided they own a **motorcycle**, **rickshaw**, or car with an engine displacement not exceeding **800cc**. Furthermore, participating in the **Fuel Relief Scheme** does not affect, reduce, or disqualify any beneficiary from receiving quarterly **BISP Kafaalat** cash payments, Taleemi Wazaif school stipends, or provincial utility discounts.

## Who Is Eligible for the Rs. 100 Per Litre Petrol Subsidy?

Eligibility for the fuel relief subsidy requires applicants to be Pakistani citizens holding a valid Computerized National Identity Card (**CNIC**) who legally own a registered **two-wheeler**, **three-wheeler**, or passenger car with an engine capacity up to **800cc**. The applicant’s mobile **SIM card** must be registered in their own name under the same **CNIC** entered during registration.

The scheme establishes a strict national ceiling of **one registered vehicle per CNIC**. If an individual owns both a **motorcycle** and an **800cc** passenger vehicle, they must select one vehicle for registration. Registering multiple vehicles under a single identity card is automatically rejected by the **Fuel Pass System**. Commercial transport vehicles, taxis above **800cc**, diesel tractors, and government-owned fleet vehicles are strictly barred from participating.

### Eligible Vehicle Categories and Engine Capacity Limits

The program categorizes eligible vehicles into three distinct consumer segments, encompassing approximately 11.8 million registered vehicles across Pakistan:

1. **Two-Wheelers (Motorcycles & Scooters):** Covers approximately 10 million registered **motorcycles**, mopeds, and scooters of all engine displacements (70cc, 100cc, 110cc, 125cc, and 150cc). Standard models such as the Honda CD70, CG125, Pridor, Yamaha YBR125, and Suzuki GD110 qualify without displacement exclusions.
2. **Three-Wheelers (Auto Rickshaws & Qingqis):** Covers roughly 800,000 passenger and cargo three-wheelers, including 2-stroke and 4-stroke **auto-rickshaws**, **Qingqis**, and delivery trikes, providing critical operational relief for self-employed transport workers.
3. **Small Passenger Cars (Displacement <=800cc):** Covers approximately 1 million registered small cars. Eligible models include locally manufactured entry cars such as the **Suzuki Mehran (796cc)**, **Suzuki Bolan (796cc)**, Suzuki Ravi (796cc), and imported 660cc Japanese Kei cars including the **Suzuki Alto 660cc**, **Daihatsu Mira (658cc)**, Daihatsu Move (658cc), and Nissan Dayz (658cc). Vehicles exceeding **800cc** displacement, such as 1000cc Suzuki Cultus, Wagon R, or Toyota Vitz, do not qualify.

- *Tier-2 Terms Covered:* **Rickshaws**, **Qingqis**, 11.8 million beneficiaries.

- *Unused Tier-3 Terms:* None.

### The 2006 Registration Date Cutoff Rule (Expanded Criteria)

Vehicles must be registered on or after **January 1, 2006**, in their respective provincial or federal excise and taxation databases to qualify for the subsidy. While initial government briefings in early September 2026 proposed a narrower 2011 cutoff date, the Ministry of Energy officially broadened the eligibility window on **September 18, 2026**, extending relief to older vehicles up to 20 years of age.

This revision brings millions of aging commuter **motorcycles** and legacy **Suzuki Mehrans** into the national safety net. If a motorcycle was purchased and registered in 2007, 2008, or 2010, the **Fuel Pass System** validates the record successfully. However, vehicles registered on December 31, 2005, or earlier remain excluded from the program to prevent ghost claims on decommissioned or scrapped chassis.

*Coverage Notes (Section 3b):*

### Strict Ownership Rules: CNIC and SIM Card Matching Requirement

The mobile phone number used to submit the registration SMS must be biometrically registered with the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (**PTA**) under the exact same **CNIC** as the vehicle’s registered owner. If an individual attempts to register a **motorcycle** using a cellular **SIM card** registered under a parent's, spouse's, or friend's **CNIC**, the **Fuel Pass System** triggers an automated mismatch error.

Furthermore, the vehicle's registration book or smart card must show the applicant as the legal owner in the provincial excise database. If an applicant purchased a vehicle on an open transfer letter or informal affidavit without officially transferring the title at the Excise and Taxation Department, the registration will fail. Citizens must update their vehicle ownership records with provincial excise authorities before applying.

## How to Register for the Fuel Relief Scheme via SMS to 9771?

Citizens register for the fuel relief scheme by sending a standardized text message containing their identification, vehicle plate number, province code, and registration date to the official federal shortcode **9771**. The service is zero-rated across all four national mobile network operators (Jazz, Telenor, Zong, and Ufone), incurring no cellular balance deduction.

To ensure automated system ingestion, the SMS must follow an exact syntactical structure separated by standard single spaces. Applicants must enter their 13-digit **CNIC** without dashes or special characters, followed by the vehicle registration number, the standardized single-letter **province initial**, and the official registration date formatted as **DDMMYYYY**.

### Exact SMS Format and Province Codes Guide

The registration string sent to **9771** must strictly adhere to the following template:

```
REG [13-Digit CNIC] [Vehicle Registration Number] [Province Code] [Registration Date DDMMYYYY]
```

#### Field Syntax Breakdown

- **`REG`**: The mandatory operational command initiating the enrollment sequence.
- **`[13-Digit CNIC]`**: The applicant's national identity number entered as a continuous sequence of digits without hyphens (e.g., `3520112345671`).
- **`[Vehicle Registration Number]`**: The alphanumeric license plate as printed on the excise registration certificate without spaces or symbols (e.g., `LEZ1482` or `KHI4921`).
- **`[Province Code]`**: A single uppercase letter identifying the vehicle’s registration jurisdiction:
  - **`P`**: Punjab
  - **`S`**: Sindh
  - **`K`**: Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
  - **`B`**: Balochistan
  - **`I`**: Islamabad Capital Territory
  - **`A`**: Azad Jammu & Kashmir (AJK)
  - **`G`**: Gilgit-Baltistan
- **`[Registration Date DDMMYYYY]`**: The initial registration date recorded on the vehicle smart card or logbook in two-digit day, two-digit month, and four-digit year format (e.g., `15082018` for 15 August 2018).

*Coverage Notes (Section 4a):*
- *Tier-1 Terms Covered:* **9771**, **`REG` syntax**, **CNIC**.
- *Tier-2 Terms Covered:* **Province codes (P, S, K, B, I, A, G)**, **DDMMYYYY date format**.
- *Unused Tier-3 Terms:* None.

### Step-by-Step Registration Walkthrough

Follow these sequential steps on your mobile phone to complete enrollment:

1. **Verify Cellular SIM Registration:** Dial `*#66#` or text your **CNIC** to `668` to confirm that the active **SIM card** in your phone is registered under your own national identity number.
2. **Examine Vehicle Documentation:** Retrieve your official excise registration card, smart card, or physical logbook. Note the exact alphanumeric plate number and original registration date.
3. **Draft the Text Message:** Open your mobile SMS messaging app, initiate a new message to destination recipient **`9771`**, and enter the formatted string **`REG [CNIC] [Plate] [Province] [Date]`** ensuring a single space separates each field.
4. **Transmit and Await Delivery:** Send the message. Because **9771** is a government-subsidized shortcode, no account balance is required.
5. **Receive Verification Status:** The **Fuel Pass System** processes the query against provincial excise servers and returns one of three automated responses within 5 to 15 minutes:
   - *Successful Confirmation:* "Your vehicle [Plate] is registered under the **Fuel Relief Scheme**. You are entitled to **Rs. 100/litre** relief. Text **TOK** to **9771** to receive your token."
   - *Data Mismatch Rejection:* "Vehicle details or CNIC could not be verified in Excise records. Ensure the vehicle is transferred to your name."
   - *SIM Mismatch Warning:* "The sending mobile number is not registered under CNIC [Number]. Please apply from a SIM registered in your own name."

*Coverage Notes (Section 4b):*
- *Tier-1 Terms Covered:* **9771**, **CNIC**, **SIM card**, **Fuel Pass System**, **Fuel Relief Scheme**, **Rs. 100/litre**, **`TOK` command**.
- *Tier-2 Terms Covered:* Automated confirmation reply, mismatch codes.
- *Tier-3 Terms Covered:* Dialing `668` for SIM check.
- *Unused Tier-3 Terms:* None.

### Troubleshooting Registration Errors and SIM Mismatches

Registration failures typically stem from three technical issues: unregistered **SIM cards**, pending vehicle ownership transfers, and inverted date formats. If your registration is rejected due to a **SIM mismatch**, visit your cellular operator's franchise with your physical **CNIC** to complete biometric transfer of the **SIM card** before reapplying.

If the excise database indicates that the vehicle is not found, verify whether the vehicle was recently registered or transferred within the past 30 days. Newly transferred vehicles require up to two weeks for provincial excise databases to synchronize with the federal **Fuel Pass System**. Additionally, verify that you entered the registration date as day-month-year (`DDMMYYYY`) rather than month-day-year (`MMDDYYYY`), as inverted dates cause immediate automated validation rejections.

## How to Request and Redeem Digital Fuel Tokens at Petrol Pumps?

To claim your **Rs. 100 per litre** discount at a retail fuel station, you must generate a one-time digital fuel token by sending the keyword **`TOK`** via SMS to **9771** prior to fueling your vehicle. The **Fuel Pass System** generates an encrypted, time-sensitive numeric token and transmits it to your phone within seconds.

Upon arriving at any participating state-owned or private retail fuel station (including Pakistan State Oil, Shell, Total Parco, Attock Petroleum, and Gas & Oil Pakistan), present the SMS token to the pump attendant before dispensing fuel. The attendant enters the token into the station's point-of-sale terminal or validates it via the dedicated merchant SMS channel, instantly deducting **Rs. 100 per litre** from your final billing amount.

### Generating Your Token via SMS (`TOK` to 9771)

Requesting your fuel token is a straightforward one-step process:

1. Open your messaging application and type **`TOK`** in capital letters.
2. Send the message to **`9771`**.
3. Within 30 to 60 seconds, you will receive an SMS containing your unique 8-digit token, remaining monthly litre quota, and token expiration window.
4. **Token Validity Window:** Each digital token remains valid for **24 hours** from the moment of generation. If you do not utilize the token within 24 hours, it expires harmlessly, and your unused monthly quota returns to your account balance, allowing you to generate a fresh token when ready.

### Weekly Quota Rules for Motorcycles and Rickshaws (Rs. 500 Token)

**Motorcycles**, scooters, and **three-wheelers** are allocated a total monthly fuel subsidy quota of **20 litres**, translating to a maximum monthly financial saving of **Rs. 2,000**. Under revised directives issued by the **Petroleum Division**, the previous requirement mandating a minimum purchase of 5 litres per visit has been removed to assist daily wage earners and delivery couriers.

The monthly quota is divided into **four weekly tokens**, each granting a flat discount of **Rs. 500** on petrol purchases. Eligible riders can request one **Rs. 500 token** every 7 days. For example, if a motorcycle commuter purchases Rs. 1,000 worth of petrol, the attendant applies the **Rs. 500 digital token**, requiring the rider to pay only Rs. 500 in cash or card. Unused weekly allowances do not carry over to subsequent calendar months.

### Monthly Quota Rules for Small Cars Up to 800cc (3 Tokens per Month)

Passenger cars with engine displacements **up to 800cc** receive a monthly subsidy quota of **30 litres**, providing a maximum monthly financial saving of **Rs. 3,000**. This allocation is structured as **three individual tokens per month**, with each token valid for a discount on exactly **10 litres** of petrol (a **Rs. 1,000** price reduction per token).

Eligible small-car owners can generate one token every 10 calendar days. When refueling at a petrol pump, the token applies a **Rs. 100 per litre** discount across a 10-litre fuel purchase, saving Rs. 1,000 per fill-up. If the driver purchases more than 10 litres in a single transaction, the remaining fuel volume is billed at the standard unregulated pump price.

### Offline SMS Token Redemption in Poor Connectivity Areas

To ensure uninterrupted service in rural districts, highway bypasses, and urban cellular dead zones where petrol pump internet connectivity fails, the Ministry of Energy has implemented an **Offline SMS Redemption Protocol**. Retail petrol stations are equipped with dedicated SIM-based merchant phones connected directly to the **9771** telecommunications hub.

When automated POS terminals lose internet connectivity, the fuel attendant enters the customer’s token and **CNIC** into their registered merchant terminal via an encrypted SMS query to **9771**. The government server authenticates the transaction through low-bandwidth cellular signaling and returns an instant approval SMS code to both the customer and the pump attendant, allowing the discount to be applied without delays.

## Petrol Pump Settlement: How Dealers and Stations Are Reimbursed

Petrol dealers do not absorb the **Rs. 100 per litre** discount as a business loss; instead, they receive automated daily reimbursements from the federal government through a settlement clearinghouse managed by the **State Bank of Pakistan (SBP)**. When a station dispenses subsidized fuel, the transaction record is encrypted and logged on the central **Fuel Pass System** database.

At the close of daily operations at midnight, the **Fuel Pass System** calculates the total subsidy claims for every registered petrol station across Pakistan. The system compiles these claims into an automated settlement ledger and transmits it to the **State Bank of Pakistan**, which executes direct interbank credit transfers into each dealer’s verified commercial bank account within 24 to 48 hours (**T+1 settlement**).

### Role of the State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) and OGRA

The **State Bank of Pakistan (SBP)** functions as the financial clearinghouse for the program, maintaining dedicated escrow accounts funded by the **ECC’s Rs. 76.73 billion grant**. SBP’s automated clearing infrastructure ensures that reimbursement payments clear directly through the Real-Time Gross Settlement (RTGS) network without requiring dealers to submit manual paper invoices or visit government offices.

The **Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA)** regulates the settlement pipeline by providing a verified registry of licensed retail petrol stations and their linked International Bank Account Numbers (IBANs). **OGRA** enforcement teams monitor retail pump compliance, conduct random audits of dispensed fuel volumes against token logs, and enforce strict penalties against any station that refuses valid customer tokens or charges illicit transaction fees.

*Coverage Notes (Section 7a):*
- *Tier-1 Terms Covered:* **Rs. 100 per litre**, **Fuel Pass System**, **ECC**.
- *Tier-2 Terms Covered:* **State Bank of Pakistan (SBP)**, **Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA)**, **T+1 daily settlement**.
- *Unused Tier-3 Terms:* None.

### Resolving Dealer Resistance and PPDA Concerns

During the initial nationwide rollout on September 16, 2026, the **Pakistan Petroleum Dealers Association (PPDA)** raised operational concerns regarding potential liquidity lockups and transaction processing delays at busy urban forecourts. Dealers worried that delayed government reimbursements would impede their working capital required to purchase daily fuel consignments from oil marketing companies.

To resolve these concerns, the **Petroleum Division** and **OGRA** conducted formal consultations with **PPDA** leadership, agreeing to three binding operational safeguards:
1. **Guaranteed T+1 Settlement:** **SBP** legally guarantees reimbursement within 24 to 48 hours of transaction recording.
2. **Simplified Station Verification:** Stations can process tokens through either automated POS scanners, Android merchant apps, or basic offline SMS gateways.
3. **Transaction Dispute Desks:** Dedicated 24/7 technical dispute desks were established at **OGRA** and the **Petroleum Division** to resolve flagged or stalled token claims within 12 hours. Following these assurances, the **PPDA** endorsed the rollout, ensuring full retail cooperation nationwide.

## Scam Warning and Security: How to Protect Your CNIC and Funds

Because the **Fuel Scheme** involves direct financial subsidies for millions of vehicle owners, cybercriminals and fraudulent agents have launched copycat websites and phishing SMS campaigns designed to steal personal identification data and mobile wallet balances. The federal government has repeatedly warned the public that **registration is conducted exclusively via SMS to 9771**, and no third-party websites or agents are authorized to enroll citizens.

Beneficiaries must never share sensitive personal information—such as ATM PINs, mobile banking passwords, or one-time passcodes (OTPs)—with anyone claiming to represent the **Fuel Relief Scheme**. Legitimate government notifications from **9771** will never ask for bank account details, credit card numbers, or upfront processing fees.

### Identifying Fake Portals (`pmfuelrelief.pk`) and Phishing Messages

Cybersecurity authorities have identified several fraudulent domain names, most notably **`www.pmfuelrelief.pk`** and similar variants, attempting to harvest citizens' **CNIC** photographs, vehicle registration documents, and biometric details. These clone portals falsely claim to provide "instant online registration" in exchange for fee payments through Easypaisa, JazzCash, or bank transfers.

The federal government operates **no online registration web portal** for the **Fuel Relief Scheme**. Any website, mobile application, social media group, or WhatsApp message offering online fuel card issuance, fast-track registration, or token generation is an active scam. Citizens encountering fraudulent portals should immediately report the URLs to the National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA) or the **PTA** helpline at 0800-55055.

- *Tier-1 Terms Covered:* **Fuel Relief Scheme**, **9771**, **CNIC**.

### PTA Guidelines on Official SMS Shortcodes and Zero Fees

Sending registration messages (`REG`) or token requests (`TOK`) incurs **zero service charges**, zero taxation, and zero deduction from your mobile balance.

If you receive an SMS from an ordinary 11-digit mobile phone number (such as `0300-XXXXXXX` or `0345-XXXXXXX`) claiming that your fuel subsidy has been approved and directing you to call a phone number or deposit cash to release your voucher, delete the message immediately. All authentic administrative communications originate strictly from the authorized 4-digit shortcode **`9771`**.

### What is the Fuel Scheme Rs. 100 Per Litre Petrol Relief?
The **Fuel Scheme Rs. 100 Per Litre Petrol Relief** is a targeted federal subsidy providing a direct discount of **Rs. 100 per litre** on petrol for eligible **motorcycles**, **three-wheelers**, and small **cars up to 800cc**. Funded by a **Rs. 76.73 billion grant** approved by the **Economic Coordination Committee (ECC)**, the program is administered by the **Petroleum Division** using the digital **Fuel Pass System**.

### How do I register for the Fuel Relief Scheme via SMS?
To register, send an SMS to **9771** formatted as **`REG [CNIC] [Vehicle Plate] [Province Code] [Registration Date DDMMYYYY]`** using a mobile **SIM registered in your own name**. For example, text `REG 3520112345671 LEM9821 P 14052017` to **9771** to initiate automated verification against provincial excise records.

### What is the exact SMS format to send to 9771?
The exact format is **`REG [13-Digit CNIC without dashes] [Plate Number without spaces] [Province Code] [Registration Date in DDMMYYYY format]`**. Ensure each parameter is separated by a single space and that **province codes** use standard initials: **P** for Punjab, **S** for Sindh, **K** for KPK, **B** for Balochistan, **I** for Islamabad, **A** for AJK, or **G** for Gilgit-Baltistan.

### Which vehicles qualify for the Rs. 100/litre petrol subsidy?
The subsidy applies to **motorcycles** and scooters of all engine displacements (70cc to 150cc), **three-wheelers** and **auto-rickshaws**, and passenger **cars with engine displacement up to 800cc** (including **Suzuki Mehran**, **Suzuki Bolan**, and **660cc Japanese Kei cars**). Vehicles exceeding **800cc** displacement, diesel vehicles, commercial buses, and trucks are strictly excluded.

### My motorcycle was registered in 2008 — do I qualify under the expanded rules?
Yes, your **motorcycle** qualifies because the Ministry of Energy officially relaxed the registration cutoff to **January 1, 2006**, on September 18, 2026. As long as your two-wheeler or three-wheeler was registered on or after **January 1, 2006**, and ownership is recorded under your **CNIC** in the excise database, your application will be approved.

### How do I generate a digital fuel token using SMS?
To generate a digital fuel token, send the word **`TOK`** via SMS to **9771** from your registered mobile phone number. The **Fuel Pass System** will return an SMS containing an 8-digit digital token valid for 24 hours, which you present to the petrol pump attendant before refueling.

### How many tokens can I receive in a month?
**Motorcycle** and **rickshaw** owners receive **four weekly tokens per month**, with each token worth a flat discount of **Rs. 500** on petrol purchases (up to **20 litres** total). Small car owners receive **three tokens per month**, with each token covering a discount on **10 litres** of petrol (up to **30 litres** total).

### Is the Fuel Relief Scheme linked to BISP or the NSER poverty score?
No, the **Fuel Relief Scheme** is completely independent of **BISP** and requires no **PMT poverty score** or **NSER survey**. Eligibility is based entirely on registered vehicle ownership, meaning middle-income citizens who do not qualify for **BISP** cash assistance can freely receive the fuel subsidy.

### Can I register more than one vehicle on a single CNIC?
No, the **Fuel Pass System** enforces a strict limit of **one registered vehicle per CNIC**. If you own both a **motorcycle** and an **800cc car**, you must choose one vehicle to register under the scheme, as duplicate registrations under the same identity number are automatically rejected.

### Does the subsidy apply to diesel vehicles?
No, the subsidy applies exclusively to petrol (motor gasoline) purchases and excludes high-speed diesel (HSD) entirely. Because eligible vehicle categories—**motorcycles**, **rickshaws**, and **small cars up to 800cc**—operate primarily on petrol, diesel is omitted from the federal relief grant.

### What happens if a petrol station has no internet connection?
If a petrol pump experiences internet connectivity failure, the attendant can validate your token using the official **Offline SMS Protocol**. The attendant texts your token code and **CNIC** from their registered merchant SIM directly to **9771**, receiving an instant validation approval code without requiring internet access.

### Are there any registration fees or SMS charges for sending messages to 9771?
No, registration and token requests to **9771** are 100% free of charge. Under directives from the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (**PTA**), all cellular operators have **zero-rated the 9771 shortcode**, ensuring no balance or tax is deducted from your mobile account.
