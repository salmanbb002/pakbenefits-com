# State Bank Pasban Scheme: Complete Guide to Eligibility, Prizes & Draw Dates

The State Bank Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme is a market-based reward program launched by the Pakistan Banks’ Association under the patronage of the State Bank of Pakistan. It distributes PKR 16 billion in annual cash prizes—PKR 4 billion every quarter across 2,521 winners—to reward beneficiaries in Pakistan receiving home remittances through legal bank accounts.

---

## What is the State Bank Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme (PRRS)?

The Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme is an industry-wide incentive framework designed to encourage overseas Pakistanis and their families to route foreign remittances exclusively through formal banking channels. Unlike traditional welfare initiatives, this program directly rewards the remittance receivers residing in Pakistan rather than the overseas senders.

### Role of SBP Governor Jameel Ahmad and the Pakistan Banks' Association

State Bank of Pakistan Governor Jameel Ahmad officially inaugurated the Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme to strengthen Pakistan’s foreign exchange reserves and discourage informal money transfer mechanisms like Hawala and Hundi. The program operates under the auspices of the Pakistan Banks’ Association and is 100% funded by participating commercial banks, placing zero financial burden on the national exchequer.

### Key Strategic Objectives and Phase-Out of Sohni Dharti

The central objective of the Pasban initiative is to build sustained banking habits among remittance recipient households. Concurrently, the State Bank of Pakistan confirmed that the previous Sohni Dharti Remittance Programme has officially ended; points earned under Sohni Dharti do not transfer to the Pasban scheme.

## Who is Eligible for the SBP Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme?

To qualify for the Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme draw, an individual beneficiary in Pakistan must receive home remittances equivalent to at least USD 100 in each of the three consecutive months of a business quarter into a personal bank account or mobile wallet.

### How Tokens Are Calculated: The USD 100+ Tier Formula

Digital entry tokens are generated based on the total value of each qualifying transfer credited to your account. Non-USD foreign currencies (Riyals, Dirhams, Pounds, Euros) are converted into USD using the SBP daily exchange rate on the processing date:

| Remittance Value (USD Equivalent) | Digital Entry Tokens Earned | Practical Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Below USD 100** | **0 Tokens** | USD 95 transfer yields 0 entries |
| **USD 100 to USD 149** | **1 Token** | USD 120 transfer yields 1 entry token |
| **USD 150 to USD 249** | **2 Tokens** | USD 200 transfer yields 2 entry tokens |
| **USD 250 to USD 349** | **3 Tokens** | USD 300 transfer yields 3 entry tokens |
| **Each Additional USD 100** | **+1 Token** | USD 1,000 transfer yields 10 entry tokens |

*Note: Splitting a single large payment into multiple artificially small transactions to hoard tokens is prohibited and can cause transaction disqualification.*

### Eligible Participating Banks List in Pakistan

As of the official October 2026 launch, 22 licensed financial institutions participate in the Pasban Scheme:
* **Commercial Banks:** Allied Bank, Al Baraka Bank, Askari Bank, Bank Alfalah, Bank AL Habib, Bank Makramah, Bank of Punjab, BankIslami Pakistan, Citibank, Dubai Islamic Bank, Faysal Bank, Habib Metropolitan Bank, JS Bank, Mashreq Bank, MCB Bank, Meezan Bank, Samba Bank, Sindh Bank, Soneri Bank, Standard Chartered Bank, Bank of Khyber.
* **Digital Banks & Microfinance:** Easypaisa Digital Bank (and mobile wallets).

*(Note: Major institutions like HBL, UBL, and NBP were not on the initial 22-bank list at launch but may join in subsequent quarters.)*

### Account Exclusions: Why Cash-Over-Counter and RDA Are Ineligible

Certain remittance categories are strictly excluded from the Pasban draw pool:
* **Cash-Over-Counter (COC):** Direct physical cash pickups over the counter do not qualify because they lack continuous bank account linkages.
* **Roshan Digital Accounts (RDA):** Foreign currency and RDA inflows already receive dedicated fiscal incentives and are excluded to prevent double benefits.
* **Commercial & Corporate Accounts:** Non-individual business accounts, corporate entities, and joint accounts with companies are ineligible.
* **Banking Staff:** Directors and staff of participating banks, PBA, SBP, and 1LINK are excluded.

Technology partner 1LINK (Pvt) Limited automatically aggregates transaction records across participating Pakistani banks. Beneficiaries receive entry confirmation statements via SMS, banking apps, or account statements from their respective bank.

### Is 8171 SMS Registration Required? (Scam Warning)

The Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme is **not** connected to the 8171 BISP SMS portal or any external registration fee. Any SMS, WhatsApp message, or phone call claiming that you must transfer money, pay a fee, or send an SMS to 8171 to activate your Pasban entries is an illegal scam.

## Technical & Legal Edge Cases: Taxes, Deceased Heirs & Dormant Accounts

To protect winning beneficiaries and uphold legal compliance, the State Bank of Pakistan and PBA established specific operational rules:

1. **Tax Withholding at Source:** Prize amounts are subject to mandatory government income tax deduction at source by the paying bank before crediting funds.
2. **Deceased Beneficiary Protocol:** If a winning customer passes away prior to prize disbursement, the cash prize is transferred to legal heirs following standard deceased customer inheritance protocols (requiring a valid succession certificate).
3. **Dormant & Blocked Accounts:** Cash prizes cannot be credited into dormant, blocked, or unverified bank accounts. Account holders must reactivate dormant accounts at their bank branch before the draw.
4. **Single-Prize Limit:** An individual account can win a maximum of one prize per quarterly draw, removing it from lower-tier prize draws during that cycle.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme?
The Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme is a market-based incentive program created by the Pakistan Banks’ Association under State Bank of Pakistan patronage. It rewards remittance recipients in Pakistan with PKR 16 billion in annual cash prizes for receiving money through formal banking channels.

### Who qualifies for the quarterly Pasban lucky draws?
Any individual beneficiary in Pakistan who receives home remittances equivalent to at least USD 100 in each of three consecutive months of a quarter into a personal bank account or mobile wallet qualifies automatically.

### Do I need to fill out a registration form or pay a fee to enter?
No registration form, ticket purchase, or fee payment is required to join the Pasban scheme. Qualification is 100% free and handled automatically by participating banks through 1LINK transaction tracking.

### What is the top prize amount in the State Bank Pasban Scheme?
The top grand prize is PKR 100 million (Rs. 10 Crore), awarded to one winning beneficiary during each quarterly draw.

### How many digital tokens does a remittance earn?
Transfers from $100 to $149 earn 1 token; $150 to $249 earn 2 tokens; $250 to $349 earn 3 tokens, with +1 token for every additional $100.

### Are Cash-Over-Counter (COC) cash pickups eligible for the draw?
Cash-Over-Counter cash pickups are not eligible for the Pasban draw. Remittances must be credited directly into a personal bank account or mobile wallet to qualify.

### Are Roshan Digital Accounts (RDA) included in the Pasban Scheme?
Roshan Digital Accounts are excluded from the Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme because RDA customers already access separate government investment incentives and tax benefits.

### Is the Pasban Scheme connected to the 8171 BISP portal?
The Pasban Scheme has no connection to the 8171 BISP SMS service. Any message asking for money or requesting an 8171 SMS payment for Pasban entry is a fraudulent scam.

### When will the first Pasban Scheme draw take place?
The first official Pasban quarterly draw will take place on January 15, 2027, covering qualifying remittances received between October 1 and December 31, 2026.

### How will winners receive their Pasban cash prizes?
Winning cash prizes will be credited directly into the verified bank accounts or mobile wallets of the winning beneficiaries after computerized draw validation and tax deduction.

### What happens if a winning beneficiary has passed away?
If a winning customer dies before prize payment, the prize money is credited to legal heirs under standard bank inheritance protocols upon presenting a valid succession certificate.

### Does the Sohni Dharti scheme still work?
No. SBP confirmed that the Sohni Dharti Remittance Programme has ended and its points do not carry over to the Pasban scheme.
