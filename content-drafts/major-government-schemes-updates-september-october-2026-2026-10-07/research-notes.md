## Step 2 — Head-Entity Research (Canonical Sources)

1. **Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) / Union Cabinet**
   - **Type:** GovernmentOrganization / Organization
   - **Canonical Name:** Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs
   - **sameAs:** `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_Committee_on_Economic_Affairs`
   - **Core Attributes:** Chaired by Prime Minister Narendra Modi; approves major financial outlays, cabinet policies, and national industrial funds.

2. **myScheme Platform**
   - **Type:** GovernmentService / WebApplication
   - **Canonical Name:** myScheme Portal
   - **sameAs:** `https://www.myscheme.gov.in/`
   - **Core Attributes:** National e-Governance platform hosting over 5,056 central and state welfare schemes for single-window search and eligibility checks.

3. **SME Growth Fund (SGF)**
   - **Type:** GovernmentScheme / FinancialProduct
   - **Canonical Name:** SME Growth Fund
   - **sameAs:** `https://www.pmindia.gov.in/` (Cabinet decision Oct 6, 2026)
   - **Core Attributes:** ₹10,000 crore equity growth fund announced under Union Budget 2026-27 (Para 28), approved by Union Cabinet on October 6, 2026; operates via Alternative Investment Funds (AIF) targeting small and medium manufacturing enterprises in Tier-II and Tier-III cities.

4. **Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (Ayushman Bharat 70+)**
   - **Type:** GovernmentScheme / HealthcareService
   - **Canonical Name:** Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana
   - **sameAs:** `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayushman_Bharat_Pradhan_Mantri_Jan_Arogya_Yojana`
   - **Core Attributes:** Universal health coverage offering ₹5 lakh annual health cover per family for all senior citizens aged 70+; card issued via "Vay Vandana Card" portal (`beneficiary.nha.gov.in`).

5. **BHAVYA Rasayan Scheme (Bharat Audyogik Vikas Yojana Rasayan)**
   - **Type:** GovernmentScheme / IndustrialPolicy
   - **Canonical Name:** BHAVYA Rasayan Scheme
   - **sameAs:** `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Chemicals_and_Petrochemicals`
   - **Core Attributes:** Outlay of ₹3,030 crore under the Department of Chemicals and Petrochemicals to establish 3 chemical parks; central grant up to ₹1,000 crore per park; state submission deadline November 30, 2026.

6. **PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana**
   - **Type:** GovernmentScheme / RenewableEnergyProgram
   - **Canonical Name:** PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana
   - **sameAs:** `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PM_Surya_Ghar:_Muft_Bijli_Yojana`
   - **Core Attributes:** Solar rooftop initiative providing up to ₹78,000 central subsidy (₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW, ₹78,000 for 3 kW+) to supply up to 300 units of free electricity per month.

7. **Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)**
   - **Type:** GovernmentScheme / FinancialSupport
   - **Canonical Name:** Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi
   - **sameAs:** `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PM-Kisan`
   - **Core Attributes:** Income support scheme disbursing ₹6,000 annually in 3 direct bank installments of ₹2,000 each; 24th installment expected October 2026; mandatory e-KYC and Aadhaar bank seeding; scheme extended for 2026–2031.
