## 2. Head-Entity Research
- **Central Head Entity:** Benazir Nashonuma Programme
  - **Type:** GovernmentNutritionProgram / SocialWelfareProgram
  - **Governing Agency:** Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP), Ministry of Poverty Alleviation and Social Safety (PASS), Government of Pakistan.
  - **International Partner:** UN World Food Programme (WFP), UNICEF.
  - **SameAs URLs:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benazir_Income_Support_Programme, https://www.wfp.org/countries/pakistan, https://bisp.gov.pk
  - **Core Attributes & Values:**
    - Target: Pregnant and Lactating Women (PLW) and children aged 0–23 months (first 1,000 days of life).
    - Conditionality: Mandatory antenatal/postnatal health checkups, child growth monitoring, immunization (EPI), and nutrition awareness sessions.
    - Cash Benefit: Rs 2,500/quarter for boy child & PLW; Rs 3,000–3,500/quarter for girl child (includes Rs 500 travel allowance).
    - Specialized Food: Specialized Nutritious Food (SNF) sachets (Wawa Mum / Maamta).
    - Verification Mechanism: Biometric verification via NADRA and DHQ/THQ Facilitation Centers.

## 7. Information-Gain Element
- **What competitors missed:** All 4 competitors confuse the 8171 web check with full online registration. Nashonuma *cannot* be registered 100% online from home; 8171 checks BISP Kafaalat base eligibility, while Nashonuma medical registration requires physical biometric verification and clinical registration at hospital facilitation desks.
