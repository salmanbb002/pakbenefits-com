# PAVE Electric Bike Scheme 2026: Complete Online Application & Subsidy Guide

> The **PAVE Electric Bike Scheme** (**Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification**) is a federal government initiative offering cash subsidies up to **Rs. 80,000 Subsidy** for **Electric Bike** models. Managed by the **Ministry of Industries and Production** via **pave.gov.pk**, Pakistani citizens aged 18+ can apply online using their **CNIC** through **Self-Finance Category** or **Bank Lease Financing**.

---

## What is the PAVE Electric Bike Scheme?

The **PAVE Scheme** is the federal government’s flagship subsidy program designed to accelerate **Electric Bike** adoption across Pakistan. Administered by the **Ministry of Industries and Production**, the **Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification** (**PAVE Scheme**) initiative aims to transition daily commuters away from high-cost fossil fuel motorcycles onto clean, energy-efficient **Lithium-ion Battery** two-wheelers.

### Key Objectives of the Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification Program

The primary goal of the **PAVE Scheme** is to reduce urban carbon emissions while offering sustainable economic relief to daily commuters. By subsidizing **Electric Bike** models, the **Ministry of Industries and Production** targets a significant reduction in national oil import bills and empowers students, women, and middle-income workers with affordable daily transportation.

### Supported Vehicle Categories: E-Bikes, E-Rickshaws, and E-Loaders

The **PAVE Scheme** supports three distinct vehicle categories to cover both personal and commercial transit requirements:
1. **Electric Bike (Two-Wheelers):** High-efficiency **Lithium-ion Battery** motorcycles designed for personal commuting.
2. **E-Rickshaw & E-Loader (Three-Wheelers):** Eco-friendly passenger rickshaws for urban transport operators.
3. **E-Rickshaw & E-Loader (Commercial Loaders):** Commercial three-wheelers built for light freight and local commercial deliveries.

## How Much Subsidy Does the PAVE Scheme Provide?

The **PAVE Scheme** provides a direct government subsidy of up to **Rs. 80,000 Subsidy** per **Electric Bike** to lower upfront purchasing costs. Subsidies are disbursed through authorized original equipment manufacturers and verified directly against applicant **CNIC** numbers with **NADRA**.

### E-Bike Cash Subsidy Breakdown (Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 80,000)

The standard cash subsidy for **Electric Bike** units under the **PAVE Scheme** ranges between Rs. 50,000 and **Rs. 80,000 Subsidy** depending on the battery capacity and vehicle performance tier:
- **Standard E-Bikes:** Rs. 50,000 upfront subsidy.
- **High-Performance Lithium-Ion E-Bikes:** Up to **Rs. 80,000 Subsidy** upfront.
- **E-Rickshaw & E-Loader:** Commercial category subsidies ranging from Rs. 200,000 to Rs. 400,000.

### Financing Options: Self-Finance vs Bank Lease Installment Plans

Applicants can choose between two flexible payment mechanisms during online registration on **pave.gov.pk**:
- **Self-Finance Category:** Applicants pay the remaining vehicle balance directly to the OEM after deducting the **Rs. 80,000 Subsidy**. Once delivered and verified, the vehicle registration is completed in the buyer's name.
- **Bank Lease Financing:** Partnered commercial banks provide easy monthly installment plans spanning 12 to 36 months via **Bank Lease Financing**, allowing buyers to take delivery with minimal down payment.

## Who is Eligible for the PAVE Electric Bike Scheme?

Eligibility for the federal **PAVE Scheme** is open to all Pakistani citizens holding a valid **CNIC**. Applicants must be at least 18 years old and pass automated **NADRA** database verification.

### Mandatory Applicant Criteria (CNIC, Age, and Residency)

To qualify for an **Electric Bike** subsidy under the **PAVE Scheme**, applicants must meet the following mandatory baseline criteria:
- **Nationality:** Must be a Pakistani citizen residing in any province, AJK, or Gilgit-Baltistan.
- **Age Limit:** Minimum 18 years of age at the time of application.
- **Identity Verification:** Valid **CNIC** record verified by **NADRA**.
- **Subsidy Limit:** One EV subsidy per **CNIC**; applicants who previously availed of government vehicle schemes are ineligible.

### License and Documentation Requirements

Applicants must submit verified digital scans during **pave.gov.pk** portal registration:
- **CNIC Copy:** Front and back scan of valid **CNIC**.
- **Driving License:** Mandatory for **E-Rickshaw & E-Loader** categories; strongly recommended for **Electric Bike** applicants.
- **Proof of Residence:** Domicile certificate or recent utility bill displaying current address.
- **Photograph:** Passport-sized photograph.

## How to Apply Online for PAVE Electric Bike Scheme at pave.gov.pk?

Applying for the **PAVE Scheme** is completed online through the official federal portal at **pave.gov.pk**. The entire registration and verification workflow is digital to ensure transparency and rapid processing.

### Step 1 — Account Registration on pave.gov.pk

Navigate to **pave.gov.pk** and click on "New Applicant Registration". Enter your 13-digit **CNIC** number, active mobile phone number, and email address. An OTP will be sent to your mobile number to verify your account identity.

### Step 2 — Vehicle & Payment Category Selection

Log in to your **pave.gov.pk** dashboard and browse approved **Electric Bike** models. Select your desired vehicle model, battery type, and choose your payment preference: **Self-Finance Category** or **Bank Lease Financing**.

### Step 3 — Document Verification & Application Submission

Fill out your personal details, employment status, and residential address. Upload crisp, clear scans of your **CNIC**, **Driving License**, proof of residence, and photograph. Submit the application for **NADRA** verification.

### Step 4 — Verification, Pay Order, and Vehicle Delivery

Once your application passes **NADRA** and **Ministry of Industries and Production** scrutiny, an approval notification will be issued. For **Self-Finance Category** applicants, issue the remaining balance via Pay Order. The OEM will coordinate vehicle handover, registration, and subsidy disbursement.

## PAVE Scheme vs. Punjab E-Bike Scheme: Key Differences

Many applicants confuse the federal **PAVE Scheme** with provincial initiatives like the **Punjab E-Bike Scheme**. The table below highlights the crucial operational and eligibility differences:

| Feature / Metric | Federal PAVE E-Bike Scheme | Punjab E-Bike Scheme |
|---|---|---|
| **Governing Authority** | **Ministry of Industries and Production** | Government of the Punjab |
| **Official Web Portal** | **pave.gov.pk** | bikes.punjab.gov.pk |
| **Target Audience** | All Pakistani Citizens (18+) | Enrolled Students in Punjab |
| **Coverage Area** | Nationwide | Punjab Province Only |
| **Subsidy Amount** | Up to **Rs. 80,000 Subsidy** | Interest-free installment subsidy |
| **Vehicle Categories** | **Electric Bike**, **E-Rickshaw & E-Loader** | Petrol & Electric Motorcycles |
| **Payment Options** | **Self-Finance Category** & **Bank Lease Financing** | Bank of Punjab Installment Lease |

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the PAVE Electric Bike Scheme?
The **PAVE Scheme** (**Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification**) is a federal government initiative offering cash subsidies up to **Rs. 80,000 Subsidy** to promote **Electric Bike** adoption across Pakistan.

### What is the official website for PAVE Electric Bike Scheme registration?
The official registration portal for the federal **PAVE Scheme** is **pave.gov.pk**. Applicants should only register through this government website.

### How much subsidy is provided under the PAVE E-Bike Scheme?
The **Ministry of Industries and Production** provides a fixed cash subsidy ranging between Rs. 50,000 and **Rs. 80,000 Subsidy** per **Electric Bike**.

### Who is eligible to apply for the PAVE E-Bike scheme?
Pakistani citizens aged 18 years and above with a valid **CNIC** verified by **NADRA** are eligible.

### Can I get an electric bike on monthly installments through PAVE?
Yes, qualified applicants can select **Bank Lease Financing** to get an **Electric Bike** on easy monthly installment plans.

### Is a driving license required to apply for PAVE electric bikes?
A **Driving License** or learner permit is recommended for **Electric Bike** applicants and mandatory for **E-Rickshaw & E-Loader** categories.

### What is the difference between PAVE and the Punjab E-Bike Scheme?
The **PAVE Scheme** is a federal program open nationwide via **pave.gov.pk**, while the **Punjab E-Bike Scheme** is specifically targeted at students in Punjab.

### How can I check my PAVE application status online?
Applicants can log in to their dashboard on **pave.gov.pk** using their **CNIC** to monitor real-time **NADRA** verification updates.

### Which documents are required for PAVE registration?
Required documents include a valid **CNIC**, recent passport-size photos, utility bill or domicile as proof of residence, and **Driving License**.

### Are electric rickshaws and loaders included in the PAVE scheme?
Yes, in addition to **Electric Bike** models, the **PAVE Scheme** provides higher subsidies up to Rs. 400,000 for **E-Rickshaw & E-Loader** vehicles.
