# PAVE Electric Bike Scheme 2026: Complete Online Application & Subsidy Guide

> The **PAVE Electric Bike Scheme** (Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification) is a federal government initiative offering cash subsidies up to **Rs. 80,000** for electric bikes. Managed by the Ministry of Industries and Production via **pave.gov.pk**, Pakistani citizens aged 18+ can apply online using their CNIC through self-finance or bank installment plans.

---

## What is the PAVE Electric Bike Scheme?

The PAVE Electric Bike Scheme is the federal government’s flagship subsidy program designed to accelerate electric vehicle adoption across Pakistan. Administered by the Ministry of Industries and Production (MoIP), the Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification (PAVE) initiative aims to transition daily commuters away from high-cost fossil fuel motorcycles onto clean, energy-efficient battery two-wheelers.

### Key Objectives of the Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification Program

The primary goal of PAVE is to reduce urban carbon emissions while offering sustainable economic relief to daily commuters. By subsidizing electric vehicles, the federal government targets a significant reduction in national oil import bills and empowers students, women, and middle-income workers with affordable daily transportation.

### Supported Vehicle Categories: E-Bikes, E-Rickshaws, and E-Loaders

PAVE supports three distinct electric vehicle categories to cover both personal and commercial transit requirements:
1. **Electric Bikes (Two-Wheelers):** High-efficiency lithium-ion and lead-acid battery motorcycles designed for personal commuting.
2. **Electric Rickshaws (Three-Wheelers):** Eco-friendly passenger rickshaws for urban transport operators.
3. **Electric Loaders:** Commercial three-wheelers built for light freight and local commercial deliveries.

---

## How Much Subsidy Does the PAVE Scheme Provide?

The federal PAVE scheme provides a direct government subsidy of up to Rs. 80,000 per electric bike to lower upfront purchasing costs. Subsidies are disbursed through authorized original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) and verified directly against applicant CNICs.

### E-Bike Cash Subsidy Breakdown (Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 80,000)

The standard cash subsidy for electric bikes under PAVE ranges between Rs. 50,000 and Rs. 80,000 depending on the battery capacity and vehicle performance tier:
- **Standard E-Bikes (Speed up to 50 km/h):** Rs. 50,000 upfront subsidy.
- **High-Performance Lithium-Ion E-Bikes:** Up to Rs. 80,000 upfront subsidy.
- **Electric Rickshaws & Loaders:** Commercial category subsidies ranging from Rs. 200,000 to Rs. 400,000.

### Financing Options: Self-Finance vs Bank Lease Installment Plans

Applicants can choose between two flexible payment mechanisms during online registration:
- **Self-Finance Option:** Applicants pay the remaining vehicle balance directly to the OEM after deducting the government subsidy. Once delivered and verified, the vehicle registration is completed in the buyer's name.
- **Bank Lease Financing:** Partnered commercial banks provide easy monthly installment plans spanning 12 to 36 months, allowing buyers to take delivery with minimal down payment.

## Who is Eligible for the PAVE Electric Bike Scheme?

Eligibility for the federal PAVE scheme is open to all Pakistani citizens holding a valid Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC). Applicants must be at least 18 years old and pass automated NADRA database verification.

### Mandatory Applicant Criteria (CNIC, Age, and Residency)

To qualify for an electric bike subsidy, applicants must meet the following mandatory baseline criteria:
- **Nationality:** Must be a Pakistani citizen residing in any province, Azad Jammu & Kashmir (AJK), or Gilgit-Baltistan (GB).
- **Age Limit:** Minimum 18 years of age at the time of application.
- **Identity Verification:** Valid CNIC record with NADRA.
- **Subsidy Limit:** One EV subsidy per CNIC; applicants who previously availed of government vehicle schemes are ineligible.

### License and Documentation Requirements

Applicants must submit verified digital scans during online portal registration:
- **CNIC Copy:** Front and back scan of valid CNIC.
- **Driving License / Learner Permit:** Mandatory for E-Rickshaws and E-Loaders; strongly recommended for electric bikes.
- **Proof of Residence:** Domicile certificate or recent utility bill displaying the applicant's current address.
- **Photograph:** Recent passport-sized photograph with a light background.

## How to Apply Online for PAVE Electric Bike Scheme at pave.gov.pk?

Applying for the PAVE Electric Bike Scheme is completed online through the official federal portal at www.pave.gov.pk. The entire registration and verification workflow is digital to ensure transparency and rapid processing.

### Step 1 — Account Registration on pave.gov.pk

Navigate to www.pave.gov.pk and click on "New Applicant Registration". Enter your 13-digit CNIC number, active mobile phone number, and email address. An One-Time Password (OTP) will be sent to your mobile number to verify your account identity.

### Step 2 — Vehicle & Payment Category Selection

Log in to your account dashboard and browse the list of approved electric bike models and manufacturers (e.g., Vlektra, Elfa Electric, Okla EV). Select your desired vehicle model, battery type, and choose your payment preference: Self-Finance or Bank Lease Installment Plan.

### Step 3 — Document Verification & Application Submission

Fill out your personal details, employment status, and residential address. Upload crisp, clear scans of your CNIC, driving license/learner permit, proof of residence, and photograph. Double-check all entered information before hitting the final submit button. Save your generated Application Tracking ID for future status checks.

### Step 4 — Verification, Pay Order, and Vehicle Delivery

Once your application passes NADRA and MoIP scrutiny, an approval SMS and email notification will be issued. For self-finance applicants, issue the remaining balance via Pay Order to the manufacturer. The OEM will coordinate vehicle handover, registration, and subsidy disbursement verification.

## PAVE Scheme vs. Punjab E-Bike Scheme: Key Differences

Many applicants confuse the federal PAVE program with provincial initiatives. The table below highlights the crucial operational and eligibility differences between the PAVE Scheme and the Punjab E-Bike Scheme:

| Feature / Metric | Federal PAVE E-Bike Scheme | Punjab E-Bike Scheme |
|---|---|---|
| **Governing Authority** | Ministry of Industries & Production (Federal) | Government of the Punjab (Provincial) |
| **Official Web Portal** | [www.pave.gov.pk](https://www.pave.gov.pk) | [bikes.punjab.gov.pk](https://bikes.punjab.gov.pk) |
| **Target Audience** | All Pakistani Citizens (18+) | Enrolled Students in Punjab |
| **Coverage Area** | Nationwide (All Provinces, AJK, GB) | Punjab Province Only |
| **Subsidy Amount** | Up to Rs. 80,000 (E-Bikes) | Interest-free bank financing / installment subsidy |
| **Vehicle Categories** | E-Bikes, E-Rickshaws, E-Loaders | Petrol & Electric Motorcycles |
| **Payment Options** | Self-Finance & Bank Lease | Bank of Punjab (BOP) Installment Lease |

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the PAVE Electric Bike Scheme?
The PAVE (Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification) Scheme is a federal government initiative offering cash subsidies up to Rs. 80,000 to promote electric bike adoption across Pakistan.

### What is the official website for PAVE Electric Bike Scheme registration?
The official registration portal for the federal PAVE scheme is www.pave.gov.pk. Applicants should only register through this government website.

### How much subsidy is provided under the PAVE E-Bike Scheme?
The government provides a fixed cash subsidy ranging between Rs. 50,000 and Rs. 80,000 per electric bike depending on the vehicle specifications and approved category.

### Who is eligible to apply for the PAVE E-Bike scheme?
Pakistani citizens aged 18 years and above with a valid CNIC and clean credit history are eligible. Applicants must not have previously received a government EV vehicle subsidy.

### Can I get an electric bike on monthly installments through PAVE?
Yes, qualified applicants can select the Bank Lease financing option to get electric bikes on easy monthly installment plans facilitated by partner commercial banks.

### Is a driving license required to apply for PAVE electric bikes?
For electric bikes, a valid driving license or learner permit is recommended, while for E-Rickshaws and E-Loaders, a driving license is mandatory.

### What is the difference between PAVE and the Punjab E-Bike Scheme?
PAVE is a federal program open nationwide via pave.gov.pk, while the Punjab E-Bike Scheme (bikes.punjab.gov.pk) is specifically targeted at students enrolled in Punjab.

### How can I check my PAVE application status online?
Applicants can log in to their account dashboard on pave.gov.pk using their CNIC and tracking ID to monitor real-time verification and approval updates.

### Which documents are required for PAVE registration?
Required documents include a valid CNIC, recent passport-size photos, utility bill or domicile as proof of residence, and driving license or learner permit.

### Are electric rickshaws and loaders included in the PAVE scheme?
Yes, in addition to two-wheeler electric bikes, the PAVE scheme provides higher subsidies up to Rs. 400,000 for three-wheeler electric rickshaws and commercial loaders.
