# Research Notes: CM Punjab Green Tractor Scheme 2026 Online Apply

- **Primary Keyword:** cm punjab green tractor scheme 2026 online apply
- **Secondary / LSI Keywords:** gts punjab gov pk registration, green tractor scheme balloting result 2026, green tractor subsidy 10 lakh, punjab agriculture green tractor eligibility criteria, cm punjab tractor scheme portal, punjab kisan tractor balloting winners list
- **Language / Voice:** Authoritative, direct, factual English matching government policy guidance.
- **Search Intent:** Procedural (Do) and Informational (Know). Farmers want to register online via gts.punjab.gov.pk, understand landholding requirements (1 to 50 acres), verify tractor horsepower ranges (50 HP to 85 HP), check computerized balloting results by CNIC, and understand Bank of Punjab challan procedures.
- **Author:** Muhammad Salman (Single author)
- **Target Portal:** https://gts.punjab.gov.pk/
- **Official Helpline:** 0800-17000 (Punjab Agriculture Department)
- **Key Official Facts:**
  1. Flat subsidy: Rs. 1,000,000 (Rs. 10 Lakh / 1 million PKR) paid by the Government of Punjab directly against the purchase price of an approved tractor model.
  2. Total fleet: 9,500 green tractors allocated transparently across all 36 districts of Punjab based on district cultivated area quotas.
  3. Land criteria: Farmers owning between 1 and 50 acres of agricultural land in Punjab verified via PLRA digital records.
  4. Approved manufacturers include Millat Tractors (Massey Ferguson), Al-Ghazi Tractors (New Holland), Orient, and other authorized domestic producers.
  5. 3-year strict resale prohibition (lock-in period); tractors are digitally tagged and registration transfer is blocked through the Excise and Taxation Department.
