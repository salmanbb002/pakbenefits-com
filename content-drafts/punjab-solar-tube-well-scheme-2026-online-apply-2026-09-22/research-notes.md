# Research Notes: Punjab Solar Tube Well Scheme 2026 Online Apply

## 1. Intent & SERP Landscape
- **Primary Search Query**: `punjab solar tube well scheme 2026 online apply`
- **Dominant Search Intent**: Transactional & Procedural. Farmers are searching for the exact registration portal URL (`cmstp.punjab.gov.pk` or `agriculture.punjab.gov.pk`), eligibility criteria, subsidy calculation (how much the government pays vs farmer's share), required land documents, and balloting dates.
- **Top Competitor Gaps**:
  - Outdated posts mention vague deadlines or don't specify the 67% government grant vs 33% farmer share breakdown.
  - Failure to clearly explain the distinction between electric tube well solarization (with DISCO net-metering option) and diesel tube well replacement (submersible off-grid systems).
  - Lack of concrete technical specs (10 HP/10 kW, 15 HP/15 kW, 20 HP/20 kW) and required land area ownership (minimum 1 acre up to 25 acres).

## 2. Scheme Specifications & Verification
- **Executive Authority**: Government of the Punjab, Agriculture Department (Water Management Wing) in collaboration with Energy Department.
- **Target Scale**: Phase 2 aims to solarize 7,000 to 10,000+ tube wells across Punjab's 36 districts.
- **Cost Sharing Model**:
  - Government Subsidy: 67% of the total approved capital benchmark cost.
  - Farmer Equity: 33% deposited into an escrow account upon successful balloting.
- **System Capacities**:
  - 10 HP system (approx. 10 kW solar PV array)
  - 15 HP system (approx. 15 kW solar PV array)
  - 20 HP system (approx. 20 kW solar PV array)
- **Application Portals**:
  - `https://cmstp.punjab.gov.pk/`
  - `https://agriculture.punjab.gov.pk/`
- **Required Documents**:
  - Valid computerized CNIC (registered mobile number).
  - Land ownership proof (Fard-e-Malkiat issued within last 3 months by Land Record Authority / PLRA or Arazi Record Center).
  - Khasra Girdawari showing active cultivation.
  - Electricity bill (for electric tube wells showing active connection and meter number) or physical verification of diesel engine.
  - Affidavit on stamp paper agreeing to terms and non-transferability for 3 years.
- **Selection Process**: District-wise quota allocated based on irrigated acreage, followed by transparent PITB computerized e-balloting.
