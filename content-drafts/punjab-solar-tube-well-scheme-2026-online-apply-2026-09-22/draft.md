# Punjab Solar Tube Well Scheme 2026 Online Apply: Eligibility, Subsidy Rates, Registration Portal & Balloting

**By Muhammad Salman** | Published September 22, 2026

To apply for the Punjab Solar Tube Well Scheme 2026 online, eligible farmers in Punjab must submit their CNIC, land ownership verification (Fard), and tube well details through the official portal at `cmstp.punjab.gov.pk` or via the Punjab Agriculture Department website (`agriculture.punjab.gov.pk`). Under the leadership of Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif, the Punjab government provides up to a 67% capital subsidy to convert existing diesel-driven and grid-connected electric tube wells to modern solar pumping systems, enabling farmers to eliminate skyrocketing fuel and tariff expenses.

With diesel prices remaining high and agricultural electricity tariffs causing severe distress to smallholders, the "Solarization of Agricultural Tube Wells Scheme" represents a revolutionary step toward energy independence in the agrarian sector. The initiative is executed through the Directorate General Agriculture (Water Management) Punjab, ensuring that transparent computerized e-balloting overseen by the Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB) determines the final beneficiary list across all 36 districts of the province.

---

## 1. Overview of the CM Punjab Solar Tube Well Scheme 2026

The Punjab Solar Tube Well Scheme 2026 is designed to convert agricultural tube wells across Punjab from expensive fossil fuels and erratic grid electricity to clean, uninterrupted photovoltaic power. By installing Tier-1 solar panels, high-efficiency inverters, and durable submersible or surface pumping machinery, the project guarantees timely irrigation for major seasonal crops including wheat, cotton, rice, sugarcane, and oilseeds.

Key highlights of the 2026 phase include:
- **Major Government Grant**: The Punjab government covers up to 67% of the total benchmark system cost, requiring the farmer to contribute only the remaining 33% equity after winning the ballot.
- **Large-Scale Target**: Solarization of 7,000 to 10,000+ agricultural tube wells distributed transparently according to cultivated district acreage.
- **Flexible System Sizes**: Subsidies are calibrated to accommodate 10 Horsepower (HP), 15 HP, and 20 HP pumping loads (ranging between 10 kW and 20 kW solar PV arrays).
- **Dual Conversion Paths**: Both diesel engine tube wells (which burn expensive fuel) and electric tube wells (burdened by fuel price adjustments and peak hour surcharges) are eligible.
- **5-Year Equipment Warranty**: Pre-qualified international-standard solar vendors provide warranties on panels (up to 25 years linear performance) and inverters/pumps (up to 5 years).

## 2. Subsidy Structure and Cost Sharing (67% Grant vs 33% Farmer Equity)

The financial mechanism is structured as a direct public-private cost sharing model. The government’s 67% subsidy is paid directly to pre-qualified solar EPC contractors upon verified installation and commissioning by the District Water Management Implementation Committee (DWMIC).

Below is the estimated benchmark cost and subsidy distribution across standard tube well capacities:

## 3. Eligibility Criteria for Agricultural Landowners

To ensure that public subsidies reach genuine practicing farmers rather than speculative investors, the Agriculture Department has enforced rigorous eligibility benchmarks:

1. **Permanent Residency & Land Location**: The applicant must be a citizen of Pakistan holding a valid NADRA CNIC, and the agricultural land must be located within the administrative boundaries of Punjab.
2. **Land Ownership Threshold**: Applicants must own between **1 acre and 25 acres** of irrigated agricultural land. Joint owners (Sharikeen Khata) can apply provided they submit an affidavit of consent from co-sharers.
3. **Existing Operational Tube Well**: The applicant must already possess an active, functional diesel tube well or an electrified tube well connection (registered in the applicant’s name or immediate ancestor's name with proof of inheritance).
4. **Viable Ground Water Quality**: Ground water at the site must be fit or marginally fit for agricultural irrigation (tested and certified through local Soil & Water Testing Laboratories).
5. **No Prior Solar Subsidy Default**: Farmers who previously availed government solar subsidies for the same land parcel within the past 3 years are ineligible.

## 4. Required Documents for Registration

Before initiating the online application, applicants should prepare digital scans or clear photographs of the following official documents:

- **Original CNIC**: Scanned front and back copies of the applicant's valid CNIC.
- **Land Ownership Record (Fard-e-Malkiat)**: Computerized Fard issued within the last 90 days by the Punjab Land Records Authority (PLRA) Arazi Record Center, specifically mentioning agricultural status.
- **Khasra Girdawari**: Latest revenue record confirming active crop cultivation on the designated land.
- **Electricity Bill (For Electric Tube Wells)**: Paid copy of the most recent electricity bill showing active connection, reference number, and sanctioned load.
- **Undertaking / Affidavit**: Attested stamp paper (Rs. 100) declaring that the solar system will not be sold, rented, transferred, or relocated for a mandatory minimum period of 3 years.

## 5. Step-by-Step Guide: How to Apply Online at cmstp.punjab.gov.pk

Farmers can submit their application from home via computer or smartphone, or visit their nearest Tehsil Agriculture (Water Management) Office for complimentary digital assistance.

### Step 1: Account Creation on the Official Portal
1. Open your browser and go to the official portal: `https://cmstp.punjab.gov.pk/` (or access the link via `agriculture.punjab.gov.pk`).
2. Click on **"New Farmer Registration" (نیا کاشتکار رجسٹریشن)**.
3. Enter your 13-digit CNIC number, active mobile phone number, district, and tehsil. An OTP code will be sent to your phone for verification.

### Step 2: Entering Land and Irrigation Details
1. Select your Division, District, Tehsil, and Mauza / Village.
2. Input your Khewat, Khatooni, and Khasra numbers exactly as written on your computerized Fard.
3. Specify your total cultivated landholding (in acres) and irrigation source (canal + tube well, or solely tube well).

### Step 3: Tube Well Technical Profile
1. Select whether you are converting a **Diesel Tube Well** or an **Electric Tube Well**.
2. For electric connections, enter your 14-digit electricity bill reference number and DISCO name (LESCO, MEPCO, FESCO, GEPCO, or IESCO).
3. State your existing pump capacity (HP), delivery pipe diameter (inches), and estimated water table depth (feet).

### Step 4: Document Upload and Submission
1. Upload scanned attachments of your CNIC, computerized Fard, Khasra Girdawari, and electricity bill.
2. Review the summary for errors and check the declaration box agreeing to the scheme terms.
3. Click **"Submit Application"**. You will receive a unique Application Tracking ID via SMS on your registered phone.

## 6. Scrutiny, E-Balloting, and System Installation Workflow

Once the application deadline closes, the Punjab Agriculture Department executes a four-phase rollout:

1. **Digital Document Scrutiny**: PLRA databases automatically cross-verify land ownership, and DISCO records verify electric meter authenticity.
2. **Physical Site Verification**: Field staff from the local Assistant Director Agriculture (Water Management) office visit the site to measure water depth and inspect the existing bore and delivery mechanism.
3. **PITB Computerized E-Balloting**: If verified applications exceed district quotas, the Punjab Information Technology Board conducts transparent public balloting. Successful farmers receive an official congratulatory SMS.
4. **Farmer Equity Deposit**: Selected beneficiaries are given 10 to 15 days to deposit their 33% equity share into a designated Bank of Punjab escrow account.
5. **Contractor Delivery & Energization**: The pre-qualified vendor delivers Tier-1 panels, mounting structure, dual-axis or seasonal tilt frames, and inverters. Following installation, the DWMIC tests pump discharge, signs the completion certificate, and activates the warranty.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. How do I apply for the Punjab Solar Tube Well Scheme 2026?
You can apply online by visiting the official scheme portal at `cmstp.punjab.gov.pk` or `agriculture.punjab.gov.pk` and submitting your CNIC, mobile number, land ownership Fard, and tube well details.

### 2. What percentage of subsidy does the Punjab government offer?
The Punjab government provides a 67% capital grant for the approved benchmark cost of the solar pumping system, while the farmer pays the remaining 33% share.

### 3. What is the minimum land requirement to qualify?
Farmers must own at least 1 acre of agricultural land, with preference given to small and medium landholders owning up to 25 acres.

### 4. Can farmers with diesel tube wells apply for this solar program?
Yes, farmers with existing functional diesel engine tube wells are eligible and prioritized to relieve them from recurring diesel expenses.

### 5. What system capacities are available under the scheme?
The scheme provides solar systems matching 10 HP, 15 HP, and 20 HP agricultural pumping requirements (approximately 10 kW to 20 kW solar panel arrays).

### 6. Where do I deposit the farmer equity share?
Successful applicants selected through computerized balloting deposit their 33% share directly into a designated government escrow account at The Bank of Punjab (BOP).

### 7. How are beneficiaries selected if applications exceed the district quota?
Beneficiaries are selected through a transparent, computerized e-balloting system administered by the Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB).

### 8. What documents are required for the solar tube well application?
You need your original CNIC, a computerized land ownership Fard issued within the last 90 days, Khasra Girdawari, an electricity bill (if converting an electric tube well), and an attested affidavit.

### 9. Can I sell or transfer the solar panels after installation?
No, the terms strictly prohibit selling, renting, or transferring the equipment to another location for a mandatory minimum period of 3 years.

### 10. Who can I contact if I need help with my application?
You can contact the Punjab Agriculture Department toll-free helpline at 0800-17000 or visit your local Tehsil Agriculture (Water Management) office for in-person support.
