## What Is the CM Punjab Rehmat Card Scheme?

### Program Objectives, Mandate, and Rs 5 Billion Zakat Fund
The CM Punjab Rehmat Card program serves as a targeted provincial safety net designed to protect exceptionally vulnerable households from acute economic deprivation. Formally sanctioned by the Punjab Cabinet in March 2026 and launched in May 2026 by Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif, the initiative operates under the statutory oversight of the Punjab Zakat & Ushr Department. Rather than relying on commercial loans or temporary international donor funding, the government allocated an initial ring-fenced budget of Rs 5 Billion directly from the provincial Zakat pool. This capital guarantees that every approved household receives dignified financial relief rooted in Islamic social welfare principles.

Administrative implementation is executed in close technical collaboration with the Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB). By replacing antiquated paper-based distribution systems with transparent digital authentication, the program ensures zero political interference or bureaucratic leakage. Across all 36 administrative districts of Punjab, local scrutiny is coordinated through designated District Zakat Committee networks. These committees cross-reference local civil records while municipal data streams sync directly into the central provincial ledger. For thousands of single-earner homes struggling with escalating living expenses, utility obligations, and essential healthcare, this state initiative provides immediate liquidity and long-term social stability.

### Grant Amount and Direct Disbursement via JazzCash Wallets
Approved beneficiaries under the initiative receive a substantial lump-sum financial assistance package of Rs 100,000. This grant is disbursed as a 100% net unconditional cash transfer with zero service deductions, hidden processing charges, or third-party bank fees. To safeguard female recipients and young guardians from traveling to crowded regional treasuries, the provincial authorities partnered with telecom branchless infrastructure to route payments directly through JazzCash mobile wallets. Once biometric clearance is confirmed, the total entitlement is transferred straight into the applicant's linked digital account.

Beneficiaries receive an automated authentication dispatch from the official government short-code, followed by an immediate SMS transaction receipt from the banking channel. Cardholders can withdraw their funds from any authorized branchless cash-out agent, biometric retail outlet, or designated partner bank ATM across Punjab without paying a single rupee in commission. For families who lack personal smartphones, physical biometric cash-collection desks at local post branches and tehsil treasury centers provide secondary collection points upon presentation of an original identity card and live thumbprint verification.

## Who Is Eligible for the CM Rehmat Card in 2026?

### Mandatory Criteria for Deserving Muslim Widows
To qualify for the CM Punjab Rehmat Card, a female applicant must be an officially recognized Muslim widow holding permanent residency and domicile in the province of Punjab. Financial entitlement requires that the applicant falls under the Shariah definition of Mustahiq-e-Zakat, meaning she does not possess assets, savings, gold, or commercial property exceeding the designated nisab threshold. Furthermore, her household must be logged within the Punjab Socio-Economic Registry (PSER) with an approved Proxy Means Test (PMT) poverty score establishing clear financial vulnerability.

A critical regulatory condition requires that the applicant’s marital status is officially registered as "Widow" within the central NADRA database. Widows whose identity credentials still reflect active marriage are automatically disqualified by automated digital cross-checks. Additionally, the applicant must possess an active, biometrically authenticated mobile SIM card issued strictly under her own national identity number to receive authentication codes and mobile wallet access credentials.

### Eligibility Rules for Parentless (Double-Parent) Orphans
Children qualifying under the orphan category must be parentless orphans who have suffered the death of both their biological mother and father. Under the official 2026 program statutes, single-parent orphans (where one parent survives) are evaluated under general household dependency quotas rather than this dedicated category. The surviving legal guardian or appointed court custodian must submit applications on behalf of minor orphans aged under 18 years, ensuring that every child is verified through official civil registration instruments.

Verification of double-parent orphanhood is established through computerized NADRA Death Certificates for both deceased parents, accompanied by a verified NADRA B-Form or Juvenile Card certifying the children's legal lineage. The guardian must be a permanent resident of Punjab, free from any disqualifying affluent assets, and registered within the provincial socio-economic survey. All financial disbursements granted for orphaned minors are legally earmarked for their living expenses, shelter, clothing, and primary or secondary school tuition fees.

### Strict Disqualifications: Sahib-e-Nisab, Pensioners, and Parallel Schemes
Applicants are categorically ineligible if any household member is classified as Sahib-e-Nisab or owns registered agricultural acreage, commercial assets, or personal vehicles exceeding the provincial poverty ceiling. Under statutory rules enforced by the Punjab Zakat & Ushr Department, current government servants, autonomous body personnel, military employees, and public-sector pensioners are disqualified from claiming funds from the provincial Zakat pool. The program actively audits tax filer records through the Federal Board of Revenue to eliminate affluent claimants attempting to exploit public welfare grants.

The legal framework enforces a strict Beggary Prohibition Clause, barring professional or habitual beggars from receiving the grant. Beneficiaries currently enrolled in parallel provincial cash transfer programs—such as the CM Himmat Card Scheme for persons with disabilities—cannot receive duplicate financial relief under the Rehmat Card during the same operational fiscal year. Automated identity matching across provincial welfare databases instantly rejects concurrent duplicate enrollments to protect program resources for unassisted families.

## Required Documents for Verification Before You Apply

### NADRA CNIC and Marital Status Update for Widows
Before starting an application, every widow must verify that her 13-digit Computerized National Identity Card reflects her updated marital status as a widow. If the card still lists her deceased husband as an active spouse without a registered termination of marriage, the automated application interface will reject the file during server-side verification. Widows must present their husband's union council death slip to their closest National Database and Registration Authority operational center to receive a modified identity document before accessing the application system.

Applicants must confirm that the national identity document is physically valid and not expired. The photograph, address of permanent residency in Punjab, and thumbprint biometric records must be current to avoid transaction failures during later mobile banking handshakes. A crisp photographic image of both the front and rear faces of the updated identity card must be prepared for digital upload through the submission portal or mobile interface.

### Death Certificates, B-Forms, and SIM Registration Rules
Applicants registering parentless orphans must gather computerized NADRA Death Certificates for both deceased biological parents along with the child's valid NADRA B-Form. Traditional handwritten death slips issued by village elders or local graveyard caretakers are legally invalid for state Zakat fund disbursements; only barcoded, computerized certificates from the municipal authorities are accepted. Guardians should confirm that the family registration certificate clearly connects the minor wards to the deceased parents.

The applicant or authorized legal guardian must own an active cellular SIM card issued under their own 13-digit national identity number. Registration platforms reject third-party mobile connections, including numbers owned by neighbors, distant relatives, or retail agents. This personal mobile connection is mandatory for receiving One-Time Passwords (OTP), real-time status notices, application tracking keys, and subsequent digital banking cash-out instructions.

## How to Apply for the CM Punjab Rehmat Card 2026

### Method 1: Online Application via the Official Web Portal
The primary digital gateway for the program is hosted at the official provincial web portal at rahmatcard.punjab.gov.pk. Applicants initiate the submission by creating a user profile using their 13-digit identity card number and verified mobile number. Upon entering these credentials, a verification code is dispatched by the server to confirm mobile ownership before granting access to the primary questionnaire.

Within the portal dashboard, applicants enter their current residential address, family unit composition, household income estimates, and their active registry identifier from the Punjab Socio-Economic Registry (PSER). Widows enter their deceased spouse’s national identity number and death registration index, while orphan guardians upload digital scans of the child's NADRA B-Form and both parental death certificates. Once all declarations are checked, the applicant submits the form to generate a unique digital tracking code for future monitoring.

### Method 2: Mobile Registration Using the PITB Rahmat Card App
Citizens with Android mobile devices can complete their enrollment through the specialized PITB Android Mobile Application titled "CM Punjab Rahmat Card," downloadable directly from the Google Play Store. Built by the Punjab Information Technology Board, the mobile app features a simple, low-bandwidth interface presented in both Urdu and English. The application is optimized to run smoothly on budget smartphones across cellular data networks in rural Punjab.

Users launch the app, grant necessary camera and storage permissions, and complete real-time document capture without needing desktop scanning equipment. The application utilizes on-device optical character recognition to read identity numbers directly from physical identity cards, reducing data entry mistakes. Once the biometric selfie and scanned certificates are uploaded, the applicant taps the final submission button, which issues an instant on-screen tracking confirmation and copies the reference code via SMS.

### Method 3: In-Person Submission at District Zakat Committee Offices
Applicants who lack smartphones, internet connectivity, or digital literacy can register offline by visiting their nearest District Zakat Committee office or local Tehsil Social Welfare complex. Every administrative district in Punjab operates designated public facilitation counters staffed by trained welfare desk officers. These clerks are legally mandated to assist deserving widows and illiterate guardians by manually scanning documents and logging records into the digital portal free of cost.

When visiting the district office, applicants must carry their original identity card, computerized death certificates, child identity documents, and active mobile handset. The facilitation officer conducts an on-site biometric fingerprint verification and directly transmits the record to the central Punjab Zakat & Ushr Department database. The applicant receives a printed physical acknowledgment slip bearing their official reference identifier and date of entry for tracking purposes.

## How to Check Your CM Rehmat Card Application Status Online

### Tracking Status by 13-Digit CNIC on rahmatcard.punjab.gov.pk
To inspect the processing stage of an active application, citizens should visit the status verification portal at rahmatcard.punjab.gov.pk. The inquiry screen requires no password; users merely type their 13-digit identity card number into the query field without inserting dashes, spaces, or slashes. After resolving an automated security captcha to prevent malicious bot queries, the user clicks the "Check Status" button to retrieve their live provincial file.

The central server interrogates the provincial social registry database and returns a real-time record showing the applicant’s name, submission date, current administrative review tier, and payment approval status. Citizens can also check their application progress by dialing the provincial government helpline at Helpline 1077 from any registered mobile network. Call center operators authenticate the caller's identity through basic personal verification questions before providing current file status updates.

### Decoding Application Status Codes: Approved, Under Scrutiny, and Ineligible
The tracking interface outputs specific administrative status codes that describe the precise operational standing of each application file:

1. **Approved for Disbursement (منظور شدہ):** Indicates that the applicant's civil documents, widowhood or orphan records, and poverty score have cleared all validation checks. The file has been transmitted to JazzCash mobile wallet systems for immediate fund issuance.
2. **Under Scrutiny / Verification Pending (زیرِ تصدیق):** Denotes that the file is actively undergoing cross-referencing between NADRA civil records, the PSER database, and local District Zakat Committee rosters. This status typically persists for 4 to 8 weeks following initial portal entry.
3. **Ineligible / Record Discrepancy (نااہل / کوائف نامکمل):** Signals that the file was rejected due to a disqualifying asset profile, active government employment, failure to meet the Proxy Means Test (PMT) threshold, or conflicting marital records in national identity archives.

## What Changed in September 2026? Phase 1 Pause and Phase 2 Rollout

### Official Payment Freeze of September 18, 2026 Explained
On September 18, 2026, the Punjab Zakat & Ushr Department announced a temporary operational pause on all first-phase financial disbursements under the CM Punjab Rehmat Card. This pause was instituted after reaching the initial milestone of 50,000 verified beneficiaries and to allow state auditors to perform comprehensive data reconciliation across branchless payment channels. Authorities detected instances where cash-out agents attempted unauthorized fee deductions from recipients, prompting a statewide operational review to protect vulnerable citizens.

Beneficiaries who submitted applications during late Phase 1 but did not receive funds prior to September 18 are not disqualified. Their records remain securely vaulted within the central PSER portal architecture. During this scheduled administrative hiatus, IT engineers and banking partners are upgrading the biometric clearing pipeline to prevent fraud, eliminate unauthorized third-party deductions, and refine digital wallet distribution before opening subsequent funding tranches.

### Expected Timeline, Budget Expansion, and Reopening for Phase 2
The Government of Punjab, under the guidance of Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif, is preparing to launch Phase 2 Rollout 2026 during the final quarter of the year. The upcoming phase will expand provincial budget allocations beyond the inaugural Rs 5 Billion threshold, extending financial relief to an estimated 75,000 additional deserving households across southern, central, and western Punjab.

When Phase 2 opens, the digital registration portal and mobile application will resume accepting fresh submissions from eligible widows and guardians of double-parent orphans. Applicants who experienced technical errors, identity mismatches, or missed the initial submission window will be permitted to file updated applications. Beneficiaries are advised to monitor official communications on `zakat.punjab.gov.pk` and avoid unauthorized private online forums claiming early access.

## Common Rejection Reasons and How to File an Official Appeal

### How to Rectify Data Errors in the PSER Database
A widespread cause of application failure is outdated or erroneous household information stored within the Punjab Socio-Economic Registry (PSER). If a family’s survey entry lists obsolete employment, inaccurate family counts, or unverified asset ownership, the automated calculation engine assigns an inflated poverty score that disqualifies the applicant. Resolving this issue requires updating the primary household record through the official registry system.

Applicants must navigate to `pser.punjab.gov.pk` or visit a local government registration kiosk to request a dynamic survey update. The applicant must submit current utility bills, rent agreements, and verified income affidavits reflecting true socio-economic circumstances. Once registry enumerators re-evaluate the household and issue an updated Proxy Means Test (PMT) score, the revised data automatically flows into the Rehmat Card processing system for re-adjudication.

### Step-by-Step Grievance Procedure at District Social Welfare Offices
Applicants who receive an official SMS notice declaring their file rejected have the statutory right to file a formal administrative grievance. The appeal must be submitted within 30 days of receiving the rejection alert at the local District Zakat Committee office or District Social Welfare complex:

1. **Obtain the Official Grievance Form:** Collect the "Zakat & Social Welfare Appeal Application" from the facilitation desk at your local district office free of charge.
2. **Attach Supporting Documentary Proof:** Provide certified copies of your NADRA CNIC (showing updated widowhood status), computerized NADRA Death Certificates, child NADRA B-Forms, and your latest PSER survey slip.
3. **Submit Written Justification:** State clearly the administrative error leading to initial rejection (e.g., identity data lag, clerical mismatch, or corrected marital status).
4. **Acquire Official Receipt:** Secure a stamped acknowledgment receipt bearing a unique grievance tracking number from the receiving officer.
5. **District Committee Review:** The District Grievance Redressal Committee reviews the appeal during its bi-weekly session, re-verifies credentials with provincial authorities, and modifies the application status to "Approved" upon validation.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the CM Punjab Rehmat Card?
The CM Punjab Rehmat Card is an official social protection grant initiated by Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif through the Punjab Zakat & Ushr Department. It delivers a one-time unconditional cash transfer of Rs 100,000 to impoverished Muslim widows and parentless orphans residing in Punjab to alleviate poverty and support essential family needs.

### How much money is given under the CM Rehmat Card scheme in 2026?
Eligible beneficiaries receive a one-time financial grant of Rs 100,000. The full amount is transferred without any service cuts, bank fees, or third-party agent charges through verified digital wallets.

### Who is eligible for the Maryam Nawaz Rehmat Card?
Eligibility is strictly restricted to deserving Muslim widows and parentless (double-parent) orphans who are permanent residents of Punjab. Applicants must qualify as Mustahiq-e-Zakat, be registered in the Punjab Socio-Economic Registry (PSER), and have valid civil credentials authenticated by NADRA.

### Can single-parent orphans apply for the Rehmat Card?
No, children with one surviving parent do not qualify under the dedicated orphan category. Program statutes mandate that orphan benefits are reserved exclusively for double-parent orphans who have lost both biological parents, though impoverished single mothers can apply directly under the widow quota.

### Why were Rehmat Card payments stopped on September 18, 2026?
Disbursements were temporarily paused on September 18, 2026, after fulfilling the inaugural Phase 1 target of 50,000 beneficiaries and to conduct a statewide audit of payment reconciliation. The pause ensures technical improvements in biometric systems before rolling out Phase 2.

### When will the CM Rehmat Card Phase 2 registration begin?
Phase 2 Rollout 2026 is scheduled to open in late 2026 under an expanded provincial funding package. The reopening will be formally announced through the Punjab Zakat & Ushr Department portal and major media channels.

### How can I apply for the CM Rehmat Card online?
Eligible citizens can apply online by visiting the official portal at rahmatcard.punjab.gov.pk or by downloading the "CM Punjab Rahmat Card" app developed by PITB from the Google Play Store. Both platforms require an identity card number and active mobile SIM authentication.

### How do I check my Rehmat Card application status using my CNIC?
To check your application status, navigate to rahmatcard.punjab.gov.pk, input your 13-digit NADRA CNIC without spaces or dashes, and click "Check Status." You can also receive live updates by calling the provincial helpline at Helpline 1077.

### Is there any fee for registering for the CM Punjab Rehmat Card?
No, registration for the program is 100% free across all web, mobile, and physical office channels. Citizens should never pay private agents or middlemen claiming to expedite approvals or charge processing fees.

### Can beneficiaries of the CM Himmat Card also apply for the Rehmat Card?
No, beneficiaries receiving stipends from parallel provincial welfare initiatives like the CM Himmat Card Scheme are excluded from concurrent enrollment. The provincial registry automatically filters duplicate beneficiaries across programs to ensure equitable fund distribution.
