## 7. Information-Gain Pass

### What Competitors Omit or Get Wrong
1. **Outdated Status vs September 18 Pause:** Competitors continue to assert that initial Phase 1 funds are freely flowing, misleading citizens whose payments were placed on temporary hold on September 18, 2026, awaiting the Phase 2 launch.
2. **The "Widow Status" CNIC Trap:** Thousands of widows apply online and face immediate rejection because their NADRA CNIC still records their marital status as "Married" rather than updated to "Widow" with a registered Death Certificate. None of the competitors provide the exact NADRA correction step.
3. **Double-Parent vs Single-Parent Orphan Distinction:** Several competitor blogs loosely write "orphans," whereas the official gazette strictly defines eligible children as **double-parent (parentless) orphans** (دونوں والدین سے محروم یتیم بچے).
4. **Appeals and Data Correction Protocol:** None of the top 4 competitors provide a structured, actionable appeal procedure when an applicant receives a rejection SMS.

### Unique Original Elements Committed
1. **Decision & Eligibility Checklist Table:** A granular matrix detailing Category, Mandatory Document, Disqualification Triggers, and Verification Body.
2. **"What Changed in September 2026" Operational Alert Box:** Date-stamped factual breakdown explaining the September 18 payment freeze, Phase 1 audit reconciliation, and the timeline for Phase 2 portal reopening.
3. **Step-by-Step Rejection Appeal & CNIC Rectification Walkthrough:** Actionable instructions on updating NADRA records, correcting PSER household entries, and filing formal appeals with the District Zakat Committee.
